**Read: a displacement-field-driven metal–insulator crossover at charge neutrality, and a
gap that is real but small.**

At D ≈ 0 the neutrality point is **metallic** — Δ = **−0.62 ± 0.17 meV**, i.e. R_CNP *rises*
with temperature (249 Ω at 1.2 K → 360 Ω at 40 K). By D/ε₀ = +0.238 V/nm it is **insulating**
— Δ = **+1.36 ± 0.14 meV**, R_CNP falling 2975 Ω → 1175 Ω over the same range. The sign of
dR/dT flips at **D = +0.126 V/nm**, and all seven isotherms cross at that single point. The
+D flank rises at 7.9 meV per V/nm.

**This is the cleanest exclusion of ABAB in the session.** A Bernal tetralayer is a
band-overlapped semimetal: displacement field gaps each bilayer-like subband but shifts them
relative to one another, so the overlap survives and neutrality stays metallic at all D.
A metal→insulator crossover driven purely by D is not that.

**The inversion-symmetry test, done properly.** Δ(D) is plainly curved near its minimum, so
extrapolating a straight line from the +D flank back to zero — which an earlier version of
this cell did, returning a meaningless −1.12 meV intercept — is the wrong statistic. The right
one is *where the minimum sits*. A parabola over the 29 points around it gives
**D = +0.016 ± 0.004 V/nm**, against run 3's independent R_CNP minimum at
**0.000 ± 0.008 V/nm**. Both sit within ~4 % of the accessible D range of zero.

**But the magnitude does not behave like a clean band gap, and that is the honest limit
here.** 1.36 meV at D ≈ 0.25 V/nm is one to two orders of magnitude below what a rhombohedral
tetralayer's single-particle gap should be at that field. Together with R_CNP's saturation
near 3 kΩ (run 3), this says the transport is **disorder- and parallel-channel-limited**:
in-gap states and a shunt path put a floor under the resistance long before the true gap
would. That is entirely ordinary for gapped graphene multilayers — measured activation
energies routinely run 10–30× below the band gap — but it means this measurement establishes
the gap's **existence and D-dependence**, not its size. Any claim of the form "the gap is
X meV" is not supported; "a gap opens with |D| and closes near D = 0" is.

The base-temperature point is excluded from every fit: R_CNP is flat between 1.2 and 8 K,
exactly as the rig's ~8 K electron temperature predicts, so those points carry no independent
information.
