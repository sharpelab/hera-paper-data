## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side, D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`)

Run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹²) resolved the pair 7.3 + 22.0 = 29.3 T (= F_L at scale 0.886, partition 0.25/0.75 — the ABAB template) plus the sum tone; the fan now tests the **n-dependence** (Step 5): both branches must track the ABAB F(n) curves under one global scale, the sum must hold across density, and the 29.3 T tone must track f₁ + f₂ exactly (sum/breakdown) rather than its own Onsager curve (a second-domain pocket). **Design (binding numbers):** fast axis n = 3.27 → 1.60×10¹² at D = 0, 61 points (Δn = 0.028×10¹²; ray spacing at B_lo = 1 T is 4eB/h = 0.097×10¹² → 3.5 points per ray period, above the ≥ 2 floor; lower edge 1.6 — below ~2×10¹² the ABAB partner is < 3.6 T and unresolvable in any window, the recipe's stated exception regime, so the claim window is n ≳ 2). Slow axis B = 1.0 → 10 T **uniform in 1/B, 101 rows** (Δ(1/B) = 0.009 T⁻¹ ≤ 1/(3·F_L(3.27) = 33.8 T) = 0.0099 — 1/3-Nyquist at the top density; also Nyquist for the 2×22 T heavy harmonic); B_lo = 1.0 T from run 6's onsets (light tone censored ≤ 0.5 T, heavy 1.4 T): rows below 1 T would double the row count for periods of the light tone only. δF per column = 1/(1/1 − 1/10) = 1.1 T. Dynamic range: fixed 100 µV = 10 kΩ FS for the worst corner (n = 1.6, 10 T, ν ≈ 6–8 with the ρxy admixture); SdH amplitudes of ~10 Ω are ~12 readout quanta at this range (run 6 data show ~4 nV granularity at 50 µV) — acceptable, clipping is the asymmetric failure. Dwell: fast_delay 1.2 s = 4 τ (the per-column FFT runs along B, the slow axis; the fast-axis lag only shifts n by ~0.006×10¹²). **Price** (run-5 gate rate 1.3 s/pt + run-6 magnet overhead): 101 rows × (61 × 1.5 s + 3 s settle + 3 s slow_delay + ~1 s flyback) ≈ 2.8 h + 9 T traverse 66 min → **≈ 3 h 55 m**, ending at 10 T with gates parked (the LK ladder then runs field sweeps down from the top). Checkpoints: first two rows (rail? leakage? 61-point rows sane), row ~25 (2 T) and row ~60 (4.5 T): per-column FFT on the landed rows — abort if the top-n columns show no tones by 4.5 T or the low-n corner rails.
