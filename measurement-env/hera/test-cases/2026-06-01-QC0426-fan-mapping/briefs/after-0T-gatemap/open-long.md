# Brief

brought up and characterized near zero field: instruments and gate ranges are set
(k_bg ∈ [−5, 4.8], k_tg ∈ [−6.5, 7.5], top gate on the DC205 / `DC_bias`), the

**Landau-fan / quantum-Hall structure and its displacement-field dependence**.
