# Brief

QC0426 (trilayer graphene, VTI fridge, base T ≈ 1.2 K) is characterized: gate
ranges set, a 0 T TG-BG gate map, a Landau fan at the charge-neutral D = 0 line
(with and without Si-gate screening), and contact + 6 T checks are all in hand.
The contacts were just reconfigured (sr0 on 42, sr1 = 09-01, Si = +95 V) and a
**0-field density cut along the D = 0.25 gate-line** (data/79) shows a clean Rxx(n)
— a sharp charge-neutrality peak at n ≈ 0 and a metallic background away from it,
with sr3 carrying a Hall-like component.

The campaign goal is to map the **Landau-fan / quantum-Hall structure as a function
of carrier density and displacement field**. Given the state above, **decide and
take the next measurement.** The (n, D) convention is the device's own
capacitance-based calc_nD (TG on the DC205 / `DC_bias`, BG on k_bg, constant-D
lines `vbg = vtg/1.3 + const`); current is sr0 with gain division. Choice of axes,
ranges, and resolution is yours.
