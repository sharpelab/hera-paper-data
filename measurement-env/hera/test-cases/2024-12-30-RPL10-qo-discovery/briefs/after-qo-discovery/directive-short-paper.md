# Brief

Measure the **temperature dependence** of the transport at the quantum-oscillation
operating point from data/159: drive the sample heater to warm the device and map
the response vs temperature (oscillation amplitude → effective mass; zero-bias /
SC feature → Tc). Land the sweep.

**Depth:** bulletproof / paper-grade — this is going into a figure that has to
survive referee scrutiny (an effective-mass extraction and/or a Tc determination).
Fine, well-spaced temperature points across the relevant range, generous settle
and averaging at each T, repeats/controls to rule out heater hysteresis and
drift, and reproducibility. Spend what it takes.
