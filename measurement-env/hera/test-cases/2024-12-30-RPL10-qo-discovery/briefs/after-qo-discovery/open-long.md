# Brief

## Where the campaign stands
This is a rhombohedral (ABC-stacked) graphene device with known superconducting
features (the SC1 / SC1' domes) and a "bubble" feature from prior cooldowns. This
run has, in order: mapped the (n, D) phase diagram in field (B = ±100 mT),
checked the bubble for multiferroism via Hall, taken a high-res B = 0 gate map,
perused DC-bias × density linecuts at several D, and then — the freshest work —
swept **n-vs-B Landau fans at D = 0.31 V/nm** (data/153–159) that **confirm a
clean quantum-oscillation regime** at base temperature, B up to 1 T, in the
n ≈ −2.9 to −2.1 (×10¹²) window.

## What's now in hand vs. open
You have established *that* oscillations exist and *roughly where*. What you do
**not** yet have: a clean extraction of the oscillation period (1/B) and
Fermi-surface degeneracy from a proper 2D fan; the **carrier effective mass**
(needs the temperature-damping of the oscillation amplitude); the SC features'
**transition temperature** and field response; and the D-dependence of any of
the above. Several legitimate next axes compete — temperature, more/finer field
fans at other D, the SC dome's field response (Fraunhofer), or returning to the
(n, D) phase diagram to place the QO window in context. There is no single
forced move; the judgment is *which* axis buys the most right now.

## State / resources
- Operating near D = 0.31 V/nm, n ≈ −2.9 to −2.1 (×10¹²), V_si = −20 V, base
