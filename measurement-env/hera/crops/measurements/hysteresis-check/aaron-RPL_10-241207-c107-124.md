This is the single most-pedagogical hysteresis section in the corpus because it shows three distinct flavors back-to-back:
- **Flavor C** (cells 107–110): Python `for` loop over a 1D line of (n, D) points, each producing a paired forward/backward field-axis hysteresis (22 files per `for` loop, each at one D-step). The per-iteration `s.add_comment(f'{i}: ({Vtg}, {Vbg})')` writes the index/(Vtg, Vbg) pair into metadata in `comment_to_gates`-parseable form.
- **Flavor A — gate-axis** (cells 111–116): paired `s.megasweep` calls with reversed slow axis. The "fast axis is vbg, sweeping from pos to neg vbg" / "neg to pos vbg" `add_comment` text labels the direction explicitly. Two cells per pair (a forward megasweep + a defensive `s.ramp(mag.field, 0)` rebuild + a backward megasweep). `plot_hysteresis(file_id)` would consume (cell-112-file, cell-114-file).
- **Supplementary 2D bubble megasweeps** (cells 117–124): denser 51×51 remapping of the bubble, NOT a hysteresis check per se. Recipe consumers should not include 117–124 when extracting the hysteresis pattern.

The bubble in question is the SC dome at (n, D) ≈ (−0.75 to −0.53, 0.2 to 0.3 V/nm) in this RPL_10 cooldown — the campaign across 11 D-points × 3 n-values × 2 sweep directions per D-point = 66 files is a comprehensive sweep over the bubble's hysteresis at all relevant points.

The `s.add_comment` is on for every cell, in two distinct forms: the section-level recipe-tag (cell 108: `'MC3, Vsi = -20V, hysteresis loops around the bubble, n_e = -0.75'`) plus per-iteration structured tag inside the `for` loop. **Both are needed** — the recipe-name tag for fuzzy matching, the structured tag for `comment_to_gates`-driven analysis. The autorange callback is *on* for cell 108 but commented out for cells 109/110 (Aaron tweaked the autorange on/off mid-campaign).
