The section uses all three flavors:
- **Flavor A** (cells 64–74): full (V_tg, V_bg) megasweeps at discrete V_si = +20, −20 V (with mid-section interludes for sensitivity adjustment, mc_3 redefinition, and the "rat cleansing" reset). Each gatemap is a 201×201 (or 101×101) megasweep with `slow_delay=3, fast_delay=1`. The autorange callback `register_run_before(autorange_sr830s, ...)` is on for the +20 V cells, off for the −20 V cells (commented out).
- **Flavor C** (cell 76): Python `for` loop over `Vsis = np.linspace(10, -40, 11)` — at each V_si, ramp the Si BG, settle 10 s, run forward and backward TG sweeps. Per-iteration `s.add_comment(f'{i}: {Vsi}')` writes the index/Vsi mapping into the sweep's metadata for downstream parsing. This is structurally identical to the gate-leakage-survey Flavor B for-loop pattern but applied to V_si instead of V_tg.
- Cell 77: a follow-up small gatemap at V_si = −30 V (Flavor A again, narrower range) — picking up on a V_si value that looked promising in cell 76.
