## Judgment calls
- **Type boundary (inference vs. verdict):** intermediate stacking-template statements made mid-campaign (e.g. "this is the ABA template" after the fan, "ABA-consistent" after the first SdH spot check) were coded as `inference`, not `verdict`, per the spec's guidance that a strength-qualified intermediate call is an inference. Only the Step-7 final-call document's three explicit determinations (overall call, and the two per-region rows plus the electron-side row, merged into 3 verdict entries: overall+per-region-table, and the electron-side row split out since it carries a distinct "uninformative" qualifier) were coded `verdict`.
- **Granularity — per-row monitoring chatter:** the agent made many near-identical "row N nominal, holding" observations while watching long sweeps (gate map, hole/electron SdH runs, the fan, the mass ladder). Pure "nominal, holding, no new information" pings with no new number or interpretation were treated as logistics/status and dropped. Rows that introduced a *new* number (a resistance value, a railing risk, a frequency, a mass, a temperature-hold band) were kept as individual observation claims — this is why the fan and gate-map sections have several sequential but numerically distinct entries rather than one merged entry.
- **Merging:** the four gate-liveness legs were merged into a small number of claims (per-leg completion + all-four-legs-clean + the post-hoc "gate C PASS" analysis paragraph) rather than one claim per leg, since the four legs are procedurally identical and the interesting content is the aggregate result plus the one leg-1 completion example used to anchor the "clean" language.
- **Design-cell decisions vs. their cost/checkpoint boilerplate:** each measurement cell's markdown carries a "Cost: ~X h, checkpoints: ..., abort criteria: ..." paragraph. These recur in near-identical form across cells (first-rows check, then periodic watch, abort on railing/leakage/T-excursion). Time-cost estimates and generic abort-criteria language were not separately extracted as claims (not something a domain expert would fact-check as physics) — only the substantive experimental-design choices (ranges, sensitivities, bias currents, step spacing, dwell/settle times, and any explicitly-flagged trade-off such as the Si-gate omission or the settle-time bend) were kept as `decision` claims.
- **Excluded as spec'd:** status/logistics ("sweep started", "waiting on X", "arming the monitor"), tool-call/kernel-plumbing chatter (import fixes, column re-indexing after a metadata layout change, kernel-binding probe), and restatements of the brief/investigation recipe names were not extracted as claims even when they appeared inside an otherwise-claim-bearing assistant turn — only the substantive numeric/interpretive/decision content of those turns was kept.
- **Redaction:** per instructions, all dates, the device identifier, session/run identifiers, corpus hashes, and personal names were scrubbed from `claim`, `context`, and `evidence` (verified via regex sweep — no residual date/device-name/personal-name/run-number tokens found in those three fields after a follow-up fix removed five run-number mentions from `evidence` text that had slipped through the first pass). The `source` field intentionally retains notebook cell numbers / transcript line numbers, since that field is for the blind-map and is stripped before scoring.
- **t_rel_h:** computed from each claim's nearest anchoring assistant-message timestamp in the transcript, relative to the first assistant message in the session. For claims sourced purely from notebook markdown cells (which are undated), the timestamp of the transcript turn immediately preceding/announcing that cell's landing was used as a close approximation; all such claims have `t_rel_h` populated (none required `null`).
