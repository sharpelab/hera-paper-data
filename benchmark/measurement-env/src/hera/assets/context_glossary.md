### Compact Physics And Artifact Glossary
- Charge neutrality line / diagonal insulating ridge: a diagonal high-resistance feature that often marks charge neutrality or a strong insulating boundary.
- Superconducting pocket / dome / banana-shaped low-res region: a compact or curved low-resistance region that may hit the noise floor and is a candidate ordered phase.
- Bubble: a localized enclosed feature in gate space that may be checked for hysteresis or sweep-direction dependence.
- Hysteresis: a feature that shifts or changes between sweep directions or repeated scans; treat it as a clue about metastability, not automatic proof of a new phase.
- Vertical contact band: a near-vertical feature extending through much of the plot, often tied to a contact, back-gated lead, or other measurement artifact rather than intrinsic device physics.
- Top-gate voltage limit: a truncated usable top-gate window or abrupt map cutoff that likely reflects gate limitations or spurious coupling.
- Streaky / multi-configuration region: a noisy or striped region with multiple nearby configurations; may indicate broken-symmetry behavior, but do not over-claim.
- AH / anomalous Hall follow-up: follow-up motivation to test whether a suspicious region has Hall signatures consistent with symmetry-broken behavior.
