### Expert Output Preferences

- Prefer a compact output set. Most scans should yield about 2 to 4 findings, not an exhaustive inventory of every visible pattern.
- Prioritize physically interpretable structures over generic visual texture.
- Prefer expert-style findings that name the likely physical role of a feature, not just its appearance.
- For example, prefer phrases like:
  - "charge-neutrality line"
  - "possible superconducting candidate pocket"
  - "streaky region suggestive of multiple configurations or possible broken symmetry"
  - "normal-state band adjacent to the low-resistance pocket"
- Avoid generic labels like "bright band", "green area", or "adjacent resistive region" unless no more physically meaningful interpretation is justified.
- Emphasize features that support an experimental hypothesis or follow-up plan:
  - charge-neutrality / main insulating boundaries
  - superconducting or other low-resistance candidate regions
  - dome cuts, trenches, or sharp diagonal boundaries
  - structured streaky regions that may indicate multiple configurations or broken symmetry
- Include artifacts only when they materially affect interpretation of the scan:
  - vertical contact or lead bands
  - gate-voltage limits or truncation windows
  - coarse-resolution or low-quality scan labels when they change how the scan should be interpreted
- Avoid weak, low-value observations that do not change the physics story or next-step decisions.
- Use approximate but physically faithful localization. Broad boxes or simple point estimates are preferred over fake precision.
- Prefer cautious language such as "candidate", "possible", or "likely" when the plot alone does not justify a definitive claim.
- When a visual feature suggests a stronger interpretation but the evidence is not decisive, state the interpretation in cautious form rather than backing off to a purely generic visual label.
- Prefer "possible TRS-broken variability", "possible multi-configuration region", or "candidate normal-state band" over a visually correct but physically empty description.
- Favor findings that naturally motivate a concrete follow-up measurement, such as:
  - phase checks near superconducting candidates
  - non-linear IV or critical current versus field
  - anomalous Hall or SdH measurements
  - hysteresis or sweep-direction checks
  - linecuts across a trench, dome, or boundary

### Inclusion Heuristic

- Include a finding if it is one of the main structures a human expert would point to first and it changes the interpretation of the scan.
- Exclude a finding if it is mostly background texture, redundant with a stronger nearby feature, or too speculative to motivate action.
