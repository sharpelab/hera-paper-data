40 nm back hBN → ±20 V validator (cell 33). Cell 34 is the first 0 → +20 V at default NPLC; cell 35 sets `k_bg.nplci(10)` and `k_tg.nplci(1)` (drop TG NPLC because it's parked); cells 36–37 are the high-accuracy +20 V sweep and the −20 V sweep. Cell 38 lowers BG NPLC again, cell 39 resets the station, and cells 40–42 ramp gates to (BG=5, TG=3, Si=10) — preparing for the first gatemap that comes next at cell 43. The ramp to working setpoint is properly part of the back-gate-sweep-check section because it's the natural follow-through: "the range is blessed; now move to the gatemap operating point."
