16 nm top hBN → ±8 V validator (cell 54). The pattern is: set Si BG to its cooldown value (cell 52, V_si=48), do a small 0 → +1 V sanity poke at the default validator (cell 53), widen the validator to ±8 V (cell 54), then 0 → +8 V (cell 55) and 0 → −8 V (cell 58) — cells 56–57 are blank. `sweep.AsyncStation` is used directly (no `async_station` wrapper class) — this is the post-class style.
