# Brief

Map the supercurrent's perpendicular-field response: **dV/dI vs (Idc, B⊥)** at
the SC operating point from data/84. Land the sweep.

**Depth:** bulletproof / paper-grade — this is going in a figure that has to

to rule out drift and artifacts, and reproducibility. Spend what it takes.
