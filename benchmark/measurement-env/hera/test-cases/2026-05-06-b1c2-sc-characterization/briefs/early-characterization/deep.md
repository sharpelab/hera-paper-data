# Brief — initial (n, D) characterization

- **Objective.** The device is freshly at base temperature with validated gates
  and no science yet. Establish its baseline **(n, D) transport landscape** —
  where it conducts, where the charge-neutrality point sits, and whether any
  band-insulator edges or superlattice features show up.
- **Target observable.** Lock-in resistance over the (Vtg, Vbg) plane, read in
  terms of (n, D) via the cell-2 helpers.
- **Operating point / scope.** Base T, Beff = 0. Cover the accessible gate
  window within the validators (`k_tg ∈ (−7, 6)`, `k_bg ∈ (−4, 4.4)`).
- **Depth.** Deep investigation — a map you can actually read the phase
  structure off: resolution fine enough to locate the CNP and any feature edges,
  delay long enough for the gate-line RC to settle. Not a paper figure yet, but a
  map you'd trust to pick where to look next.
- **Success / escalate.** Done when the (n, D) landscape is mapped well enough to
  choose regions worth following up. Escalate if gates leak, the validators look
  wrong, or the device appears dead.
- **Constraints.** Stay within the validators; the human runs every cell.

Land the sweep.
