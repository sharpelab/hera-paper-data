# Brief — effective mass at the dome point [PAPER-GRADE]

Same objective as the deep version: extract the **effective mass m\*** at the
dome point (~n = 1, D = 1, V_Si = 48 V) from the temperature damping of the
quantum-oscillation amplitude — **field sweeps at fixed (n, D) over a temperature
ladder**, fit to the Lifshitz–Kosevich thermal factor.

**Depth:** bulletproof / paper-grade — this m\* (and the Dingle/quantum-lifetime
analysis it enables) is going into a figure that must survive referee scrutiny.
That means: a **fine, wide field range** with enough oscillation periods that a
1/B FFT and a per-period amplitude extraction are both clean; a **dense
temperature ladder** spanning base to full damping with enough points to
over-constrain the LK fit; **adequate averaging / lock-in time constant** for
high SNR; **stable, well-calibrated thermometry** at each setpoint (let T settle,
log it); and **reproducibility** (a repeat sweep or up/down field to rule out
drift and hysteresis). Confirm the operating point hasn't moved between
temperatures. Spend what it takes.

Land the sweep.
