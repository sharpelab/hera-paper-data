can be found at https://wiki.sharpelab.science/doc/jm_r_s_4-AddNfD4nBj

## Raman findings

The Raman indicates a domain of rhombohedral that was subsequently isolated. The rhombohedral domain survived the cutting process but no further Raman was performed during device assembly. 

Some spectra look like noise due to the laser cutting

Call: Isolated rhombohedral domain prior to stacking

Strength: strong

## Transport findings

We utilized the following config for transport measurements

| role          | label   |
| ------------- | ------- |
| source        | T_SD    |
| drain (`sr0`) | B_SD    |
| `sr1` (Rxx)   | B_SD–L4 |
| `sr2` (Rxx)   | L4–L1   |
| `sr4` (Rxx)   | L3–L2   |

In a Landau fan, the different contact pairs provide different clarity, possibly due to either the contact quality or local inhomogeneity. Despite this, the Landau fans were all clearly consistent with Bernal with no evidence of rhombohedral. 

## Fused call

The device stacking order changed from rhombohedral to Bernal during stacking. The transformation seems to be complete in that the resulting device is homogeneously Bernal.

 Strength: strong
