## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n = −1.22 … +3.22×10¹² × B = 0.25 → 10 T

**Plan step 4** (`hera_scratch/plan.md`) — the decisive measurement for the stacking call, and the operator's requested next move.

**Why a fan, and why now.** Runs 6/7 (analysed at the handoff, ana below) established that this device quantizes properly at base T: R_xx → 0 minima at 4.3 / 7.2 T on the electron side and 1.8 T on the hole side, SdH onset ≤ 0.3 T (e) / 0.71 T (h). What they cannot do is count Fermi surfaces. Their FFTs are harmonic-rich because the lineshape is quantum-Hall-like, not sinusoidal — `sdh_onset` flags run 6's 14.1 / 22.2 / 29.5 T tones as ~2×/3×/4× of 7.1 T — and a second *series region* (run 5: ridge B, electron-doped by 0.43×10¹² relative to A) counterfeits a second pocket in any 1D trace. A fan separates all three cases by construction: fan-line slopes are dn/dB = ν·e/h, so **two Fermi surfaces give families with different slopes from one origin; two series regions give families with the same slopes offset by a constant Δn; one band gives one family converging on n = 0.** ABAB predicts two families (the minority one not extrapolating to n = 0 — band overlap), ABCA one 4-fold family on n = 0. The ν-slopes also calibrate the absolute density scale, which is still only the 9.5 nm hBN geometry prior (run 5 fixed the *ratio*, not the scale) — every F_L quoted so far carries that ~10 % error.

**Design.** B is the slow axis because the magnet blocks and is rate-fixed (0.137 T/min); one monotonic ascent, no re-traverse. Fast axis is the (V_tg, V_bg) pair co-swept along D = 0 via `multimegasweep`, 111 points over n = −1.22 … +3.22×10¹² — the full D = 0 line the validators allow, both carrier signs and the CNP, at 0.0404×10¹² steps (11.7 mV TG, 15.2 mV BG; 17/11 mV of margin to the TG limits). That is 24 points per 4-fold LL period at 10 T and 4.7 at 2 T, so LL indexing is sampled everywhere above ~1.5 T. B: 79 rows at 125 mT from 0.25 to 10.0 T (the session cap); at 125 mT a ν = 17 line moves 1.5 fast steps per row, so fan lines connect row-to-row up to the high-|n| corner. **Coverage trade:** uniform-in-B rows favour fan-line tracing over the 1/B-FFT read — at 125 mT the FFT window is Nyquist-limited below ~2.5 T, so the fan will *not* re-measure frequencies as finely as runs 6/7 did. That is deliberate: the spot checks already own the fine-1/B read at two densities, and family-counting by ν-indexing is the stronger evidence. A fan sampled uniformly in 1/B instead would need ~120 rows to reach F_max = 25 T and cost an extra ~45 min for a read we partly have.

**Settling and its one systematic.** fast_delay 0.8 s = 2.7 τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB (group delay 4τ ≈ 1.2 s ≈ 1.3 fast steps). The raster is unidirectional, so this is a *constant* n-lag of ≈ 0.05×10¹² identical in every row: it shifts fan-line **intercepts**, not slopes — and the slopes are the read. It is degenerate with n_offset and can be measured out against run 5's D = 0 cut (same direction, fast_delay 1.0 s) if the intercepts matter. Sensitivity fixed at **100 µV = 10 kΩ FS** for the whole fan (no autorange mid-row): run 5's whole map peaked at 3.07 kΩ, and the ν = 0 CNP band at 10 T is the one place that could exceed 10 kΩ — if it rails it rails in a narrow n band and reads as "insulating", which is itself information, whereas dropping to 20 kΩ FS would triple the noise (measured 0.06–0.10 % of FS in run 6) and bury the 10–20 Ω oscillations at 1–2 T.

**Preconditions to eyeball before it runs:** T_sample 1.0–1.5 K, |B| < 0.05 T (the handoff close-out left +0.003 T), V_si = 0, gate nplci 1/1, PS heater + output on, thermometry logfile advancing. Leak guard unchanged (>1 nA on either Keithley → interrupt, gates parked); note the column indices moved to 5/6 because `multimegasweep` writes three axis columns.

**Price and checkpoints.** Priced from this session's own runs: 1.10 s/pt in-row at fast_delay 1.0 and 5.8 s row turnaround (run 5), 438 s/T + 3 s settle per field set (runs 6/7) → 79 × (54.8 + 5 + 99.9 + 2.8) s ≈ **3 h 35 m**, of which 71 min is the unavoidable magnet traverse. Ends at 10 T with the gates parked at 0 — the next step rides the field down rather than re-traversing. Checkpoints: **first two rows** (0.25 / 0.375 T) — signal scale against run 5's D = 0 cut (~60 Ω metallic, ~240 Ω at the CNP), no rail, no leakage, field readback tracking; then every ~15 rows, re-forming the partial fan and checking that fan lines are emerging and countable; a decision point at **B ≈ 6 T** (~row 47, ~2 h 20 m) on whether the family count is already settled. Because B ascends monotonically, an interrupt at any row leaves a complete, usable fan up to that field — graceful degradation, so the abort criterion is "the read is already made" or "the channel is railed across the whole row", not a wash-out risk.
