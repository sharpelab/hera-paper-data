## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), B 0.9 → 10 T × n −1.2 … +3.2×10¹²

**Plan step 4** (`hera_scratch/plan.md`) — the measurement the stacking call rests on. Runs 6/7 (`sdh-spot-check`) proved the device oscillates hard (ΔR 50–200 Ω on a 2–4 Ω noise floor, onset ≲ 0.5 T on the electron side, 0.71 T on the hole side) but their single-density FFTs are **not decisive**: at n = +3.2×10¹² the tracked tones are 7.1 T plus a 22–30 T group that `sdh_onset` reads as 3×/4× harmonics, and at n = −1.2×10¹² the strongest tone (12.3 T, SNR 124) sits on F_L = 12.4 T. One density cannot separate a two-band partition from harmonics, and it cannot separate either from the **series-mosaic confound** run 5 exposed (two parallel CNP ridges in this pair; region B doped +0.43×10¹² vs A).

**What the fan settles, and how.** F(n) at D = 0 over both carrier signs:
- **ABCA** → one tone on the full-density line F_L = n·h/4e (slope 4.14 T per 10¹² cm⁻²);
- **ABAB** → two tones summing to F_L, partition ≈ 0.23/0.77, *the same on both signs* (inversion-symmetric);
- **ABCB** → two tones summing to F_L with an *electron–hole-asymmetric* partition.
- **series mosaic** (the confound) → two tones of **equal** slope h/4e offset by a constant 4.4 T — separable from a band partition only by measuring the *slope*, which is exactly what a wide-n fan does and a spot check cannot.

**Grid.** Fast = (V_tg, V_bg) co-swept along the D = 0 line (`multimegasweep`), n = −1.20 … +3.20×10¹² at Δn = 0.05×10¹² (89 pts) — the full D = 0 window the validators allow (V_tg −0.277 … +0.984 inside [−0.3, +1.0]; V_bg −0.465 … +1.166 inside [−1.3, +2.0]); Δn resolves the ν = 1 gap at 10 T (0.242×10¹²) ≈ 5 points wide. Slow = `field_invB(0.9, 10.0, 102)` — **uniform in 1/B**, so `sdh_fft` FFTs the record with no resample: δ(1/B) = 0.0100 1/T → Nyquist 50 T (above F_L,max = 33.1 T and its 3rd harmonic of the light tone), resolution δF = 0.99 T (the ABAB 7.5/25.6 T split is 18 δF wide). B_lo = 0.9 T from the step-3 onsets. **Ascending**, because the magnet sits at 0 T: data starts in 7 min instead of after a 73-min blind ramp, and ending at 10 T turns the close-out ramp into a possible measured ride-down (plan step 6) rather than dead time.

**Coverage trade.** Two things are excluded. (i) **D ≠ 0** — a D-fan would double or triple the cost and run 5 already showed the CNP gap grows with |D| for all three candidate parents, so it is not a stacking discriminator here; if step 5 is ambiguous it is the first detour to price. (ii) **B < 0.9 T** — uniform-1/B sampling makes the low-B end expensive in rows (B_lo = 0.7 T would cost +32 rows ≈ +50 min) and buys < 1 oscillation period for any tone light enough to matter there.

**Dwell / sensitivity.** `fast_delay` 1.0 s ≈ 3.3τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB — knowingly under the 7–10τ settling floor, as in run 5: the residual lag is a fixed function of position *within* a row, so it is common to every row and cannot bias the FFT along B (it shifts the effective n by ≲ 5 % of one 0.05×10¹² step). `slow_delay` 3.0 s on top of the driver's 3 s magnet settle. `sr1` fixed at **100 µV = 10 kΩ full scale** — 17× the largest R seen this cooldown (590 Ω, run 7 at 10 T), sized per the run-4 lesson for the corner we have never measured (the CNP column at 10 T); SdH at ~100 Ω is 1 % of range against a 3–5 Ω noise floor. The CNP column may still soft-clip at high B — that is an accepted and informative outcome (a railing ν = 0), and the F(n) read restricts to |n| > 0.2×10¹² if it bleeds.

**Price** (from this session's own rates: run 5 gatemap 1.17 s/pt at `fast_delay` 1 s incl. row overhead; runs 6/7 3.05 s/pt magnet overhead, both of which came in *under* their brackets): 102 rows × (89 × ~1.0 s + 3.5 s flyback + 3 s) ≈ 2 h 45 m, + 9.1 T traverse 66 min + 0.9 T start ramp 7 min ≈ **4 h 15 m** (bracket 4 h 00 m – 4 h 30 m). Ends at **B = 10 T**, gates parked at 0 V.

**Preconditions the cell prints before it moves anything:** T_sample 1.0–1.5 K with a live (advancing) logfile, |B| < 0.05 T, V_si = 0, gate `nplci` = 1/1, PS heater on + output enabled, and every (V_tg, V_bg) on the D = 0 line inside the committed validators. **Checkpoints:** (1) first 2 rows, ~5 min in — rail on `sr1`, gate leakage, I_bias, the field readback tracking its setpoint, and R across the CNP column; (2) ~row 25 (≈1 h, B ≈ 1.16 T) and ~row 60 (≈2 h 20 m, B ≈ 1.96 T) — re-form the partial map, confirm fan lines emerging from the CNP and no CNP bleed; abandon if there is no oscillation content anywhere in n by ~2 T (which would contradict runs 6–7 and mean something moved). Leak guard (>1 nA either gate → interrupt, gates parked) stays on.
