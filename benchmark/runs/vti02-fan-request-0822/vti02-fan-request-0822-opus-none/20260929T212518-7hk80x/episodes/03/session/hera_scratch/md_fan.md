## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan`) along D = 0, B 1 → 10 T × n −1.2 … +3.2×10¹²

Plan step **S4** (`hera_scratch/plan.md`) — the decisive measurement for the stacking call.

**Why this, now.** Runs 6 and 7 (`sdh-spot-check`, n = +3.2 and −1.2×10¹² on D = 0) are both complete and analysed at handoff. Restricted to the Nyquist-valid window B ≥ 1 T (below 1 T the 50 mT field grid aliases anything above ~3 T), the dominant SdH frequency is **F = 29.5 T at n = +3.2×10¹²** and **F = 12.3 T at n = −1.2×10¹²** → F/|n| = 9.2 and 10.25 T per 10¹² cm⁻² against h/4e = 10.34. That is **one 4-fold-degenerate Fermi surface carrying the full density, on both carrier signs**, to within the ~10 % uncertainty of the c_tg geometry prior. The 7.3 / 6.6 T "fundamentals" the tone-tracker reports are a harmonic ladder thrown off very sharp (R → 0) minima — their F/|n| is 2.3 vs 5.2 across the two spots, so they do not scale with n and cannot be Fermi-surface frequencies. **One frequency family argues against ABAB and against ABCB**, both of which put two inequivalent sub-bands at the Fermi level. Two spot densities and a 1.1–1.8 T spectral resolution are not enough to close that out: the fan tests it *continuously* in n, and adds the filling-factor ladder, which the spot checks cannot give at all.

**What this establishes.** (1) Whether the single frequency family persists across the whole D = 0 line — one fan of gap lines out of the CNP, or two interpenetrating families with different slope sets. (2) The **filling-factor ladder** — Δν between consecutive gaps and the ν of the first gap flanking charge neutrality. (3) Whether a ν = 0 insulator develops at **D = 0** and from what field (run 5 already has the D-dependence at B = 0: R_CNP 240 Ω at D ≈ 0 → ~2.4 kΩ by |D| ≈ 0.25 V/nm). (4) A **self-calibration of c_tg** from the fan-line slopes, which retires the 9.5 nm geometry prior from every number downstream.

**Design.** Slow axis is the field — it blocks at a fixed 0.137 T/min, so it can only be the slow axis — ascending 1.0 → 10.0 T in 0.1 T, 91 rows, starting where the magnet sits (0 T) so nothing is traversed twice; the fan ends at 10 T and the close-out ramp brings it down. Uniform in B, not in 1/B: this is a fan-line map, and runs 6/7 already hold fine-1/B spectra at the two endpoint densities (for column FFTs, use B ≥ 3 T, where the 0.1 T grid is Nyquist-clean to 46 T). Fast axis is (V_tg, V_bg) co-swept along D = 0 via `multimegasweep`, n = −1.20 → +3.20×10¹² in 141 points, Δn = 3.14×10¹⁰ cm⁻² — **7.7 points per filling factor at 10 T, 3.1 at 4 T**, so a Δν = 4 ladder is resolved over the whole map and single-ν (symmetry-broken) gaps are resolved above ~4 T. The endpoints are *exactly* the run-6 and run-7 spot voltages, so the fan's edge columns cross-validate against those two records. **sr1 at 500 µV (50 kΩ full scale)**: sized to the worst case on the map — the ν = 0 CNP column at 10 T — not to the SdH amplitude, because this rig's lock-in noise is input-referred ≈ 20 nV and flat across 20 → 100 µV (measured, run 4 vs run 5), so range costs nothing in noise. 50 kΩ is 200× the B = 0, D = 0 CNP resistance; expected SdH amplitudes are 50–600 Ω against a ~2 Ω noise floor.

**Coverage trade, stated.** `fast_delay = 0.7 s` is 2.3 τ at the front panel's τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB/oct, below the 7–10 τ step-settling floor. This is a continuously-swept axis, not a step measurement: the 4-pole group delay (4 τ = 1.2 s) is a **1.4-point lag**, i.e. a uniform 4.4×10¹⁰ cm⁻² shift in n — 0.18 of a filling factor at 10 T — which shifts the map rigidly and cancels against the CNP-referenced fan, and does not touch fan-line slopes. Buying the full settling floor would cost ~2 h on top. Also excluded: n beyond +3.20/−1.20×10¹² (the D = 0 line is TG-limited at ±0.02 V from the operating box — not probed), and any D ≠ 0 fan (a second fan at finite D would cost another ~4.5 h and is not needed unless S4 leaves ABCA vs ABCB open).

**Price.** Ramp floor (10 − 1) T ÷ 0.137 T/min = 66 min, plus 91 rows × (141 pts × 0.83 s + 3.5 s flyback + 3 s slow_delay + 3 s magnet settle) = 192 min → 4 h 18 m nominal; the per-point overhead is calibrated on run 5 (median dt 1.098 s at fast_delay 1.0 ⇒ 0.098 s/pt) plus ~30 ms of extra Keithley stepping for the co-swept back gate. Priced at **4 h 45 m** — this session's actuals have run 5–10 % over even the upper bracket.

**Preconditions checked before anything moves:** thermometry logfile live and advancing, T_sample 1.0–1.5 K, |B| < 0.1 T (the handoff left the magnet at 0), V_si = 0, gate-current NPLC = 1 on both Keithleys, PS heater on and output enabled, and every computed (V_tg, V_bg) inside the committed validators with D = 0 to round-off.

**Checkpoints** (I am watching all of them; abort is `nb-collab interrupt`, which stops at the next safe point and keeps the partial run):
1. **First rows, ~5 min in (B = 1.0–1.3 T):** signal present across the line, nothing railing — in particular the CNP column — gate leakage flat at the known ±110 pA flyback transient, field readback tracking setpoints. Wrong sensitivity dies here, not four hours in.
2. **~B = 4 T (≈ 1 h 50 m):** re-form the partial fan and count gap lines. Fan lines visible and countable → continue. Visible but only at the map's high-|n| edge → continue, the high-B rows are still ahead. No structure beyond the MR background → abandon (the read is resolution-limited at T_e ≈ 8 K) and fall back to S5, a temperature dependence at the run-6 spot for m*, which costs ~2 h.
3. **~B = 7 T (≈ 3 h 20 m):** interim filling-factor read and the one-vs-two-families verdict.
4. **Completion:** full fan → ana notebook, then the call.

A leak guard trips the sweep at |I_gate| > 1 nA on either Keithley; the gates ramp to 0 V on any exit, normal or interrupted.
