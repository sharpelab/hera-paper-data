## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`) — the stacking discriminator

**Why this, now.** The spot checks are ambiguous *by construction*, and one density cannot fix them. Run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹², `data/6`) is superb data — R_xx minima reach ~0 Ω above 2 T, oscillations from below 1 T — but its FFT over 2–10 T returns **30.0 / 22.5 / 15.0 / 7.5 T**, an exactly commensurate ladder. Two readings fit it equally well: (a) **ABCA** — one 4-fold-degenerate Fermi surface at F = 30 T carrying the whole density (F_L(3.2×10¹²) = 33.1 T from the geometry prior, 9 % high, i.e. within the uncalibrated capacitance scale), with the lower tones as harmonic/symmetry-breaking structure of a strongly non-sinusoidal oscillation; (b) **ABAB** — two bilayer-like subbands at **7.5 + 22.5 = 30 T**, closing the sum rule ΣF_i = |n|h/4e, with 15 T the 2nd harmonic of the light tone and 30 T the breakdown/sum orbit. Run 7 (n = −1.2×10¹², `data/7`) gives 5.0 / 12.5 T against F_L = 12.4 T — same knot. `sdh_onset` on run 6 separates them only suggestively: the ~7.1 T tone is still above threshold at the record's 0.3 T edge while the 22.2 / 29.5 T tones onset at 1.33 / 1.91 T.

**The discriminator is F(n), not F.** Harmonics keep an exactly integer ratio at *every* density; two real subbands do not — their ratio drifts with n because the bands have different DOS, and in Bernal tetralayer the second subband has a different band edge, so below a threshold |n| only one tone exists and a **kink** appears where the second turns on. ABCA gives one tone tracking F_L linearly through the origin on both carrier signs; ABCB gives two strongly unequal tones with marked particle–hole asymmetry. This fan measures F_i(n) on both signs in one run, and the sum rule doubles as the absolute calibration of c_tg/c_bg (run 5 fixed only their *ratio*, 0.773; the scale is still the 9.5/11 nm geometry prior).

**Design choices.** Field is the expensive axis (0.137 T/min, blocking) so it is the *slow* one, traversed once, monotonically up from where the magnet sits — 65.7 min of pure ramp inside the run, never re-traversed. The 130 rows are spaced **uniform in 1/B** (`field_invB`) so `sdh_fft` gets an already-even record: F_Nyquist = 71.7 T (above the ~60 T second harmonic of the highest tone, so nothing can alias down onto a candidate band frequency — this is what sets the row count), frequency resolution δF = 1.11 T. B_lo = 1.0 T, not lower: extending to 0.5 T at this Nyquist would cost 266 rows. The fast axis is (V_tg, V_bg) **co-swept along the calibrated D = 0 line** through the CNP, n = −1.25 … +3.25×10¹² — the full width the TG operating limit allows (it binds at −1.27 / +3.27, and the cell asserts both gate vectors inside the committed validators before anything moves). 85 points ⇒ 0.054×10¹² step, which resolves the ν-spacing-1 ladder above ~5 T and the ν = 4k ladder above ~2 T.

**Coverage trade, stated.** Below 1.0 T the fan sees nothing — so the "does the light tone outlive the heavy one" test (a tone cannot be a harmonic of a fundamental it survives below) is **not** settled here. Run 6's own low-field leg can't settle it either: it was sampled at 50 mT, i.e. F_Nyquist = 2.5 T at 0.5 T, far too coarse for a 7.5 T tone. That test is plan step 5 — fine 1/B sampling 0.4 → 1.5 T at 2–3 densities, ~40 min, riding the magnet back down from the 10 T this run ends at. Buying it inside *this* fan instead would roughly double the run. Also not covered: any D ≠ 0 (plan step 6) and any temperature dependence (LK masses are hard here anyway at T_e ≈ 8 K).

**Preconditions the cell prints before it commits** (it asserts, then stops on mismatch): T_sample 1.0–1.5 K with a live logfile, |B| < 0.05 T (the magnet is at +0.003 T after the handoff close-out), V_si = 0, gates at 0 V, Keithley nplci = 1/1, persistent switch on and output enabled. Sensitivity is fixed at **200 µV = 20 kΩ full scale** for the whole fan — 38× run 6's maximum (530 Ω), sized per the run-4 lesson to the map's largest feature rather than the 1D peak. The one thing that may still clip is the CNP column itself at high field (the ν = 0 state, |n| ≲ 0.1×10¹², ~3 of 85 columns); a clip there is a reportable lower bound on the ν = 0 gap, not a loss of the fan. Gate leakage trips the run at 1 nA (run-7 flyback transients ran ~200 pA, 5× margin).

**Checkpoints** (`hera-watch data --next --follow`, armed with this cell): **first rows at B ≈ 1 T** — signal level, railing across the row, leakage, the R(n) trace reproducing the run-5 gatemap's D = 0 cut; abort there if the channel rails broadly or the CNP peak is misplaced. Then at **~2 T**, **~4 T** and **~7 T**: per-column FFT on the landed rows — is LL structure developing in n, are two tones separating, is the sum rule closing. Abandon if no n-dependent LL structure has appeared by 3 T. The run ends with the gates parked at 0 V and the **field left at 10 T on purpose**, so plan steps 5/6 ride it down instead of paying 66 min each way.
