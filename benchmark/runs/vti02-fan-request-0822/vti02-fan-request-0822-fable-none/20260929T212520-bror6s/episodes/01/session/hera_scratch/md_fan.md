**Plan step 4 — the stacking-call measurement.** Runs 6/7 (ana cells 14–15) show a two-band electron side (F_l ≈ 7.1 T, F_h ≈ 30.0 T, breakdown comb) and a harmonic-rich hole side — single traces can't separate bands / harmonics / the region-B mosaic, and the +12% n-scale question (F_h+F_l = 37.1 T vs prior-scale 33.1 T) needs the fan's ν-line slopes.
