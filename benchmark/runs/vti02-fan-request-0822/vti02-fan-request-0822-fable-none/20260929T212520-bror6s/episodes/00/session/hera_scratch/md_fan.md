## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n × B, 0 → 10 T

**Plan step 4** (operator-requested; the campaign's central measurement). Runs 6–7 (ana) show multi-tone SdH at both carrier signs — fundamentals 7.1 T (e-side) and 6.6 / 4.7 T (h-side) with strong harmonic ladders, and no weight at the one-pocket F_L — but a fixed-n trace cannot separate a distinct pocket from a harmonic. The fan does: pocket families disperse as F(n), harmonics track ×k; ν-lines from the CNP give per-pocket degeneracy and the density partition, and the total ν-line slope recalibrates the geometry-prior n-scale. That F(n) partition is the ABAB / ABCA / ABCB discriminant.

**Design.** Fast axis: n = −1.25 → +3.24×10¹² cm⁻² along the run-5-calibrated D = 0 line, 151 pts (Δn = 3.0×10¹⁰ — resolves Δν = 4 line spacing down to B ≈ 1.2 T), co-swept (V_tg, V_bg) via `multimegasweep`, endpoints ≥ 5 mV inside the validators. Slow axis ascending from the magnet's current 0 T: rows 0 / 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 T (zero-field reference + MR background), then 128 rows **uniform in 1/B** from 1 → 10 T — even 1/B sampling puts rows where SdH information lives (max Δ(1/B) = 7.2×10⁻³ → per-column FFT Nyquist 70 T, ample for the ≤ 35 T content in runs 6–7) and hands `sdh_fft` an even record. sr1 at 100 µV (10 kΩ FS, run-4 lesson): accepts a possible narrow rail on the CNP columns at high B (ν = 0); the pocket evidence lives at |n| > 0. Leak guard 1 nA. `fast_delay` 0.9 s = 3τ (τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB) — a knowing bend of the settling floor; small per-point steps, run 5 clean at 1.0 s.

**Coverage trade:** the hole side ends at n = −1.25×10¹² (TG −0.3 V validator) — the electron side gets 2.6× the density range; full hole coverage is not available inside the committed box. No Rxy (sr2/sr3 n.c.) — degeneracy reads come from ν-line spacing in Rxx only.

**Price:** ≈ 7¼ h (19,932 pts × 0.97 s/pt measured [run 5: fast_delay + 0.07 s async read] + 73 min total ramp [0.137 T/min] + 132 × ~9 s row turnaround [run-6-calibrated 3 s magnet settle + flyback + slow_delay]; realistic headline, +5% over arithmetic). Ends at **10 T, gates parked at 0 V** — field left high on purpose (never re-traverse; step-5 follow-ups ride it down).

**Checkpoints** (hera-watch armed, backgrounded): first rows — leakage, rail, dead channel, CNP position sanity vs run 5; ~2 T (row ~40) — interim re-form, gap-line emergence, rail extent; ~4.5 T (row ~85) — interim fan + per-column FFT, continue/adjust call; ~7.5 T (row ~110) — final continue check. Abort criteria: sustained leak > 1 nA (guard trips), rail spreading beyond a narrow CNP stripe, or featureless columns where runs 6–7 showed strong oscillations (wiring/sensitivity fault).
