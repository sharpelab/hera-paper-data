## Runs 6 & 7 — SdH spot checks at D = 0 (`sdh-spot-check`): electron n = +3.2×10¹² (B 0→10 T), hole n = −1.2×10¹² (10→0.3 T)

Raw traces first, then `sdh_onset` tone tables + FFT in 1/B (1.5–10 T window; run-5 cal, F_full = |n|h/4e = 33.1 / 12.4 T at the geometry-prior scale, ±~10 %).

**Read (run 6, electron):** strong SdH from ~1.5 T; tones **7.1 / 14.1 / 22.1 / 29.5 T**. Two readings: (a) a pure harmonic ladder of F ≈ 7.1–7.4 T (near-quantum-limit, non-sinusoidal waveform); (b) **light + heavy two-band**: F_l = 7.1, F_h = 29.5, sum 36.6 T vs F_full 33.1 (+11 %, inside the scale prior), with 14.1 = 2F_l and 22.1 ≈ F_h − F_l (or 3F_l). The amplitude ordering — the 29.5 T peak matches/exceeds the 7.1 T fundamental (SNR 89 vs 40) — is implausible for a 4th harmonic, so (b) is favored: **two Fermi surfaces at D = 0, one light + one heavy — Bernal(ABAB)-consistent, but not yet a call** (an ABCB pair also fits; mosaic region B at n×1.13 would put satellites at ~8.0 / ~33 T — not resolved at dF ≈ 2 T). **Read (run 7, hole):** multi-tone low-|n| regime — 6.6 + 4.7 ≈ 11.3 T vs F_full 12.4, plus tracked non-harmonic 8.5 / 12.3 T: ≥ 2 pockets near the CNP on the hole side, consistent with a semimetallic (ungapped at D = 0) low-energy structure, too messy to weigh alone. Note: run 7 carries a standing TG current offset ≈ −0.10 nA at V_tg = −0.28 V (median, sustained; below the 1 nA trip) — bias-dependent, worth watching on the fan's hole end.

**Next:** both ambiguities are exactly what the D = 0 Landau fan resolves — per-n-column FFT branches F(n) separate harmonics (branches scale together) from bands (distinct dF/dn), fan-line slopes give the gap filling factors/degeneracies, and the CNP column vs B gives gapped-vs-semimetallic at D = 0.
