## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n × B, the stacking-call backbone

Picked up at handoff; runs 6–7 analyzed at pickup (`hera_scratch/spot_analysis.py`). The spot checks leave exactly the ambiguity a fan resolves: run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T) has a dominant light tone at **7.1 T** plus strong ~26–33 T weight that is either the 7.1 T harmonic ladder or a genuine heavy band (ABAB-like partition 7 + 26 ≈ F_L); run 7 (hole, F_L = 12.4 T) is multi-tone low-F (4.7 / 6.6 / 9.4 T), semimetal-like. Single traces cannot separate harmonics from bands; **F(n) slopes can** — harmonics scale as exact integer multiples of the fundamental at every n, an independent heavy band has its own slope with F_light + F_heavy summing to F_L (ABAB), a single branch ≈ F_L means ABCA, an asymmetric Dirac-like + heavy split means ABCB (plan step 4).

Design: fast axis co-sweeps (Vtg, Vbg) along the run-5-calibrated D = 0 line, n = −1.25 → +3.20×10¹² (validator-limited on the hole side), Δn = 0.025×10¹² (179 pts); slow axis B = 1 → 10 T ascending from where the magnet sits, **uniform in 1/B** (145 rows, δ(1/B) = 6.25×10⁻³ T⁻¹ → F_Nyquist = 80 T, per-column ΔF = 1.11 T — resolves the 7 vs 9.5 T splits seen in the spots). **Coverage trade:** rows start at 1 T (below that only the light tone survives and runs 6–7 already cover it densely in 1D); deep hole side unreachable (TG −0.3 V limit); B ends at 10 T where the next step rides it down. Sensitivity 100 µV (10 kΩ FS, run-5 sizing lesson) — the D = 0 CNP column may clip at high B; that stripe is narrow in n, flagged in analysis, and itself evidence (gap at CNP favors ABCA over semimetallic ABAB). Leak guard trips at 1 nA.

Price (calibrated on run 5: 1.10 s/pt at fast_delay 1.0; run 6 ramp overhead): 145 × (179 × 1.1 s + 6.5 s) + 66 min of ramps + settles ≈ 9.6 h → **headline ≈ 10 h 30 m** (session actuals ran 5–10% over). Checkpoints: first 3 rows (~15 min — leakage, clip fraction, signal on all channels); then ~2 h / ~5 h / ~8 h interim reads on the landed grid (partial per-column FFT over the landed 1/B window) — continue / adjust / abandon at each, via the armed `hera-watch --follow` digests.
