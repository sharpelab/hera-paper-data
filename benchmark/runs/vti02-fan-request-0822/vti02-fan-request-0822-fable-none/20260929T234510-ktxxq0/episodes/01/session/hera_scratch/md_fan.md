## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan along D = 0 (`landau-fan`) — the stacking discriminator

Plan step 4 (operator-requested next move). Runs 6/7 (ana cells 14–15) show textbook SdH on both carrier signs but the spot FFT resolution (dF ≈ 4.3 T) cannot separate the candidate ABAB heavy fundamental (≈ 25.6 T at n = +3.2×10¹²) from the 3–4× harmonics of the dominant 7.1 T light tone. The fan resolves this three ways: fundamentals **disperse with n** while harmonics stay integer-locked to their parent; the branch **partition ΣFᵢ(n) vs F_L(n) = 10.34·n[10¹²] T** distinguishes two-band ABAB (sum rule) from single-branch ABCA from an asymmetric/gapped ABCB; and the **CNP column R(B)** reads the D = 0 gap. Region-B mosaic tones (+13 % n, run 5) are a known contaminant to check per column.
