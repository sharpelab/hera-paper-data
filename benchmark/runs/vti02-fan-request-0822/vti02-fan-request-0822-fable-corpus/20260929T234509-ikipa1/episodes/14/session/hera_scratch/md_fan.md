## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side at D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`) — the stacking-call dataset

**Why now** (`tetralayer-stacking-fs-count` Step 4; spot-check read in ana runs 6–7): run 6 resolved a tone on the ABAB light branch (7.3 T at n = +3.2×10¹²) plus heavy-tone candidates (22.3 / 29.4 T) that are each degenerate with harmonics of 7.3 at a single density. The per-column FFT's **n-trajectory** is the discriminator: harmonics track k·f_light(n) → collapse toward 0 at low n; a real ABAB heavy tone rises toward F_L as the light pocket empties (model: heavy = 8.29 T ≈ F_L at n = 0.8×10¹²); an ABCA singleton stays pinned at F_L. One fan closes the fork and pins the global lever-arm scale via the Luttinger sum.

**Design (floored per `landau-fan` step 1; this is the proposal, no cheaper variant deferred):**
- **Fast axis**: n = +3.2 → +0.8×10¹² cm⁻², 76 pts (Δn = 3.2×10¹⁰), D = 0, run-5 cal. Ray-Nyquist at B_lo = 1 T: Δn_ray = 9.7×10¹⁰ → **3.0 samples/ray** (≥2 floor, at prefer-3). Gates span (Vtg, Vbg) = (+0.296…+0.984, +0.276…+1.166) V — inside validators.
- **Slow axis**: B = 1.0 → 10 T **uniform in 1/B**, 109 rows, Δ(1/B) = 1/120 T⁻¹ → **Nyquist F = 60 T** (covers heavy-2nd-harmonic 59 T at 2/period; F_L-breakdown tone ~37 T at 3.3; measured tones 29.4/22.3/7.3 at ≥4). **B_lo = 1.0 T**: run 6's onset is censored at the valid-record edge (onset ≤ 1.0 T) so every row carries signal; lowering B_lo would balloon N_B and the n-floor without being needed — per-column δF = 1/(1/1 − 1/10) = **1.11 T** separates every claim-critical pair (heavy vs F_L gap ≥ 2.4 T down to n = 1.2×10¹²). Light-tone ≥5-period floor holds for n ≳ 2.6×10¹² (f ≥ 5.6 T); below that the light tone is read at reduced period count — hedged, not claimed. B_hi = 10 T buys the heavy tone its max amplitude and the B-window breakdown split.
- **Acquisition inherited from run 6** (spot check hands the fan its sampling/settling/sensitivity): sr1 sens 50 µV (5 kΩ FS at 10 nA), drive 1 V, τ = 0.3 s with **fast_delay = 1.8 s = 6τ** (per-column-SdH settling band, base step 7). Dynamic range: worst corner is low-n × high-B (n = 0.8×10¹² at 10 T, ν ≈ 3.3, QH peaks); anchor run 7's 583 Ω at |n| = 1.2×10¹²/10 T → claim-carrying columns (n ≥ 1.2×10¹²) have ~8× headroom; the extreme corner may clip and is truncated per column by `r_full_scale` in analysis — sized to protect the claim columns, trade stated.
- **Coverage trade**: no hole-side fan — the reachable hole window (to −1.27×10¹², TG −0.3 V limit) sits inside the near-CNP exception regime (run 7: pair split < δF); a hole fan would be ~5 h for a resolvability-limited read. Full electron coverage instead.
- **Price** (calibrated: 0.1 s/pt read overhead from run 5; 0.137 T/min ramp): 8284 pts × 1.9 s = 4.4 h grid + 66 min traverse + 7 min initial ramp + ~5 min turnarounds ≈ 5.7 h → **realistic ≈ 6 h 15 m** (+8% session-evidence margin). Ends at **10 T** with gates parked at 0 — the LK mass split (`temperature-dependence-vti` Flavor B) or the close-out ride-down starts from there; no re-traverse.
- **Checkpoints** (hera-watch armed `--next --follow`): **first 2 rows (~6 min)** — column layout, signal fraction, leakage < 1 nA, T at base; **~1.5 h (row ~35, B ≈ 1.4 T)** — partial per-column FFT: light-tone branch tracking across n; **~3.5 h (B ≈ 2.7 T)** — heavy-candidate trajectory read, continue/abort call (abort ≈ `nb-collab interrupt`; partial fan keeps its rows). Leak guard trips the station at 1 nA.
