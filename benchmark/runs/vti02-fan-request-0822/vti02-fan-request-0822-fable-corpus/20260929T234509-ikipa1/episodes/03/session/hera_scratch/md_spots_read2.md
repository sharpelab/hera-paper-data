If a real pair: **7.3 + 22.3 = 29.6 = 0.89·F_L** (global scale 0.89, inside the lever-arm prior 0.8–1.2), observed partition 0.25/0.75 vs model 0.22/0.78; and **29.4 ≈ f₁+f₂** (breakdown sum tone, symmetry-allowed in ABAB) *and* ≈ 4×7.3 — underdetermined at one density. **No call from a spot check** — the n-trajectories (harmonic tracks k·f_light; heavy tracks its own Onsager curve; breakdown tracks f₁+f₂ = 0.89·F_L(n)) are exactly what the fan separates.
