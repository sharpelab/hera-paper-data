## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side (`landau-fan-vti`), n = +1.0 → +3.2×10¹² at D = 0, B 1.2 → 10 T uniform-1/B

**Why this fan (runs 6/7, ana):** the electron spot check resolved a two-fundamental set {7.05, 22.90 T} with a sum tone at 29.94 = 0.905·F_L and an intermodulation tone at 15.85 — the ABAB template with symmetry-allowed breakdown — but frequencies alone leave a shifted-harmonic reading of a single 7 T pocket unexcluded (all three upper tones sit +1·δF above the 7.05 comb). The fan's per-column F(n) decides: harmonics track k·f_light(n) exactly, a real heavy pocket follows its own Onsager curve, and the Luttinger sum f₁+f₂ = s·F_L(n) must hold across density (investigation Steps 4–5). Hole side stays un-fanned: it reaches only n = −1.27×10¹² (TG limit), inside the near-CNP exception regime where run 7 was already resolvability-limited.

**Design (floored per `landau-fan` step 1 + VTI cost regime):**
- **B rows:** `field_invB(1.2, 10.0, 124)` ascending — Δ(1/B) = 5.96×10⁻³ /T, FFT Nyquist 83.9 T > the observed 59.9 T (2× sum tone) with margin; 5.5 pts/period on the 30 T tone. **B_lo = 1.2 T, not the measured 0.3 T onset** — that onset is `censored@edge` on the *light* tone (and the sub-1 T leg of run 6 is 50 mT-coarse/aliased, so it's a bound, not a measurement); uniform-1/B rows below 1.2 T cost ~2 min each and reaching 0.5 T would add ~220 rows (~7 h). The 1.2–10 T record gives the light tone 5.2 periods and per-column δF = 1.36 T; heavy-vs-sum splits (7.0 T at n = 3.2, scaled) resolve at ≥ 2·δF for n ≳ 2.4×10¹² — below that the read is the two trajectories + the light tone, stated as a coverage limit of the claim, not fixable by rows.
- **n columns:** 61 pts, +1.0 → +3.2×10¹² at D = 0 (run-5 cal), Δn = 0.037×10¹² → 3.2 pts per ray spacing at the 1.2 T low edge (≥ 2 floor). n_max = 3.2 keeps V_tg at +0.984, 16 mV under the validator; the low edge 1.0 covers the heavy-branch bend toward 0.95·F_L (an ABAB-only signature ABCA's straight F = F_L line can't produce).
- **Dynamic range:** sensitivity stays 50 µV = 5 kΩ FS (spot-check-inherited). Worst corner is low-n × high-B: run 7 at |n| = 1.2 (closer to CNP than any fan column) peaked at 576 Ω at 10 T → ≥ 6× headroom; low-B signal ~70 Ω = 1.4 % FS matched the spots, whose FFTs were clean.
- **Settling:** `fast_delay = 1.8 s` = 6τ (τ = 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct — the per-column-FFT floor, `landau-fan` step 7); `slow_delay = 1`.

**Price (calibrated on run 5: 1.17 s/pt at fast_delay = 1.0 → per-point ≈ fast_delay + 0.17 s):** grid 124 × 61 × 1.97 s ≈ 4 h 08 m; field ramps 0 → 1.2 → 10 T ≈ 73 min + 124 × 3 s settles ≈ 6 m; flybacks ~4 m → ≈ 5 h 30 m, headline **≈ 5 h 50 m** (+5–10 % realistic). Ends at **10 T, gates parked at 0** — the follow-up (LK mass split or firming fan) rides the magnet down.

**Checkpoints:** first rows via `hera-watch` (leakage, dead/flat channel, field tracking, low-B signal level); interim per-column FFT on landed rows at ~2 h (B ≈ 2 T, light tone forming) and ~4 h (B ≈ 5–6 T, heavy/sum tones resolvable at high n) — continue/adjust/abandon each with the ~2 h remaining-cost of the balance.
