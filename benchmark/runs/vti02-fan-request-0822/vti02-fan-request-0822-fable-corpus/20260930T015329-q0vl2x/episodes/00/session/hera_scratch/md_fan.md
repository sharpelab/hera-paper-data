## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side at D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`) — the stacking-call dataset

Investigation step 4 (`tetralayer-stacking-fs-count`), following the spot-check read (ana, runs 6–7): electron side carries two tones 7.8 + 28.9 T at n = +3.2×10¹², summing to 1.108×F_L with an ABAB-like 0.21/0.79 partition — but one density cannot separate heavy-pocket from 4th-harmonic (4×7.11 T falls within δF of 28.9 T), nor pass the F_L-tone origin screen. The fan reads F(n) of every tone across density: partition robustness f₁+f₂ = F_L(n), the harmonic screen (a harmonic tracks 4f exactly; the model ABAB ratio sweeps 6.6→3.5 over this window), and the dual ABAB/trilayer overlay.

**Design (computed, `hera_scratch/fan_design.py`):**
- **Fast axis**: paired n-line at D = 0, n = 3.24 → 1.00 ×10¹² cm⁻², 101 pts (run-5 cal). n_max = 3.24 puts Vtg at +0.995 V, 5 mV under the validator. δn = 2.24×10¹⁰ → 3.0 rows per LL ray at 0.7 T (≥2 floor, prefer-3 met). Claim window is n ≳ 2×10¹² (templates separated); the 1.0–2.0 columns map the low-n exception regime and anchor the F(n) trajectory, hedged accordingly.
- **Slow axis**: B = 0.7 → 10 T **uniform in 1/B**, 161 rows, Δ(1/B) = 0.0083 T⁻¹ — 3 samples/period on F_L(n_max) even at lever-arm scale 1.2 (40.2 T), FFT fold at 60 T clears the 2×heavy harmonic (≈58 T) unaliased. δF = 1/(1/0.7 − 0.1) = **0.75 T**: heavy-vs-F_L split at n_max is 7.7 T ≈ 10δF; ABAB partner at n = 2.0×10¹² (model 3.9 T) gets 5.2 periods — the investigation's ≥5 floor. B_lo = 0.7 T is below the censored run-6 onset bound (<1 T) and prices the light tone's low-B reach; heavy-tone (m* ≈ 0.06) rows below ~1.5 T are LK-dead at T_e ≈ 8 K — expected, not a defect.
- **Sensitivity**: sr1 fixed 50 µV = 5 kΩ FS at 10 nA for the whole fan. Worst corner (low-n × 10 T): run-7 anchor 587 Ω at |n| = 1.2×10¹², 10 T → ~12% FS there; ≥4× headroom against SdH maxima. Saturation is the unrecoverable failure; resolution is sacrificed knowingly.
- **Dwell**: fast_delay = 1.8 s = 6τ (τ = 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct) — the per-column-FFT case of the base recipe's step 7; slow_delay = 1 s.
- **Coverage trade**: hole side excluded — gate-limited to |n| ≤ 1.27×10¹² (never reaches the separated-template regime, run 7 confirms the beat tangle); full coverage would cost a second ~10 h fan for uninformative columns.

**Price**: grid 161×101 = 16,261 pts × 1.97 s/pt (run-5 calibration: 0.17 s reads + fast_delay) ≈ 8.9 h; traverse 0→10 T ≈ 73 min (0.137 T/min); row settles + turnaround ≈ 25 min → **10.4 h**, headline **11 h** with the session's 5–10% overrun bias. Starts at 0 T (magnet is there), **ends at 10 T** with gates parked at 0 — the follow-up (LK mass cuts or a measured ride-down) starts from 10 T, no re-traverse.

**Checkpoints** (hera-watch armed, `data --next --follow`): first 2–3 rows — leakage, rail (>60% FS), dead channel, field-readback tracking; then ~every 2–3 h on landed-row digests; interim per-column FFT once rows pass B ≈ 2 T (~70% through the row count, where the 1/B record starts resolving tones). Abort criteria: leak trip (in-cell guard at 1 nA), rail, or T_sample off base > 1.5 K.
