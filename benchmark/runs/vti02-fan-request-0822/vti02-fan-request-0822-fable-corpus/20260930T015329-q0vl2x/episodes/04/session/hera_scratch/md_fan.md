## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side (`landau-fan-vti`), D = 0 — the stacking discriminator

**Why this fan (from runs 6–7, ana):** the electron spot check shows the ABAB light tone (7.1 T vs model 7.4 T) but its high-tone cluster (22.1 / 29.5 T) confounds the ABAB heavy tone (model 25.9 T at +3.2e12) with the 3×/4× harmonics of 7.1 — and 29.5 T = 0.89·F_L is also inside the ABCA scale band. Single columns cannot decide; the **n-trajectory screen can**: over n = 1.0 → 3.25×10¹², the model's ABAB heavy runs 9.8 → 26.2 T, 3×f₁ runs 1.7 → 22.4 T, 4×f₁ 2.3 → 29.9 T, and ABCA's F_L 10.3 → 33.5 T — divergent by ≫ δF across the window. Electron side per the investigation's cleaner-side rule (hole side is the predicted near-CNP exception regime and caps at −1.27×10¹²). **Coverage trade, stated:** the hole side is excluded from this fan — its templates merge within resolution at reachable |n| (run 7), so hole coverage would cost ~5 h for a resolvability-limited read; the electron fan carries the claim.

**Design (recipe step-1 checks, computed):** per-column SdH-FFT fan, uniform 1/B. **B-axis:** Δ(1/B) = 0.01 T⁻¹ → 91 rows, 1 → 10 T ascending; FFT Nyquist 50 T ≥ 1.2·F_L(n_max) = 41 T and the measured 4× comb (~30 T); inherits the spot check's fine-leg spacing at its low edge. **B_lo = 1.0 T:** the carrying tone is censored at run 6's analysis edge (0.30 T) but grid-resolvable only ≥ ~0.85 T on its coarse leg, and the claim tones (heavy cluster) onset at 1.4–1.9 T — a lower B_lo buys no δF on them, only cost. **B_max = 10 T** = session cap; the heavy-cluster δF (≈ 2.0–2.4 T, onset-limited) is what B_max shortens, so take it all. **n-axis:** 71 cols = 3× ray-Nyquist at 1 T (Δn_ray = 9.7×10¹⁰; 23.3 periods across the window); n_max = +3.25×10¹² is the TG-validator edge (Vtg +0.998 V). **Dynamic range:** 100 µV = 10 kΩ FS, sized to the low-n × high-B corner (ν ≈ 4.2 QH peaks, ~kΩ; ~17× the run-7 anchor), run-4's rail lesson applied. **Dwell:** fast_delay 1.8 s = 6τ (τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB — per-column-FFT case, recipe step 7); slow_delay 1.

**Price (calibrated on runs 5–7):** grid 91×71 × 1.9 s/pt (1.8 dwell + 0.10 reads, run-5 within-row) = 3.4 h + ramps 73 min (0→1 position + 1→10 traverse at 0.137 T/min) + settles/turnaround ~7 min ≈ **4.8 h computed → 5 h 15 m realistic**. Ends at **10 T, gates parked at 0**. **Checkpoints:** first rows (~10 min in — rail/leakage/dead channel/field tracking); interim per-column FFT reads at rows ~30 (B ≈ 1.4 T) and ~60 (B ≈ 2.7 T) — the light tone f₁(n) should already track; abort there if the channel rails or columns are flat.
