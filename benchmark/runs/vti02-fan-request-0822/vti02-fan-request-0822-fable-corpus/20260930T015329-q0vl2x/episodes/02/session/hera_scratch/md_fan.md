## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, D = 0, n = +2.1 → +3.2×10¹², B 0.7 → 10 T uniform-1/B

**Why this fan (from runs 6/7, ana):** run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹²) put the dominant tone at 7.10 T — 3% under the model's ABAB light pocket (7.31 T, `fs_count_b0`) — with the 22.1/29.5 T features unresolvable per-column between the ABAB heavy pocket (25.8 T) and 3×/4× harmonics of 7.1 (21.3/28.4 T). Run 7 (hole, |n| capped at 1.27×10¹² by the TG limit) sits in the low-|n| exception regime with an underdetermined {6.6, 4.7, ≈F_L} set. The discriminator is per-column FFT **F(n) trajectories**: a harmonic tracks 3×f_light(n) (11.6 → 21.9 T over this window), a real heavy pocket follows its own Onsager curve (17.9 → 25.8 T) — they never cross in-window — plus the Luttinger sum f₁+f₂ = F_L per column. Electron side per the spot checks: design regime (templates separate at |n| ≥ 2×10¹²), onset ≤ 0.7 T, SNR 40+.

**Design (recipe step-1 checks, computed):**
- **B axis**: `field_invB(0.7, 10.0, 133)` — Δ(1/B) = 0.01006 /T = 1/3-Nyquist on F_L(3.2×10¹²) = 33.1 T (unaliased to 49.7 T at 1/2). B_lo = 0.7 T: run 6's carrying tone is censored at its record edge (alive at 0.3 T) so 0.7 T is above onset for the tones this fan claims; it buys record 1.33 /T → δF = 0.75 T for tones alive from 0.7 T, and ≥5 periods of the ABAB partner at n_min (model partner 3.86 T at 2.1×10¹² → 5.1 periods). Heavy-tone per-column δF ≈ 2.3 T (its onset ~1.9 T) — per-column marginal, which is why the read is the cross-column trajectory.
- **n axis**: 37 pts, +3.2 → +2.1×10¹² (descending), step 0.031×10¹² ≤ half the ray spacing 4(e/h)·0.7 T = 0.068×10¹².
- **Dynamic range**: sr1 fixed 50 µV (5 kΩ FS at 10 nA), inherited from the spot checks; worst corner (n_min × 10 T) anchored by run 7's 576 Ω at |n| = 1.2, 10 T → ≥8× headroom.
- **Settling**: fast_delay = 2.1 s = 7τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB/oct — the settling floor (k = 7–10), required for a per-column FFT read; sr0/sr1 X+P per point.
- **Cost floor**: 133 × 37 grid at 2.2 s/pt (session-calibrated: run 5 measured 1.10 s/pt at fast_delay 1.0 → 0.1 s read overhead) = 3.0 h + traverse 0 → 0.7 → 10 T at 0.137 T/min = 73 min + 133 row settles/turnarounds ≈ 11 min → **4.4 h, headline 4 h 45 m** (session actuals run 5–10% over).

**Coverage trade, stated:** hole side excluded (TG −0.3 V caps it at |n| ≤ 1.27×10¹² — exception regime, already spot-checked as run 7); n < 2.1×10¹² excluded (partner tone < 5 periods above B_lo, and the templates converge below ~2×10¹²); a full 1.2 → 3.2 window at the same checks costs ~+2 h for columns whose partner tone is unresolvable. Price of the full-coverage variant, if the trajectory read ends ambiguous: one more fan.

**Ends at 10 T** with gates at the n-line end — the LK mass split / follow-ups ride the magnet down (never re-traverse). **Checkpoints:** first rows (~10 min: rail/leak/dead-channel, live via `hera-watch`); ~1 h (B ≈ 1 T, row digests); ~2 h 45 m (B ≈ 2 T: interim per-column FFT on the landed record, δF ≈ 1.1 T — continue/abandon with the call priced); completion. Abort = kernel interrupt (magnet completes its last setpoint; partial fan keeps its rows).
