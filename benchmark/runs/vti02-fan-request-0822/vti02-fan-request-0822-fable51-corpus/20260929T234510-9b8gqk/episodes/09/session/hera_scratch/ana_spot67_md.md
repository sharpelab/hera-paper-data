## Runs 6–7 — SdH spot checks (`sdh-spot-check`), D = 0, T = 1.2 K: electron n = +3.2×10¹² (run 6, 0 → 10 T, 10 mT above 1 T) and hole n = −1.2×10¹² (run 7, 10 → 0.3 T, 20 mT)

Raw first (left column), then the `sdh_onset` per-tone tracks and the FFT stability sweep (middle), and the B-window split (right). Channel arithmetic R = (sr1_X/1)/(sr0_X/−1e6); both runs at sr1 50 µV (5.05 kΩ full scale), peaks reached 10.5 % (run 6) / 11.6 % (run 7) of full scale — no rail; leakage flat (TG/BG medians within the ±100 pA flyback-noise band); T_sample 1.17–1.29 K.

**Run 6 (electron, F_L = 33.1 T):** strong, highly non-sinusoidal oscillations from ≲ 1 T; a ~7.1–7.5 T fundamental carries a comb at **exact** 2×/3×/4× (14.9, 22.4, 29.9 T, cut 1.3 T; `sdh_onset` flags all three as harmonics of 7.1 T, with per-tone onsets 1.36–1.91 T). No resolved tone at F_L = 33.1 T itself. The light-tone onset is **censored** at the record's low edge (0.30 T) — but the 0–1 T leg was sampled at 50 mT, which under-samples a 7 T tone below ~1 T, so the true onset is < ~1 T and otherwise unmeasured. Above 7.5 T the trace rises to a ~500 Ω plateau-like bump (8–9.5 T) that the polynomial background does not absorb — the [4, 10] T window's spectrum is not trustworthy on its own. **Two readings survive on this one density, and they cannot be separated at a single n:** (a) a *single* 7.5 T pocket with a harmonic comb — a pocket carrying only 0.72×10¹² (g = 4), i.e. a factor 4.4 below the gated density; or (b) the ABAB template at global scale ≈ 0.90: light 7.47 + heavy 22.41 = **29.88 T = F_L·0.90**, partition 0.25 / 0.75 (model at n = +3.2: 7.3 / 25.8 T, ratio 3.5; observed ratio 3.00 — the investigation's explicit warning that a ratio-only harmonic screen eats the real heavy tone when the ABAB ratio drifts through 3×), with the 29.9 T tone the symmetry-allowed inter-block breakdown sum. The discriminator is the **n-dependence**: a harmonic tracks k·f(n) exactly; an ABAB heavy pocket follows its own Onsager curve (model: 17.1 T at n = 2 vs 3×3.55 = 10.7 T) and runs to F_L as the light pocket empties near n ≈ 0.8–1.0. That is the Landau fan's job (Step 4).

**Run 7 (hole, F_L = 12.4 T):** cleaner sinusoidal record; onset 0.71 T; dominant tones at **6.2–7.0 T** and **12.4 T = F_L** (cut 1.2–1.7 T; the F_L tone strengthens relative to the ~6.5 T tone in the high-B half — the breakdown/sum-tone signature, weak in a 12 T window), plus a weaker ~4.7–5.3 T component. Model at n = −1.2: ABAB pair 5.9 / 6.5 T (close, balanced — the stated near-CNP exception regime), ABCA 12.4 T with m\* ≈ 1.1 mₑ (LK-dead at this rig's T_e ≈ 8 K, so an ABCA singleton could not have produced a strong 12.4 T tone here — that alone leans the F_L tone toward a sum tone). Hole side reaches only |n| ≤ 1.27×10¹² (TG limit) — the low-|n| exception regime; **not the side for the fan**.

**Take:** the electron side is the decisive side (|n| up to 3.2×10¹², onset < 1 T, SNR 40–90 on the fundamental). Handed to the fan: light-tone onset < 1 T (unmeasured below), the ≥ 22 T content onsets at ≥ 1.36 T, fastest content in the claim band ≤ F_L(3.2) = 33 T with only weak 45/60 T harmonics (5–7 %), settling floor 3 s = 10 τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB, sensitivity 50 µV gave ≤ 12 % of range at |n| ≥ 1.2.
