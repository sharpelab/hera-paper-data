## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side (`landau-fan-vti`), D = 0, n = +1.0 → +3.2×10¹², B 0.75 → 10 T uniform in 1/B

**Why this fan.** The spot checks (ana: runs 6 + 7) hand Step 4 the electron side: run 6 at n = +3.2×10¹² is a clean two-tone record — f₁ ≈ 7.0 T (alive to the record's 1 T sampled edge; onset *not* pinned, the 50 mT leg below 1 T under-samples it) and a dominant f₂ ≈ 30–32 T (onset 1.9 T, 2× at 60 T) — with the light tone on the model's ABAB partner (7.3 T) but the heavy tone 16 % above the model's 25.8 T, so the record forks between "ABAB pair at density scale ≈ 1.15" and "ABCA-like singleton at scale ≈ 0.93 plus an unexplained 7 T pocket"; f₂ = 4×f₁ (28.4 T) and a region-B mosaic blend (+13 % n) are the confounds. Only F(n) across density separates these: a harmonic tracks 4·f₁(n) exactly, an ABAB pair tracks the model's two curves with one global scale and sums to F_L(n), a singleton tracks F_L alone, a mosaic partner tracks ×1.13. The hole side (run 7, −1.2×10¹²) is the near-CNP exception regime and is TG-limited to |n| ≤ 1.27×10¹² — it cannot reach where the templates separate; **excluded from the fan, stated.** n < 1.0×10¹² is also excluded (templates merge there; 2.6 min per column).
