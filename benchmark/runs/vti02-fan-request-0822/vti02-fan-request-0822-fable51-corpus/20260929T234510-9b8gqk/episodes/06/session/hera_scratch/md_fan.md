## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side at D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`), n = +0.6 → +3.2×10¹², B 0.65 → 10 T uniform-1/B

**Why this fan, from the spot checks (runs 6/7, ana).** Run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T) carries a strong ~7.3 T tone from ≤ 0.8 T, tones at ~22 and ~29.3 T (difference = the 7 T tone; 29.3 ≈ F_L at a lever-arm scale of 0.89) with a 2×/3× comb of 29.3 T at 56–60 / 88–90 T, and 30–33 T dominant above 3 T. Model (`fs_count_b0`, n = 3.2): ABAB light 7.31 T (m\* 0.027) + heavy 25.8 T (m\* 0.061), ABCA singleton 33.1 T. One column cannot separate "ABAB pair + breakdown/QH sum tone at F_L" from "rescaled ABCA singleton at ~29.5 T + a second-domain 7 T tone": the fan's per-column F(n) does — an ABAB light tone empties toward n ≈ 0.8×10¹² (model: 0.57 T at n = 1.0, 3.55 at 2.0, 7.31 at 3.2) and the pair must sum to F_L at every n, while the heavy/F_L ratio runs 1.11 → 0.88 over n = 0.6 → 1.4 for ABAB but stays 1.00 for ABCA (trajectory, scale-free). Run 7 (hole, n = −1.2, F_L = 12.4 T) is the near-CNP exception regime (6.6 T + 12.3 ≈ F_L + a comb) and the D = 0 line only reaches −1.27×10¹² on that side — no density window for a hole fan. **Coverage trade:** electron side only; the hole side is excluded because the validators cap it at |n| ≤ 1.27×10¹² (inside the investigation's low-|n| exception regime), not because it is uninteresting.

**Design (landau-fan step 1 levers, computed).** *B_lo* = 0.65 T: the light tone is censored at the spot-check's record edge (onset < 0.8 T, grid-limited below), and ≥ 5 partner periods at the design-window edge n = 2.0 (f_partner = 3.55 T) needs 1/B span ≥ 1.41 T⁻¹ → B_lo ≤ 0.66 T at B_hi = 10 T.
