**Why this fan, from the spot checks (read from disk, `sdh_onset` + `sdh_fft`; ana cell to follow):** run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T) carries a dominant **7.1 T tone = 0.215·F_L** with a 2×/3×/4× harmonic comb, still above threshold at the record's low edge (**censored onset at 0.3 T**) — a very light, high-mobility pocket (model ABAB light pocket at this n: 7.3 T, m* ≈ 0.027). Above ~3 T the spectrum hands over to a **~30–33 T tone (≈ F_L)** with QH-like minima (R → 0 at 6.0 and 7.2 T) — the sum-tone / breakdown / total-density confound the investigation makes mandatory to discriminate.
