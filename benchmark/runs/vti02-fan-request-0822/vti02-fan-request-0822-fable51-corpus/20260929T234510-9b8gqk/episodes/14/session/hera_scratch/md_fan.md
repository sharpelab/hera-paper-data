## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side, D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`) — the stacking-call dataset

**Why this fan, from the spot checks (runs 6/7, read in scratch → ana).** Run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T) is clean SdH from ≲ 1 T (50 µV, peak 522 Ω = 10 % FS, no rail): `sdh_onset` finds a dominant **7.1 T** tone (censored at the record's low edge — the 50 mT leg below 1 T is aliased for it, so its onset is only bounded < 1 T) with tones at **14.1 / 22.1 / 29.5 T** flagged as ≈ 2×/3×/4× (ratios 1.99 / 3.11 / 4.15 — the 3×/4× members miss exact multiples by 0.8–1.1 T against δF ≈ 1.6–2.4 T), and the high-B half (3.5–10 T) FFTs to a single peak at **32.3 T ≈ F_L**. The ABAB model puts the pair at 7.31 / 25.8 T (light m* 0.027, heavy 0.061 mₑ) and ABCA a singleton at 33.1 T; region B (the run-5 parallel CNP ridge, n_A − 0.43) would sit at F_L,B = 28.7 T. So at one density the 7.1 T tone matches the ABAB light branch to 3 % but the 22.1/29.5 T pair is **underdetermined**: harmonic comb of 7.1, ABAB heavy tone (+14 % scale), or region B's singleton. Run 7 (hole, n = −1.2, F_L = 12.4 T) shows **6.6 T** (≈ the ABAB balanced near-CNP pair 5.9/6.5 T, unresolvable at δF 1.1 T) and a 125-SNR tone at **12.3 T ≈ F_L** — the expected sum-tone/singleton confound in the low-|n| exception regime, and the hole side is gate-limited to |n| ≤ 1.27 anyway. **Verdict: fan the electron side** — it reaches |n| ≳ 2–3×10¹² where the templates separate, and only F(n) trajectories decide the comb (a harmonic tracks k·f₁ exactly; a real pocket follows its own Onsager curve; a region-B tone tracks F_L(n − 0.43)).

**Design (recipe step 1 levers, computed).** (1) **B_lo = 1.0 T**: the electron onset is censored below 1 T on an aliased leg, so 1.0 T is the low edge of trustworthy record; the light tone at n = 2.0 (model 3.55 T) gets 3.2 periods over 1/B ∈ [0.1, 1.0] — the light branch is trackable down to n ≈ 2, below that it is resolution-limited by construction (stated hedge, not more rows). (2) **n_max = +3.2×10¹²** (TG limit, Vtg = 0.984 V), **n_min = +1.0** (the low-n exception regime is included as the trajectory's low end, not as a claim point). (3) **N_n = 56** (step 0.04×10¹²): ray Nyquist at B_lo Δn = 0.097×10¹² → 2.4 pts/period at 1 T, ≥ 3 above 1.25 T. (4) **B_max = 10 T = the cap.** **B axis uniform in 1/B, Δ(1/B) = 0.010 /T → N_B = 91**: 3.0 samples per period of F_L(n_max) = 33.1 T, sampling 100 T so a 2×29.5 T harmonic aliases to 41 T, outside the ≤ 35 T claim band; δF = 1.11 T at every column (separates 25.8 from 29.5 T at > 3 δF). **Dwell**: τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB (asserted), fast_delay 1.5 s (5 τ — the 4-pole lag is one uniform column shift, ≤ 25 % amplitude attenuation of the along-n structure at 1 T and < 10 % above 2 T; frequencies, not amplitudes, carry the read), slow_delay 3 s (10 τ after each flyback, on top of the magnet's 3 s settle). **Sensitivity 50 µV (5 kΩ FS)** inherited from runs 6/7: worst corner n = 1.0 × 10 T anchors at R(1.0, B = 0) ≈ 120 Ω (run 5 D = 0 line) × the run-7 low-n MR factor 4.2 → ~0.5 kΩ, 10× headroom; a soft-clip watch prints any sample ≥ 95 % FS (report, no stop).

**Price** (calibrators: run 5 gate axis 1.10 s/pt at fast_delay 1.0; run 6 magnet 7.85 s/pt at 10 mT): row = 56 × 1.62 s + flyback/settle 7 s ≈ 98 s; 91 rows ≈ 2.5 h; traverse 0 → 10 T at 0.137 T/min ≈ 1.2 h (positioning 0 → 1 T ≈ 7.5 min inside the cell) → ≈ 3.7 h, headline 4 h. Ends at **10 T with gates parked at 0 V** — the firming steps (grid-change spot check, LK ladder) ride the magnet down. **Coverage trade**: sub-1 T rows (light-tone onset) excluded — extending to 0.7 T costs +43 rows ≈ +1.2 h; hole side excluded (gate-limited to the exception regime); the ABAB partner below n ≈ 2 is resolution-limited, stated as such in the call.

**Checkpoints** (hera-watch armed on `data --next --follow`): **first rows** (~10 min: both gates moving, sr1 fs ≪ 0.9, TG/BG currents at the ±0.2 nA flyback transient not above, T at base); **~row 45 (B ≈ 2 T, ~1.6 h)**: per-column FFT of the landed 1–2 T rows — light-tone F(n) trajectory visible or not; **~row 75 (B ≈ 4 T, ~2.8 h)**: heavy/comb tones above their 1.4–1.9 T onsets — continue to 10 T unless the channel rails across columns or T leaves base; completion → full read in ana (Onsager overlay, Luttinger sum, B-window split, harmonic screen by n-tracking).
