## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side, D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`), n = +1.0 → +3.24×10¹², B 0.3 → 10 T uniform-1/B

**Why this fan, from the spot checks (runs 6/7, read from the TSVs).** Run 6 (electron, n_cal = +3.2×10¹²) resolves a light tone at **7.1 T** persisting to the record's low edge (censored at 0.3 T, SNR 40), and heavy content onsetting at 1.4–1.9 T at **22.3 T** and **29.4 T**. 7.1 + 22.3 = 29.4, and the raw Rxx minima at 6.0 / 7.2 T are ν = 20 / 16 for n ≈ 2.85×10¹² — i.e. the density scale is ≈ 0.89 of the run-5 calibration and 29.4 T *is* F_L there. That is the ABAB template (model at 2.85: 6.2 / 23.3 T) with the symmetry-allowed breakdown sum at F_L — but a singleton-at-F_L reading (ABCA at scale 0.89 plus unexplained tones) is not yet excluded, and the F_L-tone position moves with the FFT window (29.4 vs 33.3 T at δF = 6.7 T): a fork the F(n) trajectory resolves. Run 7 (hole, n_cal = −1.2×10¹²) shows 6.6–7.0 T + 12.3 T (= F_L) — the near-CNP hole regime where the ABAB pair (model 5.9 / 6.5 T) is unresolvable at δF = 0.77 T; the hole side cannot exceed |n| = 1.27 inside the validators, so it is non-discriminating by design. **Electron side it is.**

**Design (investigation Step 4; `landau-fan` step 1 checks).** Fast axis: paired n-line at D = 0 (run-5 cal, D_offset −0.013), **n = 1.0 → 3.24×10¹², 48 pts** (Δn = 0.048 — the ray-Nyquist floor at 1 T is 0.048; n_max is the TG limit: V_tg = 0.995 V). Slow axis: **two-segment uniform-1/B**, 0.3 → 1.2 T at Δ(1/B) = 0.030 (85 rows; only the light tone lives there — heavy onset ≥ 1.36 T at the highest n; alias edge 16.7 T clears the 7.6 T light tone and its 2nd harmonic) and 1.2 → 10 T at Δ(1/B) = 0.0100 (75 rows; ⅓-Nyquist on F_L(3.24) = 33.5 T). **159 rows.** Resolution: light tone δF = 0.31 T (0.3–10 T) — tracks the ABAB light branch's dive (model 7.3 → 3.6 → 0.6 T from n = 3.2 → 2 → 1) vs an ABCB Dirac partner; heavy tones δF ≈ 2.3 T (onset 1.9 T) — separates ABAB-heavy from F_L only at n ≳ 2.5 (split 5–7 T), which is why n_max sits at the limit; the partition test f₁ + f₂ = F_L(n) across all 48 columns is the algebraic backbone. Dwell: `fast_delay = 2.0` s = 6.7 τ (τ = 0.3 s at 24 dB/oct → 90 % settled; the run-5 gatemap's 1.0 s was a slope read). Sensitivity **100 µV = 10 kΩ FS**: worst corner is n = 1.0 × 10 T (R(1.0, 0 T) = 117 Ω from run 5; the spot checks peaked at 576 Ω at 10 T) — 17× headroom over the largest observed R, at 0.37 Ω/LSB (quantization measured on run 6). If the lowest-n columns still rail above ~9 T, their FFT windows truncate (`r_full_scale`) and the light-tone information at low B is untouched.

**Price (calibrated on run 5: 1.10 s/pt at fast_delay 1.0, 5.8 s row turnaround; run 6: 3.1 s per field set).** 2.10 s/pt × 48 + ~9 s = 110 s/row × 159 = 4.8 h grid + 9.7 T traverse 1.2 h = 6.0 h; realistic **≈ 6.5 h**, ending at **10 T** with the gates at (n = 3.24, D = 0) — the next step (LK mass split or a hole-side ride-down) starts there. Magnet is at 0 T now: the fan starts where it sits.

**Checkpoints (E1, self-driven):** (1) first 3 rows (~10 min): R levels vs 10 kΩ FS, gate currents at baseline, both gates tracking the n-line; (2) row 85 / 1.2 T (~2.7 h): partial per-column FFT of the light tone across n — does F_light(n) fall toward zero with decreasing n (ABAB) or stay linear-ish (Dirac partner)?; (3) ~4 T (~4.8 h): heavy tones emerging per column, partition sum vs F_L(n); abort at any point on a rail, leakage, or a non-tracking gate. **Hedge on the page:** the run-5 map carries a second CNP ridge (region B, electron-doped by 0.43×10¹² relative to region A) — a mosaic/contact-doping candidate; a second tone family offset in n by that amount is the signature to look for in the per-column FFTs.
