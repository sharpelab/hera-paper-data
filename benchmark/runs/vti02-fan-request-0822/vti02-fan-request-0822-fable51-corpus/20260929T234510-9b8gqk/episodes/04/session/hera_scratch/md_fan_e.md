## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), D = 0 electron side, n = +0.6 … +3.2×10¹², B 0.7 → 10 T — **E1 auto, executed by HERA**

**Why now (spot-check read, runs 6/7 — `sdh_onset` / `sdh_fft`, analysis landing in ana).** Both spot checks carry strong SdH. Electron side (run 6, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T): a dominant tone at **7.1–7.3 T** (SNR ≈ 100 at the 1 T edge of the fine leg, harmonics at 2×/3×/4× from the deep QH-like minima), further low tones at ≈ 3.0 / 4.2 / 5.2 T, and above ~5 T a ≈ 32 T tone at F_L. The model's ABAB light pocket at this density is **7.31 T** (heavy 25.8 T; ABCA singleton 33.1 T) — but a one-density record cannot separate a light pocket from a harmonic comb, a sum/breakdown tone, or an alias, and the 3–5 T tones have no template. Hole side (run 7, n = −1.2×10¹²): 4.7 / 6.6 / 12.3 T (F_L = 12.4; model ABAB pair 5.9 / 6.5 T — the near-CNP exception regime, below the record's δF), **measured onset 0.71 T**. The investigation's read is the **F(n) trajectory** — harmonics track k·f exactly, a real pocket follows its own Onsager curve, and the Luttinger sum f₁ + f₂ = F_L(n) must hold across density — so the fan is the next step, on the electron side where |n| reaches the design densities (≳ 2–3×10¹²) at which the ABAB heavy tone (26 T) and the ABCA singleton (33.6 T) separate by 7.5 T.

**Design (recipe step 1, computed).** Fast axis: paired (V_tg, V_bg) n-line at D = 0 from the run-5 calibration, **n = +3.2 → +0.6×10¹² in 0.05 steps (53 columns)**, high n first each row; n_max = 3.2 keeps 16 mV under the TG limit, n_min = 0.6 covers the model's light-pocket emptying at n ≈ 0.8×10¹² (where ABAB's heavy tone touches F_L). Slow axis: **uniform in 1/B, 0.7 → 10 T, 133 rows**, Δ(1/B) = 0.01007 /T = ⅓-Nyquist on F_L(3.2) = 33.1 T → 3.0 rows per ν = 4 period at every field; δF = 1/(1/0.7 − 1/10) = 0.75 T; the ABAB partner at n = 2.0 (3.6 T) gets 4.7 periods, at 3.2 (7.3 T) 9.7. B_lo = 0.7 T: run 7's measured onset; run 6's onset is censored at 1 T (the 50 mT leg below 1 T under-samples a 7 T tone), so the true electron onset is < 1 T and the 0.7–1.0 T rows (25 rows, ~30 min) are the low-field extension the recipe asks for, taken in-fan. n-axis Nyquist at 0.7 T: ray spacing 0.68×10¹² = 13.5 columns.
