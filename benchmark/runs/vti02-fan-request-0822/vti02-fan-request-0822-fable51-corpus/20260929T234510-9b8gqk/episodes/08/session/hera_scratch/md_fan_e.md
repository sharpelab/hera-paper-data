## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side, D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`) — n = +1.2 → +3.2×10¹², B 0.4 → 10 T

**Why the electron side (spot-check read, runs 6/7, from the TSVs):** run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T) carries a stable two-tone set — **7.3 T** (alive down to the 10 mT leg's edge at 1.0 T) and **29.4 T** (dominant, onset ≈ 1.8 T), stable across FFT cutoffs 1.5–5 T; 15/22 T are the light tone's 2×/3× comb, 59/88/120/150 T the heavy tone's harmonics (QH-like sharpening at high B), and a **≈ 37 T feature ≈ 7.3 + 29.4** is present natively — the breakdown sum-tone the investigation flags as *expected* for ABAB. Sum 36.7 T = 1.11·F_L (inside the lever-arm prior); partition 0.80/0.20 vs the model's ABAB 0.78/0.22 at this density. Run 7 (n = −1.2×10¹², the hole side's TG-limited reach) is the predicted near-CNP regime: peaks wander with the cutoff (7.8/2.2 → 5.0/12.5 → 12.8 T) — uninformative for the count. **Coverage trade:** the hole side is excluded from the fan; its D = 0 line reaches only n = −1.27×10¹² (TG −0.3 V), inside the low-|n| exception regime where the templates don't separate, so full coverage would buy ~2 h of resolution-limited columns. The fan follows the cleaner side, as the investigation prescribes.

**What the fan decides:** F(n) of both tones across n = 1.2 → 3.2. Model (ABAB, this density window): heavy/F_L runs 0.78 → 0.91 as n falls, light branch 7.3 → 1.2 T (empties toward n ≈ 0.8) — vs ABCA a single tone at F_L throughout, vs ABCB a light Dirac-shaped partner. Tests: f₁(n) + f₂(n) = s·F_L(n) with one global scale s (Luttinger, algebraic); dual Onsager overlay (ABAB vs trilayer curves); the 37 T tone's B-window behaviour (breakdown | pocket); n = 3.2 column cross-checks run 6 directly.

**Design (investigation Step 2 + `landau-fan` step 1, computed):** B_lo: `sdh_onset` on run 6's 10 mT leg gives the light tone *censored at 1.0 T* (lower bound), heavy tone 1.8 T. Rather than a separate low-field extension, the fan carries a **low leg 0.4 → 1.0 T at Δ(1/B) = 0.025** (60 rows, Nyquist 20 T — light tone + its 2× only; the heavy tone is dead there): it pins the light-tone onset per column and extends the light-branch track from n ≳ 2.0 (≥ 3 periods at B_lo = 1) down to n ≈ 1.5 (≥ 5 periods at B_lo = 0.4). Price of that leg ≈ 1 h 20 m. **Main leg 1.0 → 10 T at Δ(1/B) = 0.005** (181 rows, Nyquist 100 T). This is finer than the F_L Nyquist-3 floor (0.0088) on purpose: run 6 resampled onto candidate grids shows that at 0.0083 the heavy tone's 3rd harmonic (88 T, SNR 52) folds to **32 T — onto F_L**, which would fake exactly the singleton the origin check has to exclude; at 0.005 every harmonic through 5× folds outside 0–45 T. δF at the top: 1/(1/1.8 − 0.1) = 2.2 T (heavy vs ABCA split 7.5 T at n = 3.2, 3.6 T at n = 2.0). n-axis: 51 points, Δn = 0.04×10¹² = 2.4 per 4-fold ray at 1 T (the 2D ray-pattern read floors at B ≥ 1 T; below that the low leg is per-column-FFT-only). Dwell 1.2 s = 4τ (B is the slow axis, settled through the blocking ramp; the fast-axis lag is a common ≲ 1-column n-shift, not a tone attenuation). Sensitivity 50 µV = 5 kΩ FS, fixed: worst corner low-n × 10 T — run 7 at n = −1.2 (closer to the CNP than any fan column) peaked at 587 Ω = 0.12 FS; run 6 resolved SdH at 0.2–1 % FS from 1 T at the same setting.
