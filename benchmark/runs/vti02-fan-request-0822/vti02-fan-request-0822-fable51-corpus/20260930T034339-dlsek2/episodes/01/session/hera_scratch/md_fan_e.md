## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side at D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`), n = +1.0 → +3.24×10¹², B 1.2 → 10 T uniform in 1/B

**Why this fan (spot-check read, runs 6/7 — ana).** Run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T): a 7.2 T tone alive to the valid-sampling edge (`sdh_onset` censored at 1.0 T, SNR ≈ 100; Rxx minima reach ~0 Ω from 1.4 T), 22.3 T (onset 1.4 T), 29.4 T (onset 1.8 T), and a comb at 2×/4×/6×/8× of 7.3 T; above 4 T the record is dominated by a ~30 T tone with its own 60/90 T harmonics. Run 7 (hole, n = −1.2, F_L = 12.4 T): 4.7 / 6.6 / 12.3 T — the close-pair near-CNP regime. Two readings fit one density: **(a) ABAB at a global density scale ≈ 0.89** — pair 7.2 + 22.3 = 29.5 T = F_L′ with the symmetry-allowed sum/breakdown tone at F_L′ (model pair at n = 3.2: 7.3 + 25.8 T; partition 0.24/0.76 measured vs 0.22/0.78 model); **(b) one strongly anharmonic 7.3 T oscillation** whose 3rd/4th harmonics are the 22/29 T peaks, with a separate F_L-scale ladder at high B. Only n-dependence separates them: a harmonic sits at k·f_light at every n, while the ABAB heavy tone follows its own curve (model heavy/light ratio 6.6 at n = 1.5 → 3.5 at n = 3.25; it crosses 3 only above this fan's range). The electron side is the fan side: higher |n|, separated tones; the hole side reaches only −1.27×10¹² inside the validators and sits in the close-pair regime.

**Design (recipe step 1, computed).** Fast axis: D = 0 n-line 1.0 → 3.24×10¹², 61 pts (Δn 0.037), (V_tg, V_bg) from (+0.353, +0.351) to (+0.995, +1.181) V — 5 mV under TG_MAX; run-5 calibration (ratio 0.773, n_offset −0.089, D_offset −0.013). Slow axis: `field_invB(1.2, 10, 100)`: Δ(1/B) = 1/(4·F_L(3.24)) = 0.0074 /T → sampling 135 T, Nyquist 67.5 T — the F_L tone and its 2nd harmonic unaliased in every column; a 30 T tone's 3rd/4th harmonics alias to 45 / 15 T (stated; off the claimed tones). **B_lo = 1.2 T**: the onset is censored at the record's valid edge (1.0 T lower bound); 1.2 T is the 5-period floor for the 7.2 T partner (1.26 T) — the 22 rows in 1.0–1.2 T would cost ≈ 46 min for 0.17 /T of light-tone record and are not taken. Record 0.733 /T → δF = 1.36 T (2.2 T from the 1.8 T sum-tone onset); the 22 T tone gets 16 periods, the 29 T tone 13. n-axis Nyquist: ν = 4 ray spacing at 1.2 T = 0.116×10¹² → 3.1 pts/period (≥ 7 above 3 T). Dwell: `fast_delay` 1.8 s = 6τ (τ = 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct — the per-column-FFT floor, recipe step 7 / burn #8), `slow_delay` 1 s after the blocking field set (+3 s driver settle). Sensitivity fixed at **100 µV = 10 kΩ FS**: worst corner n = 1.0 × 10 T — R(B = 0) is 117 Ω there vs 56 Ω at n = 3.2 (run 5), so ≈ 2.1× the 530 Ω spot-check maximum ≈ 1.1 kΩ, ×2–3 for broken-symmetry QH maxima → ≥ 3× headroom; the low-B SdH swing (15 Ω) is 0.15 % FS, where the spot check at 0.3 % gave SNR ≈ 100.

**Cost (from runs 5–7 timing: 0.17 s/pt readback, 3 s settle, 0.137 T/min).** 100 rows × (61 × 1.97 s + 5 s) = 208 min + 73 min ramps (0 → 1.2 T positioning + 8.8 T traverse) = 4.7 h; realistic **≈ 5 h**. Ends at **10 T, gates parked at 0** — the next step (LK mass split, `temperature-dependence-vti`, at n = +3.2) rides the magnet down. **Coverage trade:** hole side not in this fan (doubles the row time, +3.5 h, in the weakly-discriminating close-pair regime); n < 1.0 excluded (model light pocket < 0.6 T, unresolvable in any window; CNP corner threatens the scale). Region B (electron-doped by 0.43×10¹² vs A, run 5) is a series mosaic: expect satellite tones at n + 0.43 (×1.13 at n = 3.2) — carried into the read.

**Checkpoints.** First 3 rows (~20 min after start, after positioning): signal level, no rail at the low-n end, gate currents at the pA floor, field readback tracking the 1/B grid. At ~2 T (row 45, ≈ 1 h 40 m): per-column FFT of the landed record — the 7 T tone across n. At ~4 T (row 82, ≈ 3 h 20 m): interim tone-set read (22 T tone vs 3×f_light across n). Abort on a sustained rail or a leak trip; otherwise run to 10 T. Preconditions printed at the top: T_sample at base, B ≈ 0, V_si = 0, PS heater on / output enabled, Keithley nplci = 1.
