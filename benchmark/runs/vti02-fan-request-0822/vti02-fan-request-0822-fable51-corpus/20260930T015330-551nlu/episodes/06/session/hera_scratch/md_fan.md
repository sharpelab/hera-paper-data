## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, n = +1.0 → +3.25×10¹² at D = 0, B 1 → 10 T uniform in 1/B

**Why this fan, from the spot checks (runs 6/7, read from the TSVs with `sdh_onset` / `sdh_fft`).** Both sides oscillate. Electron (run 6, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T): a 7.1–7.5 T tone (SNR 40) alive down to the 10 mT-sampled edge at 1.0 T, with a comb at 14.1 / 22.1 / 29.5 T (SNR 89 at 29.5) and the QH regime setting in above ~7.5 T. Hole (run 7, n = −1.2×10¹², F_L = 12.4 T): onset 0.7 T, strongest tone 12.3 T ≈ F_L (SNR 124) plus 6.6 / 4.7 / 8.5 / 9.4 T. Against the model's F(n) table the electron 7.3 T tone lands on the **ABAB light-pocket curve** (7.3 T at 3.2×10¹²; heavy partner predicted 25.8 T, ABCA singleton 33.1 T), while the hole 12.3 T tone sits **at F_L** — a singleton, a breakdown sum tone, or the near-balanced ABAB pair (model 6.4/6.8 T) the recipe warns is beat-like at low |n|. Two spots can't separate those readings; the F(n) trajectories and the Luttinger sum across density can. The fan goes to the **electron side**: it is the only side reaching |n| ≳ 2–3×10¹² inside the validators (D = 0 window −1.27 … +3.27), where the templates separate (heavy-ABAB 26 T vs ABCA 33 T at the top of the window). The hole side (|n| ≤ 1.27, the investigation's low-|n| exception regime) is **excluded** from this fan — it would add 64 % to the cost and its columns are trajectory support, not the claim; if the read wants it, a hole fan rides the magnet down from 10 T afterward (never re-traverse).

**Design (landau-fan step 1, computed).** *B_lo = 1.0 T*: `sdh_onset` returns onset_b = 0.30 T for run 6 but flags the carrying tone *censored* — and the sub-1 T part of that record was 50 mT-sampled (aliased for a 7 T tone), so the only validated low edge is 1.0 T; at B_lo = 1.0, B_hi = 10 the 1/B record is 0.90 T⁻¹ → **δF = 1.11 T** and 6.6 periods of the 7.3 T partner (≥ 5 required). *B axis*: uniform in 1/B (the VTI is measurement-dominated — every row costs the full n-line, so rows go where the periods are), Δ(1/B) = 9.9×10⁻³ T⁻¹ = **3.06 samples per period of F_L(3.25) = 33.6 T** (Nyquist 50.6 T; the 4× harmonic at 29.5 T is inside it), **N_B = 92** rows, ΔB 10 mT at 1 T → 0.9 T at 10 T. *n axis*: **N_n = 71** (Δn = 0.032×10¹² = 3.0 points per LL-ray period at 1 T). *Coverage trade*: the ABAB light branch is unresolvable in this window below n ≈ 2.2×10¹² (< 5 periods of a < 4.5 T tone) — the low-n columns carry the heavy branch / F_L trajectory only; and n < 1.0 is excluded (CNP neighbourhood at 10 T is the dynamic-range hazard, and carries no claim). *Sensitivity*: **sr1 100 µV = 10 kΩ FS**, fixed — worst corner n = 1.0×10¹² × 10 T (ν = 4.1; run 7 at |n| = 1.2 reached 590 Ω at 10 T, still rising) gets ≥ 10× headroom, at the price of the highest-n/lowest-B corner sitting at ~0.1–0.2 % of range (R ≈ 15 Ω) — that corner is the sub-onset region anyway; a rail is unrecoverable, resolution loss is not. *Dwell*: fast_delay 1.2 s = 4 τ (τ = 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct; recipe step-7 default — the fast-axis lag is a per-column n-label shift of < 1 step, invisible to a per-column FFT), slow_delay 1 s on top of the magnet's 3 s settle.

**Price.** 92 × 71 = 6532 points. Calibration: run 5 (gatemap, fast_delay 1.0) ran 1.17 s/pt all-in → 1.37 s/pt here → 97 s/row + 4 s row overhead → 155 min of grid, plus the 10 T traverse at 0.137 T/min = 73 min → 3 h 48 m raw, **4 h 05 m** with the session's ~8 % overrun margin. Ends at **10 T, gates parked at 0**.
