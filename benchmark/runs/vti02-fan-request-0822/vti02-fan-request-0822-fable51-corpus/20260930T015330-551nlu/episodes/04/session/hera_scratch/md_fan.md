## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, D = 0, n = +1.00 → +3.24×10¹², B 1 → 10 T

**Why this fan (spot-check read, runs 6/7 — analysis lands in ana).** Run 6 (electron, n = +3.2, F_L = 33.1 T) splits by field window: 1.5–4 T is a **7.2 T harmonic comb** (7.2 / 14.4 / 21.6 / 28.8), 4–10 T is dominated by a tone at **33.3 T = 1.00·F_L** with overtones at ≈ 60 and 87 T. Run 7 (hole, n = −1.2, F_L = 12.4 T) shows the same motif: 5.3 T + 12.3 T (= 1.00·F_L). The model (fs_count_b0, run-5 calibration) puts the ABAB light pocket at 7.3 T for n = +3.2 and 5.9/6.5 T for n = −1.2 — so the low tone matches the ABAB light pocket on both sides while the ABAB *heavy* partner (25.8 T expected) is not resolved and the F_L tone sits exactly where an inter-block breakdown sum-tone would. The alternative — an ABCA singleton at F_L plus a comb of unknown parentage — is not excluded by two densities. The investigation's discriminator is the **n-dependence**: a harmonic tracks k·f exactly, a real pocket follows its own Onsager curve, a breakdown tone tracks F_L; the fan measures all three trajectories at once. Side: the hole side of the D = 0 line reaches only |n| = 1.27 (TG −0.3 V), inside the near-CNP exception regime, so the electron side (n up to +3.24 at TG +0.995 V) is the only side with a usable window.

**Design (investigation Step 2 + landau-fan step 1, computed):** B_lo = 1.00 T — `sdh_onset` on run 6 above the coarse 50 mT segment gives onset_b = 1.00 T, *censored* (the 7.3 T tone is still above threshold at the record edge), i.e. a lower bound; taking it as B_lo rather than extending below 1 T is a stated trade (below). Slow axis uniform in 1/B (`field_invB`-equivalent), Δ(1/B) = 0.0075 T⁻¹ → **121 rows**: Nyquist 66.7 T = 1.99·F_L(n_max = 3.24) — a 2× margin over the recipe's F_L rule, chosen so the *observed* 2·F_L overtone (≈ 60 T at n = 3.2) does not fold into the claim band; the ≈ 87 T third-order content of the top few columns aliases to ≈ 45 T with a *negative* n-slope (flagged, not a pocket). Fast axis: 49 points, Δn = 0.0467×10¹² → 2.07 pts per LL ray at 1.0 T, 3.1 at 1.5 T (the floor). δF = 1/(1/B_lo − 1/B_hi) = 1.11 T; the heavy-ABAB (25.8) vs sum/ABCA (33) split is 7 T — resolved. B_hi = 10 T (session cap) keeps the high-B window where the F_L tone lives and where the B-window-split (breakdown vs pocket) read is made. Dwell: fast_delay 1.5 s = 5τ (τ = 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct); slow_delay 1 s on top of the driver's 3 s field settle.
