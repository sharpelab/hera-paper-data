## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side, D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`) — 10 → 1 T descending, n = 3.2 → 1.0×10¹²

**Why now (runs 6/7, `sdh-spot-check` read).** Run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T) is strongly oscillatory: dominant tone 7.1 T (onset ≤ 1 T — censored at the edge of the Nyquist-valid record), plus 14.1 / 22.1 / 29.5 T. Those are flagged as 2×/3×/4× of 7.1 T, but 22.1 T is also where the ABAB heavy pocket sits under a ~0.9 lever-arm scale (model 25.6 T at scale 1), and 29.5 T is also where the F_L / breakdown-sum tone sits — and both strengthen relative to 7 T at high B (window split 1.5–4 T vs 4–10 T: 0.6 → 1.8 and 0.5 → 3.1), which harmonics *and* breakdown tones both do. Only the n-dependence separates them: a harmonic tracks k·f exactly across n, a pocket follows its own Onsager curve (ABAB model: 3×light = 10.8 / 20.1 T vs heavy = 17.1 / 24.4 T at n = 2 / 3). Run 7 (hole, n = −1.2, F_L = 12.4 T) is the near-CNP semimetal regime (crowded 4.7 / 6.6 / 8.5 / 12.3 T set) and the hole side is capped at n ≈ −1.1 by the TG −0.3 V limit — **coverage trade:** hole side excluded, it cannot reach the |n| ≳ 2×10¹² regime where the templates separate. **n < 1.0×10¹² excluded**: at 10 T that corner is ν < 4 (insulating-scale R, would set the whole fan's sensitivity); n_lo = 1.0 keeps ν(10 T) ≈ 4 so the ν = 4/8/12/16 layer-number ladder lands inside the window.

**Design (computed, printed by the cell).** Fast axis: the D = 0 n-line 3.2 → 1.0×10¹² in 101 pts (Δn = 0.022×10¹² ≈ 4.4 pts per 4eB/h ray at 1 T; n_hi = the run-6 density, so the top column is a grid-change reproduction of run 6). Slow axis: 10 → 1 T, 93 rows uniform in 1/B, Δ(1/B) = 0.0098 /T = 1/(3·34 T) — 3 rows per period of F_L(n_hi); the weak ~59 T content in run 6 aliases to ~42 T, outside the read band. B_lo = 1 T: the 7 T tone persists below it but the sub-1 T record is only usable for F < 2.5 T, and the heavy/F_L tones onset at 1.4–1.9 T; δF = 1/(1/1 − 1/10) = 1.1 T, so 22 vs 25.6 T and 29.5 vs 33 T are ~3–4 δF apart. `fast_delay` = 1.2 s (4τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB): along B every point sits after the 10τ magnet settle, so the SdH amplitude per column is not attenuated; the dwell only smears n by ~1.5 pts (0.03×10¹²), well under the ray spacing. **Descending so the first row is the worst dynamic-range corner** (low n × 10 T) — the cheapest kill.

**Three chained runs in this cell.** (A) measured ride-up 0 → 10 T at n = +2.5×10¹² as a third `sdh-spot-check` (ABAB light/heavy ≈ 5.1 / 20.8 T vs 3×light = 15.3 vs F_L = 25.9 — all > 4 δF apart; +22 min over the bare 73 min traverse the fan needs anyway; 50 µV). (B) one n-line row at 10 T at 100 µV → R_max of the worst corner sets the fan's fixed sensitivity (smallest of 50/100/200/500 µV with FS ≥ 2·R_max); if that row rails at 10 kΩ the cell stops (field at 10 T, gates parked) and n_lo gets redesigned — no blind escalation. (C) the fan; ends at **B = 1 T, gates parked at 0** (the LK mass series starts from there). Leak trip 1 nA on both Keithleys; clips printed.

**Price** (calibrated: 3.1 s per field setpoint over the bare ramp, runs 6/7; 0.10 s/pt read overhead over `fast_delay`, gatemap run 5): ride-up ≈ 1.6 h, sizing ≈ 3 min, fan ≈ 93 × (101 × 1.3 s + 8 s) + 66 min ramp ≈ 4.7 h → **≈ 6.4 h, quoted 6 h 50 m**. **Checkpoints:** ride-up first 20 pts (signal, leakage, field tracking); sizing-row verdict; fan rows 1–3 (10 / 9.1 / 8.3 T — any `fs` ≥ 0.9 → interrupt and re-run at the next sensitivity, magnet still near 10 T); then every ~60–90 min a per-column FFT on the landed rows (tone count, F(n) tracks vs the 3×/4× harmonic lines) — abandon if the channel goes flat/railed or T leaves base. Known confound to watch: gatemap run 5 showed a second ridge (region B, 0.43×10¹² electron-doped vs A) — its tones would appear at F(n + 0.43).
