## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side (`landau-fan-vti`), D = 0, n = +1.0 → +3.2×10¹², B 0.7 → 10 T

**Why the electron side, and why now.** Spot check run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹², D = 0, 10 mT uniform-B, 10 τ settle) is decisive-quality: SdH from ≲ 1 T with zero-Rxx minima above 4 T; `sdh_onset` finds a dominant **7.1 T** tone (SNR 41, still above threshold at the record's 0.3 T edge → onset ≤ ~1 T, *censored*) plus **14.1 / 22.2 / 29.5 T** components (onsets 1.6 / 1.3 / 1.9 T, SNR 50–100). The harmonic screen labels those 2×/3×/4× of 7.1 — but 7.1 + 22.2 = 29.3 ≈ 0.89·F_L (F_L = 33.1 T), i.e. also exactly the ABAB light + heavy + breakdown-sum constellation (model at 3.2×10¹²: 7.3 / 25.8 T; sum tone at F_L). The investigation forbids deciding that on the ratio alone: a harmonic tracks k·f across density, a real pocket follows its own Onsager curve — that is what the fan reads. Run 7 (hole, n = −1.2, the TG-limited end of the D = 0 line) is the near-CNP balanced-pair regime (tones 4.7 / 6.6 / 8.5 / 12.3 T, F_L 12.4) — a preview, not a read; the hole side of this device cannot reach design density inside the validators. **Coverage trade:** electron side only, n from +1.0 (light ABAB pocket → 0.6 T, the "empties toward low n" trajectory; ν = 8/12/16 ladder visible for the layer-number check) to +3.2 (V_tg = 0.984 V, 16 mV under the TG limit — the validator, not the physics, sets n_max).

**Design (recipe step 1, computed).** Fast axis: 45-pt paired (V_tg, V_bg) line at D = 0 from the run-5 calibration (ratio 0.773, n_offset −0.089, D_offset −0.013), Δn = 0.05×10¹², V_tg 0.353–0.984 V, V_bg 0.351–1.166 V. Slow axis: `field_invB(0.7, 10, 121)`, Δ(1/B) = 0.0111 /T → Nyquist F = 45 T ≥ 1.2·F_L(3.2) = 40 T (lever-arm scale prior) and the 1/3-rule to 30 T; 5.5 mT steps at the bottom, 0.9 T at the top. B_lo = 0.7 T: the carrying 7.1 T tone is censored at run 6's low edge so 0.3 T is only a lower bound; 0.7 T keeps 4.7 periods of the n = 2.0 ABAB partner (3.55 T model) and 9.7 of the n = 3.2 one — the recipe's ≥ 5-period floor at the fan's design densities — while not spending 40 more rows below 0.7 T that only the (LK/Dingle-dead there) light tone could use. δF = 1/(1/0.7 − 1/10) = 0.75 T for the light tone; for the heavy tones (onset ≈ 1.3 T) δF ≈ 1.5 T, against a heavy-ABAB vs ABCA split of 3.6 T at n = 2 and 7 T at n = 3.2 (≥ 2·δF). n-axis ray Nyquist: 2.9 columns per ray period at 1.5 T, 3.9 at 2 T — the (n, B)-plane ray read is floored at **B ≥ 1.5 T**; per-column FFT (the claim) has no n-axis Nyquist. Dwell `fast_delay = 2.0 s` ≈ 6.7 τ (τ = 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct; the n-axis carries the per-column read — recipe step 7's ~6 τ). Sensitivity 100 µV = 10 kΩ FS: the global worst corner is low n × high B (n ≈ 1×10¹² near ν = 4–8 at 8–10 T, QH peaks of order kΩ on top of the run-7-like ×4 MR); the spot check's 5 kΩ FS would soft-clip there and clipping is unrecoverable, so the 10–50 Ω high-n floor pays with 0.3 Ω LSB.

**Price.** 45 × 121 = 5445 pts at 2.17 s/pt (run 5: 1.17 s/pt at `fast_delay` 1.0 → 0.17 s read overhead) → 103 s/row incl. flyback + settle; grid 208 min + 10 T blocking ramp 73 min ≈ **4.7 h**, ETA carries an 8 % margin. Starts where the magnet sits (0 T → 0.7 T), ends at **10 T** with gates parked at 0 V — the next step (hole-side fan or the LK ladder) rides the magnet down, never re-traversing.

**Checkpoints.** *First rows* (~10 min): channels live, sr1 ≪ 0.9 FS, leakage at the ±100 pA flyback level, B readback tracking setpoints, T_sample 1.1–1.4 K — abort on a rail or a dead channel. *Row 39 / 1.0 T (~75 min):* per-column FFT of the landed low-B record — light-tone presence and its n-trend. *Row 84 / 2.0 T (~2 h 40 m):* F_light(n) trajectory vs the model's ABAB partner curve vs a k·f comb. *Row 111 / 5 T (~3 h 45 m):* heavy-tone / F_L read across n — continue to 10 T unless a column clips (the recipe's non-salvageable failure → stop, resize, re-run the high-B segment). Leak guard trips the sweep at |I_gate| > 1 nA.
