## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side at D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`), n = 1.2 → 3.2×10¹², B 0.6 → 10 T

**Spot-check read (runs 6/7, ana entry follows).** Electron side, n = +3.2×10¹² (run 6, sr1 = B_1–B_2, a mixed Rxx/Rxy pair — the trace changes sign and climbs to 530 Ω at 8.7 T): three tones above the harmonic screen — **7.1 T** (light, censored at every fine-grid low edge → onset < 0.85 T), **22.3 T** (onset 1.2–1.4 T) and **29.4 T** (onset ≈ 1.75 T), with **7.1 + 22.3 = 29.4** — the investigation's ABAB-pair-plus-sum-tone constellation (F_L(3.2) = 33.1 T; 29.4 = F_L at density scale 0.89, inside the lever-arm prior; the model's ABAB pair at 3.2 is 7.3 / 25.8, partition 0.78 vs 0.76 observed). The trap: 22.3 ≈ 3.1 × 7.1 within δF = 1.6 T, so "heavy pocket" vs "3rd harmonic" is undecidable at one density — a harmonic tracks 3f exactly across n, an ABAB heavy pocket runs f_heavy/f_light from 4.8 (n = 2) to 3.5 (n = 3.2) [our-model]. Above ~3.7 T the record turns QH-like (harmonics of 29.4 at 58.6 and 88 T). Hole side, n = −1.2 (run 7): tones 7.0 / 3.9 / 12.4 T (12.4 = F_L exactly), onset 0.71 T — but the D = 0 hole reach is n = −1.27 (TG limit), inside the model's near-degenerate low-|n| regime (ABAB pair 5.9 / 6.5 T, unresolvable at δF = 0.8 T). **Coverage trade:** the fan takes the electron side only — the hole side cannot discriminate at any price within the validators; its complement is the LK mass split, not more hole rows.

**Design (Step 2, computed).** Fast axis: paired n-line n = 1.2 → 3.2×10¹² at D = 0, 76 pts (δn = 0.027; ray Nyquist Δn = 0.058 at 0.6 T → 2.2 pts/Δn, 3.6 at 1 T), V_tg +0.41 → +0.98, V_bg +0.42 → +1.17 V (run-5 calibration; inside the validators, asserted in-cell). Slow axis: 143 rows uniform in 1/B in **three segments** keyed to the run-6 onsets — 0.6–1.0 T at Δ(1/B) = 1/48 (Nyquist 24 T; only the light tone and its 2× live there), 1.0–3.5 T at 1/(3·F_L) = 1/99 (Nyquist 50 T), 3.5–10 T at 1/200 (Nyquist 100 T, so the 58.6/88 T QH harmonics land at their true positions instead of aliasing to 10.9/40.7 T inside the claim band — a flat 1/(3·F_L) grid fails this). B_lo = 0.6 T: the light tone at n = 2.0 (3.6 T) gets 5.5 periods (vs 2.6 from 1.0 T) — that is the n-dependence the harmonic-vs-pocket call and the light-pocket intercept (F → 0 near n ≈ 0.8 [our-model]) need; δF for the 22/29 T tones is set by their own onsets (1.6 / 1.8 T), not by B_lo. Sensitivity fixed at 100 µV (10 kΩ FS): worst corner n = 1.2 × 10 T ≈ 100 Ω × 6–10 MR ≈ 1 kΩ (runs 5/6/7) → ≥ 5× headroom; SdH amplitude at 1 T (~10–20 Ω) is ~100 LSB. fast_delay 1.0 s (3.3 τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB): the n-axis is not the FFT axis — the lag is a < 2-column (0.05×10¹²) smear, below δF for every tone; 6 τ would add ~2 h for no resolvable gain. slow_delay 3 s = 10 τ after the row flyback.

**Price (calibrated).** Per point 1.10 s at fast_delay 1.0 (run 5: 4158 pts in 4873 s less flyback); per row 3.1 s field settle (runs 6/7: 3.1 s/setpoint over the bare ramp) + 1.1 s gate flyback + 3 s slow_delay; bare traverse 10 T / 0.137 T·min⁻¹ = 73 min. 143 × (76 × 1.10 + 7.2) s + 73 min = 4.8 h; with the session's 5–10 % overrun → **≈ 5 h 15 m**, ending at **10 T, gates parked at 0**. The 1/B grid front-loads the rows: B = 1.0 T at +1.0 h (row 33), 1.4 T at +1.7 h, 2.5 T at +2.7 h, 3.5 T at +3.1 h, 5 T at +3.7 h, 10 T at +4.8 h.
