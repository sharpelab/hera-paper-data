## Runs 6–7 — field cuts before the Landau fan (plan step 3)

Raw voltage and drain current are plotted first, followed by measured-current Rxx and FFT window comparisons. Both runs completed: electron run 6 at nominal n=+3.20 has Rxx=0–530 ohm and drain current 9.843–9.935 nA; hole run 7 at nominal n=-1.20 has Rxx=0.38–587 ohm and 9.809–9.931 nA. Maximum longitudinal signals are 5.22/5.76 uV against 50 uV sensitivity; neither voltage channel clipped. Gate-current magnitudes stay below 0.22 nA. Only B_1–B_2 is a connected voltage pair.

The electron 1.5–4 T FFT has peaks near 7.2, 14.4, 21.6, 28.8 T (resolution 2.4 T): harmonics or overlapping bands are not distinguishable here. Hole spectra shift with the analysis window (dominant ~4.8–8.6 T); these finite-window features are not reliable band counts. The onset helper returns electron low-edge censoring at 0.3 T and hole ~0.71 T; neither is a uniquely identified band onset. These cuts advance the campaign by demonstrating oscillatory signal and setting acquisition headroom; they do not settle stacking or independently calibrate density.

Correction to the strength of earlier language in cells 10/12: two gate ridges are consistent with spatially inhomogeneous doping but do not establish two separately resolved regions. A resistance increase with D is consistent with gap opening but is not a gap measurement; the resistance minimum supplies a provisional D-zero proxy only. The fan (plan step 4) will test branch origins/crossings across both carrier signs. Absolute density still uses the geometry anchor, and the candidate domain offset must remain a competing explanation.
