## HERA — bipolar Landau fan for the stacking call (plan step 4)

Runs 6/7 show oscillations on the sole connected B_1–B_2 pair, steady 9.81–9.94 nA drain current, ≤0.216 nA absolute gate currents and peak |Rxx| 530/587 Ω. The electron spectrum has ~7, 22 and 30 T components, but harmonics and the secondary CNP of run 5 make a band-count inference premature. This fan tracks the branches across both carrier signs and both neutrality features; raw gate axes remain authoritative because run 5 fixes the capacitance ratio but leaves the absolute scale geometry-anchored and D=0 a resistance-symmetry proxy.

**Coverage and resolution:** nominal n_A −1.2..+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² at operational D≈0, 89 points (0.05×10¹² increments); TG −0.277033..+0.983594 V, BG −0.465040..+1.166281 V, Si fixed 0 V. Four context rows at B=0,0.5,0.75,1 T precede 103 rows uniform in 1/B from 1.25 to 6 T: Δ(1/B)=0.006209 T⁻¹, <0.23 cycles per row for F≤37 T. The lower context rows do not resolve high-density oscillations; at 1.25 T the nominal fourfold density period has only ~2.4 samples, improving to ~11.6 at 6 T. This first fan resolves the moderate-field branch pattern; a low-field fine fan remains conditional on what appears. The small density tails to the D≈0 operating-box edges (approximately −1.27 and +3.27) are omitted; extending them would add ~20 min. Extending 6→10 T at the same inverse-B spacing adds roughly 3 h, deferred until branch visibility warrants it. Negative field and other D lines are not covered.

**Acquisition and price:** after read-only preconditions check the handoff state, set sr1 to 100 µV (10 kΩ nominal full scale) because state0 restored 2 µV. Leave both lock-ins at τ=0.3 s / 24 dB/oct and use 3 s per point (10τ); retain gate steps and Si ramp rate. Run 5 measured 1.098 s/point with 1 s dwell and NPLC=1; budget 3.2 s/point here for dual-gate motion and extra readbacks. 9,523 points cost ~8.5 h, the 0→6 T ramp floor is 6×7.3=43.8 min, plus flybacks, 3 s magnet settles, row freshness checks and contingency. Finish at 6 T to avoid a wasted return traverse.

**Checkpoints:** inspect the first B=0 row against the session's zero-field data, then the 0.5/0.75 T rows for drain loss, leakage, clipping and phase anomalies; inspect the hera-watch digest at least every 30 min thereafter, with a branch-visibility decision near 2 T. Continue only if the measured channels remain sound; an unresolved pattern at the first few low-B rows alone is not a wash-out. All followed channels have live plots. Point guards stop for >1 nA gate current, drain outside 5–15 nA, >90% lock-in full scale, T/freshness failure or setpoint/readback mismatch. On safety failure, notify and drop E0. **VTI-specific:** a kernel interrupt does not stop an active magnet ramp; the supply continues to its current row target. Re-read actual state after any stop; operator action at the supply is required to halt that ramp.
