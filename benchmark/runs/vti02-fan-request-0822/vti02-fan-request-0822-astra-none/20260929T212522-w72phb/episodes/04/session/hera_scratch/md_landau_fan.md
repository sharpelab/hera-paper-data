## HERA — Landau fan across both neutrality ridges (plan step 4)

Runs 6/7 show unrailed oscillations on both carrier sides (Rxx peaks 530/587 Ohm; drain current >9.80 nA; |gate currents| <0.216 nA). Step 4 of `hera_scratch/plan.md` follows the run-5 inferred D=0 line through both CNPs: co-swept TG/BG over provisional n=-1.20..+3.20e12 cm^-2, 111 points, at B=0, 0.5, 0.75, 1 T and 86 uniformly spaced inverse-field points from 1.5 to 10 T. The fan tests branch connectivity and common versus shifted neutrality intercepts; spectra alone cannot separate multiple bands from the two run-5 ridges. The capacitance ratio is measured, absolute n is geometry-anchored, and D=0 from the resistance minimum remains provisional. Only B_1–B_2 is measured; two ridges are not two independently contacted regions.

Coverage: both signs and both neutrality ridges are included, with small gate margins inside the usable D=0 interval (~-1.28..+3.26e12). Beyond that interval is inaccessible without leaving this D line or the validators; no limit extension is proposed. B<1.5 T is a coarse preview, not a spectrum-quality fan: uniform inverse-field spacing above 1.5 T is 0.006667/T (~4 samples per 37 T period); delta n=0.04e12 gives ~3.6 points per fourfold LL interval at 1.5 T. A dense extension 0.75–1.5 T at the same inverse-field spacing adds ~100 rows, about 10 hours, with finer density sampling needed too. It is deferred because the electron low-edge onset estimate is censored/noisy and the hole record already samples this regime. No extra field traversal: start from the operator's zero-field handoff, finish at 10 T for a possible descending follow-up.

Timing: 10 T / 0.137 T/min = 73 min bare ramp; data/6 and data/7 measure ~3.08 s fixed field-set overhead. Data/5 gives 1.102 s/point at 1 s dwell; allow ~0.25 s overhead for co-ramped gates and added readbacks. 9990 points × 3.25 s + 73 min + row flyback/settling is ~10.4 h, priced with margin. The 3 s gate-point dwell is 10 tau at the recorded 0.3 s / 24 dB/oct setting. Sensitivity 100 uV provides 10 kOhm nominal headroom; no autoranging mid-run.

Read-only preflight prints expected versus actual base temperatures, live logfiles, heaters off, Si 0, magnet output/PS state, drive/filter/pacing and exact validators. No safety surface, Si setpoint or ramp pacing changes. All followed values are plotted, including both temperatures, lock-in phases, gate currents and field/gate readbacks. Point guards stop on >1 nA gate current, 90% full scale, current loss, base-T departure or readback mismatch; logfile freshness is rechecked each row. Read the first landed points and first three rows for liveness/rail/leakage, then every five rows (~30 minutes), with science checkpoints near 2, 4 and 6 T. Stop if no reproducible fan structure is present by 6 T; continue only while data quality and resolving power justify the remaining time. On interruption, hold and re-read actual state before another move; **a VTI kernel interrupt does not stop an active supply ramp**—operator intervention is required for ramp abort.
