**Coverage trade:** this map excludes 0<B<2 T, which is densely covered only at the two spot densities in runs 6/7; extending to 0.7 T at the same inverse-field spacing would add ~149 rows (~16 h of gate acquisition). The small density tails to the TG boundaries are omitted for margin. At 2 T, a fourfold LL density period has ~4.8 samples; the inverse-field spacing resolves a 40 T tone with four samples/period, but higher harmonics may alias. This is one nominal D and positive field only, with no Hall pair: those are explicit limits on a stacking call.

**Timing and checkpoints:** run 5 measured 1.098 s/point with 1 s dwell; allow 3.3 s/point here for 7326 points, plus 10 T/(1/7.3 T/min) = 73 min of blocking magnet ramps, ~10 min turnarounds/settles and 10–15% overhead. Continue only with live current/voltage, unrailed channels, leakage below the existing 1 nA trip and stable base T; all points have automatic guards. Lack of a separable fan is a resolution-limited result, not a stacking label. On a trip, hold gates and stop the sweep; do not auto-park or change safety limits. **Rig-specific:** kernel interrupt does not stop an in-flight CAEN ramp; the supply continues to its last target, so notify Zack and re-read actual state before recovery.
