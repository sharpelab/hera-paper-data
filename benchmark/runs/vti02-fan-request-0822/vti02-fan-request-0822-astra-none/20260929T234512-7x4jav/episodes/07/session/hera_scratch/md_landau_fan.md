## HERA — first Landau fan, both carrier sides (plan step 4)

Runs 6/7 resolve oscillations on B_1–B_2 with stable ~9.9 nA drain current, <=216 pA gate leakage and <6 uV voltage peaks; run 5 supplies the provisional constant-D line and two neutrality-ridge origins. Sweep nominal D=0 across both ridges to distinguish shifted copies from distinct branches/crossings; absolute density remains geometry-anchored and resistance symmetry only suggests the D origin. Preserve signed voltage/current/phase: negative electron R excursions make absolute-value FFT harmonics unreliable, and neither a gap nor a count of tones alone establishes stacking.

Use B=0 baseline then 1–6 T in 0.1 T steps, 111 co-swept gate points at n=-1.2..3.2e12 cm^-2 in .04e12 steps; Si stays 0. Gate endpoints are checked against committed validators before any move; preflight requires base T, live worker logs, heaters off, magnet output/PS on, NPLC=1, unchanged pacing and the recorded drive/filter settings. The 3 s dwell is 10 tau at 24 dB/oct; 100 uV voltage sensitivity provides 10 kohm headroom. Every recorded channel is plotted, including both temperatures, both phases, leakage and measured B.

Coverage trade: this first map excludes 6–10 T (~5 additional hours at the same grid), negative B and D dependence. It covers both signs of density but leaves ~0.07e12 at either gate-limited edge for margin; 1–2 T is reconnaissance (only 2.4–4.8 density samples per fourfold period), and fine high-density low-B oscillations can alias at 0.1 T spacing. Quantitative indexing requires resolved branches; refine a targeted window if needed. Run 5 measured median 1.0985 s/point with 1 s dwell/NPLC=1: allow 3.25 s/point here, ~10 s row overhead, plus 6/0.137 = 43.8 min magnet ramp floor, then 10% contingency. First field step 0→1 T costs 7.3 min; subsequent 0.1 T steps cost 43.8 s plus settle. End at 6 T for the next decision.

Checkpoints: inspect first points and first two complete rows (~20 min including first ramp), then every four rows (~25 min); assess topology at 2 and 4 T. Background hera-watch is armed before execution. Per-point guards stop on >1 nA leakage, temperature excursion, drain current outside 5–12 nA, >90% voltage/current full scale or field mismatch; thermometry freshness repeats each row. Stop on clipping, loss of structure or dead/flat channel and reassess partial data. **On this magnet a kernel interrupt does not stop an in-flight supply ramp**; it can finish the current commanded step, so read actual state before any recovery. No automatic parking or retraverse.
