## HERA — Landau fan for stacking discrimination (plan step 4)

Runs 6–7 establish measurable structure on both carrier signs: a ~30 T electron component and ~12–13 T hole component survive FFT-window changes (ana 14–16). Their additional peaks cannot distinguish independent bands from harmonics or the two neutrality ridges in run 5. This fan follows the families versus gate coordinate at nominal ridge-A D≈0, looking for distinct slopes, intercepts and crossings; neither a second FFT peak nor a resistance gap alone will be treated as a stacking identification. Only B_1–B_2 is wired, so any subregion decomposition remains inferential.

The line uses run 5's c_bg/c_tg=0.7728 and offsets; absolute density remains geometry anchored, and D=0 is the resistance-minimum proxy. Sweep n=-1.2..+3.2×10^12 cm^-2 in 111 points (0.04×10^12 spacing), preserving both gate voltages and actual readbacks. Field begins at the handoff's zero and rises through 0,0.5,0.75,1,1.25,1.5,1.75 T, then 81 uniformly spaced inverse-field points over 2–10 T (Δ[1/B]=0.005 T^-1). This gives about 6.7 samples per 30 T cycle and 4.8 density samples per fourfold LL period at 2 T; nominal 2–10 T Fourier resolution is 2.5 T. Higher harmonics or broken-degeneracy structure may require refinement. **Coverage trade:** holes beyond -1.2 are excluded by the D≈0 TG fence; dense B<2 T is omitted because the higher-frequency electron component tracks only to ~1.9 T and the automatic lower onset is background-sensitive. Extending the same dense grid down to 0.75 T would add ~167 rows (~17 hours); sparse low-field rows test whether that is warranted.

Preconditions print and assert live thermometry, base sample/vaporizer temperatures, heaters off, measured B≈0, Si=0, PS/output on, exact committed validators, unchanged gate pacing, provided wiring, 1 V/13.777 Hz drive, NPLC=1, and 0.3 s/24 dB filters. Readbacks from measure 44/46 and operator-run SAFETIES 18 establish the same-kernel gate A/B handoff; live assertions recheck before writes. Acquisition uses 3 s fast dwell (10τ), sr1=100 µV (10 kΩ nominal range) and existing sr0=20 mV. All followed values have live plots, including quadratures, leakage, both temperatures and actual B/gates; no Si ramp, heater change, pacing change or safety edit.

**Timing:** the 10 T traverse costs 10/0.137≈73 min, with blocking field sets. Data/5 measured 1.098 s median point interval at 1 s dwell/NPLC=1; allow 3.20 s per new point including two gate sets and extra readbacks. 9768 points cost 8.68 h; flyback, field settles, row thermometry checks and setup bring the estimate to ~10.3 h before margin. Runs 6/7 took 120.36/95.68 min and support the field-overhead budget. Gates and field stay at the last point after completion; the next segment can descend rather than retraverse.

**Checkpoints:** inspect the first 20 points and first complete zero-field row for liveness, baseline shape, clipping and leakage; inspect the first dense rows at 2 T, then approximately every 30 minutes and near 3,5,8 T. The in-cell guard interrupts on |gate current|>1 nA, ≥90% lock-in magnitude range, gross drain-current loss, base-T departure, stale thermometry or readback mismatch. If density-dependent oscillations remain washed out by 5–6 T, stop for analysis before spending the remaining time. The armed hera-watch supplies row digests through completion.
