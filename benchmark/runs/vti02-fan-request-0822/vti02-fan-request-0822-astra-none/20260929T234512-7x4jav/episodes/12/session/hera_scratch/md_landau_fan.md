## HERA — near-zero-D Landau fan for the stacking call

**Plan step 4** (`hera_scratch/plan.md`), requested at handoff. Runs 6 and 7 show live oscillatory transport (peak Rxx 530/587 Ω, current 9.81–9.94 nA, peak gate leakage <216 pA); their multiple FFT tones have harmonic/window ambiguities, so a density-resolved fan is the next discriminator. Run 5 fixes the CNP slope (−0.7728) and provisional near-zero-D path; absolute n remains geometry-anchored, and the CNP-resistance minimum is a D-zero proxy. Track branch origins/crossings and the second ridge's possible offset before assigning bands or stacking; B₁–B₂ is our only wired voltage pair.

Co-sweep TG/BG over nominal n=−1.2…+3.2 ×10¹² cm⁻² in 0.025 increments (177 points), at provisional D=0, Si held 0. Field rises from the handoff's zero: references at 0, 1, 1.5 T, then 57 rows uniform in 1/B from 2 to 10 T. This gives ~7.7 density points per nominal fourfold LL spacing at 2 T and ~3.7 field samples per cycle at F=37.5 T; higher-frequency structure could still alias. Both carrier signs and both zero-field ridges are covered. The small edge intervals −1.27…−1.2 and +3.2…+3.27 are excluded for gate margin. A resolved sub-2-T fan is excluded: retaining comparable density and inverse-field resolution there would roughly double or more the run; the three reference rows cannot establish low-field branch connectivity. No larger D=0 density span is available inside this operating box.

**Timing and preflight:** run 5 measured median 1.098 s/point at 1 s dwell, NPLC=1; budget 3.25 s/point here with co-sweeps and vaporizer readout. Keep τ=0.3 s/24 dB/oct, use 3 s (10τ) settling, and fixed sr1 100 µV sensitivity (10 kΩ at 10 nA). Arithmetic: 10,620 points ×3.25 s =9.59 h; monotonic 10 T ramp at 1 A/min adds 10×7.3=73 min; ≤15 s/row overhead adds 15 min, then 10% contingency. Runs 6/7 measured ~3.08/3.07 s per field point beyond the bare ramp. No return traverse. Exact validator/card agreement, base temperatures with advancing logs, heaters off, switch/output on, Si=0, wiring, excitation, NPLC and existing gate/lock-in pacing are checked before writing. End at 10 T and the last density point for a possible descending follow-up.

**Checkpoints:** read the first-10-point output, then first 0/1/1.5/2 T row digests for live current, CNP continuity, leakage, railing and temperature. Continue checks every three rows (~30–45 min); by ~3 T inspect branch contrast and decide whether completing the upper field range buys discriminating information. A sparse low-field reference alone is not grounds to wash out. Every followed channel (X, phase, both gate currents and both temperatures) has a live plot. Per-point trips use the previous 1 nA leakage threshold, base-T window, and three consecutive clipped/current-loss points; thermometry freshness and actual field are rechecked each row and field readings stored in metadata. `hera-watch data --next --follow` is armed before execution. Abort with `nb-collab interrupt`; **on this rig a kernel interrupt does not stop an in-flight magnet ramp**. Notify/drop to E0 on safety anomaly and re-read actual state before any subsequent move. A fan alone may still yield “cannot tell”; no Hall contact or independently contacted second region is available.
