## HERA — Landau fan across both carrier signs (plan step 4)

Runs 6–7 show oscillations on the live B_1–B_2 pair without clipping (R maxima 530/587 ohm, drain 9.81–9.94 nA); run 5 finds two CNP ridges, so single-density tones cannot distinguish harmonics, band families and shifted density patches. This fan follows both ridges and both carrier signs at **working D=0**, using run 5's gate-capacitance ratio and offsets; the absolute density scale remains geometry-anchored and the D zero is a resistance-symmetry convention. Look for branch origins, density-dependent frequencies and crossing patterns before assigning stacking or calibrating a lever arm; the hardwired pair does not independently resolve two spatial regions.

Grid: one 0 T baseline row, one 0.75 T diagnostic row, then 81 rows uniform in 1/B from 1.5 to 10 T; every row co-sweeps TG/BG at nominal n=-1.20..+3.20e12 cm^-2 in .04e12 steps (111 points). Physical endpoints TG=-0.277033..+0.983594 V and BG=-0.465040..+1.166281 V stay inside the unchanged validators; Si=0, base sample T, drive 10 nA. At 1.5 T, fourfold filling spacing is nominally .145e12 (~3.6 gate samples); Δ(1/B)=.007083 T^-1 gives ~4.3 samples/cycle at 33 T and Nyquist 70.6 T. Fourier resolution over 1.5–10 T is ~1.76 T. Coverage trade: excludes the narrow TG-limit edges and dense low-B mapping below 1.5 T; the hole density ceiling is imposed by the operating box and cannot be extended. A full 0.3–10 T inverse-field grid at this spacing adds ~374 rows (~40 h); a 0.8..3.2 electron-only line saves ~3 h but loses the hole trajectories and ridge-origin test. High-B spacing reaches .66 T: this first fan does not resolve narrow anticrossings.

Pacing remains operator-set: gate steps 5 mV, Si .5 V/s, τ=.3 s / 24 dB; **3 s fast dwell = 10τ**, with 3 s extra row settle. Set sr1 to 100 uV (nominal 10 kohm full scale) for unknown neutral/high-field peaks; sr0 remains 20 mV. Preconditions print expected vs actual validators, wiring, base temperatures/freshness, heaters off, Si state, drive, lock-in filters and gate pacing before writes. Every followed channel has a live plot; actual field and gate readbacks are recorded. The fixed magnet slew gives **10/(1/7.3)=73 min** bare ramp. Run 5 (NPLC=1) ordinary points took 1.0985 s at 1 s dwell; runs 6/7 imply ~3.1 s field-set overhead beyond the ramp. Budget 3.2 s per new fan point plus flyback/row reads and thermometry checks, with contingency. The first zero-field row costs about 6 min; no separate field retraverse is needed.

**Checkpoints:** inspect the first points for current, leakage and clipping, then the first two complete rows; thereafter inspect the watch digest at least every 30 min. At ~3 T check that features form coherent branches before committing the remaining high-field segment. Stop for sustained leakage, clipping, lost current, thermal drift, flat/dead voltage channel or wrong readback; weak oscillation contrast alone requires analysis against runs 6–7, not a stacking conclusion. Per-point automatic guards stop on >1 nA gate current, >=90% X full scale, drain outside 5–12 nA, temperature/readback failure; logfile freshness is checked each row. A safety stop drops E1 to E0 pending explicit re-grant. **VTI caveat:** interrupting a magnet ramp does not stop the supply; the operator must act on the supply if a ramp itself must be stopped. Normal completion holds B=10 T and the electron endpoint for the next decision; no automatic park or safety-surface changes.
