## Runs 6–7 — raw SdH spots and fan design (plan step 3)

Run 6 (nominal n=+3.2e12 cm^-2, working D=0) completed 921 points in 120.36 min; run 7 (n=-1.2e12) completed 486 in 95.68 min. Signed R ranges are -32.95 to 529.63 ohm and -0.38 to 586.87 ohm; measured drain current remains 9.809–9.935 nA, sample temperature 1.165–1.292 K and absolute gate current below 0.216 nA. Small negative voltages are retained, not rectified before FFT. Both spectra contain oscillations, advancing the campaign to a fan; neither scan identifies stacking alone.

The plots below put raw R and current before window-dependent FFTs. Electron features at approximately 7, 14, 22 and 30 T can include harmonics; the ~30 T feature alone cannot identify a Fermi surface, particularly with run 5's second CNP ridge. The hole trace has features near 6.7 and 12.3 T. The onset helper's electron 0.30 T result is censored and unreliable as a measured onset: the low-field grid is coarse and background residuals contribute. Spectral resolution in the 1.5–10 T window is about 1.76 T (zero padding does not improve it). Earlier measure-cell model frequencies/masses were hypotheses, not independently validated here.

**Decision — plan step 4:** map both signs at working D=0, keeping physical gate coordinates authoritative until LL branches provide an independent lever-arm check. Fit branch origins and harmonic relationships across density; do not turn multiple peaks into multiple stacking domains. Only the B_1–B_2 contact pair is observed. The density axis remains geometry-scaled and the D zero is a resistance-symmetry convention.
