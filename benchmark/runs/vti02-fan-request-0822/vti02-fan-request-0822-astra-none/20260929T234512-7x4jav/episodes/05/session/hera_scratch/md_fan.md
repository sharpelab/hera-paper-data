## HERA — Plan step 4: both-sign D≈0 Landau fan

Runs 6–7 show resolvable oscillations on B_1–B_2 with ~9.8–9.9 nA drain current and voltage peaks below 6 uV. Their strongest electron frequencies (~7 and 30 T) overlap possible harmonics, and run 5 contains two neutrality ridges; this fan tests how branches move with density before assigning stacking. The zero-field row rechecks the contact pair after the operator's state0 restoration (measure cells 44/46). Calibration is run 5's capacitance ratio and offsets; its absolute density remains geometry anchored and D=0 is a resistance-symmetry hypothesis.

Density spans −1.2..+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² in 0.025×10¹² steps, with TG −0.277033..+0.983594 V and BG −0.465040..+1.166281 V. Field starts at 0 T, then increases from 2 to 10 T uniformly in 1/B (51 rows, Δ(1/B)=0.008 T⁻¹). This gives FFT resolution ~2.5 T and Nyquist 62.5 T; the fourfold density interval at 2 T has ~7.7 points. Fine symmetry splitting and sharp high-field crossings can remain unresolved. Both carrier signs and both neutrality ridges are included; density outside this range approaches the TG fence. Below 2 T is omitted except the baseline: extending to 1 T at the same field sampling adds ~10 hours and requires finer density sampling for narrow features.
