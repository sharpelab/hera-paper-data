Runs 6/7 show oscillations on B_1−B_2 at nominal n=+3.2/−1.2×10¹² cm⁻², with drain current 9.81–9.94 nA, |gate current|<0.216 nA, and |Rxx|<0.59 kΩ. This fan maps continuous trajectories, their origins and crossings on both carrier sides; a D-dependent resistance peak alone is not a stacking diagnosis. Use signed V/I and retain the possible Hall/inhomogeneity contribution seen in run 6.

**Calibration and coverage:** co-sweep TG/BG along run 5's estimated D=0 line: nominal n_A=−1.2..+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² by 0.025×10¹² (177 points), TG≈−0.277..+0.984 V, BG≈−0.465..+1.166 V; Si fixed at 0. The capacitance ratio is fitted, absolute density is geometry-anchored, and the D-zero inferred from resistance symmetry is provisional. Record both gate voltages, and test the density scale against LL spacings without assuming a degeneracy to force a stacking label. This includes both signs and both neutrality origins, omitting only ~0.07×10¹² at each TG boundary; full endpoints add about 15 min and little useful margin. No unmeasured negative-field or nonzero-D coverage is implied.

**Field/resolution:** acquire B=0,0.5,0.75 T, then 1..3 T by 0.125 T and 3.25..10 T by 0.25 T (48 rows). Begin at the handoff's measured ~0.003 T and end at 10 T; no unnecessary return traverse. A fourfold LL interval has ~5.8 density samples at 1.5 T; below ~1 T and in sparsely sampled B regions this is reconnaissance, unsuitable for an unrestricted FFT. It can miss fine crossings; targeted refinement follows identifiable features. Full 0.05 T field coverage at this density resolution would cost ~35–37 h.
