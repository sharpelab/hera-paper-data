## HERA — Landau fan across both carrier signs at working D≈0

**Plan step 4** (`hera_scratch/plan.md`): data/6–7 show resolvable oscillations without clipping, so follow their density evolution on the only wired voltage pair, **B_1–B_2**. In data/6 the 29.5 T peak is near 4×7.2 T; a fan can distinguish a harmonic from an independent branch by its trajectory, crossings and common versus displaced CNP origin. No stacking label follows from tone count alone. Gate B is verified against measure cell 18 and the card; handoff cells 44/46 establish zero field/gates and the bound kernel. The cell rechecks validators and live state before any write.

Co-sweep TG/BG along the run-5 working D=0 line, **n_A −1.2…+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² in 0.04×10¹² steps**, Si=0, base T, 111 points/row. The absolute density scale remains geometry-anchored and the D offset is a symmetry estimate; save the actual gate and field readbacks so these can be revised. The window includes both CNP ridges and both available carrier signs, with small margins to TG limits; larger hole densities cannot be reached on this line inside the committed box. It does not survey other D, negative B, or Si. Those additions would require additional maps/ramp time and are deferred until this fan provides evidence.

**Field grid and resolution:** start where the magnet sits: 0, 0.7, 1, 1.25 T controls; 61 inverse-B-spaced rows from 1.5 to 4 T; 4.2…10 T in 0.2 T steps. Total 95×111=10,545 points. Low-B controls are sparse and cannot establish the oscillation onset or resolve the fast branch below 1.5 T. Above 1.5 T, Δ(1/B)=0.006944 T⁻¹ samples the observed 30 T feature at 4.8 points/period (3.8 at 38 T); the density spacing samples a fourfold LL period at 1.5 T at 3.6 points. Higher harmonics and symmetry-broken fine structure may require later refinement. At high B, finer 0.2 T spacing preserves crossings. Fixed 3 s dwell is 10τ at the operator's 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct; sr1 100 μV provides 10 kΩ nominal headroom, sr0 20 mV. All followed channels have live plots.

**Price and endpoint:** fixed magnet rate 1 A/min and 7.3 A/T give 10×7.3=73 min ramp floor; measured runs 6/7 add about 3.08 s per field set. Data/5 measured 1.098 s per gate point at 1 s dwell/NPLC1 and 5.77 s turnaround. Budget 3.3 s/point here for dual gates and added readbacks (9.67 h), plus ramp, flyback/row settles (~15 min) and contingency: **12 h**. Reprice from the first rows. Ends at **10 T, n=+3.2**, ready for a downward-field follow-up if warranted.

**Checkpoints:** inspect the first few points and first zero-field row for live current, leakage, rail/headroom and CNP consistency with data/5; inspect the 0.7/1 T rows, then at least every 30 min with scientific reviews near 2, 4 and 6 T. `hera-watch` is armed before execution. Per-point guards stop for |gate I|>1 nA, either lock-in above 90% full scale, drain current <5 nA, base-T or measured-field/gate mismatch; thermometry freshness is checked every ten points. If there is no coherent fan by 4 T despite healthy channels, review partial raw maps before spending the upper-field budget. Safety stop returns E0 and requires explicit re-grant. **CAEN caveat:** kernel interrupt does not stop a commanded magnet ramp; the supply continues to its last target. A ramp emergency needs the operator at the instrument. Actual state must be reread after any abort; no automatic zero-field return is hidden in this cell.
