## HERA — two-carrier Landau fan for the stacking test (plan step 4)

Runs 6/7 establish oscillations on both signs with drain current 9.81–9.94 nA, gate currents below 0.22 nA, and voltage signal below 6 uV. Their raw-first analysis is in ana cells 14–15: the electron peaks near 7.5/15/22.5/30 T could include harmonics, while run 5 has two neutrality ridges in the same B_1-B_2 channel. This fan tests branch dispersion and crossings across density, rather than assigning stacking from the number of peaks. Follow coherent branches back to their neutrality offsets and seek a defensible LL lever arm; do not assume fourfold degeneracy, a unique density scale, or independently contacted domains in the verdict.

**Path and calibration:** co-sweep TG=-0.277033..+0.983594 V and BG=-0.465040..+1.166281 V, 111 points, nominal n=-1.20..+3.20e12 cm^-2 in 0.04e12 increments at nominal D=0. Run 5 supplies the capacitance ratio 0.772765 and neutrality offset; c_tg=2.796059 mF/m² is still the geometry anchor and D_offset=-0.013 V/nm comes from the CNP-resistance minimum. Field starts at the handoff zero: reference rows at 0 and 1 T, then 81 rows uniform in 1/B from 2 to 10 T (83 total). This gives 6.7 samples per 30 T oscillation, 3.3 per 60 T harmonic, and 2.5 T Fourier resolution over the main window; density spacing gives ~4.8 samples per fourfold LL at 2 T under the nominal scale. Fast dwell 3 s = 10 tau at the existing 0.3 s/24 dB filters. Fixed sr1 sensitivity 100 uV (nominal 10 kOhm full scale) leaves headroom near neutrality; current channel stays at 20 mV. Both phases, temperatures, leakage channels and actual field/gate readbacks are recorded and plotted.

**Coverage trade:** both carrier signs and both observed neutrality offsets are included. The ~0.07e12 slivers at each gate-bound edge are omitted to preserve operating margin. The 0/1 T rows are references, not a finely sampled low-field fan; filling 1–2 T at the same inverse-field spacing would add ~101 rows and ~10.5 hours. This pass omits negative field and D dependence; those become targeted follow-ups only if branch interpretation warrants them.

**Timing basis:** run 5 measured ~1.10 s within-row at 1 s dwell/NPLC=1. Budget 3.2 s/point here for 3 s dwell, two gate sets and extra readbacks, plus ~7 s flyback and ~3.1 s magnet settle/read overhead per row (runs 6/7). The 0→10 T ramp floor is 10/(1/7.3)=73 min at 1 A/min. 83×111 points plus turnaround and ramp is ~9.6 h, with ~10% reserve in the headline. End at +10 T and the positive-density endpoint to avoid a wasted retraverse.

**Preconditions/checkpoints:** the opening reads verify every validator against the card, heaters off, live thermometry at base, field and gates at handoff zero, Si range/output/0.5 V/s pacing, 5 mV Keithley steps, NPLC=1, wiring, drive, and filter settings before any write. Check the first points and first two completed rows for liveness/clipping/leakage and gate trajectory; then the first three main-window rows (~2 T), every ~60 min, and at 4 and 6 T for branch contrast and useful resolution. A per-point guard stops the sweep on >1 nA gate current, voltage/current X >90% of sensitivity, loss of drain current, temperature excursion, or readback mismatch; thermometry freshness is checked each row. A wash-out through 6 T calls for reassessment, not an automatic full traverse. **VTI-specific:** kernel interrupt does not halt a supply ramp already commanded; the supply continues to its current row target. For an anomaly, notify Zack, re-read actual state before recovery, and require a fresh grant after an E0 safety drop. No automatic park on interruption.
