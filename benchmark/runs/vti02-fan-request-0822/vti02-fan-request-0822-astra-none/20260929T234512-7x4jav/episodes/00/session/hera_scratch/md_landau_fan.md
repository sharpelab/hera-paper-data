## HERA — two-carrier Landau fan for the stacking call (plan step 4)

Runs 6–7 show well-resolved oscillations on both sides of the main neutrality ridge, with drain current 9.81–9.94 nA and no clipping. Their several frequencies do not identify stacking: harmonics and the second run-5 neutrality ridge can mimic extra bands. This fan tracks the density dependence and origins of branches/crossings on the only wired pair, B_1–B_2; retain signed Rxx and gate-voltage coordinates, and test LL indexing before claiming an absolute density calibration. Nominal D=0 uses the run-5 resistance-symmetry offset; it is an operational reference, not a proven internal-field zero.

**Coverage and acquisition.** Co-sweep TG/BG along nominal n=-1.2..+3.2 ×10¹² cm⁻² in 0.04 steps: TG -0.277033..+0.983594 V, BG -0.465040..+1.166281 V, within the committed limits. Field starts at zero for a baseline, then 1.5..4 T in 51 inverse-field-spaced rows and 4.1..10 T at 0.1 T spacing (112 rows ×111 points). This covers both carrier signs and both neutrality origins, while omitting (0,1.5) T from the map; the existing field scouts cover that interval. Extending the fan down to 1 T at the same inverse-field spacing costs ~40 additional rows/~4.2 h. The small remaining gate-edge strips are omitted for margin; no gate range is extended. The grid resolves the prominent ~30 T electron branch with about four samples per cycle and the fourfold density period with ≥3.6 samples at 1.5 T; it is not a survey of fine symmetry-broken states.

**Preflight and cost.** Handoff measure cells 44/46 report state0 restored, B≈0 and kernel BOUND; SAFETIES cell 18 ran in this kernel, matches the device card and lies inside the 14 T rig limit. The cell rechecks validators, fresh sample/vaporizer logs, heaters off, base T, Si=0 (range 2/output on), wiring, drive, NPLC and 5 mV gate pacing before writes. Fixed sr1 sensitivity 100 µV allows 10 kΩ nominal full scale (run 5 reached 3.07 kΩ); sr0 remains 20 mV. At tau=0.3 s/24 dB, use 3 s per point (10 tau), plus 3 s row settling; changing these time constants or gate pacing is not part of this cell. Run 5 measured 1.098 s per point with a 1 s dwell at NPLC=1 and 5.77 s row transitions. Budget 3.2 s/point and 12 s/row with the co-swept gates and measured B, plus the mandatory 10/0.137=73 min field-ramp floor: 12.65 h subtotal, about 10% margin in the headline estimate. Field finishes at 10 T for any subsequent descending segment.

**Checkpoints and stop rule.** Watch the first baseline points and first two completed field rows for leakage, clipping, a flat/dead channel and correct current. Then inspect row digests every ~30 min; by B≈3 T, assess whether connected oscillatory branches justify the remaining acquisition, repricing from actual rows. Point guards stop for >1 nA gate current, ≥90% full-scale lock-in signal, drain current outside 5–15 nA, temperature mismatch, stale thermometry (checked each row), or measured B error >30 mT. All followed channels, including both phases and both temperatures, are plotted. Low-B smoothness alone is not a washout; no connected fan structure by the scientific checkpoint triggers review before spending the rest. On anomaly, notify and drop to E0, read actual state before another move. **VTI magnet caveat:** a kernel interrupt does not stop a field ramp already commanded; the supply continues to its bounded row target, and stopping it mid-ramp needs the operator at the instrument.
