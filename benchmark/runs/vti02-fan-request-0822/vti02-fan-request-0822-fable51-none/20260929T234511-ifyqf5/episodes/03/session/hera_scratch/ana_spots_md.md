## Runs 6–7 — SdH spot checks (`sdh-spot-check`), D = 0: n = +3.2×10¹² (run 6, 0 → 10 T, 10 mT) and n = −1.2×10¹² (run 7, 10 → 0.3 T, 20 mT)

Raw R(B) first (left), then the oscillatory part vs 1/B with the 4-fold filling factors of the nominal density marked, then the 1/B spectrum against the one-band Luttinger frequency F_L = |n|h/4e. **Run 6 (electron, F_L = 33.1 T):** clean SdH from ~1.4 T; the dominant tone is **30.0 T (0.91 F_L)** with its second harmonic at 60 T, plus a low tone at **7–8.6 T** and mixing products at 14 and 23 T (≈ 30 − k·7.5). Rxx climbs to 530 Ω across 7.7–9.7 T (one wide LL). Two frequencies at one density means either two Fermi surfaces (30 + ~8 ≈ 38 T would put n 15 % *above* the geometry-prior scale; 30 T alone puts it 9 % *below*) or a bunched-degeneracy sequence — a single point cannot separate a density-scale error from a band partition. **Run 7 (hole, F_L = 12.4 T):** dominant tone **12.3–12.9 T ≈ F_L** with a strong **5.3–6.6 T ≈ F_L/2** sub-tone (8-fold bunching at low B: deep minima near ν ≈ 16 and 24 with a broad maximum at ν ≈ 20) and, above 4 T, a fine sequence of period ≈ 0.02 T⁻¹ (≈ 50 T, i.e. fully lifted ν-step-1 levels). R(B → 0) = 53 / 148 Ω; T_sample 1.24 K on both; no leak (|I_gate| < 0.25 nA), no rail (≤ 12 % FS at 50 µV). **Take:** neither point is a stacking call; both say the fan is resolvable from ~1–1.5 T at the present T_e, and that the LL slopes of a fan (ν·e/h, capacitance-independent) are needed to pin the absolute n scale — the run-5 calibration fixes only c_bg/c_tg.
