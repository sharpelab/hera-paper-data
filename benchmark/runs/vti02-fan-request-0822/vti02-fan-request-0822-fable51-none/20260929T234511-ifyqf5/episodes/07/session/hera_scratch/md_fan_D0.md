## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan on the D = 0 line (`landau-fan`), n −1.28 → +3.26×10¹² fast × B 1 → 10 T slow

**Why now (plan step 4; picks up after the handoff with the rig at 0 T, gates 0).** The two spot checks (runs 6, 7, ana) show quantum-Hall zeros from ~3 T but **no single 1/B-periodic ladder**, and their filling factors cannot be assigned from a fixed-n cut: run 6's zeros at 5.92 / 7.16 T are ν = 22 / 18 (a 4k+2, Dirac-like sequence → ABCB reading) on the run-5 scale, or ν = 24 / 20 (a 4k sequence → ABAB/ABCA reading) if the geometry-prior density scale is 8.7 % low. A fan breaks that degeneracy independent of the calibration: each Landau-level line has slope dn/dB = νe/h, which gives ν absolutely *and* the lever arm the run-5 calibration lacks. It then reads the stacking directly — **ABAB**: two interpenetrating 4-fold ladders (crossings), first CNP gaps at ν = ±8/±4; **ABCA**: one ladder, no crossings, CNP column insulating at high B; **ABCB**: a 4(N+½) Dirac ladder interleaved with a 4k ladder, first CNP gap at ν = ±6. Region B (ridge B, run 5) will appear as shadow lines offset +0.43×10¹² — a control, not a band.

**Design.** Fast axis: the run-5-calibrated D = 0 line, co-swept (V_tg, V_bg) via `multimegasweep`, from the hole edge n = −1.28 to the electron edge +3.26×10¹² — both ends set by the TG limit (V_tg −0.30 → +1.00 V, V_bg −0.49 → +1.19 V), 100 points (Δn 0.046×10¹² = 13 mV TG steps; the ν = 4 lines are 4 columns apart at 2 T, 21 at 10 T). Slow axis: B 1 → 10 T in 100 mT (91 rows), ascending from where the magnet sits; nothing is resolvable below ~1 T at T_e ≈ 8 K (run 6). Fixed sr1 100 µV (10 kΩ FS; run-5 sizing rule — only a > 10.9 kΩ CNP column at high B could rail, and that is not the column the read needs). Lock-in τ 0.3 s / 24 dB: the 1 s fast dwell (run-5 calibrated 1.10 s/pt) carries a ≈ 4τ = 1.2 s group delay, i.e. a uniform ≈ 1-column lag along n in every row (same direction each row → a constant, correctable offset). 1 nA leak guard on both Keithleys interrupts the sweep. **Coverage trade:** the hole side reaches only −1.28×10¹² (the ν = −8 line leaves the window at 6.6 T, ν = −4 stays in); full ±3×10¹² symmetry is outside the committed box. Ends at **10 T with the gates parked at 0** — the finite-D follow-up (plan 5a) rides the magnet down.

**Price.** From run 5 (1.10 s/pt incl. reads): 100 pts + flyback (1.3 V at 5 mV ≈ 1.6 s + BG) + 4 s slow_delay + 100 mT ramp (44 s + 3 s settle) ≈ 164 s/row × 91 rows + 7 min to 1 T ≈ 4 h 17 m; realistic +8 % → **≈ 4 h 40 m**. **Checkpoints (E1):** first row (rail, leak, flat or dead channel, T); then ≈ 3 T, 5 T, 7 T — partial `fan_grids` re-form, look for LL lines converging on the CNP and count ladders; abort on a rail across many columns, a leak trip, T_sample > 1.5 K, or no LL structure by 5 T.
