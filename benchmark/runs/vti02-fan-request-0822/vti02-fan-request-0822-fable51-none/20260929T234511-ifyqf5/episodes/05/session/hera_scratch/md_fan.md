## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), B slow 1.5 → 10 T, n fast −1.25 … +3.25×10¹²

Plan step 4 (`hera_scratch/plan.md`) — the stacking measurement the operator asked for. The 1D spot checks (runs 6–7, ana) show well-formed Landau levels from ≈ 1.6 T (Rxx → 0 zeros at 1.85 T on the electron side) but minima that are not periodic in 1/B: either two sub-bands (a Bernal-like light/heavy pair) or the series-mosaic region B (Δn = +0.43×10¹²) interleaving its ladder — undecidable in 1D. In the (n, B) plane those separate: Rxx minima run along lines of constant ν = n h/eB through the origin; a second region gives the **same** slopes offset along n, a second sub-band gives **different** slopes and LL crossings. The low-ν sequence at high B (ν = ±4, ±8, ±12 …) reads the zero-LL degeneracy (16-fold for ABCA, two crossing ladders for ABAB, a Dirac-like ±2, ±6 sequence for ABCB), and the line slopes fix the absolute density scale that run 5 left at the geometry prior. **Design:** field is the expensive axis (0.137 T/min) → B slow, 1.5 → 10 T in 0.1 T (86 rows), starting where the magnet sits (0 T after the handoff; the 1.5 T lead-in is 11 min) and ending at 10 T; fast axis = (V_tg, V_bg) co-swept along the run-5 D = 0 line (D_offset −0.013 V/nm), n = −1.25 … +3.25×10¹² in 129 points (0.035×10¹² per point, ≈ 10 mV TG steps) — at 2 T a Δν = 4 line spacing is 0.19×10¹² ≈ 5–6 points, at 10 T 0.97×10¹² ≈ 28 points. **Coverage trade:** the hole side stops at n = −1.25×10¹² (TG −0.3 V limit) → ν ≥ −5 at 10 T only; the electron side reaches ν = +13 at 10 T, +52 at 2.5 T. D ≠ 0 is not covered here (step 5 candidate). `fast_delay` 1.0 s (τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB → ≈ 1-point lag, as in the run-5 map), `slow_delay` 3 s after each flyback. **Sensitivity** sr1 200 µV = 20 kΩ FS: runs 6–7 maxima ≤ 590 Ω at |n| ≥ 1.2×10¹²; the ν = 0 column at high B may exceed 20 kΩ and soft-clip — that reads as "≥ 20 kΩ, insulating", itself evidence — while metallic minima keep ≈ 0.75 Ω resolution. Leak guard 1 nA on both Keithleys (baselines −100 / +25 pA). **Price** (run-5 rate 1.17 s/pt → 1.2 s/pt with two gates): 86 × (129 × 1.2 s + 44 s ramp + 3 s settle + 3 s slow_delay + 2 s flyback) ≈ 4.95 h + 11 min lead-in ≈ 5.2 h; realistic 5 h 30 m. Ends at **10 T** with gates at n = +3.25×10¹² — the next step rides the magnet down. **Checkpoints:** first row (~14 min in: signal, sensitivity, both gate currents, gate readbacks tracking the D = 0 line), then every ~15 rows a re-formed partial fan (straight lines through the origin? region-B offset lines? broad rail?) — abort if sr1 rails across a wide band of the metallic side or a gate current leaves its baseline.
