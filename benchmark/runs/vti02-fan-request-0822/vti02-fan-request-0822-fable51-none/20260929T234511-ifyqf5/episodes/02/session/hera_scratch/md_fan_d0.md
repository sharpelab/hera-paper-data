## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n −1.25 → +3.25×10¹² fast × B 0, 1.0 → 10 T slow

**Why (plan step 4; operator's request):** the spot checks (runs 6–7, ana) show clean SdH on both carrier signs but every stacking-relevant read is degenerate on a single trace — the 7.3 / 15 / 22 / 30 T tones of run 6 are either F_L/4 harmonics (a 16-fold zero-LL family → ABCA-like) or an ABAB light + heavy pair; run 7's 7.1 + 12.6 T pair is either region B in series or an 8-fold F_L/2. A fan turns each into a slope in n: harmonics scale as fixed F/n, band pockets and mosaic regions do not, and the ν sequence with gaps at D = 0 (16 vs 8 vs 2/4 first) is read directly from the fan-line pattern. **Geometry:** fast axis = co-swept (V_tg, V_bg) along the calibrated D = 0 line (run-5 cal: c_bg/c_tg 0.773, n_offset −0.089×10¹², D_offset −0.013 V/nm — the same CAL as runs 6–7), n −1.25 → +3.25×10¹² in 0.03 steps (151 pts; V_tg −0.291 → +0.998, V_bg −0.484 → +1.185 V, steps 8.6 / 11.1 mV), which is 8 points per Δν = 1 at 10 T and ≈ 9 per Δν = 4 at 3 T; slow axis = B, one B = 0 reference row (CNP position vs run 5) then 1.0 → 10.0 T in 100 mT (92 rows). Rows are sparse relative to the 1/B period at 1–2 T on the electron side (≈ 1 row per period) — the fan lines are resolved along n row by row, so that only limits 1D cuts, not the fan; the SdH spectra come from runs 6–7. **Coverage trade:** hole side stops at n = −1.25×10¹² (TG −0.3 V limit) — only ν ≤ ~30 lines are visible there vs ≤ ~80 on the electron side; D = 0 only (a finite-D fan is the conditional step 6a, riding the magnet down). **Sensitivity:** sr1 100 µV = 10 kΩ full scale at 10 nA — sized to the ν = 0 ridge at 10 T (gatemap lesson: the extreme of the window, not the 1D peaks; runs 6–7 peaked at 590 Ω = 6 % of this range); the ν = 0 state at high B will likely rail over Δν ~ 1–2 (7–15 points) — accepted and flagged, the ν ≠ 0 lines are the measurement. **Dwell:** fast_delay 1.0 s (3.3 τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB, as in the run-5 gatemap — a ~1-point lag along n, common to all rows, correctable as a CNP shift); slow_delay 3 s after the flyback + the magnet's blocking ramp + 3 s settle. Leak guard trips at 1 nA (the TG flyback transient is ±110 pA). **Price:** per point 1.17–1.23 s (run 5: 4158 pts / 4873 s), 151 pts + ~7 s flyback/settle ≈ 193 s/row × 92 = 4.9 h, plus the 10 T ramp 73 min (bare 0.137 T/min) → ≈ 6 h 10 m; realistic **≈ 6 h 40 m**, ending at **10 T** with the gates parked at 0 V — subsequent steps ride the magnet down. **Checkpoints:** first row (B = 0: CNP at n ≈ 0 within ±0.1×10¹², R_CNP 200–350 Ω as run 5, no rail, gate currents at offsets, T 1.2–1.3 K); then at ≈ 3 T and ≈ 6 T a re-formed partial fan on the landed rows — abort if the channel rails away from ν = 0, a gate current climbs, T_sample drifts > 1.5 K, or the low-B rows show no fan structure at all.
