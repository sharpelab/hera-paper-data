## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), B 1 → 10 T × n −1.25 … +3.30×10¹²

**Why now (plan step 4; operator's request).** Runs 6–7 (ana) show ≥ 2 SdH tones on both carrier sides and deep minima whose filling factors are not multiples of 4 on the current density scale — a single-density trace cannot tell two Fermi surfaces (ABAB / ABCB) from a split single ladder (ABCA) or from the region-B series alias, and the absolute n scale still rests on the 9.5 nm c_tg prior. The fan answers all three at once: LL slopes dn/dB = νe/h calibrate n, the first-gap filling sequence at high B gives the zero-energy LL degeneracy (ν = ±8 then every 4 for a single ladder; crossings for two ladders; non-4 multiples / e-h asymmetry for ABCB), and a per-column FFT over B ≥ 3 T gives the tone evolution with n.

**Design.** Slow axis B = 1.0 → 10.0 T in 0.1 T (91 rows, uniform in B so LL lines are straight; the magnet sits at 0 T so the fan starts where it is and ends at 10 T for whatever rides down next). Fast axis: 92 co-swept (V_tg, V_bg) points on the calibrated D = 0 line (run-5 calibration: c_bg/c_tg = 0.773, c_tg = 2.796 mF/m², n_offset −0.089, D_offset −0.013), n = −1.25 → +3.30×10¹² in Δn = 0.05×10¹² (14 / 19 mV steps) — 10 pts per ν = 4 at 5 T, 20 at 10 T. **Coverage trade:** the hole side stops at −1.25×10¹² (TG −0.3 V limit on the D = 0 line; the electron side at +3.30, 12 mV inside the TG limit) — the hole side is the only side we can't extend, so the ν-sequence read leans on the electron side; the field cap (10 T) is the card's. Sensitivity **200 µV = 20 kΩ FS** (0.6 Ω LSB, oscillation amplitudes ≥ 10 Ω above 1.5 T; runs 6–7 peaked at 590 Ω) — the ν = 0 core at high B may soft-clip above 20 kΩ; that loses the CNP peak value, not the gap positions, and is accepted rather than losing the low-R oscillations. Dwell: fast_delay 1.0 s at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB (operator-set) ≈ 3.3τ — a constant ~1–2-point lag in the +n direction on every row (slopes unaffected; the ν = 0 line is read from the fan itself). Leak guard trips at 1 nA on either Keithley; gates park at 0 in `finally`, field stays at 10 T.
