## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n −1.25 … +3.25×10¹² × B 0.4 → 10 T

Plan step 4 (`hera_scratch/plan.md`), the operator's requested next move. Runs 6–7 (ana) put the device in the QH regime from ~2 T with Rxx minima at ≈ 0 Ω, but the two 1D cuts share a dominant **7.1–7.4 T tone that does not scale with n** (2.7× density change) alongside n-dependent content — a two-band (ABAB-type light band) reading and an ungated-region reading are indistinguishable in 1D. The fan separates them: gated bands fan out of the CNP, an ungated region is a horizontal stripe. It also (i) fixes the **absolute density scale** from the LL slopes ν = nh/eB (c_tg is still the 9.5 nm prior), (ii) reads the degeneracy sequence out of the CNP (±4 vs ±8), (iii) shows crossings (two bands) vs a single fan, (iv) e/h asymmetry. **Design:** fast axis = (V_tg, V_bg) co-swept along the run-5-calibrated D = 0 line, n = −1.25 → +3.25×10¹² in 131 pts (Δn 0.035×10¹² ≈ 9.9 mV TG; ≥ 5.6 pts per 4-fold gap above 2 T, 28 at 10 T); slow = B 0.4 → 10 T in 0.2 T, 49 rows, uniform in B so LL lines are straight. **Coverage trade:** the hole side stops at −1.25×10¹² (TG −0.3 V limit) vs +3.25 on the electron side — the D = 0 line is TG-limited at both ends, nothing more is reachable without leaving D = 0; the B range starts at 0.4 T (below ~1.5 T there are no oscillations at this T_e). Si held at 0 V. sr1 fixed at 100 µV (10 kΩ FS — run 5 read 50 Ω features at this range; the ν = 0 state at high B may exceed it → flat top at the CNP only, accepted as a "> 10 kΩ" read). Dwell fast_delay 1 s / slow_delay 3 s as run 5 (lag ≲ 1 step, uniform per row); the magnet's 3 s settle per row is inside the blocking set. Guards: interrupt on |I_gate| > 1 nA or T_sample > 2 K. **Price** (run 5: 1.10 s/pt; run 6: 0.134 T/min): 49 × 131 × 1.10 s = 118 min + 10 T ramp 75 min + row overhead ~8 min ≈ 3h20m; realistic ≈ 3h35m. Ends at **10 T** with the gates at the fan's end point; step 5 rides the magnet down. **Checkpoints:** first row (~4 min: signal level, rail at the CNP?, gate currents, B readback tracking); then at rows ~10 (2.2 T), ~25 (5.2 T), ~40 (8.2 T) re-form the partial fan — continue if LL lines fan from a common CNP; stop and rethink if the map is featureless or dominated by n-independent stripes.
