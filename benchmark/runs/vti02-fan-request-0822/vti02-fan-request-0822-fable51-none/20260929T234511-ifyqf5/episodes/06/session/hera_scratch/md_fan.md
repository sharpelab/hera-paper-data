## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n −1.2 → +3.2×10¹² fast, B 1 → 10 T slow

Plan step 4 (`hera_scratch/plan.md`), the operator's requested next move. The spot checks settle feasibility: run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹²) carries a 7.2 T tone with 2×/3×/4× harmonics plus a dominant 29.5–31.4 T tone, run 7 (n = −1.2×10¹²) a 6.8 T tone plus 12.3 T ≈ F_L; both resolvable from ≲ 0.5 T. At a single density these are ambiguous — a fully split single pocket (harmonics at 2F, 4F) and a light + heavy two-band pair both give this — so the discriminator is the fan: how each frequency scales with n (ABAB: two fans from one origin with a constant ratio ≈ 0.4; ABCA: one fan at F_L(n) = nh/4e; ABCB: unequal, offset fans) and which filling factors gap out of the CNP (16-fold zero level → ν = ±8 for ABAB/ABCA, 12-fold for ABCB).

**Design.** Fast axis: the calibrated D = 0 line (run-5 cal, region A), n from −1.2 to +3.2×10¹² in 0.05×10¹² steps (89 pts; TG −0.277 → +0.984 V co-swept with BG −0.465 → +1.166 V, all inside the validators; same endpoints as runs 6/7). Δn = 0.05×10¹² gives ~5 points per unit ν at 10 T and ~6 points per 4-fold gap period at 3 T. Every row runs hole → electron, so the ~1-point lag from `fast_delay = 1 s` (τ = 0.3 s, 24 dB — same dwell as run 5) is a uniform shift, not a smear. Slow axis: B 1.0 → 10.0 T in 0.1 T steps, 91 rows, ascending from the magnet's current 0 T; 0.1 T rows keep the 1/B sampling above the heavy-pocket Nyquist (F > 31 T) for B > 2.5 T, so per-column FFTs are clean where the LLs are resolved. Sensitivity fixed at 200 µV (20 kΩ FS): the spot checks peaked at 590 Ω; the ν = 0 CNP column at high B may exceed 20 kΩ and rail on a few points — accepted (a rail there still reads "gapped"), the LL structure the call needs sits away from it. **Coverage trade:** the hole side stops at n = −1.2×10¹² (TG −0.3 V limit) and D is fixed at 0; a finite-D fan is plan step 6 if step 5 leaves ABAB vs ABCB open. Ridge B (second region, +0.43×10¹² electron-doped) will show as a second fan origin at n_A ≈ −0.43 — accounted for in the ν assignment.

**Price.** Per row ≈ 44 s ramp (0.1 T at 0.137 T/min) + 3 s settle + 3 s slow delay + 89 × 1.1 s (run-5 measured per-point) + ~2 s flyback ≈ 150 s; 91 rows + the 0 → 1 T ramp ≈ 3 h 55 m, headline **≈ 4 h 15 m** (+7 %). Ends at 10 T with the gates at n = −1.2 (start of the line); the next step rides the field down. Leak guard: |I_gate| > 1 nA on either Keithley interrupts the sweep. **Checkpoints:** first row (rail, leakage, sr1 sensitivity, the gate flyback transient, B readback tracking); ~3 T (row 21) and ~6 T (row 51): interim per-column FFT + minima trace on the landed rows — abort if no LL structure is resolved by 6 T or if the channel rails over more than the CNP column.
