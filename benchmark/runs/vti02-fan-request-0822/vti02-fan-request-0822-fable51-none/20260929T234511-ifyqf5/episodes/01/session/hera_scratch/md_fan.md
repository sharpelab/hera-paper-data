## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n −1.25 … +3.2×10¹² × B 0.5 → 10 T

**Why now (plan step 4; operator request).** Runs 6/7 (ana) show clean SdH from ~1 T and QH minima with Rxx → 0 by 2.3 T on the electron side; the Δν = 4 periodicity there sits at F ≈ 29.5 T against F_L(geo) = 33.1 T, so the 9.5 nm-anchored density scale runs ~10 % high and both spot checks carry ambiguous ν assignments (hole side: minima at ν_geo ≈ 10, 8.3, 7.1, 5.9). The fan resolves all of it at once: LL lines indexed by ν give the absolute n calibration; **which ν carry the strong gaps** (ν = 8 hierarchy → Bernal blocks; ν = 16 first, then 4/8/12 → rhombohedral manifold; ν = 4 at low B → a Dirac-like block, ABCB); **one vs two line families** converging on the CNP (two bilayer-like Fermi surfaces vs one); and the ν = 0 / low-ν sector at high B where ABAB and ABCA differ most. Region B (0.43×10¹² electron-doped, run 5) should appear as a ×1.13-density replica of every feature.

**Design.** Fast axis: (V_tg, V_bg) co-swept along the run-5 D = 0 line, n_geo −1.25 → +3.2×10¹² in 0.025 steps (179 pts; TG −0.291 … +0.984 V, BG −0.484 … +1.166 V, ≥ 5 mV inside every validator — the full D = 0 window the operating box allows; the hole side is TG-limited at −1.27). Slow axis: B 0.5 → 4.0 T at 0.1 T, 4.2 → 10 T at 0.2 T (66 rows; the magnet sits at 0 so the fan climbs — no re-traverse; the Δν = 4 line spacing in n is 0.097×10¹²·B, i.e. ≥ 4 fast points from 1 T). Sensitivity fixed at 100 µV (10 kΩ FS; the ν = 0 peak at high B may rail — that is itself a read). **Settling: I set τ 0.3 → 0.1 s on both lock-ins** so fast_delay 1.0 s = 10 τ at 24 dB/oct (settled to 1 %); at the operator's 0.3 s the same dwell would leave ~half of each step unsettled and smear LL minima over ~3 fast points, and 3 s/pt would price the fan at 12 h. Acquisition setting, not a safety surface; state0 restores 0.3 s at close-out. Leak guard (> 1 nA either gate → interrupt) on; sr1 only readout (sr2/sr3 n.c.).

**Price** (run 5: 1.10 s/pt at fast_delay 1 s; run 6: 3 s settle per field set): 66 rows × (179 × 1.15 s + 7 s turnaround) ≈ 3.9 h + ramps 76 min ≈ 5.2 h, realistic ~5.5 h; ends at **10 T** with the gates at n_geo = +3.2. **Checkpoints:** first row (~6 min: CNP peak ≈ 250–300 Ω at 0.5 T as in run 5, no rail beyond the CNP, gate currents at the pA floor, T ≈ 1.2 K); row 15 (2 T, ~1.3 h): LL minima visible along n and converging on the CNP — abort if the rows are featureless or the channel rails across > 5 points/row; row 40 (5 T): interim ν-indexing and line-family count, continue/adjust call written to ana. T_sample > 1.5 K at high B → pause.
