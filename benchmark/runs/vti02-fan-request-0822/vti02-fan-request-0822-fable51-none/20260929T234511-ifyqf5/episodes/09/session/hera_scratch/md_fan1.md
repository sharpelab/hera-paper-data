## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan #1 at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n = −1.25 … +3.25×10¹² × B 1 → 10 T

**Why this, now (plan step 4; operator ask: the fan for the stacking call).** The spot checks (runs 6, 7 — my read in ana, next cell) show well-developed quantum oscillations at D = 0: run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹²) has Rxx minima to ≈ 0 Ω at ~2.3, 3.4, 4.2, 6.0, 7.2 T with tones 7.1 T (dominant) + 14.1 / 22.1 / ~30 T; run 7 (n = −1.2×10¹²) tones 12.3 T (≈ F_L = 12.4) + 6.6 / 4.7 / 8.5 T. The electron-side pair 7.1 / ~30 T is *either* two pockets (ABAB-like light + heavy bilayer bands: model 7.5 / 25.6 T) *or* one pocket at F_L ≈ 30 T with F_L/2, F_L/4 subharmonics (every 4th gap deep, ABCA-like). A 1D trace cannot tell those apart; a fan can — two pockets give two line families with different slopes through one CNP, subharmonics give one family with alternating gap depth — and it reads the zero-LL degeneracy directly (first gap at |ν| = 8 vs 16 from the CNP). Run 5's region-B mosaic (CNP offset +0.43×10¹²) will show up as a second intercept, so the fan also separates the two regions' lines.

**Design.** Fast axis: (V_tg, V_bg) co-swept along the calibrated D = 0 line (run-5 cal: ratio 0.773, c_tg prior, n_offset −0.089, D_offset −0.013), n = −1.25 → +3.25×10¹² in 151 points (0.030×10¹²; TG −0.291 … +0.998 V, BG −0.484 … +1.185 V — 2 mV inside the TG limit on the electron end, hole end TG-limited at −0.29 V). Coverage trade: the hole side reaches only n = −1.25 (TG −0.3 V limit) — at 10 T that is ν = −5, at 4 T ν = −13, so the hole-side zero-LL degeneracy is read at B ≲ 6.5 T (ν = −8) rather than 10 T; the electron side reaches ν = +13 at 10 T, +27 at 5 T. Slow axis uniform in B (LL lines are straight in (n, B)): 1.0 → 3.8 T at 0.2 T (15 rows — lines dense there, ν = 4 spacing ≈ 4 fast steps at 1 T), 4.0 → 10.0 T at 0.1 T (61 rows — ν = 1 spacing = 8 fast steps at 10 T). 76 rows × 151 = 11 476 pts. Sensitivity fixed at 200 µV (20 kΩ FS at 10 nA): runs 6/7 peaked at 590 Ω; only ν = 0 at high B can rail, and that is not diagnostic. fast_delay 1.0 s (τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB, a uniform ~1-step lag in n, slopes untouched — same as run 5), slow_delay 3 s; leak guard > 1 nA either gate → interrupt. Ends at **10 T with gates parked at 0 V** (fan #2 rides the magnet down — never re-traverse).

**Price** (from run 5: 1.0 s/pt + ~8 s row turnaround; magnet 0.137 T/min + 3 s settle): 0 → 1 T ramp 7 min; 15 coarse rows × 250 s + 61 fine rows × 206 s ≈ 4.6 h → **~5.1 h realistic (+10 %)**. **Checkpoints:** first 2 rows (signal on sr1, no rail, gate currents at the pA floor, T_sample ≈ 1.2 K, field tracking) via `hera-watch`; then ~every 1–1.5 h a re-formed partial fan (`fan_grids`) — abandon if featureless or railing across the window (price of a continue call: the remaining rows).
