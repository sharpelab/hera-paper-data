Two readings fit: (i) two pockets — light 7.1 T + heavy ~30 T, sum 37 T ≈ F_L within the ~10 % n-scale prior (the ABAB-like light/heavy bilayer-band pair); (ii) one pocket F_L ≈ 30 T with F_L/2 and F_L/4 subharmonics — every 4th gap deep (ABCA-like 16-fold pattern).
