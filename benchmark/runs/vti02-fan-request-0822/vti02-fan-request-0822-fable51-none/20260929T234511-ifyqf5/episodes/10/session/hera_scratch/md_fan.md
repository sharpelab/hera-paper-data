## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), B 1 → 10 T uniform in 1/B × n = −1.2 … +3.2×10¹² (plan step 4)

**Why now.** The spot checks (runs 6/7, read in ana below) show two-band SdH content at n = +3.2×10¹² (light tone 7.5–7.8 T with harmonics, ~22 T, and the full-density 33 T above 4 T) and 4.4 / 6.7 / 12.3 T tones at n = −1.2×10¹² — the stacking call needs the *n-dependence*: tone count, slopes dF/dn and intercepts (ABAB: two gapless bands, both through the origin; ABCB: band offsets and e/h asymmetry; ABCA: a single F_L(n) tone), plus dF_total/dn as the lever-arm check on the run-5 scale (F1+F2 ≈ 30 T vs F_L = 33 T is a 10 % question).

**Design.** Field slow (blocking, 0.137 T/min), **uniform in 1/B** (`field_invB`, 73 rows, 1 → 10 T, Δ(1/B) = 0.0125 /T): the LL-line tracking criterion F_L·Δ(1/B) = 0.41 is then field-independent and the per-n FFT record is already even in 1/B; Nyquist 40 T > F_L(3.2×10¹²) = 33 T. Fast axis: (V_tg, V_bg) co-swept along the calibrated D = 0 line (run-5 cal, D_offset −0.013), n −1.2 → +3.2×10¹² in 0.025 steps (177 pts; 5 pts per ν = 4 period at 1 T, 4 per ν = 1 period at 4 T) — the endpoints *are* the run-6/7 gate points, so each fan row cross-checks the 1D records. `fast_delay` 1 s as run 5 (same lock-in lag as the calibration map, same sweep direction); sr1 fixed at 100 µV = 10 kΩ FS (1D peaks 530–590 Ω → ≥ 10×; the ν = 0 CNP peak at high B may rail over a narrow n window — accepted, LL positions unaffected). Leak guard 1 nA; gates park at 0 in `finally`, field stays at 10 T for the next step to ride down.

**Coverage trade.** Hole side only to −1.2×10¹² (TG −0.3 V limit); B ≥ 1 T (the light band's sub-1 T oscillations live in runs 6/7); tones > 40 T alias (read from the 1D records). Region B (series mosaic, CNP at n_A = −0.43×10¹²) will double features shifted by +0.43×10¹² — a known confound, not new physics.

**Price.** 73 rows × (177 × 1.05 s + ~10 s flyback/settle) ≈ 4.0 h + 73 min of ramp (0 → 10 T) ≈ 5.2 h; realistic +8 % (run 5: 0.99–1.05 s/pt at `fast_delay` 1; run 6: 3.06 s/pt field overhead) → **≈ 5 h 40 m**, ending at 10 T, gates 0.

**Checkpoints.** First row (~7 min): rail / dead channel / leakage / field tracking — abort there if wrong. Then ~every 45 min: re-form the landed rows (`fan_grids`), image + per-n FFT; continue unless the channel rails across rows or T leaves 1.0–1.5 K.
