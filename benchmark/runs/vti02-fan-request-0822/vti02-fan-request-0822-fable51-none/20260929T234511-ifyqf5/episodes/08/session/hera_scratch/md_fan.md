## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n = −1.28 … +3.25×10¹² fast × B 1 → 10 T slow

**Why now (plan step 4; operator's request).** The spot checks read as two-band: run 7 (hole, n = −1.2×10¹², `data/7`) carries fundamentals at **5.3 and 7.1 T whose sum 12.4–12.6 T equals F_L = nh/4e**, and run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹², `data/6`) carries **7.3 T and 29.4 T** (sum 36.7 T vs F_L 33.1 T — an 11 % density-scale gap, since c_tg is still the 9.5 nm geometry prior). Two tones summing to the total-density frequency is the Bernal (ABAB) two-bilayer picture and disfavours a single ABCA band, but a stacking call needs the fan: the filling-factor sequence of the strong Rxx minima (ν = ±4 vs ±8 as the first gap → 8- vs 16-fold zero LL), Landau-level **crossings** (two ladders, ABAB) vs none (ABCA), and any ν = ±2 (Dirac-like, ABCB). The fan lines n = νeB/h also fix the absolute density scale that the spot checks flagged.

**Design.** Fast axis: (V_tg, V_bg) **co-swept along the calibrated D = 0 line** (run-5 calibration: c_bg/c_tg = 0.773, n_offset −0.089×10¹², D_offset −0.013 V/nm), n = −1.275 … +3.25×10¹² in 0.025×10¹² steps (182 pts; ≈ 10 pts per Δν = 1 at 10 T, 5 at 5 T). Slow axis: B 1.0 → 10.0 T uniform 0.2 T (46 rows), **ascending from 0 T where the magnet sits**, ending at 10 T — the next step rides it down. Below 1 T there is no SdH content at this T_e (runs 6/7 onsets ≈ 1.0–1.4 T). fast_delay 1 s (τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB, as run 5), slow_delay 3 s; sr1 fixed at **200 µV = 20 kΩ FS** — runs 6/7 reached ~590 Ω at 10 T off the CNP, and ν = 0 at 10 T may be insulating; the 60 Ω metallic floor is still ~0.3 % of FS, readable. Leak guard 1 nA on both Keithleys.

**Coverage trade.** The hole side stops at n = −1.275×10¹² (TG −0.3 V limit): ν ≥ −5.3 at 10 T, so ν = −4 is inside, ν = −8 is not. The electron side reaches ν = +13.4 at 10 T (ν = 4, 8, 12 all inside) — the degeneracy sequence is read there. Full hole coverage is not available inside the committed box.

**Price.** Run-5 rate 1.17 s/pt → 213 s/row + ~6 s flyback/turnaround + 88 s ramp + 3 s settle = 310 s/row × 46 = 3 h 58 m, + 7 min 0 → 1 T ramp, +5–10 % → **≈ 4 h 20 m**. Ends: B = 10 T, gates parked at 0 V, T base.

**Checkpoints.** (1) First rows (1.0–1.4 T, ~15 min): sr1 fs < 0.9 along the row, both gate currents at the pA floor, R range 50–600 Ω, T_sample ≤ 1.3 K — abort on a rail or leak. (2) ~4 T (~1 h 30 m): re-form the partial grid, check that minima move with n (fan lines) rather than sitting at fixed B; continue/adjust. (3) ~7 T: interim ν read on the electron side.
