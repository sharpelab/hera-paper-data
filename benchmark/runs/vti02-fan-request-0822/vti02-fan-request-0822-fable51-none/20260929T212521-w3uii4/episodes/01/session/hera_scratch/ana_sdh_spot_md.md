## Runs 6–7 — `sdh-spot-check` at D = 0: electron n = +3.2×10¹² (run 6, 0 → 10 T, 10 mT) and hole n = −1.2×10¹² (run 7, 10 → 0.3 T, 20 mT)

Raw first (top row), then 1/B and the 1/B FFT. **Run 6 (electron):** a clean quantum-Hall trace — Rxx zeros (< 5 Ω on a 5 kΩ full scale, noise 2.8 Ω rms) at 1.85, 1.99, 2.31, 2.45, 2.71, 3.24, 3.72, 4.30, 5.91, 6.08 and 7.15 T; SdH visible from ≈ 1.3–1.6 T; a broad 500 Ω maximum at 8.6–9.0 T falling to 300 Ω at 9.75 T; T_sample 1.17 → 1.29 K over the ramp (eddy heating, tolerated). **The zero sequence is not periodic in 1/B**: consecutive spacings run 0.027–0.066 T⁻¹ and three zeros come as close pairs (1.85/1.99, 2.31/2.45, 5.91/6.08 T), so no single 4-fold Landau ladder at any single density reproduces it. FFT: fundamental 7.1 T with peaks at 15.9 / 22.9 / 30.0 T (2 T resolution) — either the harmonic ladder of a very non-sinusoidal 7.1 T tone, or a light Fermi surface at ~7 T plus a heavy one at ~30 T (the calibration's ABAB estimate was 7.5 / 25.6 T; 7 + 30 = 37 T vs F_L = 33 T → density scale ≤ 12 % low, i.e. inside the geometry-prior error). Harmonic-vs-second-band is undecidable from one column. **Run 7 (hole):** shallower oscillations (zeros only at 1.68 / 2.04 T; R ≥ 30 Ω above 3 T), tones at 5.3–6.6 T and **12.3 T = F_L(g = 4) for n = 1.2×10¹² to 1 %**, plus 8.5 T; R climbs to 587 Ω at 10 T (near-CNP high-B upturn). **Take:** the device is in the QH regime from ≈ 2 T on both sides; the run-5 density scale is confirmed to ≲ 12 % (12.3 T tone, 7 + 30 T sum); 50 µV never railed (max fs 0.12), the CNP itself was not crossed at field. The two cuts do **not** make a stacking call — they tell us the fan is feasible and what it must separate: per-row (fixed-B) ν-spacing and gap-closing crossings, per-column F(n) tones (a harmonic stays at exactly k×F for every n; a second band does not).
