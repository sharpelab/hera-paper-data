## HERA — Step 3: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), B 1 → 10 T × n −1.26 … +3.24×10¹²

Plan step 3 (`hera_scratch/plan.md`). The spot checks decide the geometry: run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹², D = 0) shows Rxx → 0 quantum-Hall minima from 1.8 T with SdH tones at 7.3 / 14.8 / 22.3 / 29.4 T — either harmonics of a 7.3 T tone or a two-band set (7.3 + 22.3 ≈ 30 ≈ 0.9 F_L, ratio 0.33 ≈ the Bernal light/heavy partition 0.38); run 7 (n = −1.2×10¹²) shows 7.1 and 12.6 T (≈ F_L). A ~7 T tone at *both* densities is what a fixed-density (ungated) region produces, and the 1D cuts cannot tell a harmonic from a second band. The fan resolves both by line **slopes** in (n, B): every real gap traces n = ν·eB/h through the origin (assigning ν and fixing the absolute n scale — the geometry prior is ±10 %), a parasitic runs horizontal, and the stacking read is the ladder structure — one monotonic 4-fold sequence (ABCA) vs two interleaved ladders with minima that vanish where they cross (ABAB) vs a √B + B^{3/2} pair (ABCB). **Geometry:** B slow, ascending from where the magnet sits (0 T) — 1.0 → 10.0 T in 0.1 T (91 rows; oscillation onset is ≈ 1 T in both spot checks, so rows below 1 T buy nothing); fast axis (V_tg, V_bg) co-swept along the run-5-calibrated D = 0 line, n = −1.26 … +3.24×10¹² in 130 steps (3.5×10¹⁰/pt = 0.7 ν at 2 T, 0.15 ν at 10 T). **Coverage trade:** the hole side stops at −1.26×10¹² (TG −0.3 V) and the electron side at +3.24 (TG +1.0) — the validators, not a choice; the D = 0 line is the only cut, finite D is step 4 if the fan is ambiguous. **Sensitivity:** sr1 200 µV = 20 kΩ full scale — the ν = 0 CNP at 10 T may exceed 20 kΩ and rail (accepted; a railed CNP row is itself a read), while SdH minima (0–30 Ω) keep ≲ 1 Ω digitization. fast_delay 1 s (≈ 3τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB — a uniform lag along n, shifts every line by ≲ 1 step, slopes untouched), slow_delay 2 s after the flyback, leak guard (> 1 nA either gate → interrupt) on. **Price (measured):** run 5 gives 1.17 s/pt on the gate fast axis; run 6 gives 3.1 s magnet overhead per set → row ≈ 130 × 1.17 + 6 s flyback/dwell + 47 s (0.1 T ramp + 3 s settle) ≈ 205 s × 91 = 5.2 h + 7 min to 1 T; realistic with the session's 5–10 % overrun ≈ 5 h 50 m. **Checkpoints:** rows 1–2 (~10 min: rail, TG current at V_tg = 1.0, signal), then every ~10 rows — re-form the partial fan, trace the minima lines, abort if the channel rails across the map or the lines are featureless. Ends at **10 T** with the gates parked at (n, D) = (0, 0); whatever follows rides the magnet down.
