## HERA — Step 4: D≈0 Landau fan on B_1–B_2 (`landau-fan`)

Runs 6 and 7 show strong oscillations on both sides of the CNP, but their FFT peaks shift with the fit window and run 5 has two parallel CNP ridges. A density-resolved fan on the sole hardwired pair is the next discriminant: follow the run-5 D≈0 calibration from n=−1.20 to +3.20×10¹² cm⁻² while B rises 0→10 T. This covers both spot densities and stays inside all gate and field validators. The last ≈0.07×10¹² on the hole edge is excluded to keep TG ≈23 mV above its −0.3 V limit; the price of that edge is at most two density samples. Absolute n still uses the 9.5 nm hBN thickness prior.

Preflight reads the live thermometry logfiles, base T, field near 0, Si at 0, heaters off, magnet output/switch, bias drive and gate pacing; it stops on a mismatch. Set SR0/SR1 τ=0.1 s and wait 1.0 s per gate point (10τ at 24 dB/oct), then restore τ after the sweep. Hold sr1 sensitivity at 200 µV (≈20 kΩ full scale at 10 nA), monitor both gate currents, measured drain current and temperatures, and stop on >1 nA leakage or a base-T excursion. The field set is blocking at 0.137 T/min: 10 T costs at least 73 min; data/5 measured 1.10 s/point with 1 s delay and ≈5.8 s row turnaround, so 48×111 points plus dual-gate writes and margin price this at ≈3 h 40 m. The fan ends at 10 T for a possible ride-down; gates remain at the last density.

Checkpoints: inspect the first two completed rows for leakage, rail/clipping, live temperature and a responsive Rxx channel; then inspect at ≈2, 4, 6 and 8 T for useful Landau trajectories and continued headroom. Interrupt on leakage, sustained clipping or a dead channel; re-read the actual field and gates after any interruption. The run-5 second CNP ridge means a doubled fan may still be a series-region effect, so the verdict will depend on trajectory shifts and filling sequence, not branch count alone.
