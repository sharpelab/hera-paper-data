## HERA — Step 4: electron Landau fan at calibrated D = 0 (`landau-fan`)

Plan step 4. Run 6 (data/6, n = +3.2×10¹² cm⁻²) has SdH contrast from ~2 T upward, with 7.05, 14.09, 22.9 and 29.94 T exploratory FFT peaks; harmonics and the second series CNP region (run 5: offset −0.43×10¹² cm⁻²) prevent a pocket count from one cut. This fan varies density at each B to track branches, filling slopes and zero-mode behavior. Run 7 gives a hole-side preview but has less high-density reach. The measured run-5 CNP slope sets c_bg/c_tg = 0.773; absolute density still uses the 9.5 nm TG geometry prior, so slopes will also check that scale.

Sweep the calibrated D = 0 line on the electron side, n = +0.8…+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² in 0.05×10¹² steps (49 points): (V_tg,V_bg) ≈ (+0.296,+0.276)…(+0.984,+1.166) V, inside the committed validators. B is the slow axis, 0→4 T in 0.1 T steps and 4.2→10 T in 0.2 T steps (71 rows), starting from the handoff's measured ~0 T and ending at 10 T; the fixed 0.137 T/min magnet slew costs at least 73 min. Each gate point dwells 2.4 s = 8τ at the verified 0.3 s / 24 dB lock-in setting; `sr1` is fixed at 100 µV (≈10 kΩ full scale at 10 nA), with a rail guard and 1 nA gate-current trip. Run 5 (1 s dwell) gives ~0.17 s read overhead/point; run 6's 10 T traverse took 2 h including 921 3 s magnet settles. Those calibrate ~3 h 48 min before margin, priced at **4 h 15 min**.

Coverage trade: this first fan excludes the CNP and hole side, where the two series regions overlap and a 10 kΩ range may rail; full D = 0 coverage would add about 40 gate points per B row (~2 h) plus possible re-range. We will decide on a focused complementary slice after this fan's branch read. **Checkpoints:** first two complete B rows (~5 min) for live T, drain current, gate leakage, sensitivity rail and nonflat Rxx; then around 3, 6 and 8 T inspect landed-row digests and interim maps, continuing only if the branches remain interpretable. If interrupted, read actual field and gate readbacks before any next move; the magnet may keep ramping after a kernel interrupt.
