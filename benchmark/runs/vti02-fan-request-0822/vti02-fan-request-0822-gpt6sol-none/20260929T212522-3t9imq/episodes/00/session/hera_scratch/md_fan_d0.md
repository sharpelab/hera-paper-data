## HERA — Step 4: D≈0 Landau fan across both carrier signs (`landau-fan`)

Plan step 4 follows the run-5 CNP/D-line calibration and the completed spot checks (data/6–7): both signs oscillate, while the electron spectrum has possible harmonics, so density-moving branches are the next discriminator for ABAB, ABCA and ABCB on the sole wired B_1–B_2 pair. Co-sweep TG/BG along run-5 D≈0 from n=−1.0 to +3.2×10¹² cm⁻² (V_tg≈−0.220…+0.984 V, V_bg≈−0.391…+1.166 V), Si=0, while B rises from 1 to 10 T in 0.2 T rows from the parked 0 T; the fan excludes n below −1.0, above +3.2 and nonzero D to keep a single calibrated cut within the validators, and excludes B<1 T because the spot traces show little useful oscillation there (full low-B coverage adds ~20 min). The cell first prints and asserts live base thermometry, field/Si/output state, lock-in drive and settling, then checks both gate currents after the initial ramp; it records all live channels, stops on sustained >1 nA gate current, field-tracking error, drain-current loss or sr1 clipping, and leaves B near 10 T for a follow-up that can ride down. **Checkpoints:** inspect the first complete rows for leakage, railing, dead/flat channels and field tracking, then the rows near 3, 6 and 8 T for branch visibility and a continue/abort call; data/6 took 2h00m for a 10 T fixed-gate sweep, and the 9 T ramp floor (~66 min) plus 46×85 gate points at 2.4 s and read/turnaround overhead prices this fan at about 4h30m.
