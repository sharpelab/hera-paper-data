## HERA — Step 4: D = 0 Landau fan across both CNP regions (`landau-fan`)

Runs 6–7 show robust SdH oscillations at n_A = +3.2 and −1.2×10¹² cm⁻², but their single-density spectra mix harmonics with the second CNP region (run 5 offset ≈0.43×10¹² cm⁻²). Plan step 4 therefore traces Landau branches on the run-5 calibrated D = 0 line: field rises once from the handoff's ≈0 T to 10 T in 0.25 T rows; each row co-sweeps TG/BG from n_A = −1.2 to +3.2×10¹² in 0.04×10¹² steps. The gate endpoints are (−0.277, −0.465) and (+0.984, +1.166) V, inside the committed limits. The absolute density scale retains the 9.5 nm top-hBN geometry prior; branch slopes will check it.

The scan covers both signs and both offset CNPs at D = 0. It excludes the last ≈0.07×10¹² cm⁻² at each density edge for gate margin, finite-D structure, and negative B; finite D or finer B sampling can follow the first fan's read. Fixed sr1 sensitivity 200 µV gives ≈20 kΩ nominal full scale at 10 nA; 2.4 s gate dwell is 8τ at the recorded 0.3 s/24 dB setting. Run 5's 1.17 s/point at 1 s dwell calibrates ≈2.6 s/point here: 4440 points ≈3 h 15 m, plus the 10 T / 0.137 T/min ramp ≈73 min and a ≈10% margin gives **about five hours**. Preconditions printed by the cell: fresh base thermometry, field ≈0, Si 0, heaters off, output/PS on, gate NPLC 1, lock-in τ/slope, bias, and card caps. The cell parks TG/BG at 0 after the run; the magnet ends at its measured field.

**Checkpoints:** inspect the first full row for live channels, rail, leakage and ridge continuity; then review the landed rows near B ≈2, 4, 6 and 8 T for a developing branch pattern and measured-current stability. The guard interrupts at >1 nA on either gate or >85% sr1 voltage range. A checkpoint abort also requires reading actual field before another move: a kernel interrupt does not stop an ongoing magnet ramp. If the map is featureless or clipped, stop and revise the next scan instead of spending the remaining field traverse.
