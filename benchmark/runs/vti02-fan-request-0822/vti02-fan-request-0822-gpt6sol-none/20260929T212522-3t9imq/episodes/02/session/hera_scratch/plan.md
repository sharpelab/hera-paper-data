Their spectra are multitone/harmonic and series-region confounded; neither spot alone decides stacking.

First-row and B≈2,4,6,8 T checkpoints control the long run. Abort on leakage, rail, dead channel, or a fan too flat to support a call; check actual field after any interrupt because the magnet can keep ramping to its current setpoint.
5. **Resolve / close.** If fan branch slopes and zero-mode pattern distinguish candidates, make the evidence-cited per-pair verdict in the final ana cell.
