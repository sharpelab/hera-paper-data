## HERA — Plan step 5: fixed-D Landau fan for the stacking call (`landau-fan`)

Data/5 gives a main CNP ridge with c_bg/c_tg = 0.773, n_offset = −0.0894×10¹² cm⁻², and D_offset = −0.013 V/nm. Data/6–7 establish oscillations on both signs but cannot separate independent pockets from harmonics: electron FFT features are ≈7.1, 15.9, 22.9, 30.0 T, hole features ≈5.3, 12.3 T. This fan follows the calibrated **D_A = 0** line across n_A = −1.15…+3.20×10¹² cm⁻² in 0.05×10¹² steps, with B slow from 0.3 to 10 T in 50 rows. It covers the available hole range and the electron range through the high-density spot, while keeping TG about −0.263…+0.984 V and BG about −0.446…+1.166 V within SAFETIES. The absolute n scale remains geometry anchored; branch spacing will check it. The weaker parallel CNP ridge in data/5 means a second domain can add trajectories, so a branch count alone is not a verdict.

Field starts from the post-handoff 0 T state, rises once, and ends at 10 T. The 9.7 T measured traverse has a 71 min ramp floor at 0.137 T/min; data/5 calibrates the gate raster at 1.17 s/point with a 1 s dwell, so 4,400 points with a 2.2 s dwell cost about 174 min plus ramp and row overhead. Allowing 5–10% overhead, plan ~4 h 30 m. The 2.2 s dwell is ≥7τ at the 0.3 s/24 dB lock-in setting; a fixed 1 mV sensitivity gives ~100 kΩ headroom near the high-field CNP while retaining µV-scale oscillations. This is a long run: check the first two rows for actual gate coverage, leakage, drain current, and rail; re-check at ~3 T and ~6 T for coherent branches and the first strong CNP rise. Abort on sustained >1 nA gate leakage, a rail, or a dead channel. The fan's raw currents/voltages and re-formed Rxx map will land in ana before a stacking interpretation.
