## HERA — Plan step 4: D≈0 Landau fan (`landau-fan`)

> **Summary:** B +1.2→+9.6 T in 0.2 T rows; fixed D≈0 co-sweep n_A −1.2→+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² in 0.05 steps (89 points/row), Si=0, base T, sr1 200 µV, τ=0.1 s, 1 s fast dwell. **ETA: ~3h** including the 0→9.6 T traverse.

Plan step 4 follows runs 5–7. Run 5 gives two parallel CNP ridges separated by ~0.43×10¹² cm⁻²; runs 6–7 show a strong ~7.2–7.3 T tone and a 12.5 T hole component. A density–field fan can test whether branches track the same or distinct offsets and whether their filling slopes fit a single four-layer ladder or multiple bilayer-like ladders. The gate path uses run 5's measured c_bg/c_tg=0.773 and CNP/D offsets; its absolute density scale retains the 9.5 nm geometry prior and will be tested against fan slopes. Only the hardwired B_1–B_2 Rxx pair is measurable.

Coverage: the TG limit restricts the hole side to −1.2×10¹² and the electron side to +3.2×10¹² along D≈0. This deliberately omits larger |D| and the unreachable hole density; a later targeted D cut is justified only if the fan leaves a stacking ambiguity. Price: run 5's 1 s fast dwell plus ~0.2–0.25 s readback gives ~80 min for 3,827 points; the fixed 0.137 T/min ramp adds ~70 min from B≈0 to 9.6 T; row turnarounds and the run 6 overhead bring the realistic estimate to ~3 h. The first rows check gate leakage (<1 nA), current, lock-in rail and thermometry; at ~4 T and ~7 T inspect landed rows for coherent fan features and abort for a clear wash-out. Magnet sets block through their 3 s settle and a kernel interrupt during a ramp does **not** stop the supply; re-read field before any recovery move. The cell returns TG and BG to 0 V at the end; field remains at its measured final position for the next step.
