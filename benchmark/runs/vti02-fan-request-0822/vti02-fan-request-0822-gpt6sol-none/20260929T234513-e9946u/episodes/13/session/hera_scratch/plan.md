4. **Landau fan (next).** At base T, Si = 0 and calibrated D = 0, co-sweep TG/BG along n = −1.2 … +3.2 × 10¹² cm⁻² in 0.05 × 10¹² steps, field slow from the present 0 to 10 T in 0.25 T rows. Acquire measured drain current, B_1–B_2 voltage and phase, both gate leakage currents, and both temperatures. Use 3 s fast-point dwell (10 τ at 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct), fixed 500 µV lock-in sensitivity, 1 nA leakage and 90% sensitivity trip. First-row and periodic 2 T checkpoints decide whether to stop on leakage/railing/dead channel. Compare ridge trajectories and filling-factor intercepts with full-density and split-pocket lines; distinguish an independent light/heavy pair from harmonics by density scaling. Since there is only one wired voltage pair, the verdict can only be for B_1–B_2.
5. **Analysis and decision.** Re-form each complete fan row with hera.analysis, plot raw channels first, then Rxx with measured-current normalization. Fit branches versus B and n, and test whether data support ABAB, ABCA, ABCB or a calibrated cannot-tell. Account for unknown absolute capacitance and electron temperature. If the first fan is inconclusive, target a small D-offset confirmation or higher-resolution cut inside the committed box only where the ambiguity lives; price that next step using the fan's measured throughput. The final ana cell states the per-region verdict, numbered evidence, and limitations.
6. **Close-out.** When the operator asks return-to-initial, ramp field and all three gates to 0 at committed pacing, turn both heater ranges off, and record field/gate/T readback in session_notes.md.
