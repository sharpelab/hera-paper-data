## HERA — Step 4: calibrated D = 0 Landau fan (`landau-fan`)

Runs 6–7 establish oscillatory, unclipped transport on the provided B_1−B_2 pair, while their FFT peaks cannot yet separate independent bands from harmonics. Following plan step 4, this fan tracks features continuously across density and field: B slow from the handoff's 0 T state to the 10 T session cap, (V_tg,V_bg) co-swept fast on run 5's calibrated D = 0 line. The 81 density points span −1.2 to +3.2×10¹² cm⁻² (55×10⁹ cm⁻² spacing); all computed gate voltages stay within the SAFETIES validators. Below 1.5 T only four background rows are taken because the spot-check oscillations that bear on the call are clearer above ~1.5 T. The excluded D = 0 density slivers are −1.27…−1.2 and +3.2…+3.27×10¹² cm⁻², preserving a 16 mV margin at the TG upper limit; full dense low-field and edge coverage would cost roughly another hour without a likely gain in discrimination.

Before moving, the cell prints and asserts live sample/vaporizer thermometry, base temperature, field near 0 T, Si at 0 V, both heaters off, magnet switch/output state, lock-in bias and settling, and gate-current NPLC. The 2.5 s fast delay plus readout is at least ~8τ for the 0.3 s / 24 dB lock-ins; sr1 is fixed at 1 mV (100 kΩ nominal at 10 nA) so a high-field neutrality peak has headroom. A run-after guard stops on |I_gate| > 1 nA, sr1 > 90% full scale, or drain current < 1 nA. Check the first complete rows for flat/dead channels, leakage, rail and actual B tracking; review the landed rows near 3, 6 and 9 T for a visible fan. At any abort, read actual B/gates before a new move: the VTI magnet continues to its latest commanded row target even after a kernel interrupt.

Price from this session: run 5 took 81 min for 4158 gate-map points at 1 s fast delay; this fan has 3807 two-gate points at 2.5 s delay, plus a 10 T traverse at 0.137 T/min (73 min bare), row flyback and a ~10% allowance. It never moves Si or changes pacing, limits, heater settings, or magnet rate.
