# vti-02 stacking campaign plan (updated at handoff after data/7)

Goal: make an independent, evidence-cited ABAB / ABCA / ABCB / cannot-tell call for the only wired four-probe pair, B_1–B_2. Two CNP ridges indicate at least two series regions inside that path; the available wiring cannot isolate them. Keep that limitation explicit in the final call.

1. **Bring-up and liveness — complete.** Setup and SAFETIES ran; VERIFY printed ALL GREEN and Zack granted E1 (session_notes). Gate C runs 0–3 passed: drain current ~9.9 nA, gate currents at pA offsets, two resistance maxima. Recheck the SAFETIES execution count and numeric limits before each new measure cell.
2. **Calibrate n and D — complete, with scale hedge.** Run 5 map gives ridge-A c_bg/c_tg = 0.773, n_offset = −0.0894×10¹² cm⁻², D_offset = −0.013 V/nm. c_tg = 2.796 mF/m² remains anchored to the 9.5 nm geometry prior; absolute n scale is not independently Hall-calibrated. Ridge B is displaced by ~−0.43×10¹² cm⁻² on the A coordinate. A field fan can test the lever arm from line slopes.
3. **SdH feasibility — complete, analysis being promoted.** Runs 6 (n_A=+3.2×10¹²) and 7 (n_A=−1.2×10¹²), both D_A=0, show large oscillations from about 1 T. Multi-tone spectra may be separate bands, harmonics, or the second series region; frequencies depend on the selected B window. A one-point FFT is insufficient to identify stacking. No clipping or leakage appeared.
4. **Landau fan — designed and landed, pending execution.** Measure cells 47–48 sweep the calibrated D_A=0 line, n_A=−1.2…+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² in 0.04×10¹² steps (111 points), at B=0, 0.5, 1.0 and 1.1…10.0 T (93 rows total), ascending from the 0 T handoff state. Record T, both gate currents, current X/P and Rxx X/P at every point, and plot all. Use a fixed sensitivity sized to the high-field CNP; use 0.1 s lock-in time constants and 1 s fast dwell (10τ, 24 dB). First-row and periodic live checkpoints check leakage, rail, signal, and ridge emergence. Re-form the map from disk and compare straight-line slopes/degeneracies on each side with the A/B ridge shift. The tool's `episode complete` hook blocked execution before any hardware move and blocked the Slack notify; see session_notes.
5. **Targeted follow-up only if the fan separates hypotheses.** If the fan reveals two independent families whose sum densities track n_A across gate voltage, test ABAB; a single full-density family plus the large D-tunable gap favors ABCA; unequal or crossing families may support ABCB. Distinguish harmonics by their slope ratio and phase, and distinguish series-region copies by the measured −0.43×10¹² cm⁻² offset. If ambiguous, use a smaller higher-resolution B–n patch and/or an allowed D-offset cut, priced from the fan. Do not claim a unique stacking from a two-terminal-like series mixture.
6. **Call and close-out.** Put the per-pair verdict, run-id evidence, confidence, and limits in the final ana cell. Then ask/execute return-to-initial: 0 T, TG/BG/Si 0 V, both heater ranges off; record actual readback in session_notes.

Decision changes: abort a fan row early for >1 nA gate current or >90% sr1 full scale, read actual hardware state, and resize/replan. Abort the long fan if first rows have a dead current/Rxx channel or if early high-field rows show no structure. Reassess stacking after each completed run, not from a live raster.
