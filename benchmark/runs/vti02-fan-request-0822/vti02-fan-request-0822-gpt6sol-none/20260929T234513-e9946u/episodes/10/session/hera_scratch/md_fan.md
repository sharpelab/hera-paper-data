## HERA — Step 4: calibrated D = 0 Landau fan (`landau-fan`)

Plan step 4. Runs 6–7 show strong SdH oscillations, but their fixed-density FFT peaks can be harmonics or copies from the second series region (data/5 offset ≈ −0.43×10¹² cm⁻²). A field–density map tests whether branches move as one full-density family, two intrinsic families, or copies with that fixed offset. The only wired four-probe pair is B_1–B_2; the map cannot spatially isolate the two CNP ridges. The region-A calibration from data/5 sets D_A = 0 and n_A = −1.2…+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² (111 points, Δn = 0.04×10¹²); that line spans TG −0.277…+0.984 V and BG −0.465…+1.166 V, inside the committed limits. Absolute n still inherits the 9.5 nm top-hBN geometry prior.

Field ascends 0, 0.5, 1.0, then 1.1…10.0 T at 0.1 T (93 rows). This starts at the handoff's 0 T and ends at 10 T for a possible descending follow-up. Each row co-sweeps TG and BG to hold D_A = 0; Si remains 0. The 0.1 T spacing and 0.04×10¹² density spacing resolve the low-field fan from the run-6/7 onset, while retaining the CNP and both carrier signs. At τ = 0.1 s / 24 dB, the 1 s fast dwell is 10τ. Set sr1 to 500 µV (50 kΩ nominal R full scale at 10 nA) for high-field CNP headroom; the spot-check signals were only 10–12% of 50 µV, so their larger oscillations remain visible at this range. A >1 nA gate-current, <1 nA drain-current, >90% sr1-range, or T>1.5 K guard interrupts the sweep. Gates park at 0 after completion or interruption; the field holds where the run ends. Magnet interrupts do not stop an in-progress ramp on this rig.

Price from data/5 gate map: 4158 points in 81.2 min, ~1.17 s/point at 1 s fast dwell. The 10 T field traverse is 73 min at 0.137 T/min; 10,323 points cost ~3 h 20 m before two-gate co-sets and row overhead. Allowing ~30–50% overhead and the measured 5–10% estimate bias gives **about 6 h**. Checkpoints: first complete 0 T row for leakage, current, and rail; the first few finite-B rows for field tracking and emerging structure; then every ~10 rows (~1 T) to judge branch continuity and whether the map is still discriminating. Stop early on a guard trip, dead/flat channel, or a wash-out by ~3 T, then read actual field and gates before replanning.
