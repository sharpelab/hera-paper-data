## HERA — Plan step 1: electron Landau fan at calibrated D_A = 0 (`landau-fan`)

Runs 6 and 7 are complete SdH spot checks: the electron spot has oscillatory components near 5–9 and 30 T depending on the FFT window, while the hole spot has about 5 and 12.6 T. Those window-dependent peaks cannot count subbands reliably, and run 5 shows a second CNP ridge offset by ≈0.43×10¹² cm⁻² along this same hardwired B_1–B_2 path. This fan resolves the electron-side oscillations against calibrated density to test whether branches follow two bilayer-like pockets, a dominant rhombohedral pocket, or a mixed pattern; interpretation will allow for both spatial regions.

Run 5 calibrates c_bg/c_tg = 0.773 and the D_A = 0 offset; its absolute density scale remains anchored to the 9.5 nm hBN geometry prior. The scan uses n_A = +0.50…+3.10×10¹² cm⁻² in 0.05×10¹² steps, B = 0.5…10 T in 0.1 T steps, Si = 0, base T. This excludes the hole side and the |n|<0.5×10¹² CNP neighborhood, where the TG validator narrows coverage and high-field resistance may rail; those remain limitations for the verdict. The calibrated line corresponds to TG ≈ +0.210…+0.955 V and BG ≈ +0.165…+1.129 V, within the committed limits.

Preflight in the cell reads temperature-log freshness, both temperatures, field (expected 0 after the handoff), Si and gate setpoints, heater ranges, magnet switch/output, and gate-current NPLC before moving. The field setter blocks at each row; **10 T / 0.137 T min⁻¹ ≈ 73 min bare ramp**, plus 96×53 points at 2.1 s gate settle (7× the 0.3 s / 24 dB lock-in time constant), ~0.17 s measured read/set overhead per point from run 5, row setup/flyback, and ~10% headroom gives ≈5 h. Fixed sr1 sensitivity 100 µV gives ~10 kΩ full scale at the 10 nA bias. The cell trips on |gate current| >1 nA or |sr1 X| >90% full scale; it leaves field/gates at the last measured point for readback and planning.

**Checkpoints:** inspect the first three completed B rows for leakage, railing, stale T, and a live current channel; abort if any fails. Re-read the landed-row digests and partial map near 3, 6, and 8.5 T. If no density-dependent Landau branches appear by ~4 T, or the CNP-side points rail, stop before the expensive high-field remainder and resize or narrow the scan. The rig's field command keeps ramping if interrupted mid-ramp; involve the operator for a ramp safety concern.
