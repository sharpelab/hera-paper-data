## HERA — Step 4: electron-side Landau fan (`landau-fan`)

Plan step 4, after data/5 calibrated the main CNP ridge and data/6–7 established strong SdH signal. Run 6 has a ≈7.1 T component and ≈22–30 T structure at nominal n_A=+3.2×10¹² cm⁻²; harmonics and the second CNP ridge (offset ≈0.43×10¹²) can mimic a second pocket. This fan varies density at D≈0 to see whether distinct Landau branches disperse independently and where they meet B=0. B is the slow axis, ascending from its handoff position ≈0 to 10 T; TG/BG co-sweep the run-5 calibrated D=0 line, n_A=+0.8…+3.2×10¹² cm⁻². The absolute density scale retains the top-hBN geometry prior; branch spacing and intercepts will be compared with the two measured CNP ridges.

**Coverage trade:** n_A<+0.8×10¹² is excluded because the series region B approaches its CNP there; a high-field gap may exceed the fixed 100 µV Rxx sensitivity (≈10 kΩ at 10 nA). The hole side is excluded from this fan because run 7's limited |n| and ≈8 K electron floor make its band test weaker; it remains in the ana record. 0.25 T B spacing and 0.04×10¹² density spacing target the 2–10 T filling sequences. The magnet traverse alone costs 10/0.137 ≈73 min; 2501 points at 2.3 s settle plus dual-gate writes/readbacks, row flyback and measured run-5 overhead bring the realistic runtime near the quoted ETA.

Preflight: live sample and vaporizer logfiles, base T, B≈0, Si=0 on RNGE 2/output on, heater ranges off, magnet output/persistent-switch heater on, lock-in drive/τ and committed validators. The cell sets its own TG/BG and Rxx sensitivity, then records both gate currents, measured drain current, Rxx X/P, and both temperatures. **Checkpoint:** inspect the first 2–3 complete rows for rail, leakage, drain-current failure and actual field tracking; then inspect at roughly 2, 5 and 8 T and interrupt for a sustained rail/leak or a flat channel. The cell also trips automatically on persistent >1 nA gate current or >90% Rxx full scale. A checkpoint wash-out saves the remaining ramp; any interrupt leaves the magnet at its current field, which must be read before another move. End state if complete: B=10 T, gates at n_A=+3.2×10¹², Si=0.
