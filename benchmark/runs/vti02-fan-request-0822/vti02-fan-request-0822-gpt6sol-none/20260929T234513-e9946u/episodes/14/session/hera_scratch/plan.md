# vti-02 stacking investigation — live plan

Goal: call ABAB / ABCA / ABCB for the one hardwired longitudinal pair B_1–B_2, or report cannot tell. The contact span may contain two regions in series; separate feature families before assigning a stacking order. All gate and field moves remain within device-card limits, at base temperature unless new data motivates a temperature step.

1. Bring-up, VERIFY, and Gate C: complete (measure cells 30–34; data/0–3). Device alive, no gate leakage over the tested box.
2. CNP and D-line calibration: complete (data/5; ana cells 12–13). Two roughly parallel CNP ridges, A and B; A has c_bg/c_tg=0.773 and D_offset=-0.013 V/nm. Absolute density inherits the 9.5 nm top-hBN geometry prior. Ridge B is shifted about -0.43×10^12 cm^-2 in A-calibrated density.
3. Field spot checks on both carrier sides: complete (data/6–7). Both have oscillatory Rxx at base T and safely low leakage. Analyze raw and spectra in ana before fan. Their frequencies alone may mix two series regions and harmonics.
4. Landau fan at D_A=0: sweep field upward from the handoff's ~0 T through 10 T, density fast along the run-5 calibrated line from -1.10 to +3.20×10^12 cm^-2. Use fine enough B steps below 3 T to follow the visible early oscillations, then coarser B steps to the cap. Record drain current, Rxx, both gate currents, and sample temperature. First rows: check signal, clipping, leakage and actual field. Around 3, 6 and 8 T: inspect landed rows and decide whether to continue or interrupt. Re-form the map from disk, identify line families and their density intercepts; compare intercept spacing/slopes with A/B offset and 4-fold LL density scale, without treating the geometry density anchor as exact.
5. If the D=0 fan separates one fourfold family from two branches, test its stability at a second modest D within the validators; if families remain ambiguous, use a targeted small fan or temperature dependence based on the observed gaps. Do not commit hours to a second map until Step 4 has a quantitative read. The contact pair is fixed, so a per-region attribution may remain impossible.
6. Finish with a region-resolved evidence table and calibrated uncertainty in the final ana cell. Return to initial state only after an explicit `return-to-initial` request in the session's close-out arc.
