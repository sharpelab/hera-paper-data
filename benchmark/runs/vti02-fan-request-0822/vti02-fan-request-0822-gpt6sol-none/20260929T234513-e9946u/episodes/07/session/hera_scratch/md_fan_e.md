## HERA — Plan step 5: electron Landau fan at calibrated D = 0 (`landau-fan`)

The handoff left B≈0 and all gates at 0 (measure cell 44), and the same kernel is bound (cell 46). Run 5 gives the A-region CNP line (c_bg/c_tg=0.773, n_offset=−0.089×10¹² cm⁻², D_offset≈−0.013 V/nm), with a second parallel ridge shifted by 0.43×10¹²; run 6 has strong electron-side SdH oscillations but its ∼7 T and ∼30 T content cannot be separated into pockets, harmonics, or series regions from one trace. Run 7 confirms hole-side oscillations, though the safe D=0 hole range is short. This fan maps how the features move with density on the same D=0 line and tests whether one or two Landau families and the two local CNP offsets can be traced.

Field is the slow axis: 2.0→10.0 T in 0.2 T steps (41 rows); paired TG/BG voltages form the fast axis at A n=+0.6→+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² (41 points, Δn=0.065×10¹²) with Si fixed at 0. The gate endpoints are about (TG,BG)=(+0.239,+0.202) and (+0.984,+1.166) V, within committed limits. `fast_delay=2.5 s` gives >8× the 0.3 s, 24 dB lock-in time constant; 100 µV sr1 sensitivity gives ~10 kΩ full scale at 10 nA to allow high-field peaks without the run-4 rail. Coverage trade: the fan omits holes and |n|<0.6, where D=0 has little negative reach and a high-field insulating peak could clip; extending to the TG limits would add time and obscure the electron families. The absolute density scale retains the 9.5 nm geometry prior until checked by the Landau slopes.

Before running, the cell reads live thermometry, field, Si, magnet output/switch, gate pacing, drive, and lock-in settling. It aborts for >1 nA on either Keithley and parks TG/BG at zero on exit, leaving the magnet at its measured end field for a possible descending follow-up. Checkpoints: inspect the first two rows for live sr0/sr1 signals, no rail, and gate-current transients versus persistent leakage; reassess trajectory visibility and any wash-out near B=4, 6, and 8 T.
