## HERA — Step 4 prep: electron SdH re-acquisition at n = +3.2×10¹² (`sdh-spot-check`), uniform-1/B 0.3 → 10 T — and the fan design it feeds

**Why this leg instead of the fan directly.** The fan for the stacking call has to live on the **electron** side: at D = 0 inside the validators the hole side reaches only n = −1.27×10¹² (TG −0.3 V), which is squarely inside the investigation's low-|n| **exception regime** (ABAB's heavy tone within ~5 % of F_L, partner unresolvable) — designing the decisive fan there would be designing into the blind spot. The electron side reaches +3.2×10¹², where the templates separate (ABAB heavy ≈ 0.72·F_L vs ABCA singleton at F_L → 9.3 T apart). But **run 6, the electron spot check, is not a usable record**: sr1 sat at **0.25 % / 0.53 % / 1.6 % of full scale** over B = 0–2 / 2–5 / 5–7.4 T (X ≈ 0.13–0.80 µV on the 50 µV range), i.e. at the ADC-granularity + input-noise floor — the recipe's grainy-FFT failure. Its "step at 7.5 T" is the signal climbing out of that floor (phase settles from ±150° scatter to ±9° there), and its tone comb (7.1 T with 2×/3×/4× harmonics) is not evidence. Per the investigation's *acquisition quality is empirical* discipline, run 6 cannot be used to call the electron side bad, and `landau-fan` step 1 forbids guessing `B_lo`: the onset must come from `sdh_onset` on a real record. So this leg re-acquires it — **and it is not a detour**: the descending fan needs the magnet at 10 T anyway, and a bare 0 → 10 T positioning ramp costs 73 min and yields nothing. Measuring on the way up costs **+22 min** and buys the onset, the tone set at the fan's n_max, the digitization proof, and a grid-change reproduction (uniform-1/B here vs uniform-B in run 6 — a mandated Verify item).

**The acquisition fix.** `I_bias` 10 → **40 nA** (sr1 drive 1 → 4 V into the 100 MΩ bias resistor). The lock-in floor is an *absolute* voltage (input noise ≈ 2.5 nV at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB, measured granularity 1.86 nV on the 50 µV range), so 4× the current is 4× the SNR — the range ratio alone cannot buy this back. Heating at the worst corner: P = I²R ≈ 1 pW, V_sample ≤ 24 µV ≈ 0.3 K, far under T_e ≈ 8 K. sr1 fixed at **50 µV (1.25 kΩ FS)**: run-6's max (522 Ω) lands at 42 %, its low-B minimum (12 Ω) at 1.0 %. sr0 (Ithaco 10⁶) would read 40 mV at 40 nA, overloading its 20 mV range → moved to 100 mV. τ and filter slope untouched (0.3 s, 24 dB/oct); `delay = 0` because each `magnet.field` set blocks through its ramp plus the driver's 3 s settle = 10 τ, so every point is at the settling floor.

**Grid.** Uniform in **1/B**, 0.30 → 10.0 T, 321 pts, Δ(1/B) = 0.01010 T⁻¹ = **Nyquist ×3.0 on F_L = 33.1 T** (the fastest tone any 4L template allows at this density), δF = 0.31 T. This departs from `sdh-spot-check` step 1's blocking-magnet branch (uniform in B at ΔB = Δ(1/B)·B_lo²), deliberately: that branch assumes the points *ride* the traverse, which is false here — at B_lo = 0.3 T it prescribes ΔB = 0.9 mT, i.e. 10 800 points whose 4.0 s/pt overhead (measured, run 6) dwarfs the 0.4 s ramp between neighbours. Uniform-1/B gives identical points-per-period for 321 points; the cost is less per-period averaging at high B, which the fan's dense n-coverage there more than repays. Logged as a RECIPE FIX.

**The fan this feeds (Step 4, `landau-fan` + `-vti`), designed and priced now, committed on this leg's numbers.** Descending `field_invB(10, 1, 91)` as the slow axis × a 65-point paired n-line at D = 0, n = 3.2 → 1.2×10¹²: Δ(1/B) = 0.0100 (Nyquist ×3.02 on F_L(n_max) = 33.1 T); n-step 0.031×10¹² = **3.1 points per LL ray** at B_lo = 1 T (ray spacing 0.097×10¹²); δF = 1.11 T, against a 9.3 T ABAB-heavy-vs-F_L separation at n_max. The ≥5-period partner criterion (1/B_lo − 1/B_hi ≥ 5/f_partner) holds for **n ≥ 2.0×10¹²** — which is exactly the window where the templates separate, so the partition claim and its validity window coincide. Every lever is at its floor: B_lo = 1.0 T is *above* the onset on purpose (B_lo = 0.45 T would cost 212 rows and demand N_n ≥ 139 — 7 h+; sub-1-T content is covered by the 1D spot checks instead, stated as the coverage trade), n_max = 3.2×10¹² is 16 mV under the TG limit, N_n is at the 3-per-ray floor, B_hi is the claim. **Descending**, because that puts the *unsalvageable* failure — clipping at the low-n × high-B corner — in row 1, two minutes in, while the recoverable one (graininess at high n × low B) comes last. Priced at **≈ 4 h 50 m** from this session's own calibrations (run 5: 0.101 s/pt read overhead at nplci = 1; run 6: 4.04 s/pt non-ramp magnet overhead). Its fixed sensitivity (expected 100 µV = 2.5 kΩ FS at 40 nA, sized off the low-n × high-B corner: 99.8 Ω at B = 0 from run 5 × the ×3.8–6.3 MR factors measured in runs 6/7 ⇒ ~400–700 Ω) and its `B_lo` are the two numbers this leg pins before I land it.

**Checkpoints on this leg.** First ~20 points (0.30–0.36 T): is sr1 now off the floor — X ≳ 20 nV of oscillation, phase stable, no leakage trip, field readback tracking setpoints? Then at ~3 T and ~7 T a partial FFT on the landed record (onset tracking, tone count). Abort condition: if X is still at the granularity floor at 40 nA, the electron side is genuinely unmeasurable at this bias and the fan design changes shape — I stop and notify rather than push on. Ends at **10 T with the gates at n = +3.2×10¹²**, which is the fan's start.
