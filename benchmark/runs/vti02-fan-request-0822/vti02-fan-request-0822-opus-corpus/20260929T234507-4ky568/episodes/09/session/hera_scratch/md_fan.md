## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, D = 0, n 2.10 → 3.20×10¹², B 0.7 → 10 T

**Why the electron side.** The hole branch of the region-A D = 0 line stops at n = −1.13×10¹² (TG −0.3 V limit) — inside the investigation's low-|n| exception regime, where the forward model puts the ABAB hole pair at 5.86 / 6.54 T (0.68 T apart, unresolvable at any δF this magnet can deliver) and ABAB genuinely mimics an ABCA singleton. Run 7 shows exactly that ambiguity: one merged tone at 6.6 T plus a strong tone at 12.3 T = 0.99·F_L. The electron branch reaches +3.40×10¹², and at n = 3.2×10¹² the templates separate — model ABAB 7.31 / 25.78 T (partition 0.22 / 0.78) vs ABCA singleton at F_L = 33.09 T. Side choice is set by density reach, not by cleanliness: both spot checks are clean (tone SNR 40–124).

**What run 6 leaves open — and why only n-dependence closes it.** Over run 6's Nyquist-valid record (B ≥ 1 T; below 1 T its 50 mT step gives Δ(1/B) = 0.05–0.2 ⇒ a 2.5–10 T Nyquist ceiling, far under F_L = 33 T, so every sub-1 T tone there is alias-suspect, including the "censored at 0.30 T" onset) the peak set is {7.1, 14.4, 22.2, 29.5} T. Two readings fit one density: an **ABAB pair** 7.1 + 22.2 = 29.3 T closing the Luttinger sum against a global density rescale of 0.89 (inside the 0.8–1.2 lever-arm prior), with 29.5 T the symmetry-allowed inter-block **breakdown sum tone** and 14.4 T the 2nd harmonic; or a plain **harmonic comb** 1×/2×/3×/4× of ≈ 7.35 T with the heavy partner unresolved. One column cannot separate them — a harmonic tracks 3·f₁ exactly while a real heavy pocket follows its own Onsager curve. Across this fan's window the two predictions sit 6.28 T apart at n = 2.10 and 3.87 T apart at n = 3.20 (= 2.6 × 2δF at the tightest column), so the *trajectory* decides. Same lever kills the alternatives: ABCA = one branch on F_L; the trilayer ML+BL adjacent-N template (mandatory dual overlay) puts its partner at 0.9–1.9 T here, not 3.9–7.3 T.

**Mosaic caveat, designed for.** Run 5 found **two parallel CNP ridges** (A and B, ratios 0.773 / 0.691) offset by 0.43×10¹² — region B electron-doped relative to A — and sr1 (B_1–B_2) reads them in series. A superposed second population therefore sits 4.45 T off in F_L at every column: a parallel F(n) branch of the *same* slope, separable only by having columns. 45 columns at Δn = 0.025×10¹² spans that offset in 17 columns.

**Design (step 1, cost-floored; levers in recipe order).**
- **B_lo = 0.7 T.** `sdh_onset` on run 6 returns 0.30 T *censored* — the record's low edge, and that edge sits in the alias-suspect segment, so it is a bound, not a measurement. B_lo is set instead by what the claim needs: δF = 1/(1/B_lo − 1/B_max) = **0.75 T** against a 3.87 T binding separation, and **5.1 partner periods** at the low-n edge. Dropping to 0.35 T would give δF = 0.36 T for **12.6 h** — resolution the claim does not need. The fan's own sampling settles the onset properly on the way up.
- **n_max = 3.20×10¹²** — reproduces run 6's spot exactly as a cross-check column, 16 mV under the TG limit.
- **n_lo = 2.10×10¹²** — the lowest density whose ABAB partner (3.86 T) clears *both* ≥5 periods over the record and the ~3 T background-residual band `sdh_onset` refuses to track. Below it the partner-tone claim is unresolvable, not merely noisy.
- **N_n = 45** (Δn = 0.025×10¹² ⇒ **2.7 points per LL ray** at B_lo, floor 2) — above the bare floor of 35 to carry the two-ridge branch separation above.
- **N_B = 133, uniform in 1/B**, Δ(1/B) = 0.01006 ⇒ Nyquist ceiling **50.1 T = 1.52 × F_L(n_max)**; ΔB runs 5 mT at 0.7 T to 0.92 T at 10 T. Unlike run 6, the whole record is Nyquist-valid.
- **B_max = 10 T** — the session cap; needed for the mandatory B-window split (a breakdown tone strengthens with B; a null split in a short window is weak evidence) and for like-for-like comparison with runs 6/7.

**Sensitivity and settling.** sr1 fixed at 50 µV (5.05 kΩ FS) — inherited from both spot checks, so the n = 3.20 column is directly comparable to run 6. Worst corner (n = 2.10 × 10 T) extrapolates from run 6 (530 Ω at +3.2×10¹²) and run 7 (587 Ω at −1.2×10¹²) to ≲ 900 Ω ≈ 18 % FS, ×5.6 headroom. Trade, stated: the QH minima (R → 0) digitize coarsely — accepted, since saturation is the asymmetric failure (run 4's lesson) and a clipped column is unrecoverable by any B-window. `fast_delay = 1.8 s = 6τ` at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB — the recipe's per-column-SdH-FFT dwell, not the 3τ bend the gatemap took; `slow_delay = 2 s` on top of the blocking field set.

**Price** (calibrated on run 5 this session: 1.10 s/pt at a 1.0 s dwell ⇒ 0.10 s measured overhead; carried at 2.0 s/pt): 5985 pts ≈ 3.3 h + 133 rows × 2.9 s ≈ 0.1 h + 9.3 T traverse / 0.137 T/min ≈ 1.1 h, plus a ~5 min bare ramp 0 → 0.7 T to position. **Direction is up** because the magnet sits at 0 T — a descending fan would pay a 73 min unmeasured ramp first. Ends **at 10 T with the gates at n = 3.20**, which is where the LK mass ladder (investigation Verify) rides the field back down.

**Preconditions the cell prints before it moves anything:** T_sample 1.0–1.5 K, V_si = 0, gates at 0, nplci = 1/1, τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB (the dwell is priced against it), PS heater + output on. **Checkpoints:** first 3 rows (rail, leakage, signal level, field tracking setpoints); then a per-column FFT on the landed rows at ~2 T and ~5 T — abandon if any column clips, if the leak guard trips (>1 nA either gate → interrupt, gates parked at 0), or if no tone clears the noise floor by 2 T.
