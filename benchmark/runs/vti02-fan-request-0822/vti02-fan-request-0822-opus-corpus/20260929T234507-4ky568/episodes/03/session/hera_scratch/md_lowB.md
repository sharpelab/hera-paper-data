## HERA — Step 4a: low-field extension at n = +2.0×10¹² (`sdh-spot-check`) — pins the Landau fan's `B_lo`

**Where the session is.** Runs 6/7 are analysed (ana). The **electron side is decisively the side to fan**: at n = +3.2×10¹² the record carries a dominant tone at **7.25 T** against F_L = 33.09 T — a partner fraction of **0.219**, where the FS-count model puts the ABAB light branch at **7.31 T / 0.221** at scale 1.0 (0.8 % off) and the trilayer ML+BL partner at 1.92 T / 0.058. The rest of the run-6 peak set (14.1, 22.1, 29.4 T) sits on the 2×/3×/4× comb of that fundamental, and the ABAB heavy branch predicted at **25.8 T** is *not* resolved. The hole side is capped by the TG limit at n = −1.2×10¹², inside the model's near-CNP semimetal regime (pair 5.86/6.54 T, separation 0.68 T against δF = 0.77 T — unresolvable by construction), and its 6.98 T peak is exactly that merged pair. So: fan the electron side, and the fan's job is the **heavy-branch-vs-harmonic-comb** discrimination across density.

**Why this 25-minute cell first, before a ~4.3 h fan.** `landau-fan` step 1 makes `B_lo` the measured onset, *computed* — and run 6's `sdh_onset` returns **`censored`**: the 7.1 T tone is still above threshold at the record's low edge, so 1.0 T is a lower bound, not an onset (below 1 T run 6's grid is 50 mT, which aliases a 7 T tone at 0.3–2.4 samples/period — that leg cannot pin anything). The recipe's own remedy is a cheap low-field extension, and taking it at the fan's **lowest** density is what makes it load-bearing: n_lo is the binding column for both the partner-period count and the onset. Committing a 4.3 h fan on a guessed `B_lo` is the failure this costs 25 min to avoid. It also buys an independent second F(n) point — the ABAB light branch at n = +2.0×10¹² should sit at **3.55 T** (trilayer ML+BL: 0.55 T; ABCA: nothing there at all).

**Grid, by the recipe's arithmetic.** `sdh-spot-check` step 1, *blocking rate-limited magnet* branch → uniform in **B** (not 1/B; the points ride the traverse for free here, unlike the fan where every row costs a whole n-line): ΔB = Δ(1/B)·B_lo², Δ(1/B) = 1/(2·F_L) = 0.0242 /T at F_L(2.0×10¹²) = 20.68 T → 4.9 mT; taking **4.5 mT** puts Nyquist F_max at 22.2 T, above F_L. 234 points over 0.45 → 1.50 T, δF = 1/(1/0.45 − 1/1.5) = 0.64 T. Dwell = the magnet's 3 s blocking settle = 10τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB — the settling floor, not a bend (this recipe's Verify: under-settling buries the small tone, which here *is* the target). Sensitivity **10 µV = 1 kΩ FS**, sized off the bracketing measurements below 1.5 T (run 6 ≤ 25 Ω, run 7 ≤ 136 Ω) — the 50 µV of runs 6/7 would sit this band at 0.4–3 % of range, in the coarse-ADC zone that grains an FFT.

**What it decides.** `sdh_onset(R, B).onset_b` at n = +2.0×10¹² → the fan's `B_lo`, and with it the fan's row count. Planned fan, already priced against this session's own measured rates (gatemap run 5: 0.10 s/pt read overhead at `fast_delay=1.0`; run 6: 3 s settle + ΔB/0.137 T·min⁻¹ per field step): **n = +2.0 → +3.2×10¹² at D = 0** (the window where the ABAB heavy branch crosses the 4th harmonic of the light one near n = 2.6×10¹² and separates by ≈ 3 T at both ends — the crossing is the discriminator), **B_lo → 10.0 T uniform in 1/B**, ≈ 124 rows × 45 columns ≈ **4.3 h** at B_lo = 0.85 T. If the onset comes back above ~1.0 T, n_lo rises and the window narrows — that is exactly the fact this cell is buying.

**Preconditions it asserts** (bring-up state, replayed at the handoff): T_sample 1.0–1.5 K, |B| < 0.05 T, V_si = 0, both heater ranges 0, PS heater + output on. Gates are ramped by the cell, not asserted. **Checkpoints:** first ~20 points (signal present, no rail against the 1 kΩ FS, leakage, field tracking); then the read at completion. Gate-leakage trip at 1 nA on either Keithley, as in runs 6/7. Ends at B = 1.50 T with the gates at the n = +2.0×10¹² spot — the fan then steps down to its `B_lo` and runs upward to 10 T, so the ladder never re-traverses.
