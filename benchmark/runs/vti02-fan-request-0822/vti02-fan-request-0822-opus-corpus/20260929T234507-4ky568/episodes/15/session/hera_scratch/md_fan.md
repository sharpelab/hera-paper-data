## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, D = 0, n = +1.00 → +3.20×10¹², B 1.0 → 6.0 T

**Why this side, this window.** Spot checks read (ana): run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹²) is the clean side — one fundamental at **7.3 T** with a harmonic comb (2×/3×/4× at 14.8 / 22.3 / 29.4 T), SNR 96–127, oscillations still above threshold at the low edge of the well-sampled record. Run 7 (hole, n = −1.2×10¹²) is the near-CNP semimetal regime the investigation warns about — tones close and unbalanced (4.7 / 6.6 / 9.4 / 12.3 T against F_L = 12.4 T), onset 0.71 T, no clean partition. **The electron read is already the campaign's central fact:** F_L(+3.2×10¹²) = 33.1 T, and the forward model (`hera/models/tetralayer_fs_count/fs_count_b0.py`, u_D = 0) puts the **ABAB light branch at 7.31 T** and the heavy at 25.78 T at that exact density, against an **ABCA singleton at 33.1 T**. Measured 7.3 T vs predicted 7.31 T with **no density rescale**. But one density is not a call: the heavy partner was not resolved (25.8 T sits between the comb's 3rd and 4th members at 21.9 / 29.2 T), so the Luttinger sum is untested and a lone tone at 0.22·F_L is exactly what the investigation refuses to accept on a single column.

**What the fan decides.** Over n = 1.0 → 3.2×10¹² the two templates are never within a factor 4 of each other (model): ABCA predicts **one** tone tracking F_L, 10.3 → 33.1 T, linear through the origin; ABAB predicts **two** — a heavy branch 9.8 → 25.8 T (≈0.95·F_L at the low edge, falling to 0.78) and a light branch 0.6 → 7.3 T that only emerges above ~1.5×10¹². So the signature is a *crossover*: near-singleton at ≈F_L at low n, a slow second tone appearing and the main tone dropping below F_L at high n. Per-column F(n) plus the partition sum f₁+f₂ = F_L is the read (investigation Steps 4–5). The window deliberately spans the **low-electron-density exception regime** (n ≲ 2×10¹², where ABAB legitimately mimics an ABCA singleton) into the discriminating region — the trajectory through it is the evidence, not any one column. n_max = +3.20×10¹² is the D = 0 line's TG-limited ceiling (V_tg = +0.9836, 16 mV under the limit; same point as run 6, so the fan's top column is directly comparable to the spot check).

**Design (cost-floor, computed not eyeballed).** `B_lo` = 1.0 T: `sdh_onset` reports the 7.3 T tone **censored at the record's low edge** with SNR 96 — the true onset is below 1.0 T, but run 6's sub-1 T leg was sampled at 50 mT (≤2 pts/period, aliased) so 1.0 T is the highest *measured* lower bound; buying B_lo = 0.6 T would cost 3× the rows (ΔB ∝ B_lo²) for 1.8× δF, and is declined. Δ(1/B) inherited from the spot check = **9.90×10⁻³ /T** at the low edge → ΔB = 10 mT uniform in B (blocking magnet), F_Nyquist = **50.5 T** vs F_L(n_max) = 33.1 T. **N_B = 501.** n-axis: Δn = 0.044×10¹² = **2.20 points per LL ray** at B_lo (g = 4, ≥2 floor — ray-structure claims below ~1.5 T are floored, not claimed). **N_n = 51.** `B_hi` = 6.0 T: δF = 1/(1/1.0 − 1/6.0) = **1.20 T**, which puts the heavy-vs-comb separation at n = 3.2 (25.8 vs 21.9/29.2 T) at **3.2 δF**; past 6 T δF is flat (1.11 T at 10 T) and only LK amplitude is bought, while 7.5–10 T carries the large R feature run 6 found (530 Ω) that corrupts polynomial background removal. 6 T is the floor that delivers the claim.

**Settling — the one setting changed.** `sr0`/`sr1` **τ 0.3 s → 0.1 s** (filter slope unchanged, 24 dB/oct), `fast_delay = 1.0 s` = **10 τ**. At 24 dB/oct a 4-pole filter is only ~57% settled at 4τ, and under-settling *attenuates the oscillation* — it is how a small tone gets buried (`sdh-spot-check` Verify), and the small tone is the whole point here. Holding τ = 0.3 s at its own 10τ floor prices this fan at 22 h; dropping τ holds the settling discipline the spot check was acquired under (its 3 s magnet settle was 10τ) at 1.10 s/pt. Cost: single-sample noise ×1.73. Accepted because the per-column floor is not the limit — the partner claim is a cross-column Onsager-track across 51 columns (investigation: "never a one-column fit"), which recovers ~√51. **Operator: if you'd rather I not touch the front-panel τ, say so and I'll re-price.** `sr1` stays at 50 µV (5 kΩ FS at 9.9 nA) — worst corner is n = 1.0×10¹² × 6 T, where run 5's R(B=0) = 117 Ω and run 6's high-B MR factor (7.6×) put the estimate near 1–2.3 kΩ, ≤ 46% FS; keeping the spot check's range also keeps amplitudes comparable.

**Price** (from this session's own runs, not inherited constants): run 5 measured **1.098 s/pt** at `fast_delay = 1.0` (0.098 s read overhead) and run 6 measured **7.5 s** per 10 mT field step (4.38 s ramp at 0.137 T/min + 3.0 s `settle_s`). 501 × 51 = 25,551 points × 1.10 s = 7.81 h; 501 rows × 10.6 s (field 7.38 + gate flyback 0.98 + 1.2 measured turnaround + slow_delay 1.0) = 1.47 h; positioning 0 → 1.0 T = 7.3 min. **9.4 h computed, ≈10 h realistic** (this session's actuals have run 5–10% over).

**Direction / endpoint.** Magnet sits at 0 T, so the fan runs **upward** and ends at **6 T with the gates at n = +3.20×10¹²** — where the next step (LK mass split per branch, `temperature-dependence-vti` Flavor B, the investigation's Verify) wants them. No re-traverse.
