## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, D = 0, n = +2.0…+3.2×10¹², B 0.7 → 10 T

**Why this fan, and what it decides.** Run 6 (electron spot check, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.09 T) is the sharpest thing on disk. Read on its properly-sampled leg only — B ≥ 1.0 T, uniform 10 mT; the 50 mT leg below 1 T aliases anything above ~6 T and cannot carry a tone claim — it gives a stable two-fundamental set across every cutoff (1.0–2.5 T) and detrend order (bg 3/5/7): **f₁ ≈ 7.3 T (0.22 F_L, snr 96)** and **f₂ ≈ 29.5 T (0.89 F_L, snr 116)**, with 14.8 / 22.3 / 58.6 / 88.4 T as the comb around them. Two readings survive:

- **ABAB pair** — 7.3 + 29.5 = 36.8 T = 1.11·F_L, i.e. a pair closing the Luttinger sum under one global density rescale s = 1.11 (inside the 0.8–1.2 lever-arm band), partition 0.20/0.80 against the model's 0.22/0.78 at this density (model F(n): 7.31 / 25.78 T — the **light tone lands on the model to 1%**).
- **ABCA singleton** — 29.5 T is the single pocket at s = 0.89·F_L, and 7.3 T is then unexplained; or the mirror-image confound, 29.5 ≈ 4 × 7.35 is simply the **4th harmonic** of a 7.3 T fundamental (58.6 ≈ 2×29.4 and 88.4 ≈ 3×29.4 also fit a harmonic ladder on 29.4).

One density cannot separate these — every one of them is a linear-in-n statement. **The n-dependence does**, and that is exactly what this fan buys (investigation Steps 4–5 + the Luttinger-aware harmonic screen): over n = 2.0 → 3.2×10¹² the model's ABAB ratio f₂/f₁ sweeps **4.82 → 3.53**, crossing 4.00 near n = 2.5×10¹²; a genuine harmonic sits at exactly 4.00 at every density; an ABCA singleton tracks 1.00·F_L flat while ABAB's dominant tone drifts 0.828 → 0.779·F_L. Three separable trajectories, one 37-column fan.

**Side.** Electron, and the trade is stated: the **hole** side is the cleaner one by onset (run 7: tones censored-alive at 0.50 T, snr 127, and visibly clean oscillations from ~0.6 T, against ≥ 1.0 T on the electron side), but the D = 0 line only reaches n = −1.27×10¹² there (TG −0.3 V limit) — the whole hole side lies inside the investigation's documented near-CNP exception regime, where the ABAB pair is close and balanced (model: 5.86 / 6.54 T at −1.2×10¹²) and cannot carry the primary call. Only the electron side reaches the |n| ≳ 2–3×10¹² window where the templates separate. Run 7 stays on disk as corroboration, not as the fan.

**Grid — both Nyquist checks, computed.** Δ(1/B) = **7.464×10⁻³ T⁻¹** → f_Nyq = 67.0 T = **2.03 F_L**, deliberately finer than the recipe's 1/(2F_L) = 1.51×10⁻² and 1/(3F_L) = 1.01×10⁻² floors: run 6 carries *real* harmonic content of the 29.4 T tone out to 3f ≈ 88 T (snr 52), and at f_Nyq = 2 F_L its 2f (58.8 T) stays in band while 3f aliases to 45 T — clear of every fundamental. At f_Nyq = F_L the 58.6 T tone would fold onto 7.4 T, i.e. straight onto the tone that decides this. **N_B = 179 rows**, B 0.700 → 10.000 T, uniform in 1/B. n-axis: LL ray spacing at B_lo is Δn = 0.0677×10¹², floor 0.0339 → **N_n = 37** at Δn = 0.0333×10¹² (2.03 pts/ray — the floor, not a choice). Record resolution **δF = 1/(1/B_lo − 1/B_hi) = 0.75 T**, against a 7.3 T tone separation of the ABAB-vs-ABCA dominant-tone gap (25.8 vs 33.1 T) — 10 δF.

**B_lo = 0.7 T, and the bet in it.** Run 6's clean-grid `sdh_onset` returns `onset_b = 1.00 T`, **censored at the record edge** — a lower bound, not a measured die-off — so the recipe's B_lo contract permits going below it. Run 7 pins this device's onset at ≤ 0.50 T at |n| = 1.2×10¹², and the ABAB light pocket is the lightest thing in the model (m* = 0.027 mₑ vs 0.061 heavy), so it is the tone expected to survive lowest. 0.7 T buys the partner tone ≥ 5 periods down to n = 2.2×10¹² (9.7 periods at n = 3.2); at B_lo = 1.0 the partner would only clear 5 periods above n = 2.8×10¹², shrinking the ratio trajectory from a 27% swing to 7% and gutting the read. If the first rows show no content at 0.7–0.9 T the low rows are background and the per-column FFT windows above the fan's own measured onset — recoverable, unlike clipping.
