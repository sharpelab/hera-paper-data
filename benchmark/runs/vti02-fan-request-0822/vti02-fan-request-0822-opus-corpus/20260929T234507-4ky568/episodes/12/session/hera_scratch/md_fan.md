## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, D = 0 — the stacking call

**Why this fan, and why these numbers.** The spot checks (runs 6/7, analysed in ana) settle every design lever the investigation's Step 4 asks for. **Side:** electron. Run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹²) tracks its carrying tone down to the record's low edge (`sdh_onset`: onset_b = 0.30 T, *censored* — oscillations persist below the record), with tone SNRs 40–100; run 7 (hole, n = −1.2×10¹²) onsets at 0.71 T and the hole side of the D = 0 line reaches only −1.27×10¹², inside the near-CNP exception regime the investigation says to design *around*. **What run 6 already shows:** a pair at **7.1 T and 22.2 T** plus a tone at **29.5 T ≈ their sum** — the ABAB template (model: 7.31 / 25.78 T at this density) with the symmetry-allowed inter-block **breakdown sum-tone** sitting where an ABCA singleton would. The B-window split already leans that way (low half 1.0–1.8 T: 6.7 T dominant; high half: the 29 T tone becomes co-dominant — a breakdown tone strengthens with B, a real pocket keeps its own Dingle envelope). The pair sums to 29.3 T against F_L = 33.1 T, i.e. a global density rescale of 0.885 — inside the lever-arm prior's 0.8–1.2 band, and exactly the scale the fan must pin on the *whole* peak set across density rather than at one point. **So the fan's job is Step 5**: f_s(n) + f_b(n) = F_L(n) across density (algebraic, model-free), the branch trajectories against the ABAB curves *and* the trilayer ML+BL curves (mandatory dual overlay), and the F_L-tone origin.

**Window, computed not eyeballed.** n = **2.00 → 3.20×10¹²** at D = 0. The top column is set to 3.20 deliberately — identical to run 6's density, so the fan's top column **reproduces run 6 on a different sampling grid** (uniform-1/B here vs uniform-B there), which is the investigation's grid-change reproduction check for free. The low edge is the investigation's own design floor (|n| ≳ 2–3×10¹², where the templates separate); over 2.00–3.20 the model families are cleanly apart — ABAB partner fraction 0.172 → 0.215 vs trilayer ML+BL 0.027 → 0.053, a 4–6× gap, and ABAB f_s runs 3.55 → 6.67 T against trilayer 0.55 → 1.65 T. **B = 0.65 → 10 T.** B_lo is *not* the raw onset: the onset is censored at 0.30 T, so it is only a lower bound, and here the binding lever is the period count on the ABAB partner at the fan's lowest density — f_s(2.0×10¹²) = 3.55 T needs (1/B_lo − 1/B_hi) ≥ 5/3.55, giving B_lo ≤ 0.66 T. Run 6 tracks tones well below 0.65 T, so no low-field extension is needed to commit. Resolution δF = 1/(1/0.65 − 1/10) = **0.70 T**, against a heavy-tone-vs-F_L separation of 3.55 T at the low edge (2δF = 1.39 T) — the discrimination the whole call rests on clears by 2.6×.

**Both-axis Nyquist (asserted in the cell, not just claimed).** B-axis: Δ(1/B) = 0.00999 1/T = **3.0 samples/period** at F_L(3.20×10¹²) = 33.1 T — the recipe's preferred 1/3, and the same Δ(1/B) run 6 ran at its low-field edge, so the fan inherits the spot check's sampling. n-axis: Δn = 0.0308×10¹² against a ray spacing of 0.0629×10¹² at B_lo — 2.04 points per ray, just over the ≥2 floor. **Stated coverage trade:** sampling only to Nyquist/3 leaves harmonic content above 50 T aliased — in run 6 the 3rd-harmonic content near 91 T would fold to ≈ 9 T at the top columns. Two screens kill it and both are already mandatory: an alias's frequency *falls* with n while every real pocket's *rises* along its Onsager curve, and the top column is checkable against run 6's oversampled record. Buying that margin outright means halving Δ(1/B) — 287 rows, ≈ 10 h — which is not worth it for a confound the required n-dependence screen resolves.

**Settling and sensitivity, inherited.** fast_delay = 2.1 s = 7τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB/oct — the settling floor, non-negotiable here because the target *is* a weak tone (the partner) and under-settling buries exactly that. The cell reads τ and the filter slope and asserts the dwell against the floor rather than trusting this note. Sensitivity stays at run 6's 50 µV (5 kΩ FS at 10 nA): the worst corner is low-n × high-B, and this session's own numbers put it at R(n = 2.0×10¹², B = 0) = 72 Ω (run 5) × MR 6.5 (run 6, measured) ≈ 470 Ω = 9 % of full scale — 38 % even at MR 20. Headroom over precision; clipping is the failure no windowing recovers, and run 6 delivered SNR 40–100 at this setting.

**Preconditions and geometry to eyeball before this runs:** T_sample at base (1.0–1.5 K), field near 0 (the cell ramps up to 0.65 T itself, ~4.7 min), V_si = 0, gate nplci 1/1, PS heater on and output enabled, and a live re-check that the SAFETIES validators and the 10 T field cap are still the committed numbers. The n-line tops out at V_tg = +0.9836 V, 16 mV under the +1.0 V limit — same point run 6 sat at. The leak guard trips on **two consecutive** points over 1 nA rather than one, so the ~200 pA row-flyback transient (run 5) can't kill a 5-hour fan; sustained leakage still stops it within two points.

**Direction and endpoint:** ascending, so the 9.35 T traverse *is* the measurement rather than a dead ramp (the magnet is at 0 now), and the fan **ends at 10 T** with the gates parked at 0 — where the next step, the LK mass split (Verify: per-branch masses, the decisive F_L-tone-origin test), wants to start before riding the field back down. Note for the operator: the magnet is left at 10 T on completion, deliberately.

**Checkpoints** (a background `hera-watch` digest carries all three): **first 2 rows** (~2 min, B ≈ 0.65 T) — R ≈ 70–90 Ω across the n-line, nothing railed, leakage under 200 pA, field readback tracking setpoints, T at base; abort there if any fail. **Row ~70** (B ≈ 1.18 T, ≈ 2.4 h) — partial per-column FFT on the landed rows (δF ≈ 1.5 T there): the partner tone must be present at the *low-n* columns, since its onset at n = 2.0×10¹² is extrapolated from run 6's higher density rather than measured. If there is no oscillation content at the low-n edge by then, the low-n half of the fan is uninformative and I stop and re-plan rather than spend the remaining 2.5 h. **Row ~110** (B ≈ 2.2 T, ≈ 3.8 h) — the full tone set against F_L, continue/adjust call.
