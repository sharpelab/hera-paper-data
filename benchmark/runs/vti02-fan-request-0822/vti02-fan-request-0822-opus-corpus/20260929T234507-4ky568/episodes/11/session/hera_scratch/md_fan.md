## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, D = 0, n = +3.2 → +1.6×10¹², B 0.65 → 10 T

**Side choice (spot checks, runs 6 and 7 — ana notebook).** Run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T) is the clean side: `sdh_onset` gives **onset ≤ 0.30 T** (censored at the record's low edge — the 7.1 T tone is still above threshold there, so 0.30 T is a *lower bound*, not a measured die-off), tracked tones **7.1 / 14.1 / 22.1 / 29.5 T** with SNR 40–90, and the dominant tone in the 3–10 T window sits at ~30 T, just under F_L. Run 7 (hole, n = −1.2×10¹², F_L = 12.4 T) is the weaker side on every axis: onset 0.71 T, a crowded set (4.7 / 6.6 / 8.5 / 9.4 / 12.3 / 14.8 T) in exactly the near-CNP semimetal regime the investigation flags as the hole-side exception, and the D = 0 line is TG-limited to |n| ≤ 1.27×10¹² — below the |n| ≳ 2–3×10¹² band where the templates separate. **Coverage trade, stated:** this fan buys only the electron side; a matching hole-side fan is not affordable within the gate window (it cannot reach the design density at all), so the call will be electron-side-only and hedged as such.

**What the fan has to decide.** Run 6's peak set is genuinely ambiguous on one density: {7.1, 14.1, 22.1, 29.5} reads either as (a) the **ABAB pair** — partner 7.1 + heavy 29.5 ≈ 36.6 T against F_L = 33.1 (a 1.11 global density rescale, inside the lever-arm prior; model pair at n = 3.2 is 7.31 + 25.78) with 14.1/22.1 as harmonics, or (b) a **harmonic comb** of one ~7.4 T fundamental, or (c) an **ABCA singleton** near F_L with the low tones from elsewhere (the run-5 ridge-B mosaic sits 0.43×10¹² away). A ratio-only harmonic screen cannot separate these here: the model's ABAB ratio at our densities is **3.5–6.1**, i.e. it runs right through 4×. The discriminator is the **trajectory across density**, which is what this fan measures: the model's heavy/partner ratio is **6.06 → 4.82 → 4.07 → 3.53** at n = 1.6 / 2.0 / 2.5 / 3.2 — crossing 4.00 near **n ≈ 2.45** — while a harmonic comb stays pinned at exactly 4.00 at every column, and an ABCA singleton stays at F_L with no partner. Plus the model-independent check: f₁(n) + f₂(n) = F_L(n) across 53 columns under **one** global scale.

**Design (cost-floor, both-axis Nyquist — `hera_scratch/fan_design.py`).** `B_lo = 0.65 T` is set by the binding period-count case, not by the onset: the ABAB partner at the **n = 2.0 claim floor** is 3.55 T, and ≥ 5 periods needs a 1/B span ≥ 1.41 T⁻¹ → B_lo ≤ 0.663 T. Dropping B_lo to the censored 0.30 T onset would cost 324 rows × 86 columns ≈ 25 h for resolution the claim does not need. `B_hi = 10 T` (session cap) — past ~6 T it adds almost no 1/B record (7 rows) but carries the high-B half of the **F_L-tone origin** split and the strongest tones. **Δ(1/B) = 1/(4·F_L(n_max)) = 0.00756 T⁻¹** (f_Nyq 66 T), one notch finer than the recipe's 1/3: run 6 carries real 60 T and 90 T harmonic content, and re-sampling run 6 onto both candidate grids (`hera_scratch/alias_test.py`) shows the 1/3 grid manufacturing 38–42 T peaks the dense record does not have, while the 1/4 grid reproduces it. → **191 B rows**; **53 n columns** is the ray-Nyquist floor at B_lo (2.04 points per LL ray, 31 at 10 T). Record resolution **δF = 0.70 T** — ample against the 3.5 T ABAB-heavy-vs-F_L gap at the claim floor; the one place it cannot resolve is the 4×-crossing at n ≈ 2.45, where the trajectory on either side carries the read. Sensitivity **inherited from the spot checks at 50 µV** (5 kΩ FS at 9.9 nA): worst corner is low-n × high-B, n = 1.6×10¹² at 10 T ≈ 1.0–1.3 kΩ (83 Ω at B = 0 from run 5 × the ~10–15× MR of run 6) → ~4× headroom, and clipping is the asymmetric failure. Dwell **2.1 s = 7τ**, the settling floor at the operator-set τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB/oct — non-negotiable here because the target *is* the weak partner tone.

**Price (calibrated on this session's runs 6/7: dt = ΔB/0.137 T·min⁻¹ + 3 s settle + 0.08 s reads).** 191 × 53 = 10,123 points at 2.23 s = 6.3 h grid + 68 min magnet traverse + 5 min row turnaround ≈ 7.5 h; headline **≈ 8 h** with this session's observed 10% overage, plus a 5 min pre-position ramp to 0.65 T. Ends at **10 T with the gates at the n = 1.6×10¹² end** — the LK mass ladder (investigation Verify) then rides the magnet down rather than re-traversing.

**Preconditions the cell prints before moving anything:** T_sample 1.0–1.5 K, magnet parked at |B| < 0.05 T, V_si = 0, PS heater + output on, thermometry logfile advancing, and sr1 at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB (the dwell is priced against it). **Checkpoints** (`hera-watch` armed on the run dir): first row — leakage, rails, sr1_X amplitude, row layout; ~1 h in (rows ≲ 60, B ≲ 1.1 T) — per-point noise and SdH visibility on the high-n columns; ~4 h in (row ~120, B ≈ 2 T) — partial per-column FFT, the go/no-go: the partner and heavy tones should be resolved by then. Abort criteria: leak trip (|I_gate| > 1 nA, guarded in-cell), any channel railing above ~4 kΩ, or no oscillation content on the top-n columns by B = 2 T (which would contradict run 6 and mean a device/contact regression, not a fan worth finishing).
