## HERA — Step 4 precursor: low-field onset extension (`sdh-spot-check`), n = +3.2×10¹², B 0.40 → 1.05 T

**Spot-check read (runs 6, 7 — ana cells to follow).** Electron side, n = +3.2×10¹² (run 6, F_L = 33.1 T): clean SdH with a tracked tone set **7.3 / 22.3 / 29.4 T** (matched-filter SNR 96 / 46 / 116) plus a harmonic ladder of the 7.3 T tone. The model's F(n) table (`hera/models/tetralayer_fs_count/`) puts the **ABAB pair at 7.31 / 25.78 T** at this density and the **ABCA singleton at 33.1 T** — 7.3 T lands on the model partner, and 7.3 + 22.3 = 29.6 T closes the Luttinger sum at a global scale of 0.90 (inside the 0.8–1.2 lever-arm band), which makes the 29.4 T tone a **sum/breakdown candidate**, not a singleton. That is exactly the confound the investigation calls expected for ABAB, and it is **one density** — the fan's F(n) trajectory + partition-vs-n is what decides it, so no call is being made here. Hole side (run 7, n = −1.2×10¹², F_L = 12.4 T): higher onset (0.71 T), a messier set (4.7 / 6.6 / 8.5 / 9.4 / 12.3 T) and — decisively — the TG limit caps |n| at 1.27×10¹², **inside** the investigation's low-|n| exception regime where ABAB mimics ABCA. **The fan goes on the electron side.**

**Why this 20-minute cell before a ~3 h fan.** `landau-fan` step 1 makes `B_lo` the first cost lever and requires it be *computed* from `sdh_onset`, not eyeballed. Run 6's `onset_b` is **censored**: the 7.3 T tone is still above threshold at the low edge of its *well-sampled* record (1.0 T), and run 6's sub-1 T leg was taken at 50 mT — Δ(1/B) ≈ 0.5 1/T there, aliased for every tone in play, so the record simply never captured the onset. The recipe's named remedy is a cheap low-field extension. It matters because the 1/B span is what resolves the **partner** tone, and the partner is the claim: at B_lo = 1.0 T the span is 0.90 1/T and the partner clears 5 periods only for n ≳ 2.6×10¹² (top ~4 columns); at B_lo = 0.6 T the span is 1.57 1/T and it clears from n ≳ 1.9×10¹² — the difference between a partition check at three densities and one across half the fan. Buying that blind would cost ~40–130 min of fan rows that may sit below onset; buying it here costs ~20 min.

**Acquisition** — same (n, D) spot as run 6, re-derived from the run-5 calibration; **uniform in 1/B at Δ(1/B) = 0.0101 1/T** (run 6's spacing at its 1 T low edge, Nyquist ceiling 50 T ≫ F_L = 33.1 T), 154 points, `delay = 0` so each point's dwell is the magnet's blocking ramp + 3 s settle = 10τ. **Sensitivity 20 µV** (808 Ω full scale at 9.9 nA): R over this window is 8–57 Ω (measured, run 6) → 7 % FS with 14× rail headroom, against 1.1 % FS at run 6's 50 µV — the low-field record is digitization-limited at the fan's sensitivity and the onset is precisely what this cell must measure. The 1.00–1.05 T overlap with run 6 is the stitch check.

**Then the fan** (committed, one design — lands next, with `B_lo` the only number this cell sets): D = 0, **n = +3.2 → +1.2×10¹² in 43 columns** (2.0 points per LL ray at 1 T), B up to the 10 T session cap, slow axis uniform in 1/B in two legs — 0.0101 1/T up to 3 T (Nyquist 50 T, covers everything present below 3 T) and **0.0040 1/T above** (Nyquist 125 T, so the anharmonic harmonic ladder that appears above ~3 T — 2nd–5th harmonics at 59/87/118/147 T, up to 17 % amplitude — cannot fold into the 5–35 T claim band). Sensitivity 50 µV for the fan's worst corner (n = 1.2×10¹² × 10 T ≈ 1.0–1.5 kΩ ⇒ ~30 % FS), τ dropped to 0.1 s with a 1.0 s dwell (10τ, 1 % residual) to keep the fan near 3 h. Checkpoints: first rows, then per-column FFTs on the landed low-B leg.
