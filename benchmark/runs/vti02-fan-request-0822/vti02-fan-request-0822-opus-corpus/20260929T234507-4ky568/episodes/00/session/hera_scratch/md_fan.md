## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, D = 0, n = +0.80 → +3.20×10¹², B 1.2 → 10 T

**What runs 6/7 say** (scratch `spot_fft4.py`; ana cells land while this runs). Run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T) carries a genuine **two-tone** SdH set: a dominant periodicity whose R-minima ladder is uniform in 1/B — minima at B = 2.72, 3.30, 3.69, 4.26, 4.98, 6.00, 7.46 T, Δ(1/B) = 0.0334 ± 0.0015 → **F = 29.9 ± 1 T** (FFT peak 29.5 T) — plus a resolved partner at **7.4 T**; 14.8 / 22.2 / 36.9 / 59 T are all accounted for as harmonics (2f₁, 2f₂) and intermodulation (f₂ ∓ f₁). Per-tone tracked onsets in the well-sampled leg (`sdh_onset`): **1.36–1.8 T**; below 1 T run 6's 50 mT leg under-samples 1/B (Nyquist 2.8 T at 0.5 T), so its `onset_b = 0.3 T` is a sampling artifact, not a measurement, and is not used. Run 7 (hole, n = −1.2×10¹²) is exactly the near-CNP semimetal regime the investigation flags — model pair (5.86, 6.54) T, unresolvable at δF ≈ 1.5 T; **the electron side is the cleaner side**, and it is where the templates separate.

**Why a fan and not a call from run 6.** One column is genuinely ambiguous three ways. Model (`fs_count_b0`, the Step-2 design authority) at n = +3.2×10¹²: **ABAB** (7.31, 25.78) T summing to F_L; **ABCA** singleton 33.08 T; **ABCB** (2.75, 30.33) T. Measured (7.4, 29.5) matches ABAB's partner *and* ABCB's heavy, and sums to 36.9 T — 11% over F_L, inside the lever-arm prior (0.8–1.2) but not decidable within one column. Worse, {7.4, 22.2, 29.5} is self-consistent two ways: fundamentals (7.4, 29.5) with 22.2 = f₂ − f₁, or fundamentals (7.4, 22.2) with 29.5 = f₁ + f₂. **Only n-dependence decides** — a real pocket follows its own Onsager curve, an intermodulation tone tracks f₂ ∓ f₁ exactly, a harmonic tracks k·f. 65 columns settle it.

**The primary discriminator is rescale-invariant.** ABCA's F(n) is exactly linear through the origin (F/n = 10.34 T per 10¹² at every density); ABAB's heavy branch is sub-linear — F/n runs 10.4 → 9.77 → 8.57 → 8.06 across n = 0.8 → 1.0 → 2.0 → 3.2×10¹², a 22% bend. A global density rescale multiplies F/n uniformly and **cannot create or remove that curvature**. Measured at n = 3.2 gives F/n = 9.22, between the two templates — the trajectory across 65 columns is the read, and it does not wait on the partition.

**Design — Step 2 numbers, computed.**
- **Fast axis** n = +0.80 → +3.20×10¹² at D = 0, **N_n = 65**, Δn = 0.0375×10¹². Ray-Nyquist at B_lo: Δn_ray = (4e/h)·B_lo = 0.116×10¹² → **3.10 pts/ray** (floor 2, preferred 3). n_max = +3.20 is the D = 0 line's electron end inside the TG limit (V_tg = +0.984, 16 mV of margin); n_min = +0.80 keeps R modest (144 Ω at 0 T, run 5) and anchors the trajectory where both templates converge.
- **Slow axis** B uniform in **1/B**, 1.2 → 10.0 T, **N_B = 93**, Δ(1/B) = 0.00797 → Nyquist **62.7 T**. Clears F_L = 33.1 T on the preferred 1/3 bound (0.0101) and keeps the observed 59 T second harmonic unaliased.
- **Resolution** δF = 1/(1/B_lo − 1/B_max) = **1.36 T**; the 7.4 / 29.5 T separation is 22 T ≫ 2δF.
- **B_lo = 1.2 T** sits just under the measured 1.36 T tracked onset. It is not pushed lower: sub-onset rows are background, not signal, and a lower B_lo inflates both the 1/B span and the n-axis Nyquist (cost ∝ 1/B_lo²).
- **Sensitivity 100 µV = 10 kΩ FS** at I = 10 nA, fixed for the whole fan. Worst corner is n_min × B_max: R(n = +0.8×10¹², 0 T) = 144 Ω (run 5 at D = 0), and run 7's comparable |n| peaked at 576 Ω over 0.3–10 T → expect ≲ 1 kΩ, ≥ 10× headroom. The trade is noise: σ ≈ 6 Ω here vs 3.1 Ω at 50 µV (run 6) — accepted, because a railed column is flat-topped and unrecoverable by any B-window, and the clip would land exactly where the SdH signal is strongest.
- **`fast_delay` = 1.2 s = 4τ** (τ = 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct), not the base recipe's 6τ. Rationale: the extracted axis here is B (per-column FFT along the **slow** axis), and every column is acquired through the identical lag in the identical sweep direction, so the residual settling is a common-mode n-label shift of ≈ 0.026×10¹² (≈ 1%), not a per-column distortion. 6τ would add 1.0 h for that 1%. `slow_delay = 3 s` after the gate flyback, where the jump is large and the lag is *not* common-mode with the rest of the row.

**Coverage trade, stated.** The **partition** read (f₁ + f₂ = F_L, Step 5 item 1) needs ≥ 5 periods of the partner in the 1/B window: 5/0.733 = 6.8 T, so it is valid only for **n ≳ 3.0×10¹²** (model), marginal (3.7 periods) down to ≈ 2.5×10¹², and resolution-limited below — the low-|n| ABAB-mimics-ABCA exception regime lives there anyway. Buying it at lower n means B_lo below the measured onset, which buys record length with no signal. The **heavy-branch trajectory** and the **n-dependence tone screen**, which are the primary evidence, are readable across all 65 columns.

**Price.** 93 × 65 = 6045 pts at ≈ 1.32 s/pt (run 5 calibration: 1.098 s/pt at `fast_delay` = 1.0, + one extra gate write) = 2.2 h; field 8.8 T at 0.137 T/min = 64 min + 93 × 3 s driver settle; rows 93 × (3 s + ≈ 1.9 s flyback) = 7.6 min; positioning 0 → 1.2 T = 9 min. ≈ 3 h 35 m nominal, quoted at **3 h 50 m** on this session's over-run margin. **Ends at 10 T with the gates parked at 0** — the LK mass split (investigation Verify) then rides the magnet down rather than re-traversing.

**Preconditions the cell asserts** (assumed, not set): T_sample 1.0–1.5 K with the logfile advancing, |B| < 0.05 T (the handoff left it at +0.003 T), V_si = 0, PS heater on and output enabled, gate `nplci` = 1. **Leak guard** interrupts on |I_tg| or |I_bg| > 1 nA; the row-start flyback transient runs ±200 pA (run 5) and sits well under it. A T excursion above 1.6 K prints a warning but does not abort — T_sample is recorded per point, so the field-corrected read is post-hoc.

**Checkpoints** (armed `hera-watch`, monitor plots every live channel): **first 3 rows** at B ≈ 1.2 T — signal present, no rail, leakage clean, T at base; then at **B ≈ 2.1 T** (row 46, half the 1/B record) and **B ≈ 4.2 T** (row 75, 81%) a per-column FFT on the landed rows — abort if the tone set is absent or the heavy branch fails to track its Onsager curve. Note (rig card): interrupting this cell does **not** stop the supply — it continues to its last setpoint, at most 0.74 T beyond the interrupt and never above the 10 T cap.
