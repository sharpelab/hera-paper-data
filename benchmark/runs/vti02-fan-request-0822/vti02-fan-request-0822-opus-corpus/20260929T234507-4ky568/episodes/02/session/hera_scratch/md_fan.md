## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), D = 0 electron side, n = 0.8 → 3.2×10¹², B = 1 → 10 T

**Where the arc is.** Steps 1–3 of `tetralayer-stacking-fs-count` are done: run 5 → the (n, D) calibration, runs 6/7 → both-sign SdH spot checks. This is Step 4, the fan the stacking call rests on.

**The spot-check read that sizes this fan (runs 6/7, ana cells to follow).** Both spot checks used a coarse low-field leg (50 / 20 mT below 1 T) whose 1/B Nyquist ceiling is only 1.0–2.7 T (run 6) / 2.4–6.5 T (run 7) — every tone a naive full-record read calls "censored at the 0.30 T edge" lives in that aliased band. Re-read on the **well-sampled sub-record (B ≥ 1 T)**:

- **Run 6** (n = +3.20×10¹², F_L = 33.09 T): a single fundamental at **7.3 T** (SNR 96, still above threshold at 1.0 T) carrying a harmonic comb — 14.8 / 22.3 / 29.4 / 43.7 / 58.6 / 88.4 / 130 T at 2×, 3×, 4×, 6×, 8×, 12×, 18×. **No independent tone at F_L**, and none at the ABAB heavy position (25.8 T) that survives the harmonic screen at this resolution.
- **Run 7** (n = −1.20×10¹², F_L = 12.41 T): fundamental **7.1 T** plus an independent **12.6 T** ≈ F_L, and 18.9 T.

**The flag this fan exists to settle.** 7.3 T (run 6) and 7.1 T (run 7) are the same frequency within δF ≈ 1.1 T, at densities differing by 2.7× **and of opposite sign**. A Fermi surface cannot do that — F must track its own Onsager curve. Two readings, and they are not close in consequence:

1. **ABAB light pocket.** The forward model (`tetralayer_fs_count/fs_count_b0.py`, u_D = 0) puts the ABAB light tone at **7.31 T** at n = +3.2×10¹² — dead on the measured 7.3 T, with the heavy partner at 25.78 T (0.78 F_L). Then run 7's 7.1 T is a near-CNP hole-side coincidence (that side sits in the semimetal-overlap regime where the model gives 4.4 / 5.9 T and the partition is not yet resolved).
2. **A gate-independent parasitic.** The device card is explicit that the graphite back gate **screens Si from the channel — Si couples only to the contacts**. With V_si = 0 the contact regions sit at a *fixed* density no (V_tg, V_bg) pairing moves, so a contact-region orbit would give exactly this: one frequency, unchanged across density and carrier sign.

The fan discriminates directly and cheaply, because the two hypotheses diverge maximally across precisely the window proposed: the model's ABAB light tone runs **0.02 → 7.31 T** from n = 0.8 → 3.2×10¹², while a contact tone sits **flat at ~7.2 T**. Per-column F(n) over 61 columns separates a monotone Onsager ramp from a horizontal line with no ambiguity — and it is the same object that delivers the Luttinger partition, the F_L-tone origin split, and the adjacent-N dual overlay the investigation's Verify requires.

**Side and window (no side prior — both spot-checked).** Electron. The hole side of the D = 0 line is gate-limited to |n| ≤ 1.27×10¹², entirely inside the recipe's stated low-|n| exception regime where ABAB and ABCA are not separable; nothing about the hole side's cleanliness can rescue that. `n_max = +3.2×10¹²` is the TG ceiling (V_tg = +0.9836 V, 16 mV under the +1.0 V limit) and also where the templates separate best (ABAB heavy 0.78 F_L vs ABCA 1.00 F_L). `n_min = +0.8×10¹²` is set by the discriminator above (model light tone → 0.02 T, maximum contrast against a flat 7.2 T) and stops short of the CNP, whose low-n × high-B corner is the fan's dynamic-range worst case.

**Price: ≈ 5 h 45 m.** 122 × 61 = 7442 pts × 1.898 s (1.8 s dwell + 0.098 s measured read overhead, calibrated on run 5's 1.098 s at `fast_delay = 1.0`) = 3.92 h, + 1.09 h field traverse (9 T at 0.137 T/min), + 0.27 h row turnaround (slow_delay 3 s + gate flyback ~1.9 s + magnet settle 3 s), + ~10 % honesty margin. Starts where the magnet sits (0 T → 1 T positioning, ~7 min, blocking) and **ends at 10 T with the gates at n = +3.2×10¹²** — where the Verify LK mass split wants to begin. No re-traverse.

**Checkpoints** (`hera-watch data --next --follow` armed before the cell): **(1) first rows** at B ≈ 1.0 T — R along the n-line must land on run 5's D = 0 values (145 Ω at n = 0.8 → 56 Ω at n = 3.2), both gate columns moving, |I_gate| < 1 nA, T at base, no clipping. **(2) B ≈ 3 T** (~1/3 in): partial per-column FFT on the landed rows — δF is already 1.5 T there, enough to see whether the ~7 T tone **disperses with n or sits flat**. That is the go/no-go: a flat tone means the dominant oscillation is not the channel's and the fan's premise needs rethinking, and it is worth aborting at ~2 h rather than 6. **(3) B ≈ 6 T**: full interim FFT, tone count and partition against F_L.

**Coverage trade, stated.** This fan buys one D (= 0), one side (electron), and 2.4×10¹² of density. Excluded: the hole side (gate-limited into the non-discriminating regime — no fan there can pay), the CNP columns (dynamic-range corner; they would also give the ν = 8/12/16 layer-number ladder of Step 6, which is a *weak* complement by the investigation's own weighting and does not discriminate stacking), and any D ≠ 0 (off the operating point). Adding the CNP columns would cost ~1.7 h and risk clipping the rest.
