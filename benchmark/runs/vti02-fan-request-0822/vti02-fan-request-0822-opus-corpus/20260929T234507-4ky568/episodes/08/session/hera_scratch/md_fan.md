## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, D = 0, n = +2.10 … +3.22×10¹², B 0.7 → 7.5 T

**Why this fan, and what it has to settle.** The two spot checks are now read (ana, runs 6/7). Run 6 (electron, n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T) is excellent data — `sdh_onset` tracks a dominant tone at **7.2–7.3 T** with SNR > 100 and a harmonic comb (2×, 3×, 4×, 8×, 12×, 16×), minima reaching R ≈ 0 (fully developed gaps). The model's ABAB **light** branch at that density is **7.31 T** — a direct hit; ABCA's singleton would be one tone **at** F_L = 33.1 T, and there is no fundamental there. So the singleton template is already in trouble, and the live question is the **ABAB partner**: the model puts the heavy tone at **25.8 T** at n = +3.2×10¹², but the measured 22.3 T peak sits at **3.05 × 7.3** — i.e. degenerate with the light tone's 3rd harmonic. This is exactly the confound the investigation names ("the ABAB pair's ratio ≈ 2.6 drifts through 3× with density, and a ratio-only screen eats the real heavy tone exactly there"), and it is **not resolvable at one density**. The n-dependence screen is: a harmonic is locked at 3.00 × f₁ at every n; a real pocket follows its own Onsager curve. Over this fan's window the model's ABAB ratio runs **4.63 → 3.52** while 3× stays 3.00 — the separation f₂ − 3f₁ is **6.3 T at n = 2.10 and 3.8 T at n = 3.22**, against δF = 0.77 T (2δF = 1.54 T). That, plus the Luttinger partition f₁ + f₂ = F_L column by column, is the claim this fan buys.

**Side and window (floored, per `landau-fan` step 1).** Electron side: it is the cleaner side (run 7, hole, caps at |n| = 1.27×10¹² — the investigation's low-|n| exception regime, tones close and balanced, and it read that way) and the only side reaching the design regime |n| ≳ 2–3×10¹². **n_max = +3.22×10¹²** is the gate ceiling, not a choice: at D = 0 the TG limit binds, V_tg = +0.9894 V with 10.6 mV of headroom under the +1.0 V validator. **n_lo = +2.10×10¹²** is set by the partner-resolvability floor — the *light* tone is the small-fraction tone here (0.22·F_L), and 5 periods of the model's f₁ = 3.86 T is exactly what the 1/B record (1.2952 1/T) delivers; below that the pair read dies. **B_lo = 0.70 T**: run 7's *measured* onset is 0.71 T at |n| = 1.2×10¹² (the lower-mobility near-CNP side), and run 6's electron-side tone is still `censored@edge` at 0.90 T. **Honest caveat:** the electron-side onset below 0.9 T is *not* directly measured — run 6's sub-1 T grid was 50 mT, which under-samples a 7.3 T tone below ~0.87 T, so its low-field record cannot be read for onset. The fan's own bottom rows measure it; if they land sub-onset the loss is ~15 rows (~20 min), and the FFT cutoff is a per-column analysis choice afterwards. **B_max = 7.5 T**: where run 6's clean SdH regime ends (above it R climbs to 530 Ω through the ν ≈ 16 structure, and the 8×/12×/16× harmonics switch on). Raising B_max to 10 T changes δF by 0.02 T and costs 18 min of traverse — not bought.

**Both-axis Nyquist + dynamic range.** Δ(1/B) = **0.00989 1/T** = 1/(3·F_L(n_max)) — the recipe's *preferred* 1/3, not the bare Nyquist, because run 6 shows real harmonic content out to 8×f₁ ≈ 58 T; at 1/3 the fold-back sits at 50.6 T and keeps the 26 T heavy-tone band clean. **N_B = 132 rows, uniform in 1/B** (4.9 mT steps at 0.7 T → 518 mT at 7.5 T). n-axis: **N_n = 47**, Δn = 0.0243×10¹² = **2.78 points per LL ray** at B_lo (floor 2; ≥3 above 0.76 T). Sensitivity **inherited from the spot checks, sr1 = 50 µV (5 kΩ FS at 10 nA)** — the worst corner is low-n × high-B, where run 7 (|n| = 1.2×10¹²) peaked at 587 Ω and run 6 at 530 Ω, so 5 kΩ clears it ~8×; the trade is that the signal sits at 2–12 % of range, and the spot checks prove at this exact scale that digitization is not the limit (SNR > 100). Dwell **1.8 s = 6τ** (τ = 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct): the n-axis carries the extracted signal, and the target — the heavy partner — is the weak tone, where under-settling erases rather than lags. Not traded for wall-clock.
