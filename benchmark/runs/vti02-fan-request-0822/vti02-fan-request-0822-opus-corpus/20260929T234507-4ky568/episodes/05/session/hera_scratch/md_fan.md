## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side, D = 0, n = 1.30 → 3.24×10¹², B 0.75 → 10 T

`tetralayer-stacking-fs-count` Step 4 — the fan the stacking call rests on. Per-column SdH FFT in 1/B, read as the peak set against F_L = (h/4e)·n.

**Side (Step 3 read, runs 6 + 7).** Electron. Run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹²) is exceptional data: SdH minima reach ~0 Ω by 2 T, oscillations survive to the record's low edge (`sdh_onset`: carrying tone censored at 0.30 T, SNR ≈ 100), and the FFT over 1–10 T gives a dominant fundamental at **7.8 T = 0.235·F_L** with a harmonic ladder 2×/3×/4× (14.4 / 22.2 / 29.6 T) and content out to ~58 T. That fundamental sits on the model's ABAB **light** branch (7.5 T at n = 3.27×10¹², fraction 0.223) and is flatly incompatible with an ABCA singleton, which would sit alone at 33.5 T. Run 7 (hole, n = −1.2×10¹²) is the recipe's stated near-CNP exception regime — the model pair is 5.9 / 6.5 T, unresolvable at that record's δF = 1.1 T, and the measured single 6.7 T peak decides nothing. Hole side excluded from the fan: the D = 0 line is TG-limited to n = −1.27×10¹², so there is no hole density where the templates separate.

**What the fan has to settle.** Run 6 leaves one ambiguity, and it is the one the investigation names: the peak at 22.2 T is *both* 3× the light tone and near the ABAB heavy partner (model 26.3 T at n = 3.27×10¹²) — a ratio-only harmonic screen eats the real heavy tone exactly here. The screen the recipe prescribes is **n-dependence**, which only a fan gives: a harmonic tracks 3·f_light(n) exactly, a real pocket follows its own Onsager curve. Across this fan's window the two trajectories diverge hard — at n = 3.24 the model puts 3·f_light = 22.4 T against f_heavy = 26.1 T (3.7 T apart), but by n = 1.5 it is 6.1 T against 13.5 T. The low-n arm of the fan is therefore the discriminator, not a luxury; it also covers the documented ABAB-mimics-ABCA exception (heavy branch → 0.945·F_L as the light pocket empties) and the ABCA hypothesis's opposite prediction (singleton pinned at F_L, damping out as m\* grows from 0.074 to 0.135 mₑ).

**Grid, each number computed (`landau-fan` step 1).** *Field:* `field_invB(0.75, 10.0, 127)`, uniform in 1/B, Δ(1/B) = 0.00979 T⁻¹ — **3.05× the Nyquist floor** on F_L(n_max) = 33.5 T (recipe's preferred 3×), Nyquist F = 51 T, which also clears the light tone's 6th harmonic (45 T) and the heavy tone's 2nd (52 T) so aliases land above the 4–27 T band the claim lives in. δF = 1/(1/0.75 − 1/10) = **0.81 T** — 4.5 δF on the worst separation the claim needs (heavy vs 3×light at n_max). *`B_lo` = 0.75 T is the cost floor, not the onset*: the measured onset is censored below the spot check's 0.30 T record edge, so every row here carries signal, and 0.75 T is the **highest** B_lo still giving ≥ 5 periods on the ABAB partner tone at n = 2.2×10¹² — the density where the partition claim floors out (f_light = 4.2 T × 1.23 T⁻¹ = 5.1 periods). Dropping to the 0.30 T record edge would cost 3.4× the rows and 2.5× the n-points — ~20 h — to buy period count on a tone the model says has already emptied below n ≈ 2×10¹². *Density:* 57 points, n = 1.30 → 3.24×10¹² at D = 0; Δn = 0.0346×10¹² against a g = 4 ray spacing of 0.0725×10¹² at B_lo = **2.09 points/ray**, at the recipe's floor. n_max = 3.24 is the validator ceiling (V_tg = +0.9951 V, 4.9 mV under the +1.0 V limit). *Dynamic range:* worst corner is low n × high B — run 7 hit 587 Ω at 10 T and was still climbing, run 6 peaked at 530 Ω, run 5 gives R(B = 0) = 96 Ω at n = 1.30; sr1 stays at **50 µV = 5 kΩ full scale** (≈ 5× headroom on a ~1 kΩ worst-corner estimate, and identical to runs 6/7 so the fan columns are directly comparable to the spot checks). *Dwell:* `fast_delay = 1.8 s` = 6τ at the operator's τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB — the recipe's per-column-SdH-FFT setting, because the n-axis carries the extracted signal and fast-axis lag smears the column-to-density assignment; `slow_delay = 1.0 s` on top of the magnet's blocking ramp + 3 s settle.

**Cost, from this session's own numbers.** 127 × 57 grid at ~2.05 s/pt (1.8 s dwell + ~0.25 s reads, rig-card fp-set figure) = 4.12 h; 0 → 0.75 T positioning 0.09 h; 0.75 → 10 T traverse at 0.137 T/min = 1.13 h; row overhead 127 × (3 s settle + 0.7 s TG flyback + 1 s slow_delay) = 0.17 h. **5.5 h nominal, 6.0 h at the +10% this session's actuals have been running.** Coverage trade: this is one fan, the cheapest clearing both Nyquist checks and the partition claim — no cheaper alternative is on offer, and n < 1.30×10¹² is excluded because the light tone has emptied there and the heavy branch alone cannot close the partition.

**Direction.** Magnet sits at 0 T after the handoff; the fan ascends and ends at **10 T** with the gates parked at the n-line's low end (n = 1.30, V_tg = +0.439). The Verify-suite LK mass series then rides the magnet back down rather than re-traversing.
