Raw TSV views precede each derived spectrum below. Region B_1–B_2 is the only connected voltage pair. Run 6 is n_A=+3.2, run 7 n_A=−1.2 (10¹² cm⁻²), D_A≈0 under run-5 calibration; nominal current 10 nA, measured drain used for resistance. Both runs completed, |R|<600 Ω against 5 kΩ full scale, gate currents <0.22 nA, and sample T=1.165–1.292 K.

Run 6 has reproducible spectral content near 7, 14–15, 22–23 and 29–30 T; these are candidates, not four pockets. Run 7 shows ~4–7 T and ~12 T content, but its closest model ABAB pair (5.86, 6.54 T at −1.2) cannot be split by this usable record. Full-record electron onset reports a censored 0.30 T edge; fitting the large 8–10 T MR background contaminates low-field residuals, and the 50 mT sampling below 1 T was explicitly background-only (measure cell 40). Restricting the onset background fit to 0.3–4 T gives `sdh_onset.onset_b=1.0309096914 T` for the 6.7 T tone, uncensored; that track is 1.01 T when fitting to 6 T. The 0.30 T full-record result is not a measured onset. The upper bound choice changes the background, so the ~1.03 T design onset remains extraction-dependent and the fan must check shared-density reproduction.

**Call at this step: B_1–B_2 stacking uninformative.** Electron-side model predictions at n=3.2 are ABAB {7.31,25.78} T versus ABCA 33.09 T. The observed near-harmonic comb does not uniquely match either using one global scale; do not assign the ~29.5 T tone to ABCA or discard a partner solely by integer ratio. Run-5 secondary CNP ridge also permits density-offset regions within this pair (~0.43×10¹² cm⁻²); these are not separately addressable contacts.
