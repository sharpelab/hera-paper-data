## HERA — resolve the onset before committing the fan (`sdh-spot-check`)

Runs 6–7 are analyzed in ana cells 14–15. Run 6's electron spectrum is usable but its default `sdh_onset` is censored at 0.30 T; coarse 50 mT low-field spacing and background sensitivity prevent a defensible fan cost floor. `landau-fan` step 1 explicitly calls for a cheap low-field extension in this case. This in-arc check measures n=+3.2×10¹² cm⁻², D=0 on the run-5 calibration, from 0.2 to 2.5 T with Δ(1/B)=0.010 T⁻¹. It both extends below the censored edge and changes the sampling grid; F_L=33.1 T, or 38.0 T at +15% scale error, has >2.6 samples/period. Fixed sr1=10 µV gives 1 kΩ full scale, >7× the observed low-field maximum. Do not interpret the unresolved low-frequency comb as a stacking verdict.

Starts from the operator's 0 T handoff; preconditions print and assert validators, thermal/logfile state, magnet state, wiring, Si and filter settings. Field positioning plus traverse: 2.5 T × 7.3 min/T = 18.25 min; 461 × 3.1 s overhead (run 6 measured, includes 3 s driver settling) = 23.8 min, plus gate positioning and 10% contingency. Gates remain at the spot and field at 2.5 T for the fan decision. Coverage excludes >2.5 T because run 6 already records it; the purpose is onset, not a fresh full-range classification. First checkpoint at 20 points, then halfway and completion: inspect all channels for rails, leakage and temperature; point guards stop on >1 nA leakage, 90% rail or thermal departure. A kernel interrupt does **not** halt a VTI magnet ramp; operator intervention at the supply is required for an emergency ramp stop.
