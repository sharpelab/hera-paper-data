## Runs 6–7 — spot-check read before the Landau fan

Raw signed Rxx and drain-current traces precede the FFT/onset plots below (`sdh-spot-check`; only sr1, B_1–B_2, is connected). Both runs completed without clipping: electron run 6 at n=+3.2 reaches 530 Ohm, hole run 7 at n=-1.2 reaches 587 Ohm; drain current remains 9.81–9.94 nA. Negative excursions are retained, not rectified or log-clipped. Only positive B is available: Hall admixture and lack of even-in-B symmetrization remain limitations.

Run 6 has candidate content around 7, 15, 22 and 29 T; run 7 around 5–7 and 12–13 T. These are **candidate frequencies, not independent pocket counts**: the electron comb needs density tracking and a Luttinger-aware harmonic screen, and extraction of the slow component varies with the fit window. The global 0.3–10 T onset fit reports a censored 0.3 T edge, but its low-B residual is dominated by the background fit through the large 8–10 T MR feature; moreover the 50 mT grid below 1 T fails the full-density Nyquist test. Restricting the background fit to 0.3–5 T/degree 5 gives a non-censored headline onset of 1.018 T for the 6.8 T component. That component has the same onset with degree 3 (0.3–4 T/degree 5: 1.03 T; 0.3–6 T/degree 5: 1.01 T), although the full tone search can promote a near-residual 3.1–3.2 T component to the headline under those other fits. The chosen onset is therefore a local, component-supported estimate; low-frequency onset ambiguity remains. This local fit supplies the fan onset; the high-field global result remains a documented extraction failure mode, not evidence of low-field SdH. No absence claim is made below 1 T.

**Decision:** acquire one electron-side, D≈0 fan in the high-density part of the accessible window, including n=+3.2 for direct acquisition comparison with run 6. The hole side is gate-limited to about -1.27 and lies in the near-CNP model ambiguity. Classification remains **uninformative** until cross-density trajectories and both ABAB/adjacent-N overlays have been checked. The calibration is ridge A from run 5; its second ridge is a possible density-inhomogeneity confound, not established mixed stacking. Absolute density retains the geometry-scale uncertainty.
