**Claim and uncertainty.** This is a narrow density-dependence and sampling-reproduction measurement on the hardwired B_1−B_2 channel, not a preassigned ABAB/ABCA verdict. Run 6 has candidate ≈7, 22, 29–30 T components and higher harmonics; require a global-scale fit to the whole set, Luttinger-aware harmonic screening, ABAB and trilayer dual overlays, and the sum-tone origin audit before a call. The second CNP ridge does not establish a second stacking domain. Only positive B is measured; odd-in-B contamination and unresolved spatial contributions remain hedges.

**Onset is an extraction fork.** The full 0.3–10 T polynomial analysis returns a censored 0.30 T edge, while the large high-field MR contaminates the low-frequency background. On the existing low-field-only 0.3–4 T record, `sdh_onset(..., b_hi=4, bg_degree=5, r_full_scale=5000)` gives **1.0309097 T**, carried by the ≈6.7 T component; degree 3 gives that component 1.04 T but marks it marginal, with the 22 T headline at 1.38 T. This fan uses the measured local onset provisionally, not the censored full-record edge as a physical onset. It cannot establish whether useful oscillations exist below 1.03 T. Reproduction at n=3.2 and a post-fan per-column onset audit are required; if the useful window starts higher, recompute resolution and downgrade the call, never count sub-onset rows as signal. A lower-field extension remains the deciding measurement for this onset fork.

**Settling and dynamic range.** Preserve 10 nA, τ=0.3 s and 24 dB/oct; **fast_delay=3 s=10τ**, slow_delay=1 s plus the driver's 3 s field settle. **Price and coverage trade.** Run 5 measured 1.098 s median / 1.116 s p90 per point with 1 s dwell and NPLC=1. Budget **3.2 s/point** here for 3 s dwell, paired-gate writes and added readbacks: **127.3 min** acquisition. Total 0→7.05 T travel is **7.05×7.3=51.5 min**, including **7.53 min positioning** to the first row; runs 6/7 measured ≈3.08 s overhead per field point, so budget that per row plus 1 s slow delay and ≈0.5 s paired-gate flyback (**16.6 min**). Subtotal ≈195 min; allow ≈15% for overhead variation and preflight → headline rounded to 3 h 50 min. The full accessible D=0 gate line (−1.27→+3.27) at the same conservative sampling would require about 111 density points and >22 h, while its low-density partner would still fail the period requirement. We exclude that region and the hole side explicitly. Even n=2.7 needs B≈10.41 T for five nominal partner periods at this onset, outside the committed 10 T cap. The selected 0.4×10¹² span limits branch-trajectory discrimination; a calibrated cannot-tell remains an acceptable outcome.
