## Run 6 — onset cross-check and committed fan design

The full-record onset is sensitive to the large high-field magnetoresistance background. Across upper limits 4 and 5 T and polynomial degrees 3–5, the tracked ~6.7–6.8 T tone dies off at **1.018–1.040 T**, uncensored; it is marginal in the 4 T/degree-3,4 fits but robust in all 5 T fits. The default `sdh_onset(..., b_hi=5, r_full_scale=5000)` returns **1.0176178808 T**. The censored 0.3 T headline in the original full-record analysis is therefore not used as a physical onset. This cross-check supplies the fan's low edge without another sweep; the earlier proposed low-field extension is superseded. The 50 mT sub-1-T sampling cannot establish absence of fast tones there; the fan makes no such absence claim.

Commit one ascending `landau-fan-vti`: n_A=+2.7…+3.2e12 cm^-2 at D_A=0, B=1.0176178808…9.2 T, 120 uniform-1/B rows × 14 paired gate points. The ABAB forward model (input density in m^-2) gives light/heavy frequencies 5.721/22.190 T at n=2.7 and 7.306/25.783 T at n=3.2. At the low edge this provides 5.000 periods of the nominal partner and δF=1.144 T; the dominant-tone/singleton separation 5.721 T exceeds 2δF. The 0.5e12 density span measures branch motion while avoiding the low-density ABAB singleton trap. Five-period reach is nominal: a revised absolute density scale can reduce it, and that will limit the verdict rather than be hidden.

The mesh includes 20% density-scale headroom and the run-5 second-ridge offset 0.43e12 for sampling: F_max=45.037 T, Δ(1/B)=0.0073445 T^-1 (3.02 samples/period; Nyquist 68.1 T), Δn=0.03846e12 (2.13 samples per worst-case g=4 ray at B_lo). Strong ~59 T second harmonics are sampled; higher harmonics must still be screened by grid reproduction and B-window tests. The spectra and model overlays, rather than an assumed harmonic ratio, will decide the interpretation. Higher-order high-B structure and unknown polarized phases are outside this design guarantee.

The calculation uses the run-5 gate-current NPLC=1 timing (1.102 s/pt at 1 s dwell) and preserves the spot-check 3 s settling (10τ at 24 dB/oct). Allow 3.25 s/pt for paired gate ramps, added readbacks, and guards: 1680×3.25 s = 91.0 min, plus 9.2/0.137=67.2 min field travel from handoff zero and 120×7.5 s = 15 min turnaround, then 15% contingency ≈ **3 h 20 min**. A full -1.2…+3.2 window at this same mesh would cost approximately 16–17 h and still fail the five-period model-partner requirement at low electron density; it is excluded. Hole-side evidence remains run 7, and sr2/sr3 are disconnected. No region-resolved stacking verdict is yet justified; only B_1–B_2 is observed, with unresolved spatial mixing suggested by the two CNP ridges.
