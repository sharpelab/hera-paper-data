## Runs 6–7 — spot-check read before the stacking fan

Raw signed Rxx, measured drain current, leakage and temperature appear first, followed by FFT stability scans. Both runs completed without clipping (Rxx extrema −33…530 Ω electron, −0.4…587 Ω hole against 5 kΩ full scale); drain current remains 9.81–9.94 nA and |gate current| <0.22 nA. Only B_1−B_2 is wired: no separately resolved spatial stacking verdict is available.

Run 6 at n_A=+3.2×10¹² cm⁻², D_A≈0 shows candidate spectral groups near 7.3, 14.7, 22.2 and 29.5 T (window-dependent estimates), versus F_L=33.09 T on the geometry scale. These near-integer relationships do **not** establish four pockets; harmonics, sum/difference mixing and the second CNP region remain confounds. Run 7 at −1.2×10¹² has stronger oscillations near 4–7 T and 12.4 T, but lower |n| places the two tetralayer bands close together. Neither trace yet supports a stacking verdict. Positive-field-only measurements cannot remove odd-in-B pickup by symmetrization.

The default electron `sdh_onset` is censored at 0.30 T. Restricting its background fit to B≤3 T gives 1.30 T for degrees 3 and 5 (the ~7 T tone is marginal); B≤4 T gives 1.03–1.38 T depending on background degree. This is an extraction fork, not a precisely measured 0.30 T onset. The original 50 mT grid below 1 T also fails fastest-tone Nyquist there. A short, adequately sampled low-field repeat must resolve the onset before the expensive fan is frozen (`landau-fan` step 1). The electron side remains the design target because it reaches the discriminating +2–3×10¹² regime; the hole gate limit is −1.27×10¹².

Absolute density retains the geometry prior. The secondary ridge in run 5 implies a density offset and possibly a different capacitance ratio, so extra branches cannot be assigned to domains at a common n without testing that calibration. Next: low-field grid-change check at the shared +3.2 density, then one cost-floored fan and both tetralayer/trilayer forward overlays.
