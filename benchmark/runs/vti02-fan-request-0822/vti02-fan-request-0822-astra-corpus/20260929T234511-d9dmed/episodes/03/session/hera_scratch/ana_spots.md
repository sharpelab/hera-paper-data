## Runs 6–7 — spot-check read before the first Landau fan

This is the in-session continuation of run 5's calibration (measure cells 40/42). Each run is shown raw before its spectrum. Rxx is sr1_X divided by measured drain current sr0_X/−1e6; only B_1–B_2 is wired. Both traces are complete and unclipped at 50 µV: maxima 530/587 Ω, drain current 9.81–9.94 nA, T 1.165–1.292 K, absolute TG/BG leakage below 0.22 nA.

The electron spectrum has reproducible structure near 7, 14, 22 and 29 T, plus ~59 T; weak high-frequency structure extends toward 130 T. These are candidate frequencies, not independent pocket labels: harmonics, mixing and spatially offset regions remain unresolved. The hole trace has a strong ~6–7 T component but the model at n=−1.2e12 predicts a close 5.86/6.54 T ABAB pair: those cannot be separated by this record. Lower onset alone therefore does not make the hole side the better stacking test.

The default electron onset fit over 0.3–10 T returns a censored 0.3 T, with nonphysical low-field revivals sensitive to the high-field MR background and sparse sub-1 T sampling. Restricting the fit to 0.3–5 T gives sdh_onset=1.01762 T (6.774 T carrier; the 3.129 T candidate is marginal and excluded). Fits ending at 4–5 T place this carrier at ~1.02–1.04 T; alternative detrending can mark it marginal and let the ~22 T tone carry onset at 1.3–1.4 T. Thus ~1.02 T is a provisional usable low-field edge, not a precise mobility determination. Hole onset in 0.3–4 T is 0.737 T. No censored low-field result is being treated as established oscillations.

**Decision:** proceed to an electron fan over n=2.8–3.2e12 at calibrated D≈0, preserving the run-6 shared-density anchor. This tests the frequency trajectories and harmonic relations on the only side reaching the investigation's discriminating density range. Current B_1–B_2 stacking verdict: **uninformative** pending the fan, whole-set density-scale fit, ABAB/ABCA and adjacent-N overlays, and sum-tone checks. The two CNP ridges in run 5 are a spatial-density confound, not proof of mixed stacking.
