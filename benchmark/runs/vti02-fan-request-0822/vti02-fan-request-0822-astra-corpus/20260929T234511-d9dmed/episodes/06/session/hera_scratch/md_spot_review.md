The electron trace has reproducible spectral structure near 14.7, 22.2 and 29.5–30 T; the roughly 7 T component is sensitive to detrending and can be a residual. These are candidate spectral features, not a count of pockets: harmonics, mixing and the two CNP ridges remain unresolved. The hole trace has a stable high-window feature near 12.5 T but its low-frequency content and inferred onset also depend on window.

The 29.5 T feature has a more repeatable onset near 1.75–1.85 T. This is an extraction fork, not an established 0.3 T onset. Below 1 T run 6 used 50 mT steps, which cannot Nyquist the fastest expected frequency; its apparent low-field onset cannot set a classification-grade fan grid. A short, properly sampled low-field repeat at the shared electron density is needed to distinguish a true early-onset partner from sampling/background structure before committing the long fan.
