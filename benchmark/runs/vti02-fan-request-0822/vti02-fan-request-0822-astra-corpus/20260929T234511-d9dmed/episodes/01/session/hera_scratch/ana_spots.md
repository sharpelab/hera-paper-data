`sdh-spot-check`, measure cells 40/42: electron n=+3.2 and hole n=-1.2 ×10¹² cm⁻² at the run-5 region-A D≈0 calibration. Both runs completed; measured drain current stays 9.81–9.94 nA, gate-current magnitudes stay below 0.22 nA, and sample T stays 1.165–1.292 K. Raw signed resistance is plotted first; negative electron minima are retained, so FFT uses linear R rather than a clipped logarithm.

The electron FFT has a near-commensurate comb, not yet an identified pocket set. The hole side has clearer raw oscillations but cannot reach the investigation's preferred |n|≥2–3 window within the committed TG limit. Select the electron side for the fan's cross-density harmonic/partition test; report a fork rather than turning an integer frequency ratio into a stacking verdict. `tetralayer-stacking-fs-count` §§2–5/Verify and `landau-fan-vti` govern the design. Run 5 also shows a second neutrality ridge offset by about 0.43×10¹² cm⁻², so a single pair samples possible spatial inhomogeneity; distinct ridges are not proof of distinct stackings.

Default electron onset is censored at 0.30 T, and the 50 mT grid below 1 T undersamples the fastest tone. Degree-6 background removal gives ~0.91 T, but that extraction dependence is a design uncertainty. Resolve it with a short low-field overlap before committing the fan. The full-scale limit was 5 kΩ; observed |R|<590 Ω excludes clipping. Run-5 median cadence 1.098 s at 1 s dwell gives ~0.10 s acquisition overhead; run-6 10 mT cadence 7.464 s minus 4.38 s ramp gives ~3.08 s blocking-set/read overhead.
