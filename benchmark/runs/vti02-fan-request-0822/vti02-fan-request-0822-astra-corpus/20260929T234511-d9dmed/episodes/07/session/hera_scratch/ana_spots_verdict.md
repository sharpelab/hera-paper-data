### Read and fan decision

Both spots advance the investigation: complete traces, current 9.81–9.94 nA, sample 1.165–1.292 K, maximum |gate current| 216 pA, and |Rxx| below 590 Ω versus 5 kΩ full scale. No clipping or contact loss. Negative Rxx portions are retained as measured; with one positive-field sweep and no Hall pair, even-in-B separation is unavailable and longitudinal mixing is a hedge.

Electron candidate spectral features lie near 7, 14, 22 and 29–30 T, with higher harmonics. They are not yet independent pocket assignments: a near-integer comb, background dependence, the second neutrality ridge and a potential sum orbit all remain live. The full-record degree-5 onset returns a censored 0.30 T result; that is not a measured die-off. Restricting the background fit to B≤4 T gives 1.03 T; degree 7 over B≤8 T gives an uncensored 0.91 T for the same ~7 T component (0.90 T for that component over B≤10 T). Use 0.90 T as a conservative design edge, with an explicit ~0.9–1.05 T extraction uncertainty. The coarse sub-1-T section cannot support high-frequency absence claims. Hole onset is 0.71 T, but the allowed hole density is only −1.27×10¹²: the close model pair there gives much poorer stacking discrimination.

Proceed with one electron D≈0 fan over n_A=2.8–3.2×10¹². This samples how the candidate peaks move with density and reproduces the 3.2 spot on a changed grid. It is a first discrimination step, not a completed stacking call. The next read must compare whole peak sets with ABAB and adjacent-N curves using one global scale, screen harmonics, and resolve the origin of any F_L-like tone before a strong classification. Region B's different CNP slope and offset make its local n and D uncertain; do not assign all extra tones to a same-density domain or assert a separate region-B verdict.
