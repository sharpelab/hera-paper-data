## HERA — first electron Landau fan for the stacking investigation

**Decision and evidence.** `landau-fan-vti` under `investigations/tetralayer-stacking-fs-count`, Steps 4–5. Gates A/B remain satisfied (measure setup, SAFETIES cell 18, VERIFY cell 32; same-kernel handoff cells 44/46); Gate C passed on runs 0–3. Runs 6/7 are now read in ana cells 14–16. Electron run 6 has usable candidate tones near 7, 14, 22 and 29–30 T; a harmonic/sum-tone interpretation and the second CNP ridge remain unresolved. The changed grid and repeated n=3.2 column test acquisition quality and peak motion. This is not yet a stacking verdict.

**Coverage and onset.** One fixed-D_A≈0 fan, n_A=2.8–3.2×10¹² cm⁻², positive B=0.90→5.20 T, starting from the handoff's measured zero. The run-5 rounded calibration used in run 6 is repeated exactly: c_tg=0.002796, c_bg=0.002161 F/m²; n_offset=−0.0894×10¹², D_offset=−0.013 V/nm. TG=0.8690…0.9836 V and BG=1.0178…1.1661 V, safely inside the unchanged validators. `sdh_onset` on run 6 gives 0.9053 T with degree-7 background over B≤8 T, with a shorter-record degree-5 check at 1.03 T; the global degree-5 fit's censored 0.30 T result is background-sensitive and cannot price a fan. Use 0.90 T with explicit ~0.9–1.05 T uncertainty. The hole side is excluded because its gate-limited |n|≤1.27×10¹² leaves the ABAB tones close and ambiguous. Lower electron densities fail the five-partner-period target in this field window. Full two-sided coverage adds many hours while retaining that low-|n| limitation; no such coverage is implied. D≈0 is calibrated for ridge A; ridge B's local density/displacement and spatial origin remain uncertain.

**Computed resolution and cost floor.** Model `tetralayer_fs_count/fs_count_b0.py` predicts ABAB (6.04,22.91) T at n=2.8 and (7.31,25.78) T at n=3.2; ABCA F_L=28.95…33.09 T. The nominal low-edge partner gets 5.55 periods, δF=1.09 T over the full window. With onset raised to 1.05 T the partner gets 4.59 periods: weak-tone absence must then remain resolution-limited. For the high-frequency branch's ~1.9 T observed onset, δF≈2.99 T; the smallest 6.04 T heavy-versus-singleton split just clears 2δF. No claim about absent pockets may outrun the measured per-column onset. This is the minimal first trajectory window; a final strong call still needs the investigation's whole-set scale fit, ABAB/adjacent-N overlays and sum-tone checks.

**167 B rows × 13 densities = 2171 points.** Uniform Δ(1/B)=0.005535 T⁻¹, Nyquist=90.33 T. This covers nominal F_L, the +20% scale prior and approximate second-CNP +0.43 density offset, plus second harmonics (ceiling 90.08 T); it also samples the observed ~59 T feature. Frequencies above 90 T are not interpretable; the run-6 >88 T features occur near/above this fan's upper edge. The n step is 0.0333×10¹², below the ray-Nyquist floor 0.0363×10¹² at 0.9 T even with a 20% scale increase. Sampling below the ~1 T spot grid is newly measured, not inferred from its coarse background segment.

**Acquisition and price.** Keep τ=0.3 s / 24 dB and use a full 3 s fast-point dwell (10τ); the field driver's settle alone does not settle subsequent gate points. Keep 10 nA excitation, Si=0 and fixed sr1=50 µV (5 kΩ full scale). The entire run-6 trace peaks at 530 Ω; scaling to lower n and allowing a further factor two gives ~1.21 kΩ, comfortably inside range; the low-n/high-B corner is checked as it lands. Run 5 at NPLC=1 measured 1.098 s/point with 1 s dwell; budget 3.25 s/point with the extra field and vaporizer reads and paired gates, plus 5.5 s/row for magnet settle, slow delay and flyback. Grid term=117.6 min, row term=15.3 min; total field traverse from 0 to 5.2 T at 0.137 T/min=38.0 min (including the 6.6 min initial ramp). With setup and 10% margin the realistic budget is ~189 min. End at 5.2 T and n_A=2.8; no return traverse is included.

**Preconditions and checkpoints.** The cell prints expected/actual thermometry, heater ranges, measured field, gate/Si state, exact validators, wiring, drive, time constants and pacing before any write. Every followed channel is plotted: both temperatures, both gate currents, sr0/sr1 X and phase, and measured field. The TSV separately records measured B. First checkpoint: first complete row and first three rows (~10 min including initial ramp), checking leakage, rails, current, temperature, phase and field tracking. Then check every ~30 min and at B≈2 and 3 T, with interim FFTs only once enough periods exist. Continue only with healthy acquisition and reproducible structure; a washed-out shared-density column cannot overturn run 6. Automatic point guards stop at >1 nA leakage, approaching rails, abnormal current/T or field mismatch; thermometry freshness is checked every 30 s. On an anomaly use `nb-collab interrupt` and re-read state before another move. **VTI-specific:** a kernel interrupt does not halt a supply ramp already in progress; the operator must handle a physical ramp abort. Watcher is armed before execution.
