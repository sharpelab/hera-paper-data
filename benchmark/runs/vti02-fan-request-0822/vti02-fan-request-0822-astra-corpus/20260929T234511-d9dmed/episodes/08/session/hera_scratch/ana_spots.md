## Runs 6–7 — raw spot checks, onset audit, and fan decision

`sdh-spot-check`; measure cells 40/42. Both runs completed, with drain current 9.81–9.94 nA, sample T 1.165–1.292 K, and |gate current| < 0.22 nA. The signed sr1 resistance includes negative excursions; preserve them and use linear-R FFTs, not log clipping. Only B_1–B_2 is wired as Rxx; spatial attribution within this pair is unresolved. All fields are positive, so even-in-B symmetrization remains unavailable.

Electron run 6 (nominal n=+3.2×10¹² cm⁻², D≈0) has reproducible spectral features near 7, 14, 22, and 29–30 T. The 14 T feature could be a harmonic; 22 T cannot be discarded by its near-3:1 ratio alone. A putative 7+22≈29 T partition implies a global density scale ≈0.88 against nominal F_L=33.09 T, but that is a hypothesis, not a fitted stacking verdict. The 29 T feature could be a pocket or sum orbit. Run 5's secondary CNP ridge adds a spatial-density confound. Hole run 7 (n=−1.2×10¹²) has features near 5, 7, and 12.4 T; its limited accessible density and close model branches make it the weaker fan choice.

**Onset fork:** the full 0.3–10 T polynomial produces censored low-frequency tracks at 0.3 T. This is not a measured onset, and the 50 mT grid below 1 T also fails fast-tone Nyquist. Restricting the onset fit to 0.3–5 T removes the large 8–10 T MR excursion from the background fit: `sdh_onset(..., b_hi=5, bg_degree=5)` gives **1.01784 T** for the ~6.8 T track, uncensored; degree 4 agrees. A 4 T endpoint gives 1.031 T (degree 5), while degree 3/4 marks the light track marginal and reports the ~22 T track's 1.38 T onset. The 3 T endpoint likewise marks the light track marginal. Thus ~1.02 T is a *window-qualified design onset*, not an established cutoff independent of extraction. No absence claim below that floor is permitted. The fan's changed inverse-field grid and shared n=3.2 column will test reproduction; failure of the first rows or shared-column agreement suspends interpretation.

**Decision:** proceed with one narrow electron fan, n=2.8–3.2×10¹² at D≈0, targeting branch tracking and the 7/22/29 T ambiguity. Nominal model ABAB light branches span 6.04–7.31 T; five light-pocket periods at the low-n edge require B_max≈6.47 T. The absolute density scale is still a geometry prior; a downward rescale reduces that period count and weakens partner-absence claims. This is a refinement measurement, not a promise of a strong stacking verdict. The final read must include ABAB/ABCA and adjacent-N overlays, one scale for the full set, harmonics, and the F_L-origin audit.
