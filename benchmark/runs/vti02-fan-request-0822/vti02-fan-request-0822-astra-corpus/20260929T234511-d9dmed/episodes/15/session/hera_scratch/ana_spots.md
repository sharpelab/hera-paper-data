## Runs 6–7 — spot-check read before the stacking fan

Run 6 (electron, n=+3.2e12 cm^-2) and run 7 (hole, n=-1.2e12), both at region-A calibrated D≈0, are complete, unclipped, and at base temperature. Only sr1 / B_1–B_2 is a connected voltage region; sr2/sr3 are uninformative because unwired. The raw traces and extraction checks below precede the fan decision.

Run 6 has features around 7.3, 14.8, 22.3 and 29.4 T (frequency uncertainty is record/window limited, roughly 1–2 T in the useful windows); their near-integer ratios require density tracking before calling them distinct pockets. F_L=33.09 T at nominal n; {7.3,22.3} would sum to 29.6 T, a scale ~0.895, but this is an unverified partition hypothesis, not a fitted stacking assignment. The full 0.3–10 T onset fit is contaminated by its large 8–10 T magnetoresistance excursion: it reports censored low-field tones, including detrend residuals, on a grid that undersamples F_L below 1 T. Restricting the background-fit record to 0.3–4/5 T gives an uncensored ~7 T tone onset of 1.031/1.018 T (degree 5; degree 4 agrees at 5 T). Degree 3 can still elevate a ~3 T residual; this extraction fork is retained. Use 1.0176178808 T as the fan's lower edge, without claiming the coarse sub-1 T record excludes weaker oscillations.

Run 7 is stronger in amplitude and yields onset 0.714 T, but its accessible high-hole-density ABAB model pair is nearly degenerate: at n=-1.2, 5.863/6.545 T versus ABCA F_L=12.405 T. The minimum δF even at 10 T is 0.769 T, so the 0.682 T model split fails the 2δF criterion. Hole features around 4–7 and 12.4 T cannot settle the pocket count; lower-frequency peaks also depend on the detrending window. The electron window +2.8…+3.2 is therefore the informative fan, despite the hole trace's larger signal. Model values were computed from `tetralayer_fs_count/fs_count_b0.py:abab_fs` with density in m^-2, not bare partition ratios.

**Current verdict: B_1–B_2 stacking uninformative.** The fan must track the whole peak set with one density scale, harmonic screening, ABAB and adjacent-N overlays, and F_L-tone-origin checks before any stronger call.
