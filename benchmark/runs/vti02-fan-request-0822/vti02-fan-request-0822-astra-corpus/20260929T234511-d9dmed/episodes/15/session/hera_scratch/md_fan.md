**Decision and claim window.** Measure n=+2.8…+3.2 ×10¹² cm⁻² at region-A D≈0, Si=0, base T. Run 6 contains ~7.3, 14.8, 22.3, 29.4 T features that need n-dependence and harmonic/Luttinger checks; its near-commensurate peaks are not already a stacking call. Hole run 7 has larger oscillations, but at n=-1.2 the model's 5.863/6.545 T pair is only 0.682 T apart, below 2δF even at the 10 T cap. This fan tests the electron branch trajectories and reproduces the n=3.2 spot on a changed grid. Only B_1–B_2 is wired; no spatially independent contact verdict is possible. The two CNP ridges leave a doping/mosaic confound, and D≈0 applies to the dominant ridge calibration, not necessarily every contributing domain.

**Binding design arithmetic.** Run-6 `sdh_onset(R,B,b_hi=5,bg_degree=5,r_full_scale=5000)` gives B_lo=1.0176178808 T; degree 4 agrees and a 4 T upper cutoff gives 1.031 T. Its censored result is not used as a measured onset; ana 14–15 retains both diagnostics and the residual-background fork. The ~7 T track is uncensored in the restricted fit. At n=2.8, `fs_count_b0.abab_fs(2.8e16)` predicts [6.0379,22.9142] T, and at 3.2 predicts [7.3058,25.7831] T; ABCA F_L=28.950…33.085 T. Use Δ(1/B)=5/6.0379=0.8281034 T⁻¹, hence B_hi=6.46898685 T and nominal δF=1.20758 T. The ABAB-heavy to ABCA-singleton separation ≥6.04 T exceeds 2δF=2.42 T. Five periods is a nominal-model feasibility criterion: a smaller fitted density scale or a later onset at another column reduces the count and must weaken that column's claim.

**Grid floor.** N_B=113 uniformly in 1/B gives spacing 0.00739378 T⁻¹. A conservative fastest-fundamental allowance F_guard=1.2×(3.2+0.4263)×10.33917=44.99 T covers the +20% scale prior and the secondary-ridge density offset: 3 samples/period require ≤0.007409 T⁻¹, and the observed ~59 T harmonic is also above the 2-sample floor. Roundtrip D=0 is asserted; gate endpoints TG=0.8690…0.9836 V, BG=1.0178…1.1661 V stay strictly inside the unchanged validators. No clipping/clamping of gate vectors.

**Settling and dynamic range.** Inherit τ=0.3 s, slope=24 dB/oct, 10 nA nominal bias, sr0 20 mV and sr1 50 µV from the spot checks, after explicitly restoring the sensitivities that state0 changed. Use 3 s=10τ interruptible settling at every gate point. Full scale is 5 kΩ: run 6 reached 530 Ω even at 10 T; scaling the lower-n corner by 3.2/2.8 gives ~606 Ω, >8× headroom, while this fan stops at 6.47 T. That 1/n extrapolation is a sizing heuristic, checked at the first rows and throughout; a ≥90%-FS read stops acquisition. Every followed channel (both temperatures, both gate currents, both connected lock-in X/P pairs) is plotted live. No Si move, gate pacing change, heater move, or safety-surface edit.

**Price and coverage trade.** Runs 6/7 calibrate field-set overhead ~3.08 s beyond ΔB×438 s/T. Run 5 at NPLC=1 has 1.098 s median gate-point interval with 1 s dwell; allow 3.2 s/point here for two co-swept gates and both thermometers. N_B×N_n×t_pt=1243×3.2 s=66.3 min. Total ramp floor from 0 T is 6.469×7.3=47.2 min (including 7.43 min positioning to B_lo); row overhead is ~4.42 s including settle, 1 s slow delay and gate flyback, adding 8.3 min. Total 121.8 min plus 10% allowance gives ~134 min, rounded to the tool's ETA. Lower electron densities are excluded because the shrinking partner cannot meet five periods at this onset under the 10 T cap (n=2.5 would require far above 10 T in the nominal model). The hole side is excluded for the near-degenerate-pair limitation; extending it would add acquisition time without resolving that split. Higher n would cross the TG operating limit near 3.27e12; 6.47→10 T alone would add 25.8 min ramp plus extra rows and would not solve the low-density limitation.
