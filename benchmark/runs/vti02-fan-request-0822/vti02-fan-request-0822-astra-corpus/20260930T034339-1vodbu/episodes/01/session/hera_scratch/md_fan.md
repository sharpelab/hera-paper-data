## HERA — first stacking fan on the electron side (`landau-fan-vti`)

Run 5 established the D≈0 gate line, and spot runs 6–7 passed current, temperature and dynamic-range checks (ana cells 14–15). Choose the electron side: it reaches the investigation's separated-template density window, while the accessible holes stop at −1.27e12 cm⁻² in the close-pair regime. The fan tests the candidate frequencies' density trajectories on the sole wired region **sr1 / B_1−B_2**, reproduces run 6's n=3.2 column on a new grid, and leaves B at its upper endpoint for a descending follow-up. Stacking is presently **uninformative**; the gate-map gap and a single ~30 T tone are insufficient.

**Design and resolution.** Use the run-5 calibration c_tg=0.002796, c_bg=0.002161 F/m², n_offset=−0.0894e12 cm⁻², D_offset=−0.013 V/nm. The ratio is data-calibrated; the absolute scale is still a geometry prior. Co-sweep n=3.2→2.8e12 at D=0 in **11 points**, TG=0.983621→0.869016 V and BG=1.166125→1.017844 V; Si remains 0 V. Sweep **151 B rows, uniform in 1/B, 1.01761788→6.5 T**, ascending from the handoff's measured zero field.

The onset is computed with `sdh_onset` on run 6's low-field B≤5 T record: **1.01761788 T**, agreeing with B≤4 T at 1.03 T. The default full-record result is censored at 0.3 T and background-sensitive to the 8–9 T MR lobe; it is not a validated physical onset. The 50 mT grid below 1 T cannot establish absence of earlier oscillations. The carrying low-frequency component is itself a candidate until the fan separates harmonics/background; dominant-tone onset is ~1.73 T. These limitations remain explicit in the analysis.

The forward model `tetralayer_fs_count/fs_count_b0.py` gives ABAB [6.0379,22.9142] T at n=2.8 and [7.3058,25.7831] T at n=3.2; ABCA/Luttinger F_L=28.9497→33.0853 T. The full record has Δ(1/B)=0.828841 T⁻¹, **δF=1.2065 T**, and **5.004 periods** of the smallest nominal ABAB partner. At the dominant-tone onset the available δF≈2.36 T still separates the ~6 T nominal heavy/singleton gap, but weaker or later-onset partners and a shifted global density scale may remain unresolved. The 6.5 T ceiling is the rounded minimum for five nominal partner periods at n=2.8 (6.469 T), not the 10 T safety cap.

Both-axis checks: field spacing **0.00552561 T⁻¹** Nyquists **90.075 T**, covering the second harmonic of the highest-density singleton with +20% density scale and the possible +0.43e12 second-ridge offset. Fundamental F_L has >5 samples/period. Density spacing **0.04e12 cm⁻²** is below the g=4 ray-Nyquist floor **0.04101e12** at B_lo even with +20% scale; density-harmonic fine structure is not a claim. Higher harmonics above 90 T remain outside the guaranteed spectral band and will need the shared-column grid comparison.

**Coverage trade.** Exclude holes and n<2.8: the holes cannot reach |n|≥2–3e12 within the fixed gates, and the lower electron densities lose the five-period partner criterion within this field window (n=2.6 would need ~17.3 T, above the session cap). A nominal 0→3.2 density fan at the same B grid and conservative ray spacing would need ~80 density points and roughly 13 h, while still failing the low-density partner-count test. This 0.4e12 window buys the ABAB-vs-singleton test and a limited trajectory check; it may not settle ABCB or spatial mixtures. No safety-boundary expansion is proposed.

**Acquisition and cost.** Retain 10 nA, τ=0.3 s / 24 dB on sr0/sr1; **3 s fast dwell =10τ**, matching the spot check's field-settle quality. Fix sr1 at 50 µV (5 kΩ): run 6 peaked at 530 Ω and run 7 at 587 Ω; even a 3.2/2.8 low-density extrapolation gives ~605 Ω, leaving >8× headroom. Each acquired temperature, gate current, X, phase and actual B has a live plot. No disconnected channels are acquired. Run 5 gives 1.098 s/point at 1 s dwell and NPLC=1; budget 3.2 s/point including the longer dwell, paired gates and actual B read. Grid floor 151×11×3.2 s=88.6 min; monotonic 0→6.5 T ramp=6.5×7.3=47.45 min (initial positioning=7.43 min); 151×6 s=15.1 min for settling, flyback and row overhead, plus 10% contingency gives ~166 min. The ETA rounds this upward.

**Preflight and checkpoints.** The cell prints expected/actual base temperatures, fresh worker logs, zero starting field, heater ranges off, PS/output on, unchanged validators/caps, wiring, Si range/output/pacing, NPLC, drive and filter settings before any write. It preserves the established 1 nA leakage trip and stops on stale thermometry, temperature loss, current collapse, field mismatch or approaching a rail. Inspect the **first three complete rows**, then **every 30 min** from the armed `hera-watch`; check all channels, leakage, clipping/flatness and row stability, and revise timing from landed rows. No repeated field traversal to reach a preferred start. A cell interrupt does **not** stop this VTI supply mid-ramp: it continues to its last target; an actual ramp abort requires operator action, then measured state must be re-read.
