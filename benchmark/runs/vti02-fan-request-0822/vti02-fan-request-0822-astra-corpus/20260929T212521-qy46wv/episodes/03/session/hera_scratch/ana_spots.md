## Runs 6–7 — spot-check read before the Landau fan

Raw signed resistance first, then onset/FFT stability. Only sr1 (B_1–B_2, source T_0/drain B_3) probes a wired voltage pair. Run 6 is electron n=+3.2×10¹² cm⁻², run 7 hole n=−1.2×10¹², both D≈0 on the run-5 ridge-A calibration. Nominal Luttinger frequencies are 33.09 and 12.41 T; absolute density remains a geometry prior. The second CNP ridge gives a possible +0.43×10¹² density offset along the same contact path, not an independently resolved region.

**Read:** both runs are complete, conductive and unclipped. Electron peaks recur near 7.3, 14.7, 22.2, 29.5 T (roughly a harmonic series); the hole peak set shifts more with extraction window and is confined to the near-CNP regime where the ABAB pair is close. Neither is a classification-grade partition yet. In particular, do not label the 29.5 T tone ABCA, or automatically discard the 22 T component as a harmonic. The next measurement is the electron fan to test frequency trajectories and reproduce on a different sampling grid. Negative signed R minima and the series-path/mosaic possibility remain explicit caveats.

**Onset fork:** full 0.3–10 T polynomial subtraction makes the electron onset censored at 0.3 T; those low-field points also undersample the fastest tone (50 mT below 1 T). Restricting background estimation to ≤4 T removes the large high-field MR upturn: `sdh_onset(..., b_hi=4, bg_degree=5)` returns 1.03091 T, carried by 6.7 T. Degree 3 retains that tone at 1.04 T but flags it marginal and assigns the robust headline to the 22 T component at 1.38 T. Use 1.03091 T as the exploratory fan edge, carry 1.0–1.4 T onset uncertainty, and do not turn a missing partner into exclusion without verifying its usable record. Hole onset is ~0.71–0.75 T, but its allowed D=0 density never reaches the ≥2–3×10¹² discriminating window.

The fan interpretation must compare a shared +3.2 column to run 6, screen harmonics with density dependence and the full Luttinger sum, fit one global density scale, and overlay both ABAB and adjacent-N trilayer curves. A final strong call may need LK/F_L-origin work; this fan is the next evidence step, not a promised verdict.
