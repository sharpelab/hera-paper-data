## Runs 6 and 7 — raw SdH checks and fan-design read

Recipe: `sdh-spot-check`; governing `tetralayer-stacking-fs-count`, Steps 2–4. Raw signed Rxx and current/leakage/temperature precede onset and FFT. Both runs completed without rails (Rxx maxima 530/587 Ohm against 5 kOhm full scale), drain 9.81–9.94 nA, sample 1.165–1.292 K. Only sr1/B1–B2 is connected as Rxx; no Hall or other spatial channel is available.

The electron trace at n_A=+3.2 has reproducible candidate tones around 7, 14, 22 and 29 T; integer relationships and the two zero-field ridges make pocket assignment ambiguous. The full 0.3–10 T polynomial fit returns a censored low-field onset and large residual-background tones: it is not a valid basis for an expensive low-field fan. Limiting the onset fit to 0.3–5 T, before the large 8–9 T MR hump, and excluding the <4 T residual band gives **1.018, 1.018, 1.042 T** for degrees 3, 5, 7. The carrying 6.7–6.8 T tone is uncensored in all three fits; its onset is near the 1 T transition to fine sampling, so retain an onset/sampling uncertainty and do not claim sub-1 T absence. The <4 T exclusion is only for design: it is not a claim that smaller pockets are absent. Higher-frequency tones turn on around 1.3–1.8 T; their shorter usable records must be used for final resolution claims.

The hole trace at n_A=-1.2 has onset 0.71 T, but the model ABAB tones there are **5.86 and 6.54 T**, separated by only 0.68 T against deltaF=0.77 T even out to 10 T. Its cleaner oscillations therefore cannot separate that pair. Choose the electron side, where the calibrated gate window reaches the discriminating density regime.

**Current verdict, B1–B2: uninformative for stacking.** The spots advance the campaign by establishing oscillations, timing and an attainable fan window; they do not yet supply a reliable pocket count. The next fan tests dispersion and grid reproduction. The FS model predicts ABAB (6.04,22.91) T at n=2.8 and (7.31,25.78) T at n=3.2; ABCA gives F_L=28.95–33.09 T. Values were computed with `hera/models/tetralayer_fs_count/fs_count_b0.py:abab_fs` (density in m^-2). A global scale fit, harmonic screen, and ABAB plus trilayer overlays remain mandatory before classification; any F_L-like tone needs its origin discrimination.

Calibration caveat/correction to cell 12: the stored secondary-ridge offset is n_B_offset=-0.426 versus n_A_offset=-0.089, so at identical gates **n_B=n_A-0.337**, not a +0.43 increase. Parallel ridges alone do not establish stacking domains or a shared D-zero. Keep that extra-region hypothesis separate from the single-global-scale template fits. Absolute capacitance remains a geometry prior; the narrow accessible high-density window may leave branch-trajectory discrimination inconclusive.
