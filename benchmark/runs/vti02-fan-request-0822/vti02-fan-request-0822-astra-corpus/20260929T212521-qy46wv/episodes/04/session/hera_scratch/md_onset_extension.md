## HERA — resolve the censored electron onset before the fan
`sdh-spot-check` and `landau-fan-vti` require an onset measured on an adequately sampled record. Run 6 has 50 mT spacing below 1 T: it cannot Nyquist-resolve F_L=33.09 T there. Restricted to B>=1 T, `sdh_onset` finds a censored 7.3 T candidate (also near the background-residual band); the 22.3/29.4 T candidates start near 1.40/1.76 T. This short extension tests the low-field candidate and overlaps the original record before we price the fan; no stacking interpretation is attached to a harmonic flag.

Same n=+3.2e12, D=0, run-5 calibration and 10 nA drive. Uniform inverse-B grid .3->1.6 T, 301 rows: du=.00903/T (3.35 points per 33.09 T period; >=2 even at +20% scale and the secondary-ridge density offset). Full scale 500 Ohm at sr1 5 uV clears run-6 low-field maximum ~60 Ohm. Driver settle 3 s is 10 tau; no extra dwell. Run 6/7 measured point overhead beyond the .137 T/min ramp is ~3.08 s; 301 points cost 15.5 min plus 11.7 min total positioning/traverse and setup, budget 30 min. Leaves B=1.6 T and gates at the spot for the fan. Read-only preconditions compare all validators with the card, base-T freshness, heater/PS/output, drive, Si, wiring and pacing. Every followed channel is plotted; leakage/current/temperature/rail guards stop the sweep. Read first 20 points and completion with hera-watch.
