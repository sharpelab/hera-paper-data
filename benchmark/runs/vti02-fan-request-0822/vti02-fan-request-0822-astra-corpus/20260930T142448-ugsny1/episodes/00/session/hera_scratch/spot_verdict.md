## Spot-check verdict and fan design gate

Run 6 (n=+3.2×10¹² cm⁻², calibrated D≈0) has peaks near 7.3, 14.8, 22.3 and 29.4 T. Their near-integer relationships are a harmonic/multiple-region confound, not four proven Fermi surfaces. Across B-window/detrender choices the ~7/15/22/30 T structure persists, while the lowest-frequency background changes. Run 7 (n=−1.2×10¹²) has clearer oscillations and an onset of 0.714 T, but the **computed full model**, not the old spot-proposal estimates, predicts ABAB 5.863/6.545 T there: their 0.682 T separation is below 2δF=1.54 T even with Bmax=10 T. A hole fan cannot cleanly count that pair at this onset. This corrects the approximate 5.3/7.1 T values in measure cell 41.

The electron side is therefore the feasible fan side despite its weaker signal: model at +3.2 predicts 7.306/25.783 T versus ABCA F_L=33.085 T. Electron onset from the default helper is spuriously censored at the coarse low-field record edge; those 50 mT samples fail Nyquist on F_L below 1 T. Restricting to B≥1 T still returns a censored 7.3 T track. Per `landau-fan`'s binding cost-floor gate, acquire one short fine low-field extension at the same density before choosing B_lo. This is in-arc `sdh-spot-check`, and prevents pricing a long fan on an aliased onset. No stacking verdict yet; the run-5 parallel CNP ridge, geometry-prior density scale, harmonics and possible adjacent-N alternatives remain live confounds.
