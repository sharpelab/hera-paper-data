## HERA — electron onset extension before the Landau fan
`sdh-spot-check` and `landau-fan-vti` require an uncensored, properly sampled onset before pricing a long fan. Run 6's default 0.30 T result is censored and its 50 mT low-field steps undersample the 33.1 T Luttinger frequency; this extension at the same +3.2e12, D≈0 operating point tests the low-field onset with a changed grid. It excludes high fields already covered by run 6 and the hole side whose model pair is unresolved in the accessible density window.

Uniform Δ(1/B)=0.008333 T^-1 gives 3 points per 40 T period (including scale/local-density margin), with 3 s point dwell =10τ plus the blocking magnet's 3 s settle. From the handoff at 0 T, the total traverse to 2 T costs 2/0.137=14.6 min; 241×(3 s dwell+3 s magnet settle+~0.1 s read)=24.5 min, plus setup/margin gives the quoted estimate. This is a prerequisite within the requested fan arc.

Preflight asserts committed validators, base/fresh thermometry, heaters off, magnet ready near 0 T, unchanged drive/filter settings and Si=0. Check the first 25 points for range/leakage/current, then halfway and at completion; interrupt on sustained leakage above 1 nA, range approaching 90%, or base-T loss. Field ends at 2 T for the fan direction decision. On this VTI an interrupt does not halt an in-progress supply ramp: read actual field after interruption and notify the operator if the physical ramp must stop.
