## HERA — hole-side Landau fan for density trajectories (`landau-fan-vti`)

**Decision and evidence.** Run 7 is the cleaner low-field SdH record: `sdh_onset` returns **0.713877 T**, stable over B-background degrees 2–6; run 6's electron onset is censored and extraction-dependent (ana cells 14–15). Follow `tetralayer-stacking-fs-count` Steps 3–5 with one compact hole fan, on the provided B1−B2 pair. This tests whether candidate tones disperse as pockets, harmonics or sums; **it is not a promise of a resolved two-pocket count**. Gates A/B remain satisfied (measure setup execs 1–15; SAFETIES cell 18 exactly matches TG [−0.3,1], BG [−1.3,2], both Si validators [−25,25], B ±10 T and both setpoint caps 100 K); E1 grant and green VERIFY are logged, and operator handoff cells 44/46 leave B≈0 and the same kernel BOUND. The cell rechecks these commitments and actual state before any write.

**Coordinates and grid.** Ridge-A run-5 calibration, c_tg=2.796059 mF/m², c_bg=2.160696 mF/m², n_offset=−0.089379×10¹² cm⁻², D_offset=−0.013 V/nm; ratio data-calibrated, scale geometry-prior. D≈0, Si=0, n **−1.20→−0.95×10¹² cm⁻², 10 points**; TG −0.27703→−0.20541 V and BG −0.46504→−0.37235 V. B **0.713877→6 T, 75 points uniform in 1/B**, ascending from the present zero field and ending at 6 T for any subsequent descending acquisition. Shared n=−1.20 anchors quality against run 7; changing uniform-B to inverse-B tests aliases. The unmeasured electron side is excluded because its onset needs a separate extension; the near-CNP hole side is excluded because period count falls below five before the 10 T cap. Full D≈0 density coverage would require new onset/sensitivity validation and a substantially larger grid: at this same Blo/dwell, the entire allowed −1.27…+3.27 window would already cost roughly **30 h** with scale- and second-harmonic-aware Nyquist, before those checks. Extending only this fan from −1.20 to −1.25 adds density points and a still less resolvable pair; it does not improve the present discriminator.

**Feasibility and limits.** Direct `fs_count_b0.abab_mstar` evaluation gives ABAB F={5.863,6.545} T at n=−1.20 and {4.069,5.751} T at −0.95; ABCA F_L spans 12.407→9.822 T. The README's interpolated hole example in the earlier spot proposal was approximate; these are the solver values. Total Δ(1/B)=1.23414 T⁻¹ gives δF=0.8103 T and **5.02 periods** for the smallest predicted partner at −0.95. Six tesla is the rounded minimum endpoint for that five-period condition (~5.92 T); going to 10 T buys only ~0.27 more partner periods. The ABAB pair separation is only 0.682 T at −1.20, below 2δF; at −0.95 it is 1.682 T, only marginally above it. The observed slower 4.7 T candidate turns on at ~1.03 T, so its usable record is shorter still: **pair non-detection cannot exclude ABAB**. The measurable test is dominant-branch trajectories versus F_L, with one global scale and the secondary ridge carried as a confound, followed by ABAB/trilayer dual overlays and harmonic/sum-tone checks. ABCB remains citation-grade; independent layer number and region separation remain unresolved.

**Both-axis sampling and acquisition.** Δ(1/B)=0.0166775 T⁻¹ (Nyquist 29.98 T) covers the observed 26.2 T candidate and up to twice F_L at the high-density edge with a 20% scale allowance. It gives ≥4.8 samples per scale-expanded fundamental and improves on run 7's ~0.038 T⁻¹ spacing at onset. Ray spacing at Blo is 0.06905×10¹² cm⁻²; Δn=0.027778, expanded by 1.2 to 0.03333, stays below half that spacing. **75×10=750 points**, each with interruptible **3 s = 10τ** gate settling at τ=0.3 s / 24 dB; the magnet's settle cannot substitute for gate settling within a row. Fixed sr1=20 µV (2 kΩ full scale), sr0=20 mV, drive 1 V/100 MΩ=10 nA. Run 7's full 10 T maximum was 587 Ω; even a conservative (1.2/0.95)² low-density extrapolation gives 937 Ω, leaving >2× headroom, and this fan stops at 6 T. Gates retain existing step/pacing; no Si, temperature or safety-surface changes.

**Timing basis.** 0→6 T / 0.137 T min⁻¹ = 43.8 min bare traverse, including 5.2 min positioning to the first row. Run 5 median point interval was 1.0985 s with 1 s dwell at NPLC=1; budget **3.25 s/point** for the paired gate sets, added vaporizer read and 3 s dwell. Runs 6/7 gave 7.46/11.83 s per 0.01/0.02 T step, consistent with ~3.08 s fixed field overhead plus ramp; budget **4 s/row** for settle, short flyback and row checks. Thus 750×3.25 s +75×4 s+43.8 min ≈89.4 min; the headline includes ~12% margin.

**Checkpoints and stop conditions.** Read the first **three landed rows** (~7–10 min including initial positioning) for every recorded channel, leakage, clipping, drain-current liveness and measured field tracking; then every **20 rows or 20 min**, whichever first, and at completion. A first-rows quality pass licenses continuation, not a stacking claim; interim frequency interpretation waits for sufficient field span. The cell stops on >1 nA TG/BG current, 90% of either lock-in full scale, drain current outside 8–12 nA, nonfinite reads, base-T drift, stale thermometry or field mismatch. Every followed value (both temperatures, both gate currents, current/voltage X and phase) has a live plot. After any stop, inspect actual state before another move. **VTI-specific:** a kernel interrupt does not stop a field ramp already commanded; the supply finishes its current target, and a physical ramp stop is operator work. Complete data go raw-first to ana, then per-column FFT stability/dual overlays and a strength-qualified verdict; no automatic return traverse. On completion hold B=6 T and final gates while reading the result.
