## Runs 6–7 — SdH spot checks and fan design evidence

Raw traces precede the onset/FFT analysis below. Both runs completed with stable measured current (9.81–9.94 nA), T_sample 1.165–1.292 K, and no clipping (Rxx peaks 530 and 587 ohm against 5 kohm full scale). Run 6's electron spectrum has components near 7, 14, 22 and 29 T; their near-integer ratios do not establish harmonics, and the 7+22≈29 T partition requires density tracking. Run 7 has stronger hole-side oscillations, but the accessible D=0 hole window ends at -1.27e12, where the model ABAB pair at -1.2e12 is 5.86/6.54 T and is unresolved against delta_F≈0.77 T even over 0.714–10 T. Choose the electron side for discriminatory frequency trajectories, not on amplitude alone.

**Onset caveat:** the default run-6 fit across 0.3–10 T returns a censored 0.3 T onset with low-field revival; the large 8–10 T MR feature contaminates the B-polynomial background. Restricting the fit to 0.3–4 T gives `sdh_onset.onset_b=1.031 T` at 6.74 T (degree 5); the same tone dies off at 1.04/1.05 T with degree 3/7, although the helper excludes it as marginal in those fits and the aggregate onset becomes 1.38 T. This is an analysis-dependent onset, not evidence for oscillations at 0.3 T. Use 1.031 T as the acquisition floor and test usable windows per tone after the fan. The old 50 mT grid below 1 T also fails the fast-tone Nyquist floor there; it cannot support a claim of a low onset. The fan changes to uniform 1/B and retains 3 s settling (10 tau at 24 dB/oct).

**Current verdict: B_1–B_2 uninformative for a unique stacking assignment.** The frequencies advance feasibility, but the full set has not yet passed a global density-scale fit, density-dependent harmonic screen, ABAB-versus-trilayer overlay, or F_L-origin audit. The second CNP ridge in run 5 is a spatial-density confound; the single wired pair cannot separately resolve physical regions. Absolute n remains geometry-prior scale, D=0 follows run-5 ridge symmetry, and no positive/negative-B symmetrization is available.
