## HERA — electron D≈0 Landau fan for the stacking investigation

**Decision — `tetralayer-stacking-fs-count` Steps 4–5, acquisition `landau-fan-vti`.** Runs 6/7 are complete, unclipped and electrically live; their raw and FFT reads are in ana cells 14–15. The electron spot at n_A=+3.2e12 has candidates near 7, 14–15, 22–23 and 29–30 T. Their harmonic/sum-tone alternatives and the two run-5 CNP ridges prevent a stacking assignment from one density. Track them across **n_A=+2.8…+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² at D_A=0** on the only wired Rxx region, B_1–B_2; sr2/sr3 are n.c. and excluded. Ratio and offsets come from run 5; absolute density remains the geometry prior (carry ~10–15% scale uncertainty, fit one scale to the entire peak set later).

**Onset and range.** `sdh_onset` on run 6 over 0.3–4 T, degree 5, returns **1.03090969 T** for the ~6.7 T component (uncensored, max SNR 18.3). Degrees 3/7 place that component at 1.04–1.05 T but mark it marginal, and give the ~22 T branch onset 1.38 T. Full 0.3–10 T detrending gives a censored 0.3 T result that changes with the high-field MR background; the diagnostic is in ana. We retain this extraction fork and use the earlier, uncensored 1.03 T onset, not a sub-onset background floor. There is no absence claim below it. **B_hi=7.1 T**: the ABAB model (`fs_count_b0.abab_fs`) gives light/heavy F=6.038/22.914 T at n=2.8, 7.306/25.783 T at n=3.2. At the lower density, 5/f_light=0.8281 T⁻¹, requiring B_hi≥7.047 T; this fan gives 5.006 periods and δF=1.205 T. The ABCA–ABAB heavy-tone split is ≥6.04 T, above 2δF. Five light-tone periods are a **nominal-calibration** feasibility result, not a guarantee under the scale/onset uncertainty; reassess the observed low-density branch before claiming a complete count.

**Sampling and cost floor.** F_L(n_max)=33.09 T. Inherit the spot's finest-edge inverse-field spacing (10 mT at 1 T → Δ(1/B)=0.009901 T⁻¹); the implemented first segment is 0.009776 T⁻¹, giving 3.09 samples/F_L period. Above 3.5 T refine to 0.003449 T⁻¹ (Nyquist 145 T) because run 6 resolves ~58, 88 and 130 T harmonics at higher fields; below 3.5 T the observed highest tracked tone is ~44 T, below the first segment's 51.15 T Nyquist. This is **113 B rows**, segmented uniform in 1/B, monotonically ascending. This changed grid also tests the run-6 uniform-B aliases. On the density axis, g=4 ray spacing at B_lo is 0.09971e12 cm⁻²: the ≥2-sample floor requires **10 densities**, Δn=0.04444e12. Higher-harmonic density structure is not a map-resolution claim; analyze each n-column and compare the shared n=3.2 trace directly with run 6.

**Settling, range, price.** Hold τ=0.3 s, 24 dB/oct and **fast_delay=3 s (10τ)** at every gate point, inheriting the actual spot-check settling; magnet settling applies only between rows. Fixed sr1 sensitivity 50 µV = 5 kΩ at 10 nA, as in both spots. Run-6 R_max=530 Ω through 10 T; scaling by 3.2/2.8 and an additional factor 3 for the unmeasured low-n corner gives 1.82 kΩ, comfortably inside 5 kΩ (explicit conservative heuristic, guarded live). From run 5, median point time 1.0985 s at 1 s dwell and NPLC=1 implies ~0.10 s overhead; budget 0.30 s for paired gates plus added readbacks. Grid floor: 113×10×3.3 s = 62.2 min. Blocking ramp from actual ~0 T to 7.1 T: 7.1×7.3=51.8 min (the 0→1.03 T positioning is included). Budget 7 s/row for magnet settle, dual-gate flyback and slow dwell = 13.2 min; +10% contingency gives the stated duration. Runs 6/7 independently measured ~3.08 s non-traverse overhead per field point, including the 3 s magnet dwell.

**Coverage trade.** Exclude holes, CNP and electrons below 2.8e12: holes are restricted to |n|≤1.27e12 by the committed gates, and lower electron density fails the five-partner-period criterion in this onset/window. The chosen 0.4e12 window changes the heavy branch by 2.87 T, enough to test a trajectory but only over this region. Covering the full allowed D=0 line on the same ray grid needs ~93 densities and ~12 h with the same contingency; it would still leave those low-density pocket counts unresolved. No operating-limit expansion is proposed.

**Execution and checkpoints.** Gate A and B remain green in the same kernel (SAFETIES cell 18, VERIFY 32, handoff/probe 44/46); recorded E1 remains engaged. Abort on a quality failure; do not infer a missing pocket from the sub-onset early rows. Magnet interruption does not stop an active FAST-PS ramp (rig card); this cell only sets small monotonic steps after positioning. A stop requires actual-state readback and E0/re-grant handling.
