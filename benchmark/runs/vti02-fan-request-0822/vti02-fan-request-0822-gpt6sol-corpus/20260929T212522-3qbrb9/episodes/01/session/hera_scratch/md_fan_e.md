## HERA — electron-side D = 0 Landau fan for the tetralayer FS count (`landau-fan-vti`)

Runs 6–7 (ana cells 14–16) favor the electron side: run 6 at n = +3.2×10¹² has ≈7, 22 and 29 T content, while the hole spot is restricted to the near-CNP exception regime. This fan asks whether those components follow two branches, a branch plus harmonic/sum orbit, or one Luttinger-frequency pocket across density; the ≈29 T line is not assigned in advance. The run-5 CNP calibration fixes D = 0 and the gate ratio, but its absolute n scale remains a geometry prior; region B's parallel ridge may add a second local density.

**Grid and cost:** n = +3.2→+2.7×10¹² cm⁻² in 12 paired-gate points, B = 1→10 T in 901 uniform-B rows (10 mT), with 2.4 s fast dwell (8τ at the recorded 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct), fixed sr1 50 µV sensitivity, and 10 nA nominal bias. At n_max, F_L = 33.1 T and the worst Δ(1/B) = 0.0099 T⁻¹ gives ≈3 samples/period; the 0.0455×10¹² density step gives 2.1 samples per ray period at 1 T. The ABAB model's light branch at n_min is ≈5.7 T, giving ≈5.1 periods over 1–10 T (δF = 1.11 T). Starting from the handoff's 0 T costs ≈7 min of bare positioning; the 9 T recorded traverse is ≥66 min, 10,812 grid points × (2.4 s dwell + ~0.25 s read) ≈8.0 h, 901 driver settles × 3 s ≈45 min, plus row/reset overhead; run 6's actual 2.0 h for 921 field points supports **≈11 h** with margin. The global worst R corner is the low-n/high-B end; run 6 peaked at 530 Ω, so 5 kΩ full scale provides headroom with usable resolution. Coverage is deliberately the high-density electron window; including the entire D = 0 gate line to the hole limit would require roughly eight times as many density points at the 1 T ray-Nyquist floor and would cross the high-MR CNP.

**Preflight and checkpoints:** The cell checks live thermometry, base T, the handoff's near-zero field and gates, Si = 0, heater/output state, bias and lock-in τ/slope, and validator acceptance before moving. Watch the first three landed B rows for flat/railed channels, leakage and wrong gate coordinates; review at approximately 3, 6 and 9 T for sustained leakage, field/temperature drift, clipping, and emerging fan structure. A sustained >1 nA Keithley gate current on two consecutive grid points requests a sweep stop. After the sweep, read the native TSV before FFT, overlay both ABAB and ABCA forward-model F(n), pin one global density scale, screen harmonics and the 29 T sum-tone possibility, and retain region-B mosaic and adjacent-layer-number alternatives if the trajectories demand them. Leave the field at 10 T for the next science decision and return the gates to 0 V after the sweep.
