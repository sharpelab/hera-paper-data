The fixed-current Rxx estimate and the measured-current trace agree because drain current varies by <1% in each run. Sensitivity was 50 µV (≈5 kΩ at 10 nA); raw Rxx is −33…530 Ω (electron) and approximately 0…587 Ω (hole), with neither channel railing and gate-current extrema below 0.22 nA. The temperature logged 1.17–1.29 K. The electron run has a reproducible broad ≈7 T component and high-field ≈30 T structure; a 7 T comb/harmonic assignment remains possible, so this spot alone does not identify the stacking. The hole run has stronger low-frequency oscillations but is pinned to the model's near-CNP overlap exception and cannot span the design density. The onset tracker labels the 7.1 T electron component censored at 0.30 T; the resolved higher components appear at 1.36–1.91 T. Use B = 1.0–10 T for the fan, retaining the onset shoulder while sampling the full-density tone; this excludes sub-1 T content whose spot-check grid cannot resolve at F_L = 33 T. This is a coverage limit, not an absence claim.
