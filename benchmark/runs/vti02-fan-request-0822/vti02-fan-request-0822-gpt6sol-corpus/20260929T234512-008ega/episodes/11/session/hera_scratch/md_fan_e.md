## HERA — Step 4: D = 0 electron Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`)

Runs 6–7 establish the side choice: run 6 at n = +3.2×10¹² has oscillations and a 7 T / high-field ≈30 T peak set whose harmonic origin needs an n-dependent read; run 7's hole side is limited to n ≥ −1.27×10¹², the model's near-CNP exception. Run-5 calibration gives D = 0 gate pairs from n = +3.2 to +2.7×10¹². The model predicts ABAB [7.31, 25.78] T at +3.2 and [5.72, 22.19] T at +2.7 versus ABCA F_L = 33.09 and 27.92 T. This fan will resolve the trajectory and test a single global density scale, the Luttinger sum, and harmonic/breakdown alternatives; the spot checks alone do not make a stacking call.

Use 91 field rows, **uniform in 1/B from 1 to 10 T**, and 17 paired gate points from n = +3.2 to +2.7×10¹² at D = 0. The 1/B step is 0.010 T⁻¹: 3.0 points per period at F_L(3.2) = 33.09 T, above the 2-point Nyquist floor. The n step is 0.03125×10¹² cm⁻², giving 3.1 points per g = 4 ray period at 1 T. The light ABAB model branch at the low-density edge has 5.15 periods across this field window; ideal δF = 1.11 T. The resolved high-frequency spot-check components appeared at 1.36–1.91 T, leaving δF ≈ 1.6–2.4 T even if their onset limits the usable record; the heavy-ABAB / ABCA separation of ≈5.7–7.3 T remains testable. **Coverage trade:** the fan excludes n < +2.7, where the light branch lacks five periods on this field span, and B < 1 T, where run 6's 7.1 T tracker is censored at the record edge and its coarse spot grid does not Nyquist F_L. No absence claim applies in those excluded regions.

The run starts from the handoff's 0 T, 0 V gates and positions to 1 T with a blocking ≈7.3 min ramp; it ends at 10 T for a possible measured ride-down later. Use run 6's 50 µV fixed Rxx sensitivity and 0.3 s / 24 dB filter; 1.8 s fast delay meets 6τ, with the magnet's 3 s settle on each field row. Full scale is ≈5 kΩ at 10 nA; run 6's raw high-field maximum was ≈530 Ω, giving headroom as density decreases to +2.7. Timing from runs 6/7: ≈66 min field traverse + 91×17×(1.8 s dwell plus ≈0.2–0.3 s gates/reads) + row settles and initial position, with ≈10% overrun allowance, ≈2 h 25 m. Before moving, the cell prints and asserts live thermometry, base T, 0 T, V_si = 0, and magnet heater/output. Check the first two complete rows for leakage, flat/railed Rxx, and gate tracking; then check again near 5 and 8 T for the low-n/high-B worst corner and use a partial frequency read if the signal warrants it. Abort a safety or acquisition failure; magnet ramp interruption is operator-side only.
