## HERA — Step 4: electron D = 0 Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`)

Runs 6–7 (ana cells 14–21) contain SdH but leave the pocket count open: the electron ~7 T family has higher features near integer multiples, and the hole line is confined to the model's near-CNP exception. I choose the electron window n = +2.50…+3.20×10¹² cm⁻² at D = 0, using run 5's region-A calibration (ratio data-calibrated, absolute scale geometry-prior). The fixed 50 µV sensitivity has 5 kΩ full scale at the live 10 nA bias: run 6 reached 522 Ω at n = 3.2; scaling 1/n to n = 2.5 gives ~670 Ω, leaving >7× nominal headroom. The first rows must confirm this estimate at the low-n corner.

**Feasibility and coverage:** From the tetralayer model table, the ABAB partner at n = 2.5 is ~5.15 T (interpolated +2/+3 rows) and F_L at n = 3.2 is 33.1 T. B = 0.9→10 T gives Δ(1/B) = 1.011 T⁻¹, ~5.2 partner periods at the low-n edge, δF ≈ 0.99 T, and a ~5.15 T ABAB-heavy vs ABCA-singleton split (>2δF). The 102 uniform-1/B rows have step 0.01001 T⁻¹, within the preferred 1/(3F_L) = 0.01007; 18 density points give Δn = 0.0412×10¹² cm⁻², ~2.1 samples per fourfold LL ray at 0.9 T. This deliberately excludes n < 2.5 (too few partner periods) and the hole side (no high-|n| D = 0 reach); full D = 0 coverage would pay extra fan time while diluting the stacking test. The 0.9 T floor is above the run-6 censored ~7 T onset and avoids treating its coarsely sampled 0.3–1 T fast-tone content as resolved.

**Time and checkpoints:** 0→0.9→10 T costs ~73 min at 0.137 T/min; 102×18 points at 2.4 s fast delay plus reads, 3 s field settle per row, and gate retrace cost ~1 h 35 min. Allow ~10% over that model (runs 6–7 completed ~7–11% faster than their quoted ETAs): **~3 h**. Start with B ≈ 0, base sample/vaporizer T, heaters off, Si = 0 V, persistent-switch heater/output on, and 1 V drive with τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB; the cell prints and asserts the actuals before moving hardware. First 2–3 rows: inspect all live channels, leakage, Rxx headroom, and field readback; abort for sustained >1 nA gate current, railing, or dead/flat Rxx. Thereafter inspect the `hera-watch` row digests every ~20 rows and at ~1.5 and 3 T; continue only if temperature, leakage, and channel quality remain sound. A partial FFT before ~3 T is resolution-limited, so a null there does not justify an early physics stop. The fan ends at 10 T for a possible measured ride-down after analysis.
