## HERA — Step 4: electron D≈0 Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`)

Runs 6–7 favor the electron side for frequency counting: run 6 at n=+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² has oscillations with content near 7 and 22–30 T, while the hole side is limited by the TG validator to |n|≈1.27×10¹² and gives a close-tone blend. The run-6 low-tone onset finder is **censored** at its 0.3 T record edge and likely includes low-frequency background; the 22 T and 29.5 T components appear near 1.36 and 1.91 T. This fan tests their density trajectories against the run-5 D=0 calibration (ratio measured; absolute density still geometry-prior) and the `tetralayer-stacking-fs-count` forward model. It is evidence gathering, not a stacking assignment.

The chosen n=+2.65→+3.20×10¹² cm⁻² window excludes the CNP and hole side because the former would enlarge the dynamic-range cost and the latter cannot reach the model's separation regime under the TG limit. The model predicts, at the high end, an ABAB pair near 7.5/25.6 T versus an ABCA singleton at F_L≈33.1 T; at the low end the ABAB light partner is ≈5.6 T. B=1.0→10 T gives ≈5.0 light-partner periods at that edge and FFT resolution δF=1.11 T, so a 5.6 T partner is distinct from an F_L singleton. The 97-point uniform-1/B grid has Δ(1/B)=0.00938 T⁻¹, ≈3.2 samples per fastest 33.1 T period (Nyquist ≤0.0151); 19 n points at Δn=0.0306×10¹² cm⁻² give ≈3 samples per g=4 LL ray spacing 0.0967×10¹² at 1 T. This is the cheapest grid that meets the two-axis preferred sampling floor; it omits lower n, where the ABAB partner has too few periods, and a full gate-range fan would cost several times longer without strengthening this first read.

Run 6 reached 522 Ω at 10 T at n=3.2; the fixed sr1 sensitivity of 50 µV gives 5 kΩ full scale at 10 nA, leaving headroom for the lower-density/high-B corner. The 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct lock-in gets 2.1 s fast-axis dwell (7τ). From field 0, positioning to 1 T takes 7.3 min and the 1→10 T traverse at 0.137 T/min takes 65.7 min. The 97×19=1843 grid points at ≈2.4 s each (2.1 s dwell plus run-5/6 NPLC=1 readback) add ≈74 min; row startup/flyback and observed 5–10% overrun make **≈2 h 45 m** realistic. First-rows checkpoint: inspect the first 2–3 B rows for both gate currents, sample T, current channel, rail/clipping and the actual paired n-line; abort on sustained |I_gate|>1 nA or a dead/railed Rxx channel. Recheck at B≈3, 6, and 8 T for drift and partial oscillation structure; stop if the data no longer supports a density-dependent frequency read. The fan ends at 10 T with gates at the low-n endpoint for the next science decision.
