## HERA — Step 4: electron D = 0 Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`)

Runs 6–7 and the ana read (cells 14–17) make the electron side the discriminating choice: run 6 has strong oscillations and unresolved harmonic/sum-tone content; the hole-side D = 0 line is limited to |n| ≤ 1.27×10¹². Use run-5 ridge-A calibration (ratio 0.773 data-calibrated, absolute density geometry-prior) for n = +2.50→+3.20×10¹² cm⁻² at D = 0; ridge B may add a second series-region population shifted by +0.43×10¹², so classify its tracks separately in analysis.

**Design floor.** The inverse-field grid is B = 0.75→10 T in 140 rows (Δ(1/B) = 0.00887 T⁻¹): 3.0 samples per period at the worst expected F_L = 37.5 T including ridge B, and δF = 0.81 T. The 23-point n-line has Δn = 0.0318×10¹², 2.28 samples per g = 4 ray at 0.75 T. At n = 2.5 the ABAB model's small tone is 5.09 T, giving 6.3 periods (5.0 even at a 0.8 density scale); the model's heavy-vs-ABCA split is 5.09 T. The 0.3–0.75 T record is excluded to keep this first fan near four hours: the spot check's 7 T onset is censored at 0.3 T, while its low-field 50 mT grid aliases the fast tone. This fan tests the needed frequency separation with a correctly sampled grid; lower n and that extra low-field span would cost many more rows and cannot make the narrow hole side decisive.

**Quality and price.** Keep sr1 at 50 µV sensitivity (5 kΩ at ~10 nA), fixed through the fan: run 6 peaks at 530 Ω, run 7 at 587 Ω, leaving margin for the lower-n/high-B corner. Use 2.4 s fast delay = 8τ at τ = 0.3 s/24 dB; run 5 gives ~0.17 s per point beyond its 1 s delay. Price: 3,220 points × ~2.57 s ≈ 2 h 18 m, plus 10 T/0.137 ≈ 73 m bare field traverse, 140 × 3 s field-set settle ≈ 7 m, gate turnarounds and 10% margin → ~4 h 10 m. Preconditions below require live base thermometry, field near 0, Si at 0, heaters off, magnet output/PS on, unchanged validators and contact drive; the gates and field are then set inside the committed envelope. Check the first two rows for leakage, railing and a responsive sr1 channel; revisit the landed-row digest near 2, 4, 6 and 8 T for continued signal and temperature. Abort on sustained leakage (>1 nA) or rail (≥90% full scale); a watchdog abort leaves the field where its completed set landed. End at 10 T with gates at n = 2.5 for a possible follow-up without a retraverse.
