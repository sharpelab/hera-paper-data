## HERA — Step 4: D≈0 electron Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`)

Runs 6–7 are live and unsaturated; run 6 has electron content near 7 T and 22–30 T whose harmonic/independent-pocket origin changes with the FFT window. Run 7 is restricted to |n|≤1.27×10¹² by the TG limit, where the tetralayer model's hole tones are a near-CNP exception. This fan follows the cleaner, broader electron side; it measures how tones move with density before any stacking verdict.

**Design and coverage:** run-5 region-A calibration (ratio 0.773, geometry-prior scale, D offset −0.013 V/nm) gives D=0 and n=2.3→3.2×10¹² cm⁻² at TG +0.726→+0.984 V and BG +0.833→+1.166 V, inside committed validators. The ABAB model predicts light/heavy tones 4.48/19.3 T at n=2.3 and 7.31/25.78 T at n=3.2; ABCA has one tone at F_L=23.78→33.09 T. The 0.6→10 T record has Δ(1/B)=1.567 T⁻¹: ≥7 periods of the binding 4.48 T tone, δF=0.638 T, and >2δF separation between the heavy ABAB branch and F_L. 157 uniform-1/B rows give Δ(1/B)=0.01004 T⁻¹, 3.01 samples per period at F_L,max; 33 n points give Δn=0.0281×10¹² cm⁻², 2.06 points per ray period at 0.6 T. The onset helper on run 6 calls 0.30 T a **censored record edge**, not a measured die-off; retaining 0.3–0.6 T would improve δF to ~0.31 T but, at the same sampling floors, multiply the grid to ~20,000 points. This cost choice excludes that low-field band and any claim requiring it, while retaining the five-period criterion at n=2.3.

**Dynamic range and time:** run 6 peaks at 530 Ω at n=3.2, B=10 T; run 7 reaches 587 Ω at the lower accessible |n|. The worst corner here is n=2.3, B=10 T, with no direct anchor; SR1 at 200 µV gives 20 kΩ full scale at 10 nA (>30× the measured spot maxima), and early/periodic rail checks guard that extrapolation. The fan has 5,181 points. Run 5 measured 1.17 s/point at NPLC=1; adding 1.8 s fast dwell (6τ for τ=0.3 s, 24 dB) gives ~4.3 h of grid time, plus 10 T/0.137 T min⁻¹ = 73 min of field travel, 0.6 T positioning, and ~10% slack: **about 6 h**. The field rises monotonically from the current 0 T to 10 T; gates end at the last density and stay there for the follow-up decision.

**Checkpoints:** first three B rows: leakage, T, SR1 clipping, SR0 current and a live Rxx trace; abort on >1 nA sustained gate current, a rail, or dead/flat signal.
