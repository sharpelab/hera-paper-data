## HERA — Step 4: electron-side D = 0 Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`)

Runs 6–7 and the ana read above favor the electron side: run 6 at +3.2×10¹² cm⁻² resolves a ~30 T high-frequency component, while its possible 5–9 T partner changes with FFT cutoff; the hole side is confined to the investigation's near-CNP exception regime. Run 5 calibrates the D = 0 line; this fan covers n = +2.4…+3.2×10¹² cm⁻² on that line, with 25 density points and 91 rows uniform in 1/B from 1 to 10 T. The +2.4…+2.7 edge is exploratory: its predicted light ABAB pocket has fewer than five periods in this field window, so absence there cannot rule it out; frequencies at n ≥ +2.7 are the claim region. This excludes the hole side and lower electron densities because their attainable 1/B record cannot distinguish the ABAB partner from a singleton at this electron temperature.

The grid gives Δ(1/B) = 0.0100 T⁻¹, at least three samples per 33.1 T Luttinger period at n_max, and Δn = 0.0333×10¹² cm⁻², about 2.9 samples per n-axis LL spacing at 1 T. Its frequency resolution is δF = 1.11 T, much smaller than the model's heavy-ABAB versus ABCA separation near +3×10¹². The fixed 50 µV sensitivity gives 5 kΩ full scale at 10 nA: run 6 peaked at 523 Ω, and the run-5 zero-field resistance at the fan's low-n edge is ~66 Ω; the high-B low-n corner still gets a first-rows rail check. At τ = 0.3 s and 24 dB/oct, 3 s fast delay is 10τ. Run 6 took 120 min for 921 points over 10 T; using its ~3.1 s/point beyond ramp and the fan's 2,275 points at 3 s gate delay gives ~3 h 25 min including the 0→10 T traverse, priced at **3 h 40 min** with the observed 5–10% overrun margin.

Before motion, the cell reads live thermometry, field, Si, heater ranges, PS/output, lock-in settling, and validator values. First checkpoint: the first 2–3 B rows for leakage, clipping, dead channel, and temperature; stop if any fail. Then inspect landed rows near 3 T and 6 T for coherent dispersing oscillations and a preliminary per-column frequency read; stop early if the fan has washed out, saving the remaining sweep. The magnet ramp is blocking and continues to its target if the kernel is interrupted mid-ramp; do not use interrupt as a magnet stop. The cell ends at 10 T and leaves the gates on the last density point for the next science decision.
