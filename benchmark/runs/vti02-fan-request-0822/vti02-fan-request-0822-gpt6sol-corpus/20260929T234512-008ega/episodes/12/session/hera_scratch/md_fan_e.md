## HERA — Step 4: D = 0 electron Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`)

Runs 6–7 establish live SdH on the hardwired B₁–B₂ pair; the electron-side 7/22/29 T family at n = +3.2×10¹² cm⁻² needs an n-trajectory to separate genuine pockets from harmonics and a possible F_L sum orbit. This fan follows region A's run-5 D = 0 calibration over n = +2.2→+3.2×10¹² cm⁻², where the tetralayer forward model predicts an ABAB light branch ≈4–7.5 T and heavy branch ≈19–26 T, against an ABCA singleton F_L = 22.7–33.1 T; the hole side is excluded because its gate limit reaches only −1.27×10¹² and run 7 sits in the model's near-CNP two-tone exception regime. At the 0.7→10 T, 101-row uniform-1/B grid, Δ(1/B) = 0.01329 T⁻¹ gives 2.27 samples per fastest F_L period; 33 n points give Δn = 0.03125×10¹² cm⁻², 2.17 samples per ray at 0.7 T. The 1/B span is 1.329 T⁻¹ (δF = 0.75 T), giving ~5.6 periods of the lowest-density ~4.2 T partner and resolving its separation from F_L; the 0.7 T floor uses run 7's measured 0.71 T onset, while run 6's 0.3 T onset is censored at the record edge and contaminated by low-frequency residuals.

Run 6's Rxx max was 0.53 kΩ and run 7's 0.59 kΩ at 10 T; 50 µV is 5 kΩ full scale at the measured 10 nA drive, leaving headroom at the lower-density/high-field corner while retaining useful resolution. Fast delay 1.8 s is 6τ at the read-back τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB; slow delay is 0 because each blocking `magnet.field` set includes its 3 s settle. From the handoff B ≈ 0, the bare ramp to 0.7 T costs 5 min and the 9.3 T fan traverse costs 68 min at 0.137 T/min; 3333 points at ~2.1–2.2 s each (run 5's 1.17 s/pt with 1 s fast delay, plus 0.8 s) and row returns give **~3 h 30 min**. Check the first 2–3 landed rows for gate leakage, drain current, rails and live temperature; recheck near B ≈ 1.5, 3 and 6 T for a dispersing signal and stop early if the channel clips, leakage trips, temperature leaves base, or no useful oscillations survive. The field ends at 10 T for a possible measured return leg; gates park at 0 V on exit.
