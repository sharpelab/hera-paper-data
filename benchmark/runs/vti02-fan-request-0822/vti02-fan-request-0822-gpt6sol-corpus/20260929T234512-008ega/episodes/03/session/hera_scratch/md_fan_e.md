## HERA — Step 4: D≈0 electron Landau fan (`landau-fan-vti`)

Runs 6–7 put the useful field-dependent content on the electron side: run 6 at n=+3.2×10¹² has a ~30 T high-field feature and ~7/15/22 T peaks whose harmonic relationship prevents a one-point stacking call; run 7 is near the hole-side CNP and its tones overlap. This fan follows `tetralayer-stacking-fs-count` Step 4, using run-5 region-A calibration at D=0 and the run-6 settling and 50 µV sensitivity. It scans n=+1.8→+3.2×10¹² cm⁻²; the hole side is excluded because the D=0 gate line reaches only −1.27×10¹² inside the validators and the −1.2 spot is model-exception/thermal-limited. A full two-side fan would cost a second multi-hour traverse yet not supply a high-|n| hole discriminator.

**Computed feasibility.** The forward model at n=+1.8 gives ABAB (2.94, 15.67) T versus ABCA 18.61 T; at +3.2 it gives (7.31, 25.78) T versus 33.08 T. The electron ~30 T spot feature is not an unambiguous ABCA pocket: a fourth harmonic of ~7.5 T or a breakdown/sum orbit can land there. The 1.2→10 T record gives δF=1.36 T; the ABAB-heavy/ABCA split is 2.94 T at the low-density edge (just above 2δF) and 7.31 T at the high edge. The ABAB light branch has ~5.4 periods only at the high-density end; absence at low n will carry no weight. Uniform-1/B grid: **74 B rows** at Δ(1/B)=0.01005 T⁻¹, meeting 3 samples per F_L=33.08 T period. **31 density points** at Δn=0.0467×10¹², giving 2.49 samples per fourfold LL ray at B=1.2 T. Run-5 Rxx at n=1.8 is ~383 Ω at 0 T; scaling by run-6's ~3.7× MR gives ~1.4 kΩ for the worst corner, below 5 kΩ full scale with ~3.5× headroom. Stop at the first rows if that estimate fails.

**Price and checkpoints.** 2,294 points × (3.0 s fast dwell + ~0.2 s read/set overhead from run 5) ≈ 2 h 02 m; 8.8 T magnet traverse at 0.137 T/min ≈ 64 m, plus 0→1.2 T positioning ≈ 9 m, row overhead and margin → **3 h 40 m**. The fast dwell is 10τ for SR1 at τ=0.3 s / 24 dB, preserving FFT amplitudes across the paired gate line. First 2–3 rows: inspect all channels, gate currents, temperature freshness, clipping and gate trajectory; abort on sustained >1 nA leakage, rails, wrong state or a dead channel. Then check landed rows near 3, 6 and 8 T, with partial raw maps and frequency tracks where the 1/B span permits; continue only while the record can distinguish branch trajectories. The cell parks TG/BG at 0 after completion and leaves B at 10 T for a possible measured ride-down.
