The field goes **0→0.8 T by a blocking bare ramp** (0.8/0.137 = 5.8 min) and then 0.8→10 T as 116 monotonic rows uniform in 1/B. The 9.2 T traverse floor is 67.2 min. The full record has 1.15 T⁻¹ extent, δF = 0.87 T and ≥5.5 periods of the model's 4.79 T edge partner. Check the first rows for leakage, current, rail, T and field; revisit at B ≈ 2, 4, 6 and 8 T for a raw row and partial spectral read, and interrupt on a failed channel or sustained leak.
