## HERA — Step 4: electron D = 0 Landau fan for the stacking call (`landau-fan-vti`)

Run 5 calibrated the region-A D = 0 line and exposed a parallel region-B ridge (density offset ≈ +0.43×10¹² cm⁻²). Run 6 at n = +3.20×10¹² shows ~7–8 T low-frequency content and a ~30 T high-field feature, but their origin is ambiguous in one trace; run 7 is near the hole-side CNP and its peaks change with FFT window (ana cells 14–18). The governing `tetralayer-stacking-fs-count` Step 4 therefore calls for **one electron-side fan**: independent F(n) branches, their Luttinger sum, and the onset/field dependence can separate ABAB pockets, an ABCA singleton, harmonics, and possible series-mosaic contributions. No stacking assignment is made from either spot check alone.

The gate line has 22 points, n = +2.40→+3.20×10¹² cm⁻² (0.0381×10¹² step), corresponding to Vtg +0.7544→+0.9836 V and Vbg +0.8697→+1.1663 V. The field grid is 131 rows, +0.8→+10.0 T, uniform in 1/B. Run at Si 0 V, sr1 50 µV, fast_delay 2.1 s (7τ at 0.3 s/24 dB), slow_delay 1 s. The price includes the 0→0.8 T positioning and 0.8→10 T traverse, calibrated to run 5's 1.17 s/point at 1 s fast delay and runs 6–7's blocking magnet timing.

The high-frequency Nyquist target is F_L for region B at n ≈ 3.63×10¹², 37.5 T: Δ(1/B) = 0.00885 T⁻¹ gives ≥3 samples per cycle. At B = 0.8 T the fourfold LL density period is 0.0774×10¹² cm⁻²; the 0.0381 step gives ≥2 samples per ray. For the model's light ABAB pocket at n = 2.4 (≈4.8 T), the full 1/B span gives ≈5.5 periods (δF = 0.87 T), while the cleanest stacking claim will come from the upper-density columns. **Coverage trade:** the hole side and electron densities below +2.4 are excluded: the former lies in the near-CNP ABAB exception and thermally heavy ABCA regime, and the latter has fewer resolvable light-pocket periods and a higher high-B resistance risk. Including them would add gate points and weaken the fixed-sensitivity read.
