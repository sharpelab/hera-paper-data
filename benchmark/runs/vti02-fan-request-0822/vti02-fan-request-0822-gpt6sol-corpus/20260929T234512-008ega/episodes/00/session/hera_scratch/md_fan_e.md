## HERA — Step 4: region-A electron Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`)

Runs 6 and 7 are complete and read in ana cells 14–17. The electron cut (run 6, +3.2 × 10¹² cm⁻²) has a persistent ~7.3 T tone and a 14/22/29 T near-integer comb; a separate heavy pocket is unresolved at one density. The hole cut is confined to −1.2 × 10¹², where the forward model predicts a close near-CNP pair and ABCA is LK-suppressed. I choose one electron fan on the run-5 calibrated D = 0 line, n = +2.5…+3.2 × 10¹², to test whether the ~7 T branch and higher peaks follow separate F(n) curves. The parallel CNP ridge in run 5 implies a second region shifted by ~0.43 × 10¹²; extraction will inspect the possible mosaic tones before a region-A stacking call.

**Coverage and grid:** B = 0.9→8.0 T, 100 rows uniform in 1/B; 27 n points (Δn = 0.0269 × 10¹²). At n_max, F_L = 33.1 T and Δ(1/B) = 0.00996 T⁻¹ gives ~3.0 rows per fastest period. At B_lo, a fourfold LL ray spans 0.087 × 10¹² in n, giving ~3.2 n points per ray. The model gives the lowest-n ABAB light tone ~5.2 T, yielding ~5.1 periods across this field window; δF = 1.01 T and the ABAB heavy–ABCA split is >5 T. The grid starts 0.1 T below the censored 1 T electron onset: run 6's 50 mT points below 1 T under-sample a 7 T tone, so those points cannot establish a lower onset. The fan tests it with a Nyquist-safe grid. B > 8 T is excluded because the period and separation checks already close; n < +2.5 is excluded because the light-tone period count fails and low-n/high-B resistance increases. Thus this fan does not test the hole side or the low-density exception regime.

**Acquisition and price:** Fixed sr1 sensitivity 50 µV gives 5 kΩ full scale at the 10 nA drive; runs 6–7 stayed below 0.59 kΩ even at 10 T. Fast dwell 2.7 s = 9τ for the observed 0.3 s, 24 dB lock-in; magnet set blocks and settles 3 s per row. Field ends at 8 T; gates return to 0 V. Preconditions printed and asserted in the cell: live base thermometry, B near 0, Si 0, magnet PS/output, lock-in τ/slope and drive, committed caps. Check first two landed rows for leakage >1 nA, clipped/flat Rxx and wrong setpoint tracking; then check roughly every 20 rows for temperature drift, channel integrity and whether the fan branches disperse.
