## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side, D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`, `tetralayer-stacking-fs-count` Step 4)

**Why this fan.** The spot checks (ana, runs 6–7) found oscillations on both sides but leave the call open. At n = +3.2×10¹² the tones are 7.3 / 14.9 / 22.4 / 29.4 T. The helper reads that as a harmonic comb of 7.3 T, but 7.3 + 22.4 ≈ 29.4 closes a Luttinger sum at scale ≈ 0.90, so the same peaks fit two readings: an ABAB pair plus a breakdown sum-tone, or a 7.3 T fundamental plus an F_L singleton. This is the investigation's "ratio ≈ 3" trap. Only **n-dependence** resolves it. Over this window the model's heavy tone moves 15.7 → 26.0 T, while 3 × light moves 8.8 → 22.3 T and F_L moves 18.6 → 33.3 T (scale 1), so the trajectories separate. The hole side stays capped at −1.27×10¹², where the ABAB pair is 0.7 T apart and cannot be resolved (ana), so the fan goes on the **electron side**.

**Design (computed; `landau-fan` step-1 checks).** n = +3.22 → +1.80×10¹² at D = 0 (run-5 calibration). The top end sits 11 mV under the TG limit. The bottom end is where the heavy-vs-F_L split (≈ 2.6 T) still clears ~2·δF. **B_lo = 1.0 T**: `sdh_onset` on run 6 gives 1.0 T, but that is censored at the record edge, because the 7.3 T tone is still alive where the 10 mT grid starts. The heavy/F_L tones start at 1.2–1.8 T. **B_hi = 10 T** keeps a long high-B half for the F_L-tone origin B-window split (the 29 T tone grows with B in run 6). **Slow axis: uniform in 1/B, 103 rows, Δ(1/B) = 0.0088 T⁻¹.** That is 1/3 of the fastest expected tone, 37.7 T, the region-B mosaic F_L (n + 0.43×10¹²) at scale 1. δF = 1.11 T. **Fast axis: 31 points, Δn = 0.047×10¹²**, which is 2 samples per LL ray at B_lo (the floor). **Dynamic range:** sr1 is fixed at 50 µV = 5 kΩ FS, inherited from the spot checks. The worst corner is n = 1.8 at 10 T: R(0) = 77 Ω (run 5) × an MR factor ≲ 20, i.e. ≲ 1.5 kΩ, leaving ≥ 3× headroom. A near-rail counter prints if it's wrong. **Settling:** τ = 0.3 s at 24 dB/oct is asserted. fast_delay = 2.1 s = 7τ (the rig-card floor); 3 s after each flyback. **Price:** grid 103 × 31 × 2.2 s/pt (run 6: 0.08 s reads per point) = 117 min, plus 0 → 10 T ramp 73 min, plus per-row turnaround ~7 s × 103 = 12 min, ≈ 3 h 20 m. Ends at **10 T, gates parked at 0**. The next step (the LK mass split) rides the field down. **Coverage trade:** n < 1.8×10¹² and the sub-1 T record are excluded. Below 1.8 the light tone is < 3 T and unresolvable, and heavy vs F_L merges within 2δF. Adding 0.6–1 T would cost ~70 rows ≈ +1.5 h for a record where only the light tone lives.

**Checkpoints.** (1) **First rows** (~9 min in, B ≈ 1.0 T): R across n ≈ 15–80 Ω, sr0 ≈ 9.9 nA, gate currents at the pA floor, no rail. (2) **B ≈ 2 T, row 57, ~1 h 25 m in:** per-column FFT on the 1–2 T record. The ~7 T light tone must appear in the high-n columns (run 6 had it at SNR ~100) and should move with n. If there is no oscillation content anywhere, abort: calibration or contacts have broken, and continuing costs 2 h. (3) **B ≈ 3.4 T, row 80, ~2 h 05 m in:** the n = 3.2 column must reproduce run 6's 22 / 29 T pair (shared-density check). Early read of the heavy-vs-F_L trajectories.
