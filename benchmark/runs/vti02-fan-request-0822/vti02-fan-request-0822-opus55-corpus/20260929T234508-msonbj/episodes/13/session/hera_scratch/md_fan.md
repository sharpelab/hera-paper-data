## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side, D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`, for `tetralayer-stacking-fs-count`)

**What the spot checks said (runs 6/7, read in scratch; the ana record comes next while the fan runs).** Electron side, n = +3.2×10¹² (run 6): tones at **7.2, 14.9, 22.4, 29.4 T** plus 39–41 T, and harmonics of 29.4 at 58.6 / 88.2 / 117 T. Onsets: 22.4 T at 1.2–1.4 T, 29.4 T at 1.8 T, 7.2 T censored at 1.0 T (it is still there at the bottom of the 10 mT part of the record). Hole side, n = −1.2×10¹² (run 7): 12.3 T plus 4.7 / 6.6 T. That is the low-|n| exception regime, and holes can't go past −1.27×10¹² at the TG limit. So the fan goes on the **electron side**, the only side that reaches the design densities. **The single density can't decide it:** 7.2 + 22.4 ≈ 29.6 ≈ the 29.4 T tone, and at this density 22.4 ≈ 3×7.2. So the run-6 set reads equally as an ABAB pair (model at the matching scale: ~6.5 / 23.5 T) plus a sum/breakdown tone at F_L, or as a single tone near F_L plus a harmonic comb. The two differ in how the tones move with n. In ABAB the heavy/light ratio walks from 4.8 at n = 2.0 to 3.5 at n = 3.24 (FS-count model); a harmonic stays at exactly k·f. So the fan's job is **F(n) trajectories over 2.0–3.24×10¹²**. The per-column FFT also gives the mandatory F_L-tone B-window split (in run 6 the pair dominates at 1–3 T and the 29.4 T tone dominates above 3 T), plus the partition sum.

**Design (binding numbers).** **B_lo = 1.0 T** is `sdh_onset(...).onset_b` on run 6's uniform 10 mT record. It is censored (the light tone persists down to that edge). Below 1 T run 6 was sampled at 50 mT, which aliases a 7 T tone, so the onset there is unmeasured. B_hi = 10 T (session cap): the B-window split needs a high-B half, and the rows above 3.5 T are mostly ramp. **n = +2.00 → +3.24×10¹², 0.04 step, 32 columns.** The top is the TG limit (V_tg = 0.995 V). n = 3.20 is on the grid, so column 30 reproduces run 6 on a different grid (the alias check comes for free). The bottom, 2.0, is the recipe's design-density floor. **n-axis Nyquist:** the ray spacing g·e·B/h is 0.097×10¹² at 1.0 T, so there are 2.4 points per period (floor 2), rising above 3 by 1.2 T. **B-axis, uniform in 1/B, 108 rows in two segments.** From 1.0 to 3.5 T, Δ(1/B) = 0.0094 (Nyquist 53 T): this covers the fundamentals up to 42 T, and the 2nd harmonic only appears at ≥ 3.8 T. From 3.5 to 10 T, Δ(1/B) = 0.0060 (Nyquist 83 T): that keeps the measured 3rd and 4th harmonics of the F_L tone (90 / 120 T) from folding into the 0–45 T band. A single-segment grid at Nyquist = 2·F_L would fold the 3rd harmonic right onto F_L, only at high B, and fake a breakdown signature. A 1/B spacing uniform at the fine value would cost 42 more rows (~50 min) and buys nothing below 3.5 T. **Settling:** fast_delay = 1.8 s = 6τ (τ = 0.3 s, 24 dB, asserted). That is below the 7–10τ step floor, and here is the cost: 24 dB attenuation of a tone's oscillation along n is ≤ 9% at the 22 T / 29 T onsets (4.5–4.7 points per n-period there) and → 0 above 2 T. There is also a uniform lag of ≈ 0.025×10¹² in the column labels. slow_delay = 3 s after flyback, plus the 3 s magnet settle. **Sensitivity 50 µV (5 kΩ FS)**, the same as runs 6/7. Worst corner is n = 2.0 at 10 T: R₀ = 73 Ω (run 5) × ~15 MR+SdH ≈ 1.1 kΩ, so ≥ 4× headroom. The guard trips on 3 railed points and on leakage > 1 nA.

**Price.** 108 rows × (32 × 1.92 s + 7 s turnaround) = 123 min, plus a 9 T traverse at 0.137 T/min = 66 min, plus 8 min positioning 0 → 1 T, ≈ 3 h 17 m. Headline ~3 h 35 m allows for this session's ~5–10% overrun (per-point overhead calibrated on run 5 at 1.17 s/pt with fast_delay = 1.0; the magnet traverse is at the bare rate per runs 6/7). **Coverage trade:** going down to B_lo = 0.8 T would let the light partner reach ≥ 5 periods down to n ≈ 2.3 (at 1.0 T it gets there only for n ≳ 2.7). It would cost about +1.5 h (30 rows, plus a tighter n-axis), and it isn't taken: the heavy-vs-F_L split is the primary discriminator and it is fully covered. **Ends at 10 T with the gates parked at 0 V.** The LK mass split (`temperature-dependence-vti`) then rides the field down.

(2) B ≈ 2 T (row 53, ~1 h 15 m): re-form the raw map from the TSV and do a per-column FFT on the 1–2 T record. Do oscillations disperse across n? Abort if they don't. (3) B ≈ 3.5 T (row 76, ~1 h 55 m): per-column FFT on 1–3.5 T (δF ≈ 1.4 T). This is the interim trajectory read: heavy vs F_L vs harmonic comb. Continue to 10 T for the high-B half.
