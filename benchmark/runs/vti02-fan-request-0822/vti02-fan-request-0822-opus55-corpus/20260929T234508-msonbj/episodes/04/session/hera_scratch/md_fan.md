## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan for the stacking call (`landau-fan-vti`), electron side D = 0 — preceded by a 17-min low-field extension that pins B_lo

**What the spot checks say (runs 6/7, read in ana).** Electron (n = +3.2×10¹², F_L = 33.1 T): tones at **7.3 / 22.3 / 29.5 T** (+14.8 T). Two readings fit: (a) an ABAB pair (model 7.3 / 25.8 T) plus the sum tone at F_L, with a lever-arm scale of ≈ 0.89 (7.3 + 22.3 = 29.6 T; partition 0.25 / 0.75); (b) one 7.3 T tone with its 2×/3×/4× harmonics. One density can't tell these apart: 22.3 ≈ 3 × 7.3 is the ratio-only harmonic trap the investigation warns about. **Only the n-dependence decides it.** A harmonic tracks k·f₁(n) exactly, while ABAB's heavy/light ratio drifts from 3.4 to 4.8 between n = 3.2 and 2.0, and the light branch is supra-linear in n. Hole side (n = −1.2×10¹², F_L = 12.4 T): cleaner (onset ≤ 0.72 T), with tones at 7.0 / 12.5 T (+5.0 T). But it sits in the near-CNP exception regime (ABAB 6.2 / 6.7 T, close and balanced; ABCA m* ≈ 1.1 mₑ, LK-dead). Its densities can't separate the templates.

**Design (the one fan, floored per `landau-fan` step 1):**
- **Side / window: electron, D = 0, n = +2.00 → +3.23×10¹², Δn = 0.03 (42 pts).** Only the electron side reaches |n| ≳ 2×10¹², where the templates separate. n_max is the TG limit (V_tg = +0.992 V at 3.23; 3.26 would breach +1.0 V). The column at 3.20 repeats run 6's density on a uniform-1/B grid, which is the investigation's grid-change reproduction check.
- **Field: uniform in 1/B, B_lo → 5.0 T ascending**, Δ(1/B) = 1/(3·F_L(3.23)) = 0.0100 T⁻¹. That is 3 points per period of the fastest expected fundamental. I chose 1/3 rather than 1/2 because run 6 shows a 59 T tone (2× the F_L tone, onset 3.9 T): at 1/2 it would fold to ≈ 10 T, inside the band being read; at 1/3 it folds to 45 T, outside it. B_hi = 5 T: above that it's QH territory (run 6's 8–9 T peak) plus 90 T-class harmonics (onset 5 T). δF gains nothing (1.25 T vs 1.20 T at 6 T).
- **B_lo is pinned by the extension below.** The Nyquist-valid part of run 6 (sub-1 T was 50 mT-sampled, Nyquist ≈ 2.5 T there) gives `sdh_onset` = 1.01 T, **censored** on the light tone. The recipe says pin a censored onset with a cheap extension before a long fan. The binding case is the ABAB partner at the lowest fan density: n = 2.0 (≈ 3.2 T at scale 0.89). So the extension sits at **n = +2.0, B 0.3 → 1.2 T**, uniform 1/B, 157 pts. The fan then takes B_lo = its measured onset, rounded up to 0.05 T and clipped to **[0.65, 1.0] T**. The 0.65 T floor keeps the n-axis at ≥ 2 points per LL ray (Δn_ray = 4eB/h); 1.0 T is the run-6 bound.
- **Settling / lock-in:** fast_delay 1.8 s = 6τ (τ 0.3 s, 24 dB/oct; the n-axis carries per-column SdH content). slow_delay 3 s after each row's gate flyback. **sr1 at 20 µV = 2 kΩ FS.** The worst corner is n = 2.0 × 5 T, which is ν ≈ 16, the same filling as run 6's 530 Ω peak. I estimate 450–700 Ω there, so ≥ 2.8× headroom. The extension runs at the same 20 µV.
- **Price** (per point = fast_delay + 0.12 s from run 5; field step = ΔB/0.137 T/min + 3.08 s from runs 6/7): row = 42 × 1.92 + 7 s ≈ 88 s. **B_lo 1.0 → 82 rows, ≈ 2 h 40 m; B_lo 0.8 → 107 rows, ≈ 3 h 25 m; B_lo 0.65 → 138 rows, ≈ 4 h 20 m** (≈ +7 % realism included). Plus the 17 min extension. The fan ends at 5.0 T with the gates parked at 0. The field stays put for the next step: the LK mass split, riding down.

**Coverage trade, stated.** The hole side is excluded. At D = 0 it only reaches −1.2×10¹², where no template separates (a hole fan would cost ≈ 2 h and could at best read *consistent-with*). With B_lo = 1.0, the light partner gets ≥ 5 periods only for n ≳ 2.9×10¹². Below that, the n-dependence read leans on the heavy branch and the F_L tone. Every 0.1 T lower in B_lo buys ≈ +15 % record for ≈ +20 min, which is why the extension decides B_lo rather than a guess. Region B of the run-5 map (+0.43×10¹² doping offset) could add a second family on its own F_L. It gets checked in the read, per-channel, on the same scale.

**Checkpoints (E1).** Extension: in-kernel `sdh_onset` print, then my read before the fan cell lands. Fan first rows (≤ 3 rows): sr1 fs < 0.5, gate current < 1 nA away from the flyback transient, T_sample < 1.5 K, both lock-ins live; abort there otherwise. Guards in-cell: gate leak > 1 nA or T_sample > 2 K interrupts. Periodic: at ~50 % and ~75 % of rows (B ≈ 1.4 / 2.0 T), a partial per-column FFT on the landed rows. Continue if the light and heavy tones are tracking; abandon if no column carries a tone by the 75 % mark (then it's a resolution-limited cannot-tell on this rig).
