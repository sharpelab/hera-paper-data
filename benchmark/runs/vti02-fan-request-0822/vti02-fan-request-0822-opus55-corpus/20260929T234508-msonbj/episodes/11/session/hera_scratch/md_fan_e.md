## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan, electron side, D = 0 (`landau-fan-vti`), n 1.4 → 3.2×10¹², B 0.8 → 5 T uniform in 1/B

**Why this fan.** Runs 6/7 (ana, spot checks): the electron spot at n = +3.2×10¹² shows tones at ≈ 7.2 / 22.3 / 29.4 T (F_L = 33.1 T). One density reads this three ways: an ABAB pair at density scale ≈ 0.88 plus a sum/breakdown tone at the F_L position, a 7.2 T harmonic comb, or an ABCA singleton plus extras. Only the **n-dependence** separates them (investigation Step 4/5): a pocket follows its own Onsager curve, a harmonic follows k·f₁(n), and a sum tone follows f₁ + f₂ = F_L(n). The hole side is cleaner (onset 0.7 T) but is TG-limited to |n| ≤ 1.27×10¹² on D = 0. That is the near-CNP exception regime, where the ABAB pair (≈ 6.0 / 6.6 T) cannot be resolved. So the fan goes on the electron side, where the templates separate: at 2.5×10¹² the ABAB heavy tone is 20.7 T against F_L 25.8 T.

**Design (floored, per `landau-fan` step 1).**
- **B_hi = 5 T.** Above ~4–5 T, QH harmonics (58 / 88 / 117 T, onsets 3.8–6 T in run 6) would fold into the claim band at this Δ(1/B). The record above the heavy-tone onset (1.4 T) still gives δF = 1.9 T, so heavy vs F_L is split by ≥ 2 δF for n ≳ 2.1×10¹².
- **B_lo = 0.8 T.** The light (partner-candidate) tone is still above threshold at the 1.0 T edge of run 6's finely sampled record, so its onset is below 1 T, and its expected onset scales with m* to ≈ 0.6 T. 0.8 T gives the ABAB partner ≥ 5 periods across n ≈ 2.5–3.2×10¹² and ≈ 4 at 2.2×10¹².
- **B grid:** Δ(1/B) = 0.0100 T⁻¹, which is 3.0 samples per period at F_L(3.2×10¹²) and 2.75 if the scale is +10 %. That gives **N_B = 106**.
- **n axis:** Δn = 0.030×10¹², which is 2.6 points per LL at 0.8 T and 3.2 at 1 T. That gives **N_n = 61**.
- **Dwell:** 2.1 s = 7 τ (0.3 s, 24 dB). This is the settle floor, and it keeps the lock-in smear along n at ≤ 0.1 LL period at B_lo. The n-sweep runs in one direction every row, so the residual lag is a uniform ~0.6-point n offset (≤ 1 % in F).
- **Sensitivity:** 50 µV (5 kΩ FS), fixed, inherited from runs 6/7. The worst corner is n = 1.4×10¹² × 5 T: 90 Ω at 0 T × ≤ 6× MR comes to ~10 % FS.
- **Price:** 106 rows × (61 × 2.23 s + ~6.5 s) = 4.2 h, plus the in-fan ramp (31 min) and positioning (0 → 0.8 T, 6 min). About 4.8 h floor, quoted at +7 %. Ends at **5 T** with the gates parked at 0. The field stays there on purpose, because the next step (LK mass split, or a hole-side read) rides it down.

**Coverage trade.** Excluded: the hole side (not resolvable inside the validators, above), and electron n < 1.4×10¹². That is the ABAB low-density mimic regime (heavy tone → 0.95 F_L), where the partner can't be resolved at any practical B_lo; extending to 1.0×10¹² would add ~25 % (≈ 1.2 h). Two things to watch for in the per-column FFTs. Region B (run 5, electron-doped by 0.43×10¹²) is a series-mosaic confound: it would add a parallel tone family at F_L(n + 0.43). There is also a small Hall admixture in B_1–B_2 (Rxx < 0 at QH minima in run 6). Only one Rxx pair exists, so the call is single-region.

**Guards / checkpoints.** Preconditions: T at base, B ≈ 0, heaters off, Si 0, lock-in τ / slope / drive read-checked. In-run trips on |I_gate| > 1 nA or T > 1.8 K; `sr1` rail events are printed. Checkpoint 1 is the first 3 rows (~8 min in): signal level vs FS, 10 nA drain, leakage, paired gate columns, B tracking. Checkpoint 2 is ~1.2 h (row 25, B ≈ 1.0 T): the n = 3.2 column against run 6. Checkpoint 3 is ~2.5 h (row 58, B ≈ 1.5 T): partial per-column FFT, where the partner tone should appear across n ≈ 2.5–3.2; abandon if it doesn't. Checkpoint 4 is ~3.7 h (row 85, B ≈ 2.5 T): heavy tones, and rail check at low n.
