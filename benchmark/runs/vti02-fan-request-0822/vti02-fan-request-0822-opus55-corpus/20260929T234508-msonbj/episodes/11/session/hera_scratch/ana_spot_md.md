## Runs 6–7 — SdH spot checks (`sdh-spot-check`), D = 0, electron n = +3.2×10¹² and hole n = −1.2×10¹²

Both complete (not interrupted), no leakage (≤ 0.2 nA), T 1.17–1.29 K, `sr1` peak ≤ 12 % of the 50 µV full scale (no clipping). Raw first, then background-subtracted, then per-tone onsets (`sdh_onset`) and windowed FFTs against F_L and the ABAB model (scale 1).

**Electron, run 6 (F_L = 33.1 T; ABAB model 7.3 / 25.8 T, ABCA 33.1 T).** Strong SdH above ~1.3 T that goes into the QH regime above ~5 T. Rxx minima reach 0 and slightly negative, so the B_1–B_2 pair has a small Hall admixture. Tones: **≈ 7.2 T** (SNR 100–120; still above threshold at the 1.0 T edge of the 10 mT-sampled record, so **its onset is below 1 T**. Run 6 is 50 mT-sampled below 1 T, which is blind to F ≳ 7 T there.) Also **≈ 22.3 T** (onset 1.4 T), **≈ 29.4 T** (onset 1.8 T), ≈ 14.8 T, and high-field-only combs at 39–118 T (onset ≥ 2.6 T; QH harmonics). `sdh_onset`'s ratio screen flags 14.8 / 22.3 / 29.4 as 2× / 3× / 4× of 7.2. The investigation forbids settling that on the ratio alone. 7.2 + 22.3 = 29.5 ≈ the 29.4 tone, so the same set reads as an ABAB pair at density scale ≈ 0.87–0.9 (model 7.3 / 25.8 → 6.4–6.6 / 22.4–23.2) plus a sum/breakdown tone at the F_L position. It also reads as a 7.2 T harmonic comb, or as an ABCA singleton at scale 0.89 with extras. **One density cannot separate these. The n-dependence can:** a harmonic tracks k·f₁(n), a real pocket tracks its own Onsager curve, and a sum tone tracks f₁ + f₂. That is the fan's job.

**Hole, run 7 (F_L = 12.4 T; ABAB model ≈ 6.0 / 6.6 T, ABCA 12.4 T at m* ≈ 1.1).** This side is cleaner: onset 0.7 T. Tones ≈ 6.6 T (dominant), ≈ 4.7 T, ≈ 12.3 T (strongest above 3 T), and ≈ 8.5 T. These sit in the investigation's **near-CNP hole exception regime**: on the D = 0 line the hole side is TG-limited at |n| ≤ 1.27×10¹². There the ABAB pair is close and balanced, and the 12.3 T tone could be F_L, 2 × 6.6 or a sum tone. **Not decisive.**

**Take:** the hole side is cleaner at its one density, but only the **electron side** reaches the |n| ≳ 2–3×10¹² band where the templates separate, and it carries SNR 50–120 tones there. The fan goes on the electron side. Onset inputs: the light tone is below 1 T (censored at the record edge), the heavy tones are at 1.4–1.8 T. The sensitivity (50 µV) and settling (10 τ) are inherited.
