## HERA — Landau fan, step 0: low-field onset pin at n = +3.2×10¹² (`sdh-spot-check` extension, `landau-fan` step 1 lever 1)

**Spot-check read (runs 6/7, ana to follow):** the fan goes on the **electron side**. At D = 0 the hole side reaches only −1.27×10¹² (TG limit): that is the near-CNP exception regime, where the ABAB pair is close and balanced (model 5.9 / 6.5 T) and ABCA m* ≈ 1.1 mₑ is LK-dead. Run 7's peak set there (6.6, 4.7, 12.3 T) cannot discriminate. Run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹²) shows tones at **7.1, 22.2 and 29.5 T** (F_L = 33.1 T). 7.1 + 22.2 ≈ 29.5 is consistent with an ABAB pair at a global density scale of ≈ 0.89 plus a sum tone, *or* with a scaled ABCA singleton. `sdh_onset` also flags 22.2 and 29.5 as ~3× and ~4× of 7.1. A single density cannot resolve that harmonic ambiguity. The fan's n-dependence can: harmonics track k·f(n), real pockets follow their own Onsager curves.

**Why this cell before the fan:** the fan's B_lo is set by the light partner, which is the binding case at the lowest fan density. `sdh_onset` reports that tone (7.1 T) as **censored at 0.30 T**, but run 6 used 50 mT steps below 1 T, which only resolve a 7 T tone above ≈ 0.84 T (ΔB ≤ B²/2F). The onset was never measured. B_lo moves the fan between ≈ 3.9 h (B_lo = 1.0) and ≈ 5.8 h (0.6), so this ~30 min pin earns its place. The grid is **uniform in 1/B at Δ(1/B) = 0.01 T⁻¹**, the fan's own row grid (Nyquist ⅓ on F_L = 33 T), from 0.3 to 1.5 T: 267 pts, delay 0 (3 s driver settle = 10τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB), sr1 50 µV (5 kΩ FS, the fan's sensitivity; noise floor ≈ 3 Ω rms from run 6, ≫ the 0.19 Ω LSB). It also repeats the 1.0–1.5 T stretch of run 6 on a different grid (the spot-check Verify check that separates real tones from aliases). The field ends at 1.5 T; the fan ramps down to B_lo and runs up to 10 T.

**Fan design (lands next, B_lo = `sdh_onset(...).onset_b` from this run):** D = 0; n = 3.25 → 2.0×10¹² (fast, paired gates; top of the D = 0 window, and the floor where the light partner still gets ~5 periods); Δn = 0.025 (≥ 2.3 pts per LL period at B = 0.6 T), 51 pts; field_invB(B_lo, 10 T) at Δ(1/B) = 0.01. B_max = 10 T because the n = 2.0 column's heavy-vs-F_L split needs δF ≤ 1.6 T, i.e. B_max ≳ 8 T, and the high-field half is the lever for the F_L-tone-origin (breakdown) split. fast_delay 1.8 s (6τ), slow_delay 3 s, sr1 50 µV (worst corner: n = 2, 10 T ≈ 1.7 kΩ estimated, 3× headroom). The fan ends at 10 T for the LK ride-down.

Preconditions: T_sample 1.0–1.5 K and logfile live; |B| < 0.05 T; V_si = 0; PS heater + output on; drive 1.0 V. Guards: gate leakage above 1 nA, or T_sample above 1.8 K, interrupts the sweep. Checkpoint: first 20 points via `hera-watch` (signal, leakage, field tracking); full read at the end.
