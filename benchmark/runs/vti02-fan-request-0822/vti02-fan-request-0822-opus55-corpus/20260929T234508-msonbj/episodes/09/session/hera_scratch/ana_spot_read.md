- **Electron side (run 6, n = +3.2).** Clean SdH/QH, with minima reaching R ≈ 0 by 2–7 T. Peak set ≈ **7.1–7.3, 14.1–14.8, 22.0–22.9, 29.3–30.0 T** (plus 44, 58 T at high B). `sdh_onset` groups everything as a **comb on ≈ 7.3 T** (22.3 ≈ 3×, 29.4 ≈ 4×). This is the trap the investigation names: the ABAB heavy/light ratio drifts through 3× with density, and a ratio-only screen then eats the real heavy tone. 7.3 + 22.3 ≈ 29.6, so the ≈ 30 T tone can be read three ways:
  - F_L itself, at a global scale ≈ 0.90 (inside the 0.8–1.2 prior). Then ABAB reads light 0.24 / heavy 0.76 of F_L, near the model's 0.21 / 0.79 at n ≈ 2.9. The 30 T tone is then a sum/breakdown tone, or an ABCA domain.
  - A harmonic comb.
  - A second region: the run-5 ridge B sits +0.43×10¹² off ridge A.

  **One density cannot separate these.** The n-dependence can: a harmonic tracks k·f₁(n) exactly, the ABAB heavy tone runs ≈ 0.7× the F_L slope, and a region-B tone runs parallel to F_L with an offset. Onset: the 7.3 T tone is censored at the 1.0 T record edge and flagged near-residual. The heavy tone's onset is ≈ 1.4 T.
- **Hole side (run 7, n = −1.2).** Strong low-field oscillations (onset < 1 T, censored at the 0.5 T edge). Tones ≈ 5.3 (dominant) and 12.3 T (≈ nominal F_L), with a noisy spread (3.5, 4.6, 8.5, 15.8). The window-to-window peak positions are unstable (4.1 → 5.3 → 10.0 T). Inside the validators the hole side of D = 0 reaches only −1.27×10¹². That is the near-CNP semimetal exception regime, where the ABAB pair is close and balanced and n_offset errors move F_L by ~1 T. **It is not a design-density side.**
- **Fan side: electron.** Only the electron side reaches the |n| ≳ 2–3×10¹² design densities, and it has the cleaner, stable peak set.
