## HERA — Step 3: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n −1.25 → +3.20×10¹² × B 0 → 10 T

**Plan step 3** (`hera_scratch/plan.md`), requested by the operator as the stacking measurement. Why a D = 0 fan: the stackings differ most clearly in their Landau-level structure. **ABAB** has two bilayer-like bands (light + heavy), so it shows two LL families and a dense crossing pattern on both carrier sides. **ABCA** has one chiral family: gaps at ν = ±8, ±12, ±16… without crossings at these densities, plus an early ν = 0 insulator. **ABCB** also shows crossings, but is e–h and ±D asymmetric (step 4 tests ±D). The spot checks already lean ABAB: run 6 (n = +3.2e12) shows tones at 7.1 and 22.2 T that sum to ≈ 29.3 T, next to a 29.5 T tone, vs a nominal F_L of 33.1 T. The fan tests that lean across density. It also calibrates the **density scale**: c_tg is still the 9.5 nm geometry prior, and the QH-line slopes dn/dB = ν·e/h fix it, with the run-6 hint suggesting ×0.89.

**Design.** Fast axis: n along the calibrated D = 0 line (run 5: ratio 0.773, n_offset −0.089e12, D_offset −0.013 V/nm). It co-sweeps (V_tg, V_bg) from (−0.291, −0.484) to (+0.984, +1.166) V over 179 points at 0.025e12. That gives ≥ 4 points per ν-period down to 1 T and covers the full validator-limited D = 0 window (−1.28 … +3.26e12). It also includes region B's CNP (ridge B, n_A = −0.43e12), so a second family converging there will be visible and can't be mistaken for a second band. Slow axis: B goes **up** from where the magnet sits (0 T), so it never re-traverses: 0, 0.25, then 0.05 T steps to 2 T (onset and low-B crossings), 0.1 T to 6 T, and 0.2 T to 10 T. That is 93 rows; the wide ν-lines at high B don't need finer steps. Per point: fast_delay 1 s = 3.3 τ (the run-5 setting; any lag is a uniform sub-point shift). slow_delay is 3 s after each flyback and field step. `sr1` is fixed at 100 µV = 10 kΩ full scale (runs 6/7 peak 0.6 kΩ; run 5's ridge 3 kΩ). **Coverage trade:** if ν = 0 at the CNP becomes strongly insulating at high B, it can rail over a narrow n window; that costs only the CNP amplitude, not the fan. Guards: auto-abort on |I_gate| > 1 nA or T_sample > 2.5 K. The field readback is recorded per point.

**Price** (run 5: 1.10 s/pt at fast_delay 1 s; runs 6/7: ramp 0.137 T/min + 3.1 s settle per field set): 93 × (179 × 1.12 s + ~10 s turnaround) + 73 min ramp ≈ 6.7 h model, **≈ 7.2 h realistic**. It ends at **10 T** with the gates parked at 0, ready for step 4 (±D at high B).

**Checkpoints.** (1) **First rows:** the B = 0 row should reproduce run 5's D = 0 cut (CNP near n = 0, R ≈ 300 Ω; region-B peak near −0.43e12). Also check: no rail, gate currents at the pA floor, B readback tracking the setpoints. (2) **2 T (~2.2 h):** QH lines, ν assignment, density-scale fit, crossing read on both sides. (3) **6 T (~5.0 h):** decide whether to continue to 10 T (~2 h more) or interrupt and start step 4 at 6 T.
