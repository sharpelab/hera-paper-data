## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), n −1.25 … +3.25×10¹², B 1 → 10 T

**Plan step 4** (`hera_scratch/plan.md`). This is the operator's requested move. The SdH spot checks (runs 6, 7; ana to follow) show strong oscillations from ≈ 0.5–1.3 T and near-zero QH minima from ~2 T. Run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹²) shows **two main tones, 7.2 T and 22.2 T** (ratio 0.25 : 0.75, sum 29.4 T vs the prior F_L = 33.1 T). A 29.5–31.3 T component is also present, and it is ambiguous: either a 4×7.2 harmonic or a single F_L tone. With one density, one spot can't tell "two bands (ABAB-like)" apart from "one band + mosaic/harmonic (ABCA)". The fan measures that n-dependence directly. (a) An FFT per n column gives F_i(n): how many tones, whether their ratio stays fixed, and whether they sum to F_L. (b) The QH-minimum lines in (n, B) give the ν sequence and degeneracy, and an **absolute density calibration** (dn/dB = νe/h), replacing the 9.5 nm geometry prior. (c) It shows whether LL crossings appear at D = 0 (the two-band / ABAB signature). (d) It shows the region-B fan whose origin should sit near n_A ≈ −0.43×10¹² (run 5 mosaic), so that tone can be identified and discounted.

**Design.** The fast axis is n along the calibrated D = 0 line: co-swept (V_tg, V_bg), 151 points, δn = 0.03×10¹², ≈ 3 points per 4-fold LL at 1 T. It covers the full D = 0 reach inside the validators on both carrier signs (TG-limited at both ends). The slow axis is B from 1 → 10 T. Rows are **uniform in 1/B** (δ = 0.009 T⁻¹, Nyquist 55 T, which is >1.6× the highest tone expected at n = 3.25), capped at ΔB = 0.2 T above 4.8 T so the high-field QH structure stays dense enough to trace. That gives 115 rows. The field goes upward from where the magnet sits (0 T) and ends at **10 T**, where step 5 (the fixed-B (n, D) map for ±D asymmetry) starts, so nothing is re-traversed. Gates return to 0 at the end. **Coverage trade:** B < 1 T is excluded. Oscillation onset is ≥ 0.5 T and only the lightest tone lives there, so continuing the 1/B grid down to 0.7 T would add ~45 rows ≈ 2.5 h for the MR background only. **Sensitivity** is fixed at **200 µV = 20 kΩ FS**; the handoff restore left sr1 at 2 µV. That is ×35 over the largest Rxx in runs 6/7 (587 Ω), so only the ν = 0 CNP insulator at high B may rail, and a rail there is itself informative. The low-R cost is small: a 10 Ω SdH amplitude is 5×10⁻⁴ FS, which is readable. Dwell is fast_delay 1 s at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB (as in run 5, where the uniform lag only shifts position). slow_delay is 5 s after each field step plus the gate flyback. The 1 nA leak guard stays on.

**Price.** From run 5 (1.17 s/pt at fast_delay 1 s, incl. turnaround), plus the co-sweep overhead, ≈ 1.25 s/pt: 115 rows × (151 × 1.25 s + 12 s) + the 10 T bare ramp (73 min) ≈ 7.6 h. The headline adds +8 % per the session's overrun record. **Checkpoints:** (1) **first row** at 1 T (≈ 11 min in): signal level vs FS, drain current ≈ 10 nA, gate currents at the pA floor, T at base, and both CNP peaks (A at n = 0, B near −0.43) present. Abort on a rail across the row, a dead channel or leakage. (2) **Row 38 (1.5 T, ≈ 2.3 h)** and **row 56 (2 T, ≈ 3.3 h):** re-form the partial fan and check that QH/SdH minima fan out from the CNP. Also run the per-column FFT on 1–2 T (resolution 2 T) to see whether the 7 T and 22 T tones separate and scale with n. Continue if structure is present; abandon if the map is featureless (not expected from runs 6/7). (3) **Row 89 (5 T, ≈ 5.3 h):** interim tone count + ν assignment on the landed record.
