## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`) — the band-count read for the stacking call

**Why (plan step 4, `hera_scratch/plan.md`).** Runs 6 / 7 (ana cell "Runs 6 / 7") show SdH on both sides but one density can't decide the band count: the electron-side tone set 7.1 / 15.9 / 22.9 / 30.0 T fits *two* Fermi surfaces with mixing tones (F1, F2−F1, F2, F1+F2; shares 0.24 / 0.76 ≈ Bernal) and *also* a single 7.1 T family with harmonics, within ΔF = 1.8 T. The fan decides it: ABAB → two families, each F_i ∝ n with a fixed share, ΣF_i = F_L; ABCA → one family at F_L (F_L/3 pockets at low n); harmonics scale as k·F1(n), mixing tones as F2 ± F1. The QH ν-lines give the absolute density scale (dn/dB = ν·e/h) independently of stacking, which fixes c_tg (run 6 hints ~0.91× the 9.5 nm prior) and makes the Luttinger-sum test quantitative.

**Design.** Fast axis (Vtg, Vbg) co-swept along the run-5 D = 0 line, n = −1.25 → +3.25×10¹² (the full D = 0 window inside the validators, TG-limited at both ends; 25×10⁹ steps for |n| ≤ 0.75 where the band-edge physics sits, 50×10⁹ beyond — resolves ν = 4 LLs from ~2.5 T, and the per-column FFT does not need fine n). The D = 0 line doesn't depend on the absolute c scale (only the ratio), so recalibrating later moves n labels, not the path. Slow B 1.0 → 10.0 T, **uniform in 1/B**, 81 rows: Nyquist 44 T > F_L(3.25) ≈ 30–34 T, ΔF = 1.1 T over the full window; B_lo = 1 T from the run-6/7 onsets (light tones from ≤ 0.7 T, heavy 1.3–1.9 T). sr1 fixed at **100 µV = 10 kΩ FS** (state0 had left it at 2 µV); τ 0.3 s / 24 dB, fast_delay 1.0 s as in run 5. Same sweep direction every row, so the lock-in lag (~1 point) is a constant n offset, not a slope error. Guards: |I_gate| > 1 nA or T_sample > 2.5 K interrupts. Gates park at 0 at the end; **the field stays at 10 T** so step 5 (±D) can ride it down.

**Coverage trade.** The hole side only reaches −1.25×10¹² (vs +3.25 electron), so hole-side F(n) comes from a short lever arm. 1/B sampling leaves only 9 rows above 5 T: the high-field QH structure is read per row along n, not densely in B. Adding uniform-B high-field rows costs ~2.4 min each. D ≠ 0 is not covered here (step 5).

**Price.** Per row: 121 × 1.10 s (run 5's measured in-row rate) + ~11 s turnaround/settle ≈ 144 s → 81 rows ≈ 3 h 15 m. Field: 10 T at 0.137 T/min = 73 min, including the 0 → 1 T lead-in. Total ≈ 4 h 30 m, **priced at 4 h 45 m** after the session's +5–10 % over-run. **Checkpoints:** (1) first rows (~15 min, after the 7 min lead-in ramp): gate currents at the pA offsets, sr1 not railed away from the CNP column, CNP column near n ≈ 0 (run-5 cal), I_drain ≈ 9.9 nA, R scale 20–300 Ω. (2) ~2 h (B ≈ 2 T, row 44): a 1–2 T column FFT on the landed rows — do the F_i(n) lines appear and how many? (3) ~3 h 40 m (B ≈ 5 T, row 71): preliminary F_i(n) at ΔF 1.25 T. Abort/resize if sr1 rails across many columns (not just at the CNP), or on any trip.
