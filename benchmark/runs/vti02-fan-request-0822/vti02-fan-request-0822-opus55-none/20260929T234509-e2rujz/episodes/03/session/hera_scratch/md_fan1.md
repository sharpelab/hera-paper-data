## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), B 0 → 8 T — the stacking discriminator

Plan step 4 (`hera_scratch/plan.md`), at Zack's request. The spot checks (runs 6/7; the ana write-up lands while this runs) give the lean. At n = +3.2×10¹², run 6 carries **two independent SdH tones: 7.2 T (light, onset < 0.5 T) and ~30–31 T (heavy)**, with QH zeros from ~3.5 T and an Rxx step-up at 7.5–8 T ≈ the light tone's frequency, a plausible light-pocket quantum limit. That is the ABAB two-band signature. ABCA should give a single tone at F_L ≈ 33 T. A single-n record can't separate stackings on its own, though. The fan can: **two interpenetrating LL families with crossings** (ABAB), a **single family with ν = ±8 as the first robust gaps** (ABCA), or a **gapped ν = 0 at D = 0** (ABCB). It also shows whether the light-pocket quantum-limit line B_QL(n) runs across the electron side, and whether region B (the series ridge at n_A ≈ −0.43×10¹²) adds a second fan origin.

**Geometry.** `multimegasweep`: B is the slow axis, ascending from where the magnet sits (0 T): 50 mT steps to 1.5 T (low-field onsets, early crossings), then 100 mT to 8 T, 96 rows. Fast axis: (V_tg, V_bg) co-swept along the calibrated D = 0 line (run-5 cal), n −1.25 … +3.25×10¹² in 0.025×10¹² steps (181 pts). That is ≥ 4 pts per 4-fold LL spacing from 1 T up. fast_delay 1 s and slow_delay 3 s, as in run 5; sr1 fixed at 100 µV (10 kΩ FS). Leak guard at 1 nA is on. Gates are parked at 0 on exit, and the **field stays at 8 T** so step 5 (finite D at high field) starts there with no re-traverse. **Coverage trade:** n is bounded at both ends by the TG validator (−0.3 / +1.0 V; the endpoints sit 9 mV / 2 mV inside), so the hole side stops at −1.25×10¹². B stops at 8 T, not 10: the light-pocket QL for n ≤ 3.25×10¹² falls at ≲ 8 T per run 6, which already covers 8–10 T at the top density. The 8–10 T rows would add ~45 min.

**Price.** Run 5 gives 1.10 s/pt in-row. Run 6 gives each field step as the bare ramp (0.137 T/min) + 3.1 s. Per row that is 181 × 1.10 s + ramp + ~3.5 s flyback + 3 s slow_delay, so 96 rows ≈ 6.5 h bare. Headline ≈ 7 h. **Checkpoints:** first 2 rows (~8 min): no rail, drain current ≈ 9.9 nA, gate currents at the pA floor, and the R(n) profile matching run 5's D = 0 cut (peak ~300 Ω at the CNP). Then about every 15 rows (~1 h): re-form the partial fan. The decisive interim read is at ~2–3 T (≈ 2.5 h in): one family or two.
