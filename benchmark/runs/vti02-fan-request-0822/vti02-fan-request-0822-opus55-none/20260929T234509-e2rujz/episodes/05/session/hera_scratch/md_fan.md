## HERA — Step 4: D = 0 Landau fan (`landau-fan`) — n × B, the stacking discriminator

**Why this, now (plan step 4).** The spot checks (runs 6/7, ana cells 14–16) show SdH from ≲ 1 T and deep QH by 7 T on the electron side. The tones there are 7.1 + 22.2 T (sum 29.3 ≈ 0.89 F_L) plus 29.5 T, and the Rxx minima are **not periodic in 1/B**. That leans Bernal (two bilayer-like pockets ≈ 0.24/0.76) but is ambiguous: 22 and 29.5 T sit within 4 % of 3× and 4× the 7.1 T tone. A single trace can't separate these; a fan can. Along n it should show whether there are two tone families with F₁(n) + F₂(n) = F_L(n) and LL crossings (ABAB) or one family at F_L on a clean 4-fold ladder (ABCA). The QH fan-line slopes also give the **density scale**, which is still the geometry prior and reads ~11 % high.

**Design.** Fast axis: n along the run-5-calibrated **D = 0** line, co-swept (V_tg, V_bg), −1.25 … +3.24×10¹² (cal units), 150 pts (Δn = 0.030; ≥ 6 pts per 4-fold period at 1.5 T). Every point is asserted inside the validators. Slow axis: **B 1 → 10 T**, uniform in 1/B (Δ = 0.0101 T⁻¹, FFT Nyquist ≈ 50 T) up to 3.1 T, then 0.1 T steps to 10 T, 137 rows. The first half serves the per-n FFT; the second half resolves QH fan lines and crossings. Starts where the magnet sits (0 T) and ends at **10 T** with gates at the e-side end, so step 5 rides the field down and nothing is re-traversed. `sr1` is fixed at **100 µV = 10 kΩ FS** (the handoff's state0 restore left it at 2 µV); fast_delay 1.0 s at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB, same as run 5. Guards: |I_gate| > 1 nA → interrupt + park gates; T_sample > 3 K → interrupt.

**Coverage trades, stated.** (i) Hole side stops at −1.25×10¹² and the electron side at +3.24×10¹²: the TG limits (−0.3 / +1.0 V) end the D = 0 line there. There is no way to widen this within the operating box. (ii) B < 1 T is excluded. Only the light 7 T tone lives there, and extending uniform-1/B to 0.5 T would add ~100 rows ≈ 5 h. (iii) The ν = 0 CNP state at high B may exceed 10 kΩ and rail a few columns around n ≈ 0. I accept that: the discriminating content is at |n| ≳ 0.3×10¹². (iv) Monodirectional fast sweeps plus ~4τ lock-in lag give a uniform ≈ +1-point (0.03×10¹²) n shift, corrected in analysis.

**Price.** From run 5: 1.10 s/pt at fast_delay 1 s, +0.05 s for the second gate write, so 1.15 s/pt. Per row: 150 × 1.15 + 3 s slow_delay + ~3.5 s flyback + 3 s magnet settle ≈ 182 s, × 137 rows ≈ 6.9 h. Add field ramps (1 → 10 T at 0.137 T/min = 66 min), the 0 → 1 T ramp (7.5 min) and gate ramps: ≈ 8.2 h, **headline ~9 h** with the ~8 % session overrun.

**Checkpoints.** *First rows* (≈ 15 min in, rows 1–2 at 1.0 T): no rail away from the CNP, CNP peak near n ≈ 0 (confirms the D = 0 path), gate currents at the ~100 pA baseline, T at base. *Row ≈ 45* (B ≈ 1.8 T, ~2.7 h): partial per-column FFT over the landed rows, looking for the light tone vs n. *Row ≈ 90* (B ≈ 5.5 T, ~5.8 h): fan-line slopes → density scale; crossings present or absent. At each checkpoint I decide continue / abandon; the price of the remaining rows is on the watch ETA.
