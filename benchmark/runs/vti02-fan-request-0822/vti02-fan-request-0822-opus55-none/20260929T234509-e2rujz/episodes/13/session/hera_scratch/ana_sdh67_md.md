## Runs 6–7 — SdH spot checks at D = 0 (`sdh-spot-check`), n = +3.2 / −1.2×10¹² (run-5 calibration)

**Read (both runs):** the device is high quality at base temperature. Oscillations appear at ≤ 0.5 T, and on the electron side Rxx drops to ≈ 0 (quantum Hall) at many fields between 1.5 and 7 T. **Run 6 (n = +3.2):** the strongest tones are **7.2, 22.2 and 30 T**. 7.2 + 22.2 = 29.4 ≈ 30 T, which is what an ABAB two-band pair looks like (light + heavy = F_L). But 22.2 ≈ 3 × 7.2 and 30 ≈ 4 × 7.2, which is what one tone plus its harmonics looks like, and `sdh_onset` flags them as harmonics. A single density cannot separate the two readings; tracking each tone against n can. Also, 30 T is about 0.9 × F_L(prior scale) = 33.1 T, so the geometry-prior density scale may be about 10 % high; the QH lines in a fan will calibrate it. **Run 7 (n = −1.2, close to the CNP):** many tones between 3.5 and 22 T and no clean pair. This regime is complicated by the semimetal overlap and the region-B mosaic (its CNP is offset by −0.43×10¹²), so no read from it alone. **Verdict so far:** consistent with ABAB and with a single band plus harmonics. Not a call. Next: the D = 0 Landau fan (plan Step 4).
