## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`), B 0.6 → 9 T × n −1.20 … +3.25×10¹²

**Why (plan Step 4).** Run 6 (n = +3.2×10¹²) shows tones at 7.2, 22.2 and 30 T. 7.2 + 22.2 ≈ 30 fits an ABAB light + heavy pair summing to F_L, but 22.2 ≈ 3 × 7.2 and 30 ≈ 4 × 7.2 also fit one tone plus harmonics (ana, runs 6–7). A single density can't separate these readings. The density dependence can: an independent band has its own F(n) slope and F₁ + F₂ = F_L(n) at every n, while a harmonic stays at exactly k × its parent. The fan also gives (i) the **absolute density scale** from the QH minima lines n = ν·eB/h (run 5's c_tg is still the geometry prior; 30 T ≈ 0.9 F_L suggests it is ~10 % high), (ii) the LL structure (ABAB: light/heavy LL crossings; ABCA: 16-fold zero LL, no crossings in the low-index fan at D = 0), and (iii) whether region B (CNP offset −0.43×10¹²) has its own fan.

**Geometry.** Fast: co-swept (V_tg, V_bg) on the region-A D = 0 line (run-5 cal), n = −1.20 … +3.25×10¹² in 91 points (Δn = 0.05×10¹²), hole → electron. That is the whole D = 0 window inside the validators (−1.28 … +3.26), 2–7 mV margin to the TG limit at each end. Slow: B ascending from where the magnet sits. **0.6 → 3 T uniform in 1/B** (108 rows, Δ(1/B) = 0.0125 T⁻¹, Nyquist 40 T > F_L max 33.6 T), then **0.1 T steps 3.1 → 9 T** (60 rows), 168 rows in all. **Coverage trade:** stopping at 9 T instead of the 10 T cap saves ~30 min. The stacking discriminators (tone slopes, low-index LL crossings) live below ~6 T. Ends at 9 T with gates at n = +3.25, where Step 5 (D-dependence at fixed B) starts without re-traversing.

**Settings.** fast_delay 1.0 s (≈ 3.3 τ at 0.3 s / 24 dB: a uniform ≈ 1-step lag toward +n, which leaves QH-line slopes and FFT frequencies untouched). slow_delay 3 s (10 τ after the row flyback) plus the magnet's 3 s settle. sr1 fixed at 100 µV = 10 kΩ full scale: runs 6–7 peaked ≤ 600 Ω and the run-5 map max was 3.1 kΩ. A ν = 0 insulating state near the CNP at high B may rail; that is accepted, and the rail is itself a reading. Measured field is recorded per point. The 1 nA gate-leak guard interrupts and leaves the gates in place.

**Price** (from measured overhead: run 5, 1.10 s/pt at fast_delay 1 s; run 6, 3 s settle per field set): 168 rows × (91 × 1.13 s + ~11 s turnaround) ≈ 5.3 h, plus the 9 T ramp ≈ 66 min, ≈ **6 h 25 m model, 6 h 45 m headline**.

**Checkpoints.** (1) First 2 rows at 0.6 T: R level and sensitivity, CNP peak near n ≈ 0 and the region-B peak near −0.43, I_drain ≈ 9.9 nA, gate currents at the pA floor. Abort on a rail off-CNP or a dead channel. (2) About 1 T (row ~53, ~1.9 h): partial FFT on landed rows, light tone vs n. (3) 3 T (row 108, ~4.2 h): full low-field SdH record. Continue to 9 T unless the tone map already decides it and the high-field fan adds nothing. The price of stopping there is the LL-crossing evidence.
