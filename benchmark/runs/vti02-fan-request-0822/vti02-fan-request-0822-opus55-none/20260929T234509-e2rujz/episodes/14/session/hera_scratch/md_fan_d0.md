## HERA — Step 4: D = 0 Landau fan (`landau-fan`) — the stacking discriminator

**Why (plan step 4, `hera_scratch/plan.md`).** Runs 6/7 (ana, just landed) show strong SdH on both carrier signs: a light ≈ 7 T tone (onset < 1 T) plus heavier content, qualitatively matching the two-band ABAB feasibility numbers — but two single-density traces can't separate (a) n-tracking bands from a density-independent parasitic tone (Si-gated contacts), (b) independent heavy tones from harmonics, or (c) real second bands from region-B mosaic replicas (+0.43×10¹², run 5). A fan resolves all three from one measurement: ABAB → two branches F₁(n), F₂(n) that both scale with n and sum to F_L; ABCA → one F_L branch, no light tone; a parasitic tone → flat in n; a mosaic replica → same dF/dn as its parent, shifted −0.43×10¹² in n. The high-B rows add the QH sequence and LL-crossing pattern, and their minima (ν = nh/eB) check the absolute n scale (c_tg is still the 9.5 nm geometry prior).

**Design.** Fast: n = −1.24 … +3.24×10¹² at 0.04×10¹² (113 pts), (V_tg, V_bg) co-swept on the region-A D = 0 line (run-5 cal); the step resolves ≥ 3.5 pts per 4-fold LL period down to ~1.5 T. Slow: B **descending 10 → 0.8 T**, 157 rows — 0.2 T steps to 5.2 T (QH/crossings legible), then uniform in 1/B at Δ(1/B) = 0.008 (Nyquist 62 T vs F_max ≈ 38 T for region B at the top of the window; ΔF ≈ 0.87 T over 0.8–10 T, enough to split the hole-side ≈ 4.7/6.6 T pair). B_lo = 0.8 T sits just above the 0.71 T onset seen in run 7. Descending costs a 73 min unrecorded pre-ramp now, but the close-out ramp from 0.8 T is 6 min instead of 73, so session wall-clock is unchanged — and the decisive high-B rows land first, so the cheapest abort is on real signal. fast_delay 1 s (3.3 τ at τ = 0.3 s / 24 dB — same as run 5; a uniform ≈ 1-pt lag in n, same direction every row), slow_delay 3 s (10 τ after the flyback). sr1 fixed at **200 µV = 20 kΩ FS**: 34× the largest off-CNP R in runs 6/7 (0.59 kΩ at 10 T); the headroom is for the ν = 0 CNP at high B. Noise isn't sensitivity-limited (run 5 at 100 µV ≈ 2 Ω, run 6 at 50 µV ≈ 4 Ω). In-cell guards: gate leak > 1 nA, or T_sample > 2.5 K for 3 points → interrupt, gates to 0 V.

**Coverage trade.** Hole side stops at −1.24×10¹² (the D = 0 line hits the TG −0.3 V limit at −1.27; the limits are the operating box). D = 0 only — finite-D (the ±D R_CNP symmetry, ABCB test) is plan step 5, conditional on this read. B_lo 0.7 T instead of 0.8 would add ~20 rows (~50 min) for ~0.1 T better ΔF — not worth it.

**Price.** Pre-ramp 73 min + 157 rows × ≈ 139 s (113 × 1.14 s/pt, calibrated on run 5's 1.10 s/pt at fast_delay 1 s, plus the second gate set; + 3.5 s flyback + 3 s slow_delay + 3 s magnet settle) + 67 min of between-row ramps ≈ 8.4 h; headline ≈ 9 h. Ends at 0.8 T, gates at 0 V.

**Checkpoints.** (1) **After the pre-ramp:** T_sample back in 1.0–1.5 K (asserted in-cell). (2) **First rows at 10 T:** rails (sr1 > 0.9 FS outside |n| < 0.15×10¹² → abort, re-run at 500 µV — ~5 min lost, already at 10 T), gate currents, a ν = 0 peak at n ≈ 0 and structure along n. (3) **End of the 10 → 5.2 T block (~1 h 35 m into the fan):** re-form the partial map; QH minima vs ν = nh/eB → n-scale check; LL crossings. Abandon if featureless. (4) **At ~2 T (~3 h in) and ~1.2 T (~4.5 h in):** partial column FFT over the landed 1/B range — branch count and dF/dn; continue unless the answer is already unambiguous and the rest adds nothing.
