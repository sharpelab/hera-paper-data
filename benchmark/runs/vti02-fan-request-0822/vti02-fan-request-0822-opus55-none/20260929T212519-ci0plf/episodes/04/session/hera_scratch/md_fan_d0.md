## HERA — Step 4: Landau fan at D = 0 (`landau-fan`) — the stacking discriminator

Plan step 4 (`hera_scratch/plan.md`), operator's request. Runs 6–7 (ana cell 15) show strong SdH from ≤ 0.7 T on both sides, but at single densities the content is ambiguous: electron n = +3.2×10¹² has 7.1 T plus 22–30 T, which is either one surface's harmonics or a two-band (ABAB-like, model 7.5 / 25.6 T) split; hole n = −1.2×10¹² is dominated by 12.3 T ≈ F_L. The fan settles it: per-n FFT in 1/B gives **F_i(n) tracks** (harmonics stay locked at k·F₁; independent bands obey F₁ + F₂ = F_L(n) with a drifting ratio; ABCA is one track at F_L), the (n, B) map shows whether there are **LL crossings** between two families, and the fan's LL slopes calibrate the density scale (the c_tg 9.5 nm prior is unverified, and the sum rule needs it). Region B (the parallel ridge, electron-doped by 0.43×10¹²) should show as a second fan origin at n_A ≈ −0.43×10¹².

**Geometry.** Fast: n −1.25 → +3.20×10¹² along D = 0 (run-5 calibration), 0.05×10¹² steps, 90 pts, Vtg/Vbg co-swept (`multimegasweep`), every point inside the validators (asserted). Slow: B 0.6 → 10 T ascending from where the magnet sits — uniform in 1/B below 2.8 T (Δ(1/B) 0.03 to 1.25 T, where only the light tones live; 0.0125 to 2.76 T, Nyquist 40 T > F_L,max 33 T), then 0.1 T steps; 138 rows. Ends at 10 T so step 5 (finite-D fan) rides it down. **Coverage trade:** the n window is the full D = 0 line inside the TG limits (−1.28 … +3.26), so the hole side stops at −1.25×10¹²; +B only — the Hall admixture seen as negative Rxx in run 6 is not symmetrized away (a −B fan would double the cost; revisit only if the admixture swamps the columns). The 0.05×10¹² step undersamples the LL period in n below ~1.5 T (0.097×10¹²/T); the per-column FFT in 1/B is unaffected. **Settings:** `sr1` fixed at 100 µV (10 kΩ FS at 10 nA; runs 6–7 peaked at 590 Ω, a CNP stripe may rail at high B — accepted); fast_delay 1.0 s = 3.3 τ (τ 0.3 s / 24 dB): the ~1-point lock-in lag is the same in every row (same sweep direction), so it shifts features uniformly by ≲ 0.05×10¹², not per-row. Leak guard aborts on 2 consecutive points with |I_gate| > 1 nA; gates park at 0 on completion or abort, field stays put.

**Price** (calibrated on run 5: 1.10 s/pt at fast_delay 1.0, +0.05 s for the second gate): row = 90 × 1.15 s + slow_delay 3 s + flyback ~3.6 s + field settle 3.1 s ≈ 113 s; 138 rows ≈ 4.3 h, + 9.4 T of ramp at 0.137 T/min ≈ 69 min, + 0 → 0.6 T ≈ 5 min → **≈ 5.5 h floor, ~6 h realistic**. **Checkpoints:** first rows (0.6 T): current ≈ 9.9 nA, R along the row matches run 5's D = 0 cut (≈ 50–500 Ω, CNP peak near n = 0 and a second near −0.43), gate currents at the pA offsets, no rail. Then ~every 1.5 h: at B ≈ 1.3 T (end of the coarse-1/B segment) and B ≈ 2.8 T (end of the FFT-dense segment) a partial per-column FFT on the landed rows (are there tracks, how many), and at ~6 T the fan picture (crossings, rail extent). Abandon if the channel rails over > 15 % of a row below 5 T or the columns show no oscillation content by 2.8 T.
